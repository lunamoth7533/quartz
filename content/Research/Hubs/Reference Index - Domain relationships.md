## Relationship register

Curated cross-domain relationships: the relation type, the two domains, the concept articles that carry the connection, and the hub where the statement is developed. The vocabulary is deliberately small: `supplies` (this domain provides material the other builds on), `places` (locates mechanisms in structure), `models` (formalises phenomena), `measures` (supplies measurement or inference rules), `applies` (applies constructs clinically), `shares` (shared construct, method or differential), `part-of`, `associated`. An entry here is a curation statement about how two domains fit together, not a causal claim: follow the linked articles and their source blocks for what is actually established.
