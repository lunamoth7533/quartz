# Circadian rhythms and sleep

**Definition.** Sleep timing is regulated by circadian rhythms interacting with homeostatic sleep drive. [[F14 NINDS Brain basics understanding sleep#^f14-rhythm|Brain Basics: Understanding Sleep]]

## Supported claims

- Sleep cycles through REM and non-REM stages with different patterns of brain activity. [[F14 NINDS Brain basics understanding sleep#^f14-stages|Brain Basics: Understanding Sleep]]
- Sleep regulation combines a circadian clock with homeostatic pressure, so timing and duration are separate questions. [[F14 NINDS Brain basics understanding sleep#^f14-rhythm|Brain Basics: Understanding Sleep]]

## Limitation or common misconception

The two-process description is a model of average behaviour; shift work, illness, medication and light exposure complicate it.
