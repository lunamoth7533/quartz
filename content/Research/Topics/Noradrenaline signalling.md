# Noradrenaline signalling

**Definition.** Noradrenaline is a catecholamine transmitter associated with arousal and alertness across the brain and body. [[F06 OpenStax Cells of the nervous system#^f06-transmitters|Cells of the Nervous System]]

## Supported claims

- Too little or too much noradrenaline impairs prefrontal function in the reviewed model. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- Noradrenaline benefit is attributed to postsynaptic alpha-2A receptors. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Arousal is easy to measure badly, and much of the mechanistic evidence is animal-based.
