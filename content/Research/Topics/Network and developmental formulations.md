# Network and developmental formulations

## Definition

Formulation is a structured account of why this presentation, for this person, now. Network formulations describe interactions among symptoms; developmental formulations describe how risk and experience accumulate over time.

## How it works

**Network account.** Symptoms are treated as interacting components rather than indicators of a latent disorder, predicting sudden transitions and treating comorbidity as network overlap. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-claim|A network theory of mental disorders]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|A network theory of mental disorders]]

**Developmental account.** Risk and protective factors interact across development, with sensitive periods and overlapping maturational processes shaping when difficulties appear. [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-overlap|The basics of brain development]] [[P16261181 Hensch 2005 Critical period plasticity#^p16261181-trigger|Critical period plasticity]]

**Social context.** Stigma and disadvantage operate as structural causes of inequality, which makes them part of the formulation rather than background. [[P23488505 Hatzenbuehler 2013 Stigma as fundamental cause#^p23488505-argument|Stigma as a fundamental cause of health inequalities]]

**Status.** Network theory is a research programme with early support; developmental formulation is standard clinical practice with varying evidence per element. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-status|A network theory of mental disorders]]

## Evidence and status

Formulation improves communication and individualises care; its testability is weaker than that of categorical diagnosis, which is a genuine trade-off rather than a defect of either approach.

> [!info]- In depth: two frameworks that change how a presentation is described
> **The network formulation.** The network approach treats symptoms as interacting components rather than as indicators of one latent disorder, so the disorder is the network; it predicts hysteresis and sudden transitions, and treats comorbidity as overlap between symptom networks rather than as distinct co-occurring entities. The framework is a theoretical proposal with early empirical support rather than a replacement for classification in clinical use, and its own literature states that limitation. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-claim|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-status|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-limit|Network theory]]
>
> **What it changes in practice.** A network description makes the strength of individual symptom-to-symptom links the object of study, so two people with identical symptom counts can differ in which links are strong - and an intervention can target a high-centrality symptom rather than a diagnosis. That is a design and theory choice that changes what a study measures, not a settled clinical method.
>
> **The developmental formulation.** Brain development is organised into overlapping processes - neurogenesis, migration, differentiation, synaptogenesis, pruning and myelination - that overlap in time across regions rather than completing in sequence, which is why structure-function relations shift with age. Early experience interacts with genetically guided development, so variation in outcome is expected rather than exceptional. [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-processes|The basics of brain development]] [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-overlap|The basics of brain development]] [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-experience|The basics of brain development]]
>
> **Windows have machinery.** In primary visual cortex, activity-dependent development is triggered by functional maturation of local inhibitory connections, and structural consolidation of competing inputs involves proteolytic reorganisation of the extracellular matrix, with reactivation proposed as a research strategy for recovery. Sensitive periods are therefore windows with identifiable components rather than convenient ages. [[P16261181 Hensch 2005 Critical period plasticity#^p16261181-trigger|Critical period plasticity]] [[P16261181 Hensch 2005 Critical period plasticity#^p16261181-consolidation|Critical period plasticity]] [[P16261181 Hensch 2005 Critical period plasticity#^p16261181-reactivation|Critical period plasticity]]
>
> **Where a developmental claim stops.** The developmental literature described here concerns typical development and generalises across samples; it does not transfer directly to a clinical claim about one person, and timing findings from one system do not transfer to another. [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-limit|The basics of brain development]] [[P19794405 Tau 2010 Normal development of brain circuits#^p19794405-timing|Normal development of brain circuits]]
>
> **Why the two belong together.** Both frameworks replace a single hidden cause with a structured process - interacting symptoms in one case, interacting developmental processes in the other - and both make the same demand: name the mechanism and the level at which it is claimed. [[P24808851 Leitner 2014 Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD#^p24808851-caution-label|AI appraisal: Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD]]

> [!info]- Further depth: two frameworks that replace a hidden cause with a process
> The network approach treats symptoms as interacting components, so the disorder is the network, comorbidity is overlap between networks and hysteresis plus sudden transitions are predictions - a theoretical proposal with early empirical support rather than a clinical replacement. Developmental formulations describe overlapping processes that overlap in time across regions, with early experience interacting with genetically guided development so variation is expected, and sensitive windows have identified machinery rather than mere ages. Both frameworks demand that a claim name its mechanism and level.
