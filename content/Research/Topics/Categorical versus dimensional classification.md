# Categorical versus dimensional classification

## Definition

Categorical classification assigns people to groups; dimensional classification scores them along continuous variables. Most psychopathology measures are dimensional in their raw form, while clinical systems use categories with thresholds.

## How it works

**Problems with categories.** Arbitrary thresholds, unclear boundaries between disorders, frequent co-occurrence, within-disorder heterogeneity and diagnostic instability are the documented weaknesses that motivated dimensional models. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]]

**HiTOP.** Syndromes that covary are grouped into spectra, and phenomena are characterised dimensionally, which is intended to reduce heterogeneity and map comorbidity explicitly. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]]

**RDoC.** The NIMH framework organises research around dimensions of functioning across units of analysis from genes to self-report, explicitly as a research approach rather than a clinical system. [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-purpose|Research Domain Criteria]] [[F122 NIMH RDoC constructs#^f122-domains|Definitions of the RDoC Domains and Constructs]]

**Network alternatives.** A further proposal treats symptoms as interacting components rather than indicators of a latent disorder, predicting sudden transitions and treating comorbidity as network overlap. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-claim|A network theory of mental disorders]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|A network theory of mental disorders]]

**Current practice.** Clinical systems remain categorical because they support decisions and communication; research increasingly uses dimensional measures alongside them. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-purpose|HiTOP]]

## Evidence and status

The empirical case for dimensionality is strong for many constructs; how to translate dimensions into decisions is unresolved, which is why hybrid models are proposed.

> [!info]- In depth: the trade-offs on both sides of a long argument
> **What categories buy.** Categories compress information into a shared label, they let clinical services and treatment trials define who is being studied, and the evidence base in most of this library is organised by them. ICD-11's clinical descriptions provide essential features, boundaries and course qualifiers for each category precisely so that the label means the same thing to different clinicians. [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-structure|ICD-11 CDDR]] [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-purpose|ICD-11 CDDR]]
>
> **What categories cost.** Because a threshold has to fall somewhere, categories produce arbitrary boundaries, frequent co-occurrence and within-disorder heterogeneity, and people near a boundary can move between labels across assessments. HiTOP's proposal is to group syndromes that covary into dimensional spectra so that heterogeneity is represented rather than averaged away, with the explicit framing that this is a research programme rather than an installed clinical system. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-purpose|HiTOP]]
>
> **A third option: the network approach.** The network approach treats symptoms as interacting components rather than as indicators of one latent disorder - the disorder is the network - and predicts hysteresis and sudden transitions, treating comorbidity as overlap between symptom networks rather than as distinct co-occurring entities. It is a theoretical proposal with early empirical support, not a replacement for classification in clinical use, and its own literature discusses its limitations. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-claim|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-status|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-limit|Network theory]]
>
> **Dimensional alternatives are not assumption-free.** A spectrum has to be measured, and comparing scores across groups or over time assumes measurement invariance - configural, metric and scalar constraints that are testable and often untested. Partial or absent invariance changes what a comparison means, whether the comparison is categorical or dimensional. [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-definition|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-levels|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-practice|Measurement invariance]]
>
> **Where the argument actually matters for a reader.** Descriptive claims such as 'these features cluster together' survive either framework; claims about thresholds, prevalence and diagnostic stability are framework-dependent; and treatment evidence is usually organised by whatever definition a trial used. Naming the model a statement depends on is the practical discipline this note asks for. [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-scope|RDoC]]

> [!info]- Further depth: what each framework buys and costs
> Categories compress information, let services and trials define who is studied, and organise most of the evidence base; their costs are arbitrary thresholds, unclear boundaries, frequent co-occurrence, within-disorder heterogeneity and diagnostic instability. Dimensional alternatives organise covarying syndromes into spectra as a research effort, and the network approach treats comorbidity as overlap between symptom networks with hysteresis and sudden transitions as predictions - a theoretical proposal with early support rather than an installed system. Dimensional models are not assumption-free: comparing scores still requires invariance. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-status|Network theory]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-levels|Measurement invariance]]
>
> **Each system wins where its assumptions hold.** Categories communicate efficiently, match service and regulatory structures, and work where a qualitative break exists or is a useful convention; dimensions preserve information, avoid threshold artefacts, and work where variation is continuous with graded risk. The documented pathologies - arbitrary thresholds, heterogeneity within categories, co-occurrence across them - are the category system's costs; complexity and communication burden are the dimension system's. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]] [[F47 OpenStax Psychology 2e psychological disorders#^f47-cultural|What Are Psychological Disorders]]
>
> **The hybrid is the current settlement, not a truce of convenience.** Practice uses categorical diagnoses for communication and service decisions while research increasingly measures dimensions, and several classifications now add dimensional measures beneath categorical diagnoses. The HiTOP-style research spectra are the dimensional research programme's concrete output; the categorical system persists because its functions - coding, coverage, communication - are real. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]] [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-matrix|Research Domain Criteria]]
>
