# Neuroimmune interactions and microglia

## Definition

Microglia are resident immune cells of the central nervous system that survey the parenchyma and respond to injury, infection and other perturbations. They are part of the neuroimmune interface, alongside circulating immune signals that reach the brain.

## How it works

**Surveillance.** In vivo imaging showed microglia in uninjured cortex continually extending and retracting processes to sample their surroundings. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]]

**Rapid response.** Barrier disruption produced immediate, focal activation, switching behaviour from surveillance to shielding the affected site. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-response|Microglial surveillance]]

**Signalling.** Peripheral immune signals reach the brain through several routes and produce coordinated changes in behaviour, sleep and appetite known as sickness behaviour. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-sickness|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]

**Reach into disease models.** Sustained inflammatory signalling has been proposed as a contributor to depressive symptoms in some individuals, presented as a hypothesis with proposed mechanisms. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-depression|From inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-mechanism|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]

## Evidence and status

Microglial surveillance and injury response are demonstrated directly in animals; human evidence for immune contributions to psychiatric conditions is correlational plus experimental challenge studies, and effect sizes vary.

> [!info]- In depth: surveillance, activation and the limits of the marker literature
> **Resting microglia are not resting.** Two-photon imaging in living cortex showed that microglia in their so-called resting state continuously sample their territory with motile processes, and that blood-brain barrier disruption produced immediate, focal microglial activation that switched their behaviour. Surveillance is the baseline state, which is why 'activation' needs a specified trigger and phenotype. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-response|Microglial surveillance]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-limit|Microglial surveillance]]
>
> **Peripheral immune signals reach the brain and change behaviour.** Peripheral immune signals produce sickness behaviour - a coordinated set of changes in activity, appetite, sleep and mood - and prolonged inflammatory signalling is proposed to contribute to depressive symptoms in vulnerable individuals through cytokine signalling, altered tryptophan metabolism and vagal transmission. The routes are specified, which makes the proposal testable even where it is not established. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-sickness|Inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-depression|Inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-mechanism|Inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-limit|Inflammation to sickness and depression]]
>
> **Gut-to-brain signalling overlaps here.** The microbiota review lists immune signalling among its communication routes and treats microbial metabolites as candidate mediators while emphasising that many findings remain correlational with causal direction unresolved. The immune arm is therefore the best-specified of the microbiome routes, not a settled one. [[P31460832 Cryan 2019 Microbiota-gut-brain axis#^p31460832-routes|The microbiota-gut-brain axis]] [[P31460832 Cryan 2019 Microbiota-gut-brain axis#^p31460832-status|The microbiota-gut-brain axis]]
>
> **The barrier shapes what can act.** The interface is selective rather than absolute: gases diffuse freely while ions, nutrients and larger molecules depend on specific transporters, and regulated transport, leakage and injury are distinct phenomena. A circulating cytokine's effect on the brain therefore depends on a route, not on diffusion alone. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood-brain barrier]] [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-permeability|The blood-brain barrier]] [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-glia|Nervous Tissue]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Specify the cell, the mediator, the model system and the direction of inference; 'neuroinflammation' spans everything from microglial surveillance to cytokine-driven sickness behaviour, and the popular version of the term sits at the far end of that range. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-limit|Microglial surveillance]] [[P22749156 Munkholm 2013 Cytokines in bipolar disorder#^p22749156-caution|Cytokines in bipolar disorder]]
>
> **Activation is a repertoire, not a switch.** Microglial states differ in what they do - phagocytosis, antigen presentation, cytokine release, trophic support - and the two-state activated/resting picture from early literature has given way to a spectrum of context-dependent phenotypes. That refinement matters for interpretation: a marker of "activation" in human tissue or imaging identifies that something in the microglial state changed, not which behaviour changed or whether it is harmful. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-response|Microglial surveillance]]
>
> **Sickness behaviour is the model that anchors the psychiatric claims.** The inflammation-to-behaviour literature works because the sickness-behaviour syndrome is well characterised: immune activation produces coordinated changes in activity, appetite, sleep and social behaviour, with defined signalling routes. The psychiatric extension - that some depressive symptoms are maintained by inflammatory signalling in some individuals - is a hypothesis with supportive subgroup evidence, and the subset framing exists precisely because population-wide claims failed. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-sickness|From inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-depression|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]
>
