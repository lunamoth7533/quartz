# Efficacy versus tolerability

**Definition.** Efficacy is how well a treatment reduces the target problem under study; tolerability is the burden and side effects it creates. [[P42134365 Nourredine 2026 ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis#^p42134365-tolerability|ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis]]

## Supported claims

- Dose-effect analyses treat efficacy and discontinuation due to adverse events as separate outcomes, with dose-dependent dropout for amphetamines and methylphenidate. [[P42134365 Nourredine 2026 ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis#^p42134365-tolerability|ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis]]
- In adults, atomoxetine and guanfacine had lower acceptability than placebo in a component network meta-analysis. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-acceptability|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]
- Amphetamines were less well tolerated than placebo in both children and adults in an earlier network meta-analysis. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|ADHD medication network meta-analysis]]

> [!info]- In depth: two axes that trade off, measured on different scales
> **Two primary axes.** Efficacy summarises symptom reduction; tolerability summarises adverse effects and discontinuation. A network meta-analysis of ADHD medications found stimulants and atomoxetine more efficacious than placebo on core symptoms while most components were similar to placebo on all-cause discontinuation, with atomoxetine and guanfacine showing lower acceptability - an efficacy–tolerability split within one class comparison. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]] [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-acceptability|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]
>
> **Rankings are not decisions.** Network estimates are averages with uncertainty, and the sources here caution that the choice among options depends on the outcome that matters to the person and on contraindications the trials did not measure. [[P27913917 Network meta-analysis introduction#^p27913917-interpretation|Network meta-analysis: an introduction for clinicians]]
>
> **Certainty varies by outcome.** Confidence in ADHD efficacy estimates ranged between very low and moderate in the adult synthesis, which means two outcomes from the same trials can carry different evidential weight. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-limit|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Report efficacy and tolerability separately, with the outcome scale and the comparator; combined single statements ('drug X is better') hide the trade-off that matters clinically. [[Trial endpoints, benefit and harms]]

> [!info]- Reference: two outcomes that must be read together
> **Separate endpoints.** Efficacy describes benefit on a defined outcome; tolerability describes the burden of adverse effects and discontinuation. A treatment can be effective and poorly tolerated, or well tolerated and ineffective. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]]
>
> **Rater dependence.** Efficacy estimates change with who rates the outcome: in the ADHD network meta-analysis, effects on clinician ratings and teacher ratings differed substantially. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]]
>
> **Comparator dependence.** Effect estimates are relative to a specified comparator, so results against placebo and against an active drug answer different questions. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-stimulants|Comparative interventions for ADHD]]
>
> **Ranking is not prescribing.** Group-level rankings have wide uncertainty intervals and describe average populations, which is why they are not individual guidance. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-caution-choice|AI appraisal: ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]]
>
> **Synthesis caution.** Network meta-analysis depends on the transitivity assumption, so indirect comparisons rest on the similarity of the studies combined. [[P27913917 Network meta-analysis introduction#^p27913917-assumption|Network meta-analysis]]
>
> **Why the two axes trade off mechanically.** The same receptor breadth that produces benefit produces burden: a drug acting across several systems pays for its target effect with effects elsewhere, so efficacy and tolerability are not independent properties that a better drug simply maximises and minimises. Dose-effect modelling makes the trade visible - efficacy rises with dose while discontinuation for adverse effects rises too - so the clinical question is where on the trade-off the balance sits for an outcome that matters, not which drug wins one axis outright. [[P42134365 Nourredine 2026 ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis#^p42134365-doseeffect|ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis]]
>
> **What dropout does and does not measure.** Discontinuation is the trial-friendly tolerability endpoint: it is countable and comparable. It undercounts effects people endure without leaving the trial, and it conflates intolerance with decision changes unrelated to adverse effects, so a clean tolerability result is evidence about persistence, not about the absence of burden. Instruments that ask about specific effects measure burden directly but are less comparable across trials. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-acceptability|Comparative interventions for ADHD]]
>
> **Certainty labels belong to outcomes, not drugs.** In the adult synthesis, confidence ranged from very low to moderate depending on comparison and outcome, so a single sentence like "drug X is well supported" flattens a table in which efficacy on one rater's scale and acceptability on another carry different weights. The honest summary names the outcome, the comparator and the certainty grade together. [[Trial endpoints, benefit and harms]] [[Meta-analysis and review limits]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** This article is the pharmacology domain's application of the methods domain's outcome-measurement discipline: raters, comparators and endpoints are methodological choices first and clinical facts only after the design is understood. [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]] [[Measurement validity and reliability]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Trials measure tolerability mainly through dropout, which undercounts bothersome effects that people endure rather than report.
