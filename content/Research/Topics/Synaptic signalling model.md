# Synaptic signalling model

**Definition.** The synaptic signalling model describes release, receptor binding, signal termination and modulation at chemical synapses. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-release|Communication Between Neurons]]

## Supported claims

- Acetylcholine, dopamine, serotonin, noradrenaline, GABA and glutamate are used across systems with different associated functions. [[F06 OpenStax Cells of the nervous system#^f06-transmitters|Cells of the Nervous System]]
- The postsynaptic effect depends on the receptor, so the same transmitter can excite or inhibit. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]]
- Electrical synapses formed by gap junctions are much less common than chemical synapses and much faster. [[F06 OpenStax Cells of the nervous system#^f06-electrical|Cells of the Nervous System]]

> [!info]- In depth: the standard model, its four assumptions, and where each one breaks
> **The model in one paragraph.** A presynaptic action potential opens calcium channels; vesicles fuse; transmitter crosses the cleft and binds postsynaptic receptors; the postsynaptic effect depends on receptor identity; clearance by reuptake and enzymes ends the signal. This account is the reference frame against which every other mechanism in the domain is described, and it is accurate for fast chemical synapses in the preparation where it was established. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-release|Communication Between Neurons]] [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]] [[F115 Neuroscience Online transmitter release#^f115-quanta|Mechanisms of Neurotransmitter Release]]
>
> **Assumption 1: transmission is synaptic and point-to-point.** Volume transmission breaks this: peptidergic and gaseous messengers are released at sites that are not aligned with a single postsynaptic density, and peptides can act on nearby targets. For those systems the model is the wrong shape rather than an approximation. [[F107 Neuroscience Online neuropeptides and nitric oxide#^f107-transmission|Neuropeptides and Nitric Oxide]]
>
> **Assumption 2: an action potential is required.** Release depends on calcium availability and on the state of the release machinery, so the same action potential can produce different amounts of transmitter, and some cells release transmitter in proportion to graded input without a spike threshold at all. [[F115 Neuroscience Online transmitter release#^f115-requirements|Mechanisms of Neurotransmitter Release]] [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-caution-summation|AI synthesis: Communication Between Neurons]]
>
> **Assumption 3: transmitter identity determines effect.** Receptor identity determines sign, so a transmitter can be excitatory at one synapse and inhibitory at another, or slow instead of fast. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]] [[F106 Neuroscience Online amino acid transmitters#^f106-spatial|Amino Acid Transmitters]]
>
> **Assumption 4: the circuit's wiring fixes its function.** Neuromodulators change the properties of neurons and synapses, so the same anatomical network can produce different functional outputs, and different parameter sets can produce similar outputs - degeneracy. Wiring diagram and function are related but not identical. [[P23040802 Marder 2012 Neuromodulation#^p23040802-reconfigure|Neuromodulation]] [[P23040802 Marder 2012 Neuromodulation#^p23040802-degeneracy|Neuromodulation]]
>
> **What the model still buys.** For fast amino-acid and monoamine transmission in the systems where it was developed, the model predicts quantitative relationships - quantal amplitude, calcium dependence, receptor pharmacology - that can be tested with precision. The right reading is that it is a strong local theory with a defined scope, and that the failures above occur exactly where the theory's assumptions are violated rather than at random. [[F115 Neuroscience Online transmitter release#^f115-limit|Mechanisms of Neurotransmitter Release]] [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-limit|Communication Between Neurons]]

> [!info]- Reference: the model in full, with its assumptions exposed
> **Seven steps.** The standard model runs: synthesis of transmitter; packaging into vesicles; calcium-dependent release; receptor binding on the postsynaptic membrane; postsynaptic ion flow or second-messenger action; clearance by reuptake or enzymatic breakdown; and modulation by other inputs. Every step is a place where drugs and disease can act. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-release|Communication Between Neurons]] [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]] [[F108 Neuroscience Online acetylcholine#^f108-inactivation|Acetylcholine transmission]]
>
> **What the model gets right.** Each step has been demonstrated directly in identified preparations: calcium dependence and quantal release at the neuromuscular junction, receptor-determined postsynaptic effects, and defined clearance mechanisms. [[F115 Neuroscience Online transmitter release#^f115-calcium|Transmitter release]] [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]]
>
> **Where it misleads.** It describes a point-to-point contact, but transmission includes volume effects, extrasynaptic receptors, retrograde signalling by endocannabinoids and gas messengers, and neuromodulators that change the properties of whole circuits rather than one synapse. [[P26698193 Lu 2016 Endogenous cannabinoid system#^p26698193-retrograde|Endogenous cannabinoid system]] [[F107 Neuroscience Online neuropeptides and nitric oxide#^f107-transmission|Neuropeptides and nitric oxide]] [[P23040802 Marder 2012 Neuromodulation#^p23040802-reconfigure|Neuromodulation]]
>
> **Consequence for explanation.** If the same transmitter can act at different receptors with different timescales and be modulated by circuit state, then 'increasing' or 'decreasing' a transmitter is a description of an intervention, not a prediction of its effect. [[P23040802 Marder 2012 Neuromodulation#^p23040802-state|Neuromodulation]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** The synaptic signalling model is the shared frame the neurochemistry and pharmacology domains work within - transmitter identity, receptor type and clearance are the three levers those domains pull - and its stated assumptions are the constraints that keep claims in those domains consistent. ## Limitation or common misconception

Transmitter names describe molecules rather than moods, and the same molecule does different jobs in different circuits.
