# Bipolar genetics and polygenic risk

**Definition.** Genomic studies of bipolar disorder find many common variants of small effect, alongside differences related to ascertainment and subtype. [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-ascertainment|Bipolar disorder genomics]]

## Supported claims

- A multi-ancestry analysis of 158,036 cases and 2.8 million controls identified 298 loci and 36 credible genes. [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-loci|Bipolar disorder genomics]]
- Genetic architecture differed by ascertainment strategy and bipolar subtype. [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-ascertainment|Bipolar disorder genomics]]
- Risk architecture is statistical and does not predict an individual's course. [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-caution-prediction|AI synthesis: Bipolar disorder genomics]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Risk architecture is not determinism, and genetic findings do not define the diagnosis.
