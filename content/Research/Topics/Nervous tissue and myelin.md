# Nervous tissue and myelin

**Definition.** Grey matter concentrates cell bodies and local processing while white matter contains long-range myelinated axons. [[F01 OpenStax Nervous system structure and function#^f01-matter|Basic Structure and Function of the Nervous System]]

## Supported claims

- In some axons a myelin sheath insulates the fibre and increases conduction speed, interrupted by the nodes of Ranvier. [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-myelin|Nervous Tissue]]
- Grey and white matter describe tissue composition rather than a clean functional split in real circuits. [[F01 OpenStax Nervous system structure and function#^f01-matter|Basic Structure and Function of the Nervous System]]

## Limitation or common misconception

A volume or thickness difference on a scan does not map onto 'more' or 'better' tissue.
