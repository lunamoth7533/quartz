# Theories of consciousness compared

## Definition

Several theories address how consciousness arises: functional accounts such as global workspace and higher-order theories, predictive-processing accounts, and causal-structure accounts such as integrated information theory and recurrent processing theory.

## How it works

**Groups.** Function-based theories explain consciousness in terms of what the system does - accessing, representing, predicting. Causal-structure theories identify it with how the system is organised. [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-target|The unfolding argument]]

**Competing predictions.** The adversarial collaboration approach sets out to test theories with paradigms designed so that their predictions diverge. The Cogitate Consortium (2025) tested integrated information theory (IIT) and global neuronal workspace theory (GNWT) in 256 human participants using fMRI, MEG and intracranial EEG, with preregistered divergent predictions on decoding location, maintenance profile and inter-areal connectivity. Results aligned with some predictions of each theory while substantially challenging key tenets of both: IIT was challenged by the absence of sustained gamma-band synchronisation within posterior cortex, and GNWT by the general lack of ignition at stimulus offset and limited prefrontal representation of certain conscious dimensions. These are challenges to the tested predictions in this paradigm, not definitive refutations of either theory. [[P40307561 Cogitate 2025 Adversarial consciousness#^p40307561-results|Cogitate 2025]]

**Problems.** Definitions of the target phenomenon differ, measures are theory-laden, and some frameworks are broad enough to accommodate many results. [[P20068583 Friston 2010 Free-energy principle#^p20068583-status|The free-energy principle]]

**Why it belongs in a reference library.** These debates are the clearest demonstration that theoretical commitments shape which measurements count as evidence, a point that generalises to psychiatric research. [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-debate|The unfolding argument]]

## Evidence and status

Empirical work continues, including paradigms designed for theory comparison; no theory is currently established, and this note is deliberately agnostic.

> [!info]- In depth: what comparing theories of consciousness requires
> **The theories make committed claims.** Integrated information theory begins from phenomenological axioms and translates them into postulates, defining quantities - notably integrated information - that are intended to measure how much a system's causal structure contributes to its own state; it commits to claims such as that feedforward systems cannot be conscious. Global neuronal workspace theory identifies conscious access with global broadcasting and late amplification ('ignition') across interconnected higher-order sensory, parietal and prefrontal networks. Predictions that exclude systems or patterns are what make a theory testable. [[P24811198 Oizumi 2014 Integrated information theory#^p24811198-axioms|Integrated information theory]] [[P24811198 Oizumi 2014 Integrated information theory#^p24811198-phi|Integrated information theory]] [[P24811198 Oizumi 2014 Integrated information theory#^p24811198-claim|Integrated information theory]]
>
> **The critique that frames the comparison.** A theoretical critique targets theories identifying consciousness with causal structure, using computability results to argue they are either false or unfalsifiable, and it is a contribution to an ongoing debate rather than a finding that settles the question. The practical consequence is that comparing theories means examining their exclusion criteria. [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-target|The unfolding argument]] [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-argument|The unfolding argument]] [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-debate|The unfolding argument]]
>
> **Breadth is the recurring problem.** A framework accommodating many theories can be difficult to falsify in specific cases, and predictive-coding algorithms differ substantially while sharing one idea, so evidence for one is not evidence for another. These are the same structural criticisms in different literatures. [[P20068583 Friston 2010 Free-energy principle#^p20068583-status|The free-energy principle]] [[P26809759 Spratling 2017 Predictive coding algorithms#^p26809759-plural|Predictive coding algorithms]] [[P26809759 Spratling 2017 Predictive coding algorithms#^p26809759-difference|Predictive coding algorithms]]
>
> **What discriminating evidence would look like.** Where two theories predict the same pattern, the informative study is the one built to separate them - a contrast whose outcome excludes at least one account - and model-comparison discipline requires reporting uncertainty rather than a winner. [[P31769410 Wilson 2019 Computational modelling rules#^p31769410-identifiability|Computational modelling rules]] [[P24811198 Oizumi 2014 Integrated information theory#^p24811198-limit|Integrated information theory]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Ask what each theory forbids, then whether the study could have detected the forbidden outcome. A comparison that cannot fail is a review of positions rather than a test. [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-limit|The unfolding argument]]
>
> **What the adversarial-collaboration format buys.** Because most consciousness measures are theory-laden, simple comparisons cannot separate frameworks. The Cogitate Consortium's adversarial collaboration illustrates the format's procedural contribution: theory proponents and a neutral consortium preregistered divergent predictions, expected outcomes and interpretation before data collection, and combined fMRI, MEG and intracranial EEG in 256 participants to test them. Its limits are also instructive: the study tested predictions within a specific visual paradigm, and the authors' own framing distinguishes predictions challenged from theories definitively rejected - a failed prediction can be absorbed by adjusting auxiliary assumptions or by restricting a theory's scope. [[P24811198 Oizumi 2014 Integrated information theory#^p24811198-claim|Integrated information theory]] [[P31078047 Doerig 2019 Unfolding argument#^p31078047-debate|The unfolding argument]]
>
> **The general lesson this note exists to carry.** Definitions of the target phenomenon differ across theories, so results do not transfer between them without a translation argument - the same level-of-analysis discipline applied to the hardest case. For a psychiatric reference library the value is the exported habit: state which definition, which measure, which theory before reading any claim about consciousness or about constructs measured by theory-laden instruments.
