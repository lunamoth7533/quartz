# Bipolar psychotherapy evidence

**Definition.** Psychological treatments for bipolar disorder are tested as additions to medication, usually as manualized protocols with named components. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]

## Supported claims

- Adjunctive manualized psychotherapy was linked to lower recurrence than control (OR 0.56). [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-recurrence|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
- CBT was linked to stabilizing depressive symptoms (SMD -0.32), with family and interpersonal therapy pointing the same way less certainly. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-symptoms|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
- Component analyses estimate average contributions; they do not identify what produced one person's improvement. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-caution-ingredients|AI synthesis: Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]

> [!info]- In depth: what adjunctive psychotherapy adds, and how the components were tested
> **The pooled result.** Across 20 two-group trials, adding manualised psychotherapy to medication was associated with lower recurrence than control (OR 0.56), and CBT was linked to stabilising depressive symptoms (SMD -0.32), with family and interpersonal therapy pointing the same way less certainly. Retention was higher for family or conjoint therapy and for brief psychoeducation. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-recurrence|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-symptoms|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-retention|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]]
>
> **The component analysis is the interesting part.** A component analysis of 13 therapy elements found that psychoeducation with supervised skills practice delivered in family or group sessions was linked to fewer recurrences than the same content delivered individually (OR 0.12) - a large difference in a comparison of formats rather than of whole protocols. Component analyses estimate average contributions across heterogeneous protocols and cannot identify which ingredient produced one person's improvement. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-caution-ingredients|AI appraisal: Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]]
>
> **The evidence base has stated limits.** The review is a systematic review and network meta-analysis of 39 randomised trials in outpatients, and its authors temper the conclusions for heterogeneity in populations, duration and follow-up. Reading the effect size without that sentence overstates the precision of the estimate. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-limit|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]]
>
> **Where it sits in care.** National guidance recommends structured psychological approaches alongside long-term pharmacological management, so psychotherapy is an adjunct in this literature rather than a substitute; the trials all sit on top of medication. [[F94 NICE Bipolar disorder CG185#^f94-longterm|Bipolar disorder CG185]] [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-treatment|Bipolar disorder]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Name the comparator, the format, the outcome and the follow-up when quoting a psychotherapy effect; the format difference in the component analysis shows how much the delivery model matters. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]]

> [!info]- Reference: adjunctive psychotherapy in bipolar disorder
> **What is tested.** Psychological treatments are studied as additions to medication, usually as manualised protocols with named components, which is what makes component-level questions answerable at all. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
>
> **Recurrence.** Adjunctive manualised psychotherapy was associated with lower recurrence than control (OR 0.56). [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-recurrence|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
>
> **Symptom outcomes.** Cognitive behavioural therapy was associated with stabilising depressive symptoms (SMD -0.32), with family and interpersonal approaches pointing the same way less certainly. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-symptoms|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
>
> **Guideline framing.** National guidance treats psychological and psychosocial interventions as part of longer-term management alongside medication rather than as substitutes for it. [[F94 NICE Bipolar disorder CG185#^f94-longterm|Bipolar disorder CG185]]
>
> **Limits.** Component analyses estimate average contributions across heterogeneous trials - populations, treatment duration and follow-up differ - and they cannot identify what produced one person's improvement. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-caution-ingredients|AI synthesis: Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]] [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-limit|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
>
> **Why format mattered more than content in the component analysis.** The largest component effect - psychoeducation with supervised skills practice in family or group sessions beating the same content delivered individually - says that how a therapy is delivered changed recurrence more than which named therapy was delivered. That is an uncomfortable result for brand-based comparisons and a productive one for design: the delivery model is itself an ingredient, and component analyses are the tool that surfaced it. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]
>
> **Retention is an outcome, not housekeeping.** The finding that family or conjoint formats and brief psychoeducation retained more participants is clinically load-bearing: a therapy that works but loses people delivers less than its effect size suggests. Trials that report retention beside effect sizes - as this review does - allow that computation; trials that do not, hide it. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-retention|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] [[Efficacy versus tolerability]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** This article and the phase-specific treatment article are the two halves of bipolar intervention evidence - psychological and pharmacological - read against the same phase structure and the same maintenance-trial difficulties, which is why their limitations sections sound alike. [[Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence]] [[Longitudinal and within-person inference]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Conclusions are tempered by heterogeneity in populations, treatment duration and follow-up, as the authors state.
