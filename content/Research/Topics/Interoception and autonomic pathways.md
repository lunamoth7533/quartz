# Interoception and autonomic pathways

## Definition

Interoception is the sensing of the physiological condition of the body, distinct from exteroception such as touch and proprioception. Autonomic pathways are the output side: the sympathetic and parasympathetic systems that regulate internal organs.

## How it works

**A distinct afferent system.** Converging evidence describes an interoceptive system carrying homeostatic afferent activity from tissues, with primary representation in the dorsal posterior insula, separate from cutaneous mechanoreception. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]]

**Re-representation.** A meta-representation in the right anterior insula is proposed as a basis for the subjective feeling of bodily state. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]]

**Central control.** Sites receiving visceral input interconnect with those controlling sympathetic and parasympathetic output and with endocrine and limbic circuitry, forming a central autonomic network. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-network|Central Control of the Autonomic Nervous System]] Thermoregulation illustrates the integration of autonomic, endocrine and behavioural responses. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Control of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

**Output branches.** Sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions differ in outflow and signalling, and most organs receive both. [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-outflow|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

## Evidence and status

Autonomic anatomy and physiology are well established; interoceptive representation maps are supported by converging evidence, largely from imaging and clinical studies. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-limit|Interoception]]

> [!info]- In depth: sensing the body's condition and controlling it
> **What interoception covers.** Interoception is the sensing of the physiological condition of the body - visceral state, temperature, pain, hunger, muscular and autonomic signals - carried by afferents that ascend in parallel with the classical exteroceptive pathways. The distinction that matters is that these signals report the body's internal state rather than the external world. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-neuron|Nervous Tissue]]
>
> **The insular route and its interpretation.** Interoceptive afferents are described as converging on insular cortex, which is why the insula appears in accounts of bodily awareness and of emotional experience. That claim is anatomical and functional at the group level; the further step - that interoceptive signals constitute a sense of self - is an interpretation offered by the review rather than a measured outcome. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]]
>
> **Autonomic control is central and distributed.** Pre-autonomic neurons sit in several populations and receive visceral input; thermoregulation illustrates how autonomic, endocrine and behavioural outputs are coordinated for a single physiological goal. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-network|Central Autonomic Control]] [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Autonomic Control]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Interoception claims should specify the signal (cardiac, respiratory, gastric), the measure (awareness task, evoked potential, self-report) and the level (afferent, insular representation, behaviour). Studies that use an interoceptive task to index 'emotional awareness' extend the construct well beyond what the measure captures. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-limit|Interoception]] [[Autonomic measurement and heart rate variability]]

> [!info]- Further depth: the afferent side of autonomic control
> The interoceptive system carries homeostatic afferent activity from all tissues separately from cutaneous mechanoreception and proprioception, with primary representation placed in the dorsal posterior insula and a re-representation in the right anterior insula proposed as a basis for the bodily sense of self. That mapping is a model supported by converging evidence rather than a measurement anyone can take. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-limit|Interoception]]
>
> Efferent control is organised centrally: thermoregulation demonstrates integration of autonomic, endocrine and behavioural responses to a single physiological demand, and the two autonomic divisions are cooperative as well as opposing, with most organs receiving both. Recorded signals such as heart rate variability reflect this integrated system, so a change in one channel rarely identifies one cause. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Autonomic Control]] [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-cooperation|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-caution-marker|AI appraisal: Heart rate variability metrics]]
>
> **The lamina-I route is what makes interoception distinct.** Small-diameter afferents from tissues ascend in the lamina-I spinothalamic channel and its cranial homologues, projecting to autonomic brainstem nuclei and then to the dorsal posterior insula - a pathway dedicated to physiological state rather than to the external world. That anatomical claim is what separates interoception from touch and from pain-as-sensation, and it is the reason the insula, not the postcentral gyrus, is the structure the literature points to. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]]
>
> **Two honest boundaries on the feeling-of-self extension.** First, the meta-representation proposal - right anterior insula as the basis for subjective bodily feeling - is the review's interpretation of converging imaging, demonstrated at group level. Second, clinical and individual claims built on it (for example, that a given emotion is a given bodily signal) require their own measurement, because the proposal does not deliver a readout. The construct is anatomically grounded and psychologically unfinished, and this note keeps both halves visible. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** The neuroendocrine domain's autonomic-measurement article is the applied inverse of this anatomy: heart-rate variability and related indices estimate the output arm from outside, while interoception describes the afferent and central arm; neither substitutes for the other, and both carry the same measurement cautions.
