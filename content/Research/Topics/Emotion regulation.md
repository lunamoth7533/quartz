# Emotion regulation

**Definition.** Emotion regulation covers the processes that shape which emotions arise, when, and how they are expressed and recovered from. [[F17 VA National Center for PTSD Complex PTSD history and definitions#^f17-dso|Complex PTSD: History and Definitions]]

## Supported claims

- Appraisal is one place regulation acts before physiology follows. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-appraisal|What Is Stress?]]
- Difficulties with affect regulation form one of the self-organization disturbances in complex PTSD. [[F17 VA National Center for PTSD Complex PTSD history and definitions#^f17-dso|Complex PTSD: History and Definitions]]

> [!info]- In depth: when regulation happens, and what it changes
> **Emotion as a multi-component process.** Emotion is described through physiological, cognitive, behavioural and expressive components that unfold over time and can be modified at different points, and the classic theories differ mainly in the order they assign to arousal and interpretation. Regulation strategies are classified partly by when they intervene: situation selection and modification act before the emotion develops, attentional deployment during it, reappraisal changes its meaning, and suppression acts on the response. [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion#^f44-components|Emotion]] [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion#^f44-theories|Emotion]] [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion#^f44-evidence|Emotion]]
>
> **Strategies have different profiles.** Reappraisal is generally associated with better affective and social outcomes than expressive suppression in the correlational literature, while suppression is associated with costs including impaired memory for the interaction and physiological load. Those associations are reliable in aggregate and heterogeneous in individuals, and the direction of causation is not established by them. [[F09 Noba Research designs#^f09-correlation|Research designs]]
>
> **Measurement triangulation.** Self-report, physiology and behaviour diverge systematically, so a regulation claim should specify which channel it measures; a person can report regulating successfully while physiology and behaviour tell a different story. [[Autonomic measurement and heart rate variability]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-caution-marker|AI synthesis: Heart rate variability metrics]]
>
> **Context dependency.** Whether a strategy helps depends on the situation, the goal and the person's repertoire; 'adaptive' and 'maladaptive' labels apply to strategy-situation fits rather than to strategies in general. [[Measurement validity and reliability]]

> [!info]- Reference: components, strategies and evidence
> **Components.** Emotion is described through physiological, cognitive, behavioural and expressive components, which is why different measures tell different stories. [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion#^f44-components|Emotion]]
>
> **Theories differ by ordering.** James-Lange, Cannon-Bard and Schachter-Singer theories disagree about the sequence of bodily response and interpretation, and the section presents them as competing rather than settled. [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion#^f44-theories|Emotion]]
>
> **Regulation strategies.** Reappraisal and suppression have different profiles: reappraisal is typically associated with better outcomes, while suppression is associated with costs in memory and social interaction. These are group-level patterns, not rules for individuals.
>
> **Bodily context.** Interoceptive signals contribute to emotional experience, which is why autonomic measures and emotion constructs are often studied together. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]]
>
> **Measurement caution.** Self-report, physiological and behavioural indices of emotion regulation correlate modestly; treating one as the construct overstates what any study shows. [[P18509902 Shiffman 2008 Ecological momentary assessment#^p18509902-rationale|Ecological momentary assessment]]

> [!info]- Further depth: when regulation happens, and what it changes
> Emotion has physiological, cognitive, behavioural and expressive components, which is why regulation can intervene at several points: before an appraisal forms, during the response, or in how the response is expressed. The complex-trauma literature treats affect regulation as one of the self-organization domains, and the phase-comparison meta-analysis found that phase-based, multi-phase and exposure interventions showed greater improvement on that domain - one of the few outcome-specific findings in a mostly null set of contrasts. [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion#^f44-components|Emotion]] [[F17 VA National Center for PTSD Complex PTSD history and definitions#^f17-dso|Complex PTSD: history and definitions]] [[P41949043 Lee 2026 Phase-based versus non-phase-based trauma therapy#^p41949043-outcomes|Phase-based versus non-phase-based trauma therapy]]
>
> **Where in the sequence regulation acts.** Regulation strategies differ by timing: situation selection and modification act before an emotion arises, attentional deployment shapes what gets processed, appraisal changes what the situation means, and response modulation acts on the output - suppressing expression, or in the pharmacological limit, changing physiology. The timing matters because the strategies have different costs and different evidence: response suppression is the best-studied failure case, reappraisal the best-studied success case, and the timing structure explains why. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-appraisal|What Is Stress?]]
>
> **Why the three measures disagree.** Self-report, physiological recording and observed behaviour measure different components - the felt emotion, the bodily response, the expression - and they are loosely coupled by design. A person can report calm with elevated cortisol, suppress expression without changing arousal, or change behaviour without reporting change. Divergence is therefore a finding about the construct's structure, not measurement error, and any claim that "regulation worked" must say which component it measured. [[F44 OpenStax Psychology 2e emotion]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** The neuroendocrine domain supplies the physiology half: appraisal's effect on the stress axis is the mechanism by which a psychological strategy becomes a cortisol value, and the measurement caveats from both domains apply to any claim connecting them. [[Stress response and the HPA axis]] [[Allostasis and chronic stress]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Regulation strategies are context-dependent: suppression, avoidance and reappraisal each help or cost depending on the situation.
