# Attention and executive function

**Definition.** Attention and executive function describe selection of information and the control processes that hold goals, inhibit habits and switch tasks. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

## Supported claims

- The prefrontal cortex is critical for screening distractions, inhibiting impulses and planning. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- Prefrontal regulation depends on dopamine and noradrenaline released by arousal state. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- How far executive dysfunction belongs in the definition of adult ADHD remains an open question. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Laboratory tasks are narrow proxies for daily functioning, and group performance says little about one person.
