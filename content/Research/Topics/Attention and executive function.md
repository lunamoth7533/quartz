# Attention and executive function

**Definition.** Attention and executive function describe selection of information and the control processes that hold goals, inhibit habits and switch tasks. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

## Supported claims

- The prefrontal cortex is critical for screening distractions, inhibiting impulses and planning. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- Prefrontal regulation depends on dopamine and noradrenaline released by arousal state. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- How far executive dysfunction belongs in the definition of adult ADHD remains an open question. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]

> [!info]- In depth: the construct, its dissociations and its measurement
> **What the construct covers.** Executive function names the control operations that hold a goal, shield it from interference, switch between rules and inhibit prepotent responses. Working memory, inhibition and shifting are the components most often distinguished, and they are separable in factor analyses even though they correlate. [[Working memory]] [[Attention]]
>
> **Dissociations are the key evidence.** The standard demonstration is that measures of these components can dissociate within and across clinical groups: children with ADHD, their siblings and controls differ on inhibitory, delay-related and timing tasks that behave as independent components rather than one deficit. That pattern is why 'executive dysfunction' is a description of a profile rather than a single mechanism. [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-finding|Beyond the dual pathway model]] [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-implication|Beyond the dual pathway model]]
>
> **Task impurity is the measurement problem.** Any executive task also engages perception, memory and motor processes, so a difference in performance is not attributable to control alone without additional design work (baseline tasks, process models). This is the main reason executive-function findings are harder to interpret than they look. [[F09 Noba Research designs#^f09-confounds|Research designs]]
>
> **Everyday relevance is a separate claim.** Laboratory executive measures correlate weakly with everyday function, so evidence that a group differs on a task does not establish impairment in daily life; functional measures have to be collected separately. [[Functional outcomes and measurement]] [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]]

> [!info]- Reference: attention, control and the difference between them
> **Attention is selection.** Attention determines which of many available signals is processed further, and both the selection and the cost of switching it can be measured behaviourally. [[F37 OpenStax Psychology 2e sensation versus perception#^f37-sensation|Sensation versus Perception]]
>
> **Executive function is a family.** Inhibitory control, working memory updating and set-shifting are separable components that correlate but do not move together, which is why 'executive function' should not be used as a single quantity. [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-finding|Beyond the dual pathway model]]
>
> **Neural basis.** Executive processes are associated with prefrontal and association cortex, and with modulatory input from noradrenaline and dopamine, in an inverted-U relationship to arousal. [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-executive|Association and executive processing]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]]
>
> **Measurement.** Laboratory tasks are narrow measures: they correlate weakly with each other and with everyday functioning, which is why questionnaire and performance measures often disagree. [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-implication|Beyond the dual pathway model]]

> [!info]- Further depth: the construct boundary and the dissociation evidence
> Executive function is described as a set of interacting processes rather than one faculty, supported by lesion evidence from anterior association cortex, and it overlaps attention without being identical to it: attention selects, while executive processes maintain, inhibit and switch. The ADHD cognitive-profile study is the concrete demonstration that the components dissociate within one diagnostic group, which is why a single executive-function score cannot stand in for the construct. [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-executive|Association and Executive Processing]] [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-finding|Beyond the dual pathway model]] [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-implication|Beyond the dual pathway model]]
>
> **Why the constructs are plural.** Attention names several separable operations - sustained attention, selective attention, attentional switching, divided attention - that correlate imperfectly; executive function covers inhibition, working-memory updating and shifting, which dissociate in factor-analytic work. The plurals matter clinically: a profile can show one component impaired and others intact, and a claim about "attention deficits" that does not name the component is untestable. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
>
> **The task-life gap is the measurement story.** Laboratory executive tasks are reliable, sensitive to prefrontal state, and weakly correlated with everyday self-regulation; everyday function is measured by reports that have their own rater problems. Neither replaces the other, and the adult ADHD literature's open question about how far executive dysfunction belongs in the definition is exactly this gap seen diagnostically. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** The neurochemistry domain supplies the state dependence - catecholamines tune prefrontal networks in an inverted-U - so attention and executive performance are state variables as well as traits, and measurement articles should say which they are treating them as. [[ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines]] [[Noradrenaline signalling]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Laboratory tasks are narrow proxies for daily functioning, and group performance says little about one person.
