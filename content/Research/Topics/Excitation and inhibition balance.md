# Excitation and inhibition balance

**Definition.** Whether a neuron fires depends on the summed excitatory and inhibitory influence reaching the point where the spike begins. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-initial-segment|Communication Between Neurons]]

## Supported claims

- Graded potentials summate at the initial segment, where voltage-gated sodium channels are dense. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-initial-segment|Communication Between Neurons]]
- Excitatory and inhibitory postsynaptic potentials move the membrane toward or away from threshold respectively. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-initial-segment|Communication Between Neurons]]
- In autism genetics, imbalance appears as one path among several rather than a universal explanation. [[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing#^p31981491-model|Autism exome sequencing study]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Balance is usually measured indirectly, and a difference in one circuit says little about the whole brain.
