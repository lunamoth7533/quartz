# Bipolar MRI findings and their limits

**Definition.** Bipolar neuroimaging research reports group-level structural associations, including cortical thickness differences and medication-related signals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]

## Supported claims

- Cortical grey matter was thinner in frontal, temporal and parietal regions across 6503 individuals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]
- Lithium, antiepileptic and antipsychotic treatment showed associations with cortical measures. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]
- Medication exposure confounds simple case-control readings of these differences. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI synthesis: ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]

> [!info]- In depth: what a large consortium analysis can and cannot establish
> **The finding.** In a large multi-site cortical analysis, grey matter was thinner bilaterally in frontal, temporal and parietal regions, with the largest effects in left pars opercularis, fusiform gyrus and rostral middle frontal cortex. Group averages of that kind describe samples rather than individuals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI appraisal: ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]]
>
> **Confounds were studied rather than assumed away.** Commonly prescribed drugs including lithium, antiepileptics and antipsychotics were associated with cortical measures even after accounting for multiple medications; longer illness duration was associated with reduced cortical thickness, reduced surface area was associated with a history of psychosis, and mood state showed no association. The authors describe the study as an extensive analysis of potential confounding variables, which is the correct framing - the associations with medication and course remain confounds for the diagnosis-based comparison. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-course|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-limit|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]]
>
> **The method's own caveats apply.** Neuroimaging has documented analytic flexibility and low-power problems that exaggerate effect sizes in brain-behaviour correlations, with data sharing, preregistration, larger samples and multiverse analyses proposed as responses. Consortium work addresses power; it does not remove the interpretive limits. [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-power|Neuroimaging reproducibility]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-practice|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]
>
> **What it does not license.** A thinner cortex on average cannot establish causation, cannot diagnose an individual, and does not identify a mechanism; the modality measures magnetic tissue properties rather than function. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI appraisal: ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-mri|Brain Imaging]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Report the sample, the contrast, the covariates examined and the effect size, and state explicitly that individual-level inference is out of reach. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-caution-modality|AI appraisal: Brain Imaging]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-limit|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

> [!info]- Reference: group findings, confounds and inference
> **What is reported.** Large consortium analyses report group differences in cortical thickness and surface area between people with bipolar disorder and controls. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]
>
> **Overlap.** Group distributions overlap substantially, so a difference in means does not classify individuals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI appraisal: ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]
>
> **Confounds.** Medication exposure, illness duration and comorbidity are associated with structural measures, which makes attribution to the condition itself difficult. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-course|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]
>
> **Method.** Analysis choice affects imaging results, and reproducibility requires large samples and preregistration. [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]
>
> **Use.** Findings support research on pathophysiology; they are not diagnostic tests or prognoses. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-limit|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]
>
> **Why consortium design changed the inference available.** Pre-consortium imaging studies were typically small enough that between-group differences were unstable and effect sizes inflated; the ENIGMA-scale aggregation of thousands of scans across sites is what made a cortical-thickness finding robust enough to be worth interpreting at all - and even then only as a group-level association with named confounds. The design upgrade, not a new analysis trick, is what moved the evidence. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-power|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]
>
> **Medication-associated structural signals are findings in their own right.** Lithium, antiepileptic and antipsychotic associations with cortical measures - even after mutual adjustment - are clinically interesting and scientifically complicating: they mean the case-control contrast mixes illness with its treatment, and they open treatment-effects questions that diagnosis-based designs cannot separate. This is the article's central interpretive fact and the reason the finding cannot be converted into an illness marker without more design. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence]]
>

## Limitation or common misconception

Observational group designs generate hypotheses rather than personal diagnostic information.
