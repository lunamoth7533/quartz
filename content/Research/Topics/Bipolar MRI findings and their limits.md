# Bipolar MRI findings and their limits

**Definition.** Bipolar neuroimaging research reports group-level structural associations, including cortical thickness differences and medication-related signals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]

## Supported claims

- Cortical grey matter was thinner in frontal, temporal and parietal regions across 6503 individuals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]
- Lithium, antiepileptic and antipsychotic treatment showed associations with cortical measures. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]
- Medication exposure confounds simple case-control readings of these differences. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI synthesis: ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Observational group designs generate hypotheses rather than personal diagnostic information.
