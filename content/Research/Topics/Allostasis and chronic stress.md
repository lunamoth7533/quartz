# Allostasis and chronic stress

## Definition

Allostasis describes the maintenance of stability through change: physiological mediators adjust to demand rather than holding a fixed set point. Allostatic load is the cumulative cost of repeated or poorly regulated responses.

## How it works

**Stability through change.** Allostasis describes stability through change: mediators adapt to demand rather than holding a constant set point, which is why the same hormone can be protective acutely and damaging when frequent, prolonged or poorly regulated. Allostatic load is the cumulative cost of repeated or poorly managed stress responses, and the framework treats the brain as both the controller of these responses and a target of them - which is why it is used to link stress to cognitive and emotional outcomes. [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-allostasis|Stress mediators]] [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-load|Stress mediators]]

**The mediators work as a system.** Glucocorticoids, autonomic activity and inflammatory signals are the mediators, and because they interact, measuring one of them captures only part of the system. The classification of glucocorticoid actions as permissive, suppressive, stimulatory or preparative depending on context makes the same point at the receptor level. [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-mediators|Stress mediators]] [[P10696570 Sapolsky 2000 Glucocorticoids and stress#^p10696570-four|Glucocorticoids and stress]]

**Social conditions are part of the exposure.** Social status, low control and chronic strain are described as conditions that shape stress exposure and health outcomes rather than as background variables, and chronic stressors are linked with altered immune function in the reviewed studies, with the relationship framed as bidirectional. The strongest population-level finding is distributional - sustained activation tracks social disadvantage - and says where to look for causes rather than measuring any individual: the pathway between measured social conditions and measured mediators runs through behaviours, environments and biology that few studies measure together. [[F65 OpenStax Stress and illness#^f65-social|Stress and Illness]] [[F65 OpenStax Stress and illness#^f65-immune|Stress and Illness]] [[P23488505 Hatzenbuehler 2013 Stigma as fundamental cause#^p23488505-argument|Stigma as a fundamental cause of health inequalities]]

**Measurement: a framework, not a threshold.** There is no single chronic-stress variable: exposure, appraisal, physiological mediators and cumulative outcome are four constructs measured four different ways, and a study that measures one and claims the others has overreached. [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-limit|Stress mediators]] [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-appraisal|What is stress?]] Load is estimated through composite indices combining hormonal, cardiovascular, metabolic and inflammatory markers, because no single measure represents it, and studies select different components and weightings, so two indices can rank the same people differently. That flexibility lets the framework connect literatures and makes cross-study comparison fragile - any load claim should name its index. [[F65 OpenStax Stress and illness#^f65-immune|Stress and Illness]]

**Reading rule.** Name the mediator, the timescale and the outcome. Acute adaptive responses, sustained dysregulation and cumulative wear are different claims, and the framework's value is that it keeps them connected without conflating them. [[P10696570 Sapolsky 2000 Glucocorticoids and stress#^p10696570-timing|Glucocorticoids and stress]]

## Evidence and status

The framework is widely used and supported by animal and human correlational evidence; composite load indices vary between studies, and causal direction is difficult to establish in observational work.
