# Psychosis constructs

## Definition

Psychosis refers to altered perception and thought, including hallucinations and delusions. Schizophrenia is a named construct involving psychosis with additional symptom domains and functional impact. [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-psychosis|Schizophrenia]]

## How it works

**Symptom domains.** Symptoms are grouped as positive, negative and cognitive, which is why two people can meet the same label with very different profiles. [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-features|Schizophrenia]]

**Course and onset.** Onset is typically in late adolescence or early adulthood, and early treatment is associated with better course, which has driven early-intervention services. [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-course|Schizophrenia]]

**Dopamine link.** D2 receptor blockade is the mechanism identified for antipsychotic response in the dopamine-blocking class, and the same receptor produces motor and endocrine side effects through different pathways - basal-ganglia circuitry for movement and the hypothalamic-pituitary route for prolactin. [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-d2|Antipsychotics mechanisms]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-prolactin|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]]

**Dimensional view.** Psychosis-like experiences occur in the general population at lower intensity, which is one reason dimensional models are proposed alongside categorical diagnosis. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]]

## Evidence and status

Symptom domains and treatment response are well characterised; the pathophysiology is heterogeneous and no single mechanism explains the construct.

> [!info]- In depth: symptom domains, course, and what the pharmacology can and cannot carry
> **The construct.** Schizophrenia is described as a serious mental illness involving psychosis, with changes in perception, thought and behaviour that interfere with functioning; symptoms are grouped into positive, negative and cognitive domains, which is why two people can meet the same diagnostic label with different profiles. Onset is often in late adolescence or early adulthood, early treatment matters for course, and research into mechanisms continues. [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-psychosis|Schizophrenia]] [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-features|Schizophrenia]] [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-course|Schizophrenia]] [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-limit|Schizophrenia]]
>
> **Domain profiles are not mechanisms.** A positive-symptom profile and a negative-symptom profile point to different functional consequences without identifying different causes, and the same diagnosis can be reached through different developmental routes. Classifications define categories with boundaries and qualifiers rather than mechanisms, and their rules differ between ICD-11 and DSM-5-TR. [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-use|ICD-11 CDDR]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]]
>
> **What antipsychotic mechanism evidence supports.** D2 receptor blockade is identified as the mechanism behind clinical response for the dopamine-blocking drugs, and also behind endocrine and motor side effects that run through separate pathways, which implies a therapeutic window rather than a simple more-is-better rule. Off-target binding at serotonergic, histaminergic, cholinergic and adrenergic receptors contributes to effects such as weight gain, sedation and dysphoria, and the review notes that antipsychotics are ineffective for some patients and that response varies, so mechanism does not translate into uniform benefit. [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-d2|Antipsychotics mechanisms]] [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-offtarget|Antipsychotics mechanisms]] [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-limits|Antipsychotics mechanisms]] [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-limit|Antipsychotics mechanisms]]
>
> **Dopamine claims need the same care everywhere.** Dopamine neurons carry salience and movement-related signals in addition to reward-prediction error, and different populations carry different signals, so 'dopamine' findings in psychosis are not interchangeable with the reward literature in other domains. [[P27069377 Schultz 2016 Reward prediction error#^p27069377-scope|Reward prediction error]] [[P29760524 Berke 2018 What does dopamine mean#^p29760524-heterogeneity|What does dopamine mean?]]
>
> **Research framing.** RDoC studies psychosis-relevant function through dimensional units and domains across conditions rather than through the category, which is the design choice that makes mechanism research comparable across diagnoses. [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-purpose|RDoC]] [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-matrix|RDoC]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Name the symptom domain, the phase, and whether a statement is descriptive, mechanistic or about treatment response. The strongest mechanism claim in this domain - D2 blockade for positive symptoms, with side effects sharing the mechanism - is narrower than the label suggests. [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-limits|Antipsychotics mechanisms]]

> **Psychosis is a descriptive term carrying several diagnoses.** Hallucinations and delusions define psychotic experiences; the disorders that carry them - schizophrenia-spectrum, affective psychoses, substance-induced and secondary psychoses - differ in course, associated features and treatment implications, so descriptive similarity does not imply shared cause or prognosis. The differential work - substance, medical, affective, primary - is part of the construct's definition rather than an afterthought. [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-features|Schizophrenia]] [[Co-occurrence and differential reasoning]]
>
> **The continuum finding reframed the field.** Psychotic-like experiences in the general population are far more common than psychotic disorders, and most do not persist or convert; transition depends on persistence, distress and functional impact. That finding moved the field from categorical-only thinking toward continuum models with clinical-high-risk identification - and generated its own caution, since most screened positives do not transition, so high-risk services carry false-positive burdens. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]] [[Categorical versus dimensional classification]]
