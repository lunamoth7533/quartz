# Adult autism assessment and differential considerations

**Definition.** Adult autism assessment combines developmental history, multi-source information and clinical judgement; screening instruments support referral rather than diagnosis. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-comprehensive|NICE CG142]]

## Supported claims

- Assessment is considered when persistent social-communication difficulties or restricted, repetitive behaviours co-occur with functional, social or service-contact criteria. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-identification|F24]]
- For adults without moderate or severe learning disability the AQ-10 may screen; a score of six or more, or clinical judgement, leads to a comprehensive assessment. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-screening|F24]]
- Comprehensive assessment is team-based and uses informant or documentary evidence of early development and current behaviour. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-comprehensive|F24]]
- It covers features present since childhood, functioning, physical and mental disorders, and other neurodevelopmental conditions. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-domains|F24]]
- Differential assessment includes mental, neurological and physical conditions, communication difficulties and sensory sensitivities; biological, genetic and neuroimaging tests are not used routinely for diagnosis. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-differential|F24]]
- ADHD and autism frequently co-occur, and the earlier exclusion of a dual diagnosis limited research until the criteria were revised. [[P24808851 Leitner 2014 Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD#^p24808851-history|P24808851]]
- Autism varies widely between people in features and support needs, so assessment describes a profile rather than a position on one severity line. [[P31949163 Lord 2020 Autism spectrum disorder primer#^p31949163-construct|Lord 2020 Autism spectrum disorder primer]]
- Screens can produce false positives and false negatives; a score alone neither confirms nor excludes autism, so it leads to assessment rather than diagnosis. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-caution|AI synthesis: F24]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Assessment pathways, instruments and eligibility differ by country and age group; the guidance cited here describes UK services, and no instrument in this library is a self-test.
