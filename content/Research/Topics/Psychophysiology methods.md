# Psychophysiology methods

## Definition

Psychophysiology measures body signals - heart activity, skin conductance, pupil size, muscle tension, hormones - as indices of psychological or neural processes. Each measure is downstream of several control systems.

## How it works

**Signals and sources.** Heart rate variability reflects respiratory, baroreflex and autonomic influences; skin conductance reflects sweat-gland activity driven by sympathetic fibres; pupil size reflects light plus arousal and cognitive load. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart rate variability metrics]]

**Brain-body loops.** Interoceptive afferents carry internal state to cortex, so peripheral measures are also inputs to the system being studied, not only readouts. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]]

**Task context.** Values depend on posture, breathing, time of day, medication, movement and emotional state, so recording conditions are part of the measurement. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Control of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

**Inference steps.** Moving from a signal to a construct such as stress or emotion regulation requires a chain of assumptions, and each link should be stated. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-limit|Heart rate variability metrics]]

## Evidence and status

Signal physiology is well characterised; construct validity of psychophysiological indices varies and is often weaker than the literature implies.

> [!info]- In depth: recording bodily signals and the discipline of not over-reading them
> **What the approach measures.** Psychophysiology records physiological signals - heart rate and its variability, electrodermal activity and others - as indices of autonomic and central state. The signals are continuous and non-invasive, which makes them attractive; they are also influenced by many things at once, which is the central methodological problem. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart rate variability metrics]]
>
> **The worked example.** HRV metrics fall into time-domain, frequency-domain and non-linear families with different recording requirements; baseline values depend on recording length, age, sex and posture; published norms are not interchangeable; and the LF/HF ratio is controversial because autonomic interactions are non-linear and respiration confounds the band. Breathing, heart rate, medication and individual factors all influence recorded values. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-context|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-lfhf|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-confounds|Heart rate variability metrics]]
>
> **Interoception is a construct, not a readout.** The interoceptive system carries homeostatic afferent activity from all tissues, separately from cutaneous mechanoreception and proprioception, with primary representation placed in the dorsal posterior insula and a re-representation in the right anterior insula proposed as a basis for the bodily sense of self. That mapping is a model supported by converging evidence rather than a measurement someone can take. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-limit|Interoception]]
>
> **Integration is the reason single signals mislead.** Thermoregulation demonstrates integration of autonomic, endocrine and behavioural responses to a single physiological demand, so a change in one recorded channel reflects a coordinated response rather than one effector's state. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Autonomic Control]] [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-cooperation|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]]
>
> **Reading rule.** For any psychophysiology claim, name the signal, the recording conditions, the construct being inferred, and the confounds controlled. The library's own caution is explicit: heart rate variability is not a direct readout of stress, calm or one autonomic branch, and a single short recording cannot separate the influences involved. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-caution-marker|AI appraisal: Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-limit|Heart rate variability metrics]]
>
> **Each signal indexes a different physiological layer.** Skin conductance indexes sympathetic arousal through sweat glands; heart rate and its variability index cardiac autonomic control through multiple rhythms; EEG indexes summed cortical postsynaptic currents; cortisol indexes the slower hormonal arm. Because the layers are coupled but not identical, agreement and disagreement between them are both informative - and a claim from one layer about another (heart rate from EEG, emotion from cortisol) needs the coupling evidence stated. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-caution-marker|AI appraisal: Heart rate variability metrics]] [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-axis|What Is Stress?]]
>
> **Recording conditions are part of the measurement.** Recording length, posture, breathing, time of day, caffeine, medication and recent activity all move these signals by amounts comparable to the effects being studied, which is why standardised protocols and baseline windows are method content rather than lab furniture. A psychophysiological claim that omits its recording conditions cannot be evaluated. [[P18609093 Hansen 2008 Salivary cortisol sources of variation#^p18609093-recommendations|Salivary cortisol sources of variation]] [[Autonomic measurement and heart rate variability]]
>
