# Autonomic measurement and heart rate variability

## Definition

Autonomic measurement estimates the activity of sympathetic and parasympathetic pathways through indirect indices, of which heart rate variability is the most widely used. Each index reflects several influences at once.

## How it works

**Signals.** Heart rate, blood pressure, skin conductance, pupil diameter and heart rate variability are the common measures; each is downstream of multiple control loops. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Control of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

**Variability metrics.** Time-domain, frequency-domain and non-linear metrics describe different aspects of the same record, and they are not interchangeable. Respiratory influences and baroreflex activity both contribute. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart rate variability metrics]]

**Task and state dependence.** Autonomic measures respond to posture, breathing, movement, emotion and time of day, so recording conditions determine what a number means. [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-cooperation|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

**Afferent side.** Interoceptive signals reaching cortex carry information about internal state, which is why autonomic measures are also used in studies of emotional processing. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]]

## Evidence and status

The physiological sources of heart rate variability are well characterised; the leap from a variability index to a psychological construct such as 'emotion regulation capacity' is generally not warranted by measurement alone. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-limit|Heart rate variability metrics]]

> [!info]- In depth: what the autonomic measures can and cannot support
> **Rest and reflex control.** Autonomic control keeps short-term blood pressure and heart rate stable, with parasympathetic activity predominating at rest, and the two divisions are described as cooperative as well as opposing - most organs receive both, and regulation depends on the balance of their activity rather than on one branch at a time. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-auto|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-cooperation|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]]
>
> **The metric families and their requirements.** HRV metrics fall into time-domain, frequency-domain and non-linear families with different recording requirements; baseline values depend on recording length, age, sex and posture, and published norms are not interchangeable. Direction of the LF/HF controversy is worth stating plainly: the ratio is not a clean index of autonomic balance because the interactions are non-linear and respiration contaminates the band. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-context|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-lfhf|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-confounds|Heart rate variability metrics]]
>
> **What the measures are used to infer.** Autonomic indices are used as markers of stress, emotion regulation and interoceptive state in the wider literature, and the interoceptive system they feed has a proposed cortical representation. The inference from a beat-to-beat series to a psychological state crosses at least two jumps - signal to physiology, physiology to construct - and each jump needs its own evidence. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]]
>
> **Integration again.** Thermoregulation demonstrates how autonomic, endocrine and behavioural responses are coordinated to a single demand, which is why a recorded change in one channel rarely identifies one cause. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Autonomic Control]]
>
> **Reading rule.** State the metric, the recording conditions, the population norm used and the construct claimed; then check whether the study reports all four. The library's stance is that autonomic claims stay at the level of physiology and model-building rather than personal interpretation. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-caution-marker|AI appraisal: Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-limit|Heart rate variability metrics]]

> [!info]- Further depth: metric families, recording conditions and the construct jump
> Time-domain, frequency-domain and non-linear metric families have different recording requirements; baseline values depend on recording length, age, sex and posture; published norms are not interchangeable; and the LF/HF ratio is controversial because autonomic interactions are non-linear and respiration confounds the band. The inference from a beat-to-beat series to a psychological state crosses two jumps - signal to physiology and physiology to construct - and a single short recording cannot separate the influences involved, which is why this library keeps autonomic claims at the level of physiology and model-building.
