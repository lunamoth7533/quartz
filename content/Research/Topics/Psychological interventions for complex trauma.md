# Psychological interventions for complex trauma

**Definition.** This topic covers whether psychological therapies help adults with complex trauma presentations, separately from which structure they use. [[P40154799 Hu 2025 Psychological interventions for CPTSD#^p40154799-improvement|Psychological interventions for complex PTSD]]

## Supported claims

- Pooled psychotherapy effects in a 2025 meta-analysis were significant for PTSD, depression, anxiety and dissociation outcomes. [[P40154799 Hu 2025 Psychological interventions for CPTSD#^p40154799-improvement|Psychological interventions for complex PTSD]]
- Effects decreased slightly at follow-up but remained significant except for anxiety and dissociation. [[P40154799 Hu 2025 Psychological interventions for CPTSD#^p40154799-followup|Psychological interventions for complex PTSD]]
- Participants with childhood trauma showed lower effects, risk of bias moderated results, and the authors report some indications of publication bias. [[P40154799 Hu 2025 Psychological interventions for CPTSD#^p40154799-moderators|Psychological interventions for complex PTSD]]
- Complex trauma exposure in these trials is not the same category as a formal ICD-11 complex PTSD diagnosis. [[P40154799 Hu 2025 Psychological interventions for CPTSD#^p40154799-caution-category|AI synthesis: Psychological interventions for complex PTSD]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Heterogeneous samples and moderator findings mean the pooled effect describes an average, not a prognosis for one person.
