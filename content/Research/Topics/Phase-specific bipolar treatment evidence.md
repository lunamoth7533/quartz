# Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence

**Definition.** Bipolar treatment evidence is organized by illness phase, and options are ranked by efficacy, safety and tolerability together. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-phases|CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines]]

## Supported claims

- Guideline recommendations are organized by phase and ranked first-, second- and third-line. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-phases|CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines]]
- Ranking weighs efficacy, safety and tolerability together rather than efficacy alone. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-balance|CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines]]
- NIMH patient material frames treatment as combining medication and psychotherapy across the lifetime course. [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-treatment|Bipolar Disorder]]

## Limitation or common misconception

This record summarizes a guideline summary; medication choice, dose and monitoring belong to clinical care.
