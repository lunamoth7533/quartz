# Lithium mechanisms and uncertainty

**Definition.** Lithium is a mood stabilizer used in bipolar disorder whose mechanism spans several cellular signalling pathways. [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-signalling|Lithium pharmacology review]]

## Supported claims

- Lithium affects multiple steps in cellular signalling, usually enhancing basal and inhibiting stimulated activity. [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-signalling|Lithium pharmacology review]]
- The mechanism preventing recurrences remains only partially understood, and distinct hypotheses have not been conclusively settled. [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-unknown|Lithium pharmacology review]]
- Narrow-therapeutic-index drugs need tighter exposure control and often monitoring; this is a general property, not a lithium-specific finding of one review. [[F18 FDA Narrow therapeutic index drugs#^f18-nti|Setting and Implementing Standards for Narrow Therapeutic Index Drugs]]; [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-caution-monitoring|AI synthesis: Lithium pharmacology review]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Mechanistic uncertainty does not equal clinical uncertainty about the main effects; it does mean simple explanations of how it works are stories.
