# Inheritance and variation

## Definition

Inheritance describes how genetic variants are transmitted from parents to offspring; variation describes the differences between individuals that result. Most behavioural and psychiatric traits are polygenic and influenced by many variants of small effect.

## How it works

**Patterns.** Single-gene conditions follow recognisable inheritance patterns, but most conditions relevant to this library are not single-gene, which limits how far Mendelian reasoning extends. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-expression|Human genetics]]

**Family and twin designs.** Heritability estimates compare resemblance between relatives with different degrees of genetic sharing; they describe population variation, not individual causation. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-heritability|Human genetics]]

**De novo variation.** Some variants arise in the gametes or early embryo and are not present in parents, which is one reason sequencing has to include parents to interpret findings. [[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing#^p31981491-model|Autism exome sequencing]]

**Shared and specific influences.** Family and twin evidence suggests partly shared genetic contributions across conditions - for example between autism and ADHD - which is a statement about overlapping architecture rather than one shared cause. [[P24808851 Leitner 2014 Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD#^p24808851-shared|Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD]]

## Evidence and status

Inheritance patterns for monogenic conditions are established; heritability estimates for behavioural traits are consistent in direction but sensitive to sample and design.

> [!info]- In depth: how variation is transmitted, and what heritability does not say
> **Mendelian and complex inheritance are different problems.** Single-gene inheritance produces predictable ratios within families, while complex traits involve many variants of small effect plus environmental contributions, which is why the same family patterns cannot be read the same way in both cases. Genes are expressed in interaction with environment and experience, so a genotype is a set of instructions read differently in different conditions rather than a fixed destiny. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-expression|Human Genetics]] [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-heritability|Human Genetics]]
>
> **Heritability is a population statistic.** Heritability estimates describe how much of the variation in a population is associated with genetic differences, not how strongly genes determine one individual; a high estimate in one population does not license a claim about a person or about another population with different environmental variation. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-heritability|Human Genetics]] [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-limit|Human Genetics]]
>
> **Genomics changes the scale of the question.** Genomics studies the whole set of genetic material rather than single genes, which changes the kinds of question that can be asked about a trait, and it connects to areas such as pharmacogenomics and drug development each with its own evidence base and time frame. [[F66 NHGRI Genomics fact sheet#^f66-scope|Genomics fact sheet]] [[F66 NHGRI Genomics fact sheet#^f66-applications|Genomics fact sheet]] [[F66 NHGRI Genomics fact sheet#^f66-updates|Genomics fact sheet]]
>
> **Variation is the normal state.** Most human variation is common and shared rather than rare and private, which is why association studies are powered by sample size and why rare-variant work needs sequencing: the two designs detect different parts of the same architecture. [[F67 NHGRI GWAS fact sheet#^f67-scale|GWAS fact sheet]] [[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing#^p31981491-genes|Autism exome sequencing]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Distinguish transmission, heritability, association and individual prediction; each is a different claim, and moving between them silently is the most common error in popular genetics writing. [[P28686856 Visscher 2017 10 years of GWAS discovery#^p28686856-caution-individual|AI appraisal: 10 years of GWAS discovery]] [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-limit|Human Genetics]]
>
> **Variation is the raw material the designs act on.** Genetically variable traits come from sequence differences - single nucleotide changes, insertions and deletions, copy-number differences - and their consequences depend on where they sit: coding sequence, regulatory sequence or non-functional territory. Most of the variants association studies find sit in regulatory territory, which is why the inference chain from variant to changed protein is usually indirect and runs through expression. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics]] [[P29844615 Schaid 2018 Fine-mapping#^p29844615-problem|Fine-mapping]]
>
> **Family resemblance decomposes into more than genes.** Relatives share genes and environments, and the two shares are themselves correlated - parents provide both - so raw family resemblance overstates genetic influence until designs separate the components: twins differing in relatedness, adoptees separating genetic and rearing parents. Heritability estimates are outputs of those designs and inherit their assumptions, which is why the estimate belongs to the design and population, not to the trait. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-heritability|Human genetics]] [[Gene-environment interplay and epigenetics]]
>
> **Cross-domain connection (curation).** The pharmacology domain's pharmacogenomics interest and this note's complex-inheritance limits meet at the same boundary: single-gene prediction works where single genes are decisive, and psychiatricrelevant traits sit mostly on the polygenic side of that line.
