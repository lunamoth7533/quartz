# Therapeutic index, monitoring and interactions

**Definition.** The therapeutic window is the concentration range between too little and too much drug; a narrow window makes small exposure changes consequential. [[F18 FDA Narrow therapeutic index drugs#^f18-window|Setting and Implementing Standards for Narrow Therapeutic Index Drugs]]

## Supported claims

- Every medicine sits between a concentration that does too little and one that produces toxicity. [[F18 FDA Narrow therapeutic index drugs#^f18-window|Setting and Implementing Standards for Narrow Therapeutic Index Drugs]]
- Narrow therapeutic index drugs need tighter exposure control and often biological monitoring. [[F18 FDA Narrow therapeutic index drugs#^f18-nti|Setting and Implementing Standards for Narrow Therapeutic Index Drugs]]
- Interactions include drug-drug, drug-food and drug-condition types and can change how much drug reaches its target. [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-types|Drug Interactions: What You Should Know]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Monitoring manages concentration rather than the whole clinical picture; individual decisions belong with clinicians and product labelling.
