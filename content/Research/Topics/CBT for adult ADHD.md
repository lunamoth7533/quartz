# CBT for adult ADHD

**Definition.** Cognitive behavioural approaches for adult ADHD target practical skills, planning and the thought patterns that interact with attention difficulties. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-symptoms|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]

## Supported claims

- CBT with medication was more effective than medication alone for core symptoms in a Cochrane review. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-symptoms|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]
- The authors rated the evidence low-quality, limited by short follow-up, heterogeneous outcomes and geography. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-certainty|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]
- Secondary outcomes such as self-reported depression and anxiety improved in places, while quality-of-life results varied. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-secondary|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Low-certainty, short-term evidence with varied outcome measures leaves durability and functional gains unresolved.
