# Blood-brain barrier

## Definition

The blood-brain barrier is the selective interface between blood and central nervous system tissue, formed primarily by brain endothelial cells with supporting cells around them.

## How it works

**Tight junctions.** Brain endothelial cells are joined by continuous tight junctions preventing paracellular movement, and they lack the transendothelial vesicular pathways found in other tissues. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-tight|Blood Brain Barrier]]

**Support cells.** Astrocytes and other cells are required for the barrier to develop and maintain its properties. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-support|Blood Brain Barrier]] This is the structural link between the barrier and the glial material in the neurobiology domain. [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-glia|Nervous Tissue]]

**Selective, not sealed.** Gases diffuse freely while ions, nutrients and larger molecules depend on specific transporters, so permeability is regulated and route-specific. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood Brain Barrier]]

**Why precision matters.** Regulated transport, leakage and immune cell entry are distinct phenomena, and barrier integrity is not a binary state. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-permeability|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]] The review is organised around unresolved questions about how the barrier changes across regions, ages and diseases. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-open|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]]

## Evidence and status

Barrier structure and transport are established in cell and animal studies; human measurement relies on imaging and cerebrospinal fluid markers with substantial limitations. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-components|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]]

> [!info]- In depth: a selective interface rather than a wall
> **What the barrier is made of.** Brain endothelial cells joined by continuous tight junctions form the barrier, supported by pericytes and other cells; tight junctions prevent paracellular movement and the endothelium lacks the transendothelial vesicular pathways found elsewhere, and astrocytes and other supporting cells are required for it to develop and maintain its properties. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-components|The blood-brain barrier]] [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-tight|Blood-brain barrier]] [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-support|Blood-brain barrier]]
>
> **Selectivity, not closure.** Barrier function is selective rather than absolute: gases diffuse freely while ions, nutrients and larger molecules depend on specific transporters. Regulated transport, leakage and injury are distinct phenomena, and the review cautions that barrier integrity is not simply open or closed - which is why 'leaky barrier' claims need to specify the route and the measurement. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood-brain barrier]] [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-permeability|The blood-brain barrier]]
>
> **Glia contribute to the interface.** Glial cells provide scaffolding, insulation, nutrient transport, waste handling and immune mediation, with recent work suggesting a roughly 1:1 ratio of glia to neurons rather than a large excess - which matters because the barrier and the immune surveillance of the brain are supported functions rather than passive properties of the endothelium. [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-glia|Nervous Tissue]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]]
>
> **Where the open questions are.** One review is organised around unanswered questions, including how the barrier changes across regions, ages and disease states, so claims that a treatment works by opening or protecting the barrier are research questions rather than established mechanisms. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-open|The blood-brain barrier]] [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-limit|The blood-brain barrier]]
>
> **Reading rule.** Distinguish barrier permeability, regulated transport and immune surveillance; they involve different cells and different measurements, and the brain's interface is only a wall in the loosest sense. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-limit|Blood-brain barrier]]
>
> **Permeability claims need a route and a molecule.** Because gases diffuse, ions and nutrients ride specific transporters, and larger molecules are excluded or actively shuttled, "the barrier is leaky" is meaningless without naming which route and which cargo changed. The review's framing - integrity is not binary, and leakage, transport change and immune-cell entry are distinct phenomena - is the discipline that separates barrier biology from barrier marketing, including claims that supplements "strengthen" a barrier whose transport is selectively regulated, not simply closed. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-permeability|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]] [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood-brain barrier]]
>
> **Why the barrier complicates drug delivery and disease interpretation.** For pharmacology, the barrier is the first selectivity filter a psychotropic must pass, by design: lipophilic agents cross, polar ones need transporters, and efflux pumps expel some drugs outright. For disease interpretation, barrier change is entangled with inflammation and age - the review's open questions include how the barrier changes across regions and diseases - so imaging-based barrier findings are part of a causal story, not its conclusion.
