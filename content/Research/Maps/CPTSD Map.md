# CPTSD Map

Complex PTSD is an ICD-11 construct combining PTSD symptoms with disturbances in self-organization. This library holds no complex PTSD specific biological studies, so the neurobiology section is marked as an evidence gap rather than filled with transferred PTSD findings.

## Evidence gaps

- Complex trauma exposure, ICD-11 CPTSD and DSM-5 PTSD are different categories across studies.
- Phase-based comparisons rest on a small trial base with one main analysis time point.
- Biological findings come from PTSD research and should not be assumed to extend to CPTSD.

## Study question

What evidence would justify changing clinical guidance on phase-based sequencing?
