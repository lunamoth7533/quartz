## Reference overview

This domain covers two linked subjects: how genetic variation is organised and measured, and how
the nervous system is built. The link is not that genes specify circuits - they do not - but that
development is the process through which inherited variation and experience jointly produce
structure, and that the statistical tools used to study one are the tools used to study the other.

**From sequence to product.** DNA is transcribed and translated under regulatory control, so a
genotype is a set of instructions read differently in different cells and conditions; heritability
estimates describe variation within a population, not the development of an individual.
[[DNA RNA and gene expression]] [[Inheritance and variation]]

**Common and rare variation answer different questions.** Common variants of small effect are
detected by genome-wide association and aggregated into polygenic scores whose accuracy depends on
the discovery sample and whose interpretation requires calibration for the individual case; rare
variants of larger effect are found by sequencing and implicate specific genes. Fine-mapping
narrows an association signal to a credible set of variants without establishing mechanism.
[[Common and rare variants]] [[Genome-wide association studies]] [[Polygenic scores and prediction]] [[P29844615 Schaid 2018 Fine-mapping#^p29844615-limits|Fine-mapping]]

**Environment enters through correlation and interaction.** Genotype and environment are
associated through passive, evocative and active routes, which is why environmental associations
require designs that hold genetic variation constant; epigenetic marks are tissue-specific
regulators of gene use rather than a general mechanism of inheritance.
[[Gene-environment interplay and epigenetics]] [[Genes, environment and polygenic risk]]

**Development is a sequence with overlapping windows.** Proliferation, migration,
differentiation, axon guidance, synaptogenesis, pruning and myelination proceed in overlapping
schedules; trophic competition selects which connections survive; and inhibitory maturation opens
sensitive periods during which experience has outsized effects. Because schedules differ by
system, age of onset and sensitive windows differ by function. [[Neurogenesis and migration]]
[[Axon guidance and synaptogenesis]] [[Synaptic pruning and myelination]] [[Sensitive periods]]
[[Circuit development and homeostasis]]

**Model systems carry a translation burden.** Animal and cellular models establish mechanism with
control that human designs cannot achieve, and translating them requires assuming conservation of
the relevant process. That assumption is testable, and where it fails, a mechanism claim loses its
human interpretation. [[Translational validity]] [[Reading a study and matching populations]]

**Evidence boundaries.** Polygenic scores summarise average risks across a sample and need
calibration before any individual reading; a risk gene does not determine traits or support needs;
and genetic correlation between traits does not establish a shared mechanism. [[P28686856 Visscher 2017 10 years of GWAS discovery#^p28686856-caution-individual|AI appraisal: 10 years of GWAS discovery]]
[[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing#^p31981491-caution-risk|AI appraisal: Autism exome sequencing]]

**Where to start.** [[DNA RNA and gene expression]] and [[Inheritance and variation]] for the basis, [[Genome-wide association studies]] and [[Genetic inference and polygenic scores]] for how evidence is produced, and [[Neurogenesis and migration]] through [[Sensitive periods]] for development. ## Reference spine

Genetic claims rest on measurement. Genetics studies single genes and genomics studies the whole set of genetic material; common variants are detected by association studies while rare variants are detected by sequencing, and the two regimes coexist in most conditions. [[F66 NHGRI Genomics fact sheet#^f66-scope|Genomics fact sheet]] [[F67 NHGRI GWAS fact sheet#^f67-design|GWAS fact sheet]] [[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing#^p31981491-model|Autism exome sequencing]]

Interpretation has defined limits. An association signal marks a region, fine-mapping narrows candidate variants, and neither identifies a mechanism without functional work. Polygenic scores aggregate small effects and transfer poorly across ancestries, so they support research stratification far more than individual prediction. [[F67 NHGRI GWAS fact sheet#^f67-meaning|GWAS fact sheet]] [[P29844615 Schaid 2018 Fine-mapping#^p29844615-methods|From GWAS to candidate causal variants by fine-mapping]] [[F68 NHGRI Polygenic risk scores#^f68-limits|Polygenic risk scores]]

Environment enters in several distinct ways: interaction, correlation between genotype and environment, and epigenomic marks that change how DNA is used without changing sequence. Treating these as one mechanism obscures the designs needed to study them. [[F07 OpenStax Human genetics#^f07-expression|Human genetics]] [[F69 NHGRI Epigenomics fact sheet#^f69-definition|Epigenomics fact sheet]] [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-experience|The basics of brain development]]

Development runs through overlapping processes - proliferation, migration, differentiation, synaptogenesis, pruning and myelination - with different schedules across systems. Axon guidance uses conserved cue families, and refinement removes as much as it adds. [[P19794405 Tau 2010 Normal development of brain circuits#^p19794405-sequence|Normal development of brain circuits]] [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-overlap|The basics of brain development]] [[P12471249 Dickson 2002 Molecular mechanisms of axon guidance#^p12471249-cues|Molecular mechanisms of axon guidance]] [[F101 Neuroscience Online synapse formation and elimination#^f101-elimination|Synapse Formation, Survival, and Elimination]]

Sensitive periods give experience disproportionate influence during defined windows, with circuit mechanisms that open and close them. [[P16261181 Hensch 2005 Critical period plasticity#^p16261181-trigger|Critical period plasticity]] [[P16261181 Hensch 2005 Critical period plasticity#^p16261181-consolidation|Critical period plasticity]]

## Evidence boundaries

Embryonic development is established in animals and human tissue; adult human neurogenesis and the translation of model-organism mechanisms remain disputed or incomplete. Genetic findings describe populations, and a risk variant is not a diagnosis.

## Reading a genetic or developmental claim

Four distinctions carry most of the weight in this domain. Variant class matters, because common and rare variation are detected by different designs and describe different parts of an architecture. Level of claim matters, because association, fine-mapping, mechanism and prediction are consecutive steps with separate evidence. Population matters, because heritability and polygenic scores are statistics of the samples they were estimated in and do not transport freely across ancestry or ascertainment. And timing matters, because development runs as overlapping processes rather than a schedule, so a mechanism demonstrated in one system does not transfer to another age or region.
