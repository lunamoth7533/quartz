# Bipolar I Map

Bipolar I is defined by mania, then treated across acute and maintenance phases. This map connects the condition to the five foundation domains and marks where the biology is group-level rather than personal.

## Evidence gaps

- Most treatment evidence is short-term relative to a lifetime condition; maintenance data is thinner than acute-phase data.
- Imaging findings are group averages with medication confounds and do not support individual diagnosis.
- Genomic findings describe risk architecture in populations and do not predict individual course.
