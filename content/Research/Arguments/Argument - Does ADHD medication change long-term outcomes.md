# ADHD diagnosis and measurement

**Definition.** ADHD assessment combines history, rating scales and impairment across settings, because no confirmatory laboratory test exists. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]

## Supported claims

- The use of objective diagnostic measures is listed among the open questions in adult ADHD. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]
- Evidence from included studies came from large samples and specific meta-analysis rules. [[P33549739 Faraone 2021 World Federation ADHD consensus statement#^p33549739-methods|World Federation ADHD consensus statement]]
- Non-pharmacological interventions showed benefit only on clinician-rated measures in a 2025 adult synthesis. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]
- Clinician-reported and self-reported outcomes can disagree, so a benefit claim should name the rater. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-caution-rater|AI synthesis: CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Rating scales measure reports rather than mechanisms, and informant disagreement does not settle a diagnosis either way.

# Does ADHD medication change long-term outcomes or mainly short-term symptoms?

**Question.** Randomized evidence covers weeks to months. Do those short-term symptom gains translate into long-term function, quality of life and safety?

**Origin.** AI-generated starter question, written for this library. It does not represent anyone's beliefs or history.

## Competing explanations

**Short-term gains should compound.** If core symptoms improve, downstream functioning should improve over time for many people, and early treatment may prevent secondary problems. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-stimulants|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]

**Short-term gains may not generalize.** Trial evidence is short and focused on symptoms, reviews keep long-term outcomes open, and the strongest synthesis found insufficient data at 26 and 52 weeks. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-longterm|ADHD medication network meta-analysis]]; [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]

**Different outcomes need different studies.** Randomized trials estimate average efficacy; function and safety over years usually need large observational cohorts with their own confounding problems. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-caution-choice|AI synthesis: ADHD medication network meta-analysis]]

## Evidence that would decide

- Long-running randomized extension studies where feasible, and large well-adjusted observational cohorts.
- Outcome measures that include function, education or work, quality of life and adverse-event burden.
- Analyses that separate the effects of dose, duration, adherence and age at start.

## Cautions

Absence of evidence for a long-term benefit is not proof of harm or futility; and observational associations do not establish treatment effects.
