# Research Home

This is a curated starting library for Bipolar I, ADHD, autism and complex PTSD, plus the neurobiology, neurochemistry, psychology, pharmacology and neurology underneath them. Every substantive claim in a topic note links to a block in a source note so you can check it.

It is a research library, not an individual treatment plan. Nothing here is a diagnosis, a dose or a substitute for clinical advice.

**Coverage.** 21 journal publications and 21 educational or official sources - 42 source records in total. All are queued; verification labels say what was actually checked. The library also contains 60 topic notes, 9 maps, 3 starter research questions and 4 templates. This is a curated starter library rather than a systematic review: sources were screened at abstract or official-page level, not read in full, and file availability is tracked separately from reading status. The journal set includes reviews, guidelines and one editorial, so each record states its own `study_type` rather than claiming uniform peer review.

Source records separate what the checked abstract reports from the analyst's own methodological cautions, and topics label any link into those cautions as AI synthesis. Where a mechanism has no verified source yet - synaptic plasticity is the current example - the note says so and points to the advanced queue instead of filling the gap.
