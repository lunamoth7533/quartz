# Learning Path

This sequence moves from how to read evidence to how the nervous system works, and only then to the four conditions. Each stage assumes the previous one, so skipping to conditions means reading group averages without the tools to interpret them.

## Stage 1 - Research literacy (start here)

Read these five notes first; they change how everything after them is read.

1. [[Evidence types and causal inference]] - what each design can and cannot show.
2. [[Bias and confounding]] - why a real effect can still be a distorted estimate.
3. [[Reading a study and matching populations]] - the population, measurement and window.
4. [[Association versus individual prediction]] - keeping group statistics and individual questions apart.
5. [[Reviews, guidelines and preprints]] - how to use summaries without trusting them blindly.

Practice: take one paper from [[Library]] and write its population and design in two sentences.

## Stage 2 - Cells and circuits

[[Neurons and glia]] then [[Nervous tissue and myelin]]; then [[Ion gradients and membrane potential]] and [[Action potentials]]; then [[Synapses and plasticity]] and [[Brain regions and networks]].

Practice: explain why an action potential is stereotyped but a circuit response is not.

## Stage 3 - Chemistry

[[Synaptic signalling model]] first, then [[Dopamine signalling]], [[Noradrenaline signalling]], [[Serotonin signalling]], [[Glutamate and GABA]] and [[Acetylcholine signalling]]. Finish with [[Excitation and inhibition balance]] and the misconception note [[Chemical imbalance framing]]; then regulation systems [[Stress response and the HPA axis]] and [[Circadian rhythms and sleep]].

Practice: pick one transmitter and describe two different jobs it does in two circuits.

## Stage 4 - Psychology

[[Attention and executive function]], [[Learning and conditioning]], [[Memory processes]], [[Emotion regulation]], [[Trauma and stress responses]], then the therapy evidence notes [[CBT and its evidence base]] and [[Psychoeducation and social rhythms]].

Practice: state one limit of symptom-scale outcomes for therapy trials.

## Stage 5 - Pharmacology

[[Pharmacokinetics and ADME]], [[Pharmacodynamics and receptors]], [[Half-life and steady state]], then [[Efficacy versus tolerability]], [[Short-term versus long-term outcomes]] and [[Therapeutic index, monitoring and interactions]]. Applied reading: [[Lithium mechanisms and uncertainty]], [[ADHD medication evidence]], [[Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence]].

Practice: explain why a drug's half-life matters clinically without giving any dosing advice.

## Stage 6 - Neurology

[[Nervous system divisions]], [[Neurological examination]], [[Structural versus functional measures]], [[MRI versus EEG]], [[Interpreting group brain differences]].

Practice: write an honest one-sentence summary of a group imaging finding.

## Stage 7 - Conditions

Read the map first, then its topics, then at least one primary source per section.

- [[Bipolar I Map]] - [[Bipolar I episodes and course]], [[Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence]], [[Dopamine hypothesis in bipolar disorder]], [[Bipolar genetics and polygenic risk]], [[Bipolar MRI findings and their limits]], [[Bipolar psychotherapy evidence]].
- [[ADHD Map]] - [[ADHD across the lifespan]], [[ADHD diagnosis and measurement]], [[ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines]], [[ADHD medication evidence]], [[CBT for adult ADHD]].
- [[Autism Map]] - [[Autism heterogeneity and support needs]], [[Autism genetics and rare variants]], [[Autism support and services]].
- [[CPTSD Map]] - [[CPTSD and disturbances in self-organization]], [[ICD-11 versus DSM-5 classification]], [[Psychological interventions for complex trauma]], [[Phase-based trauma therapy evidence]], [[PTSD biology and CPTSD evidence limits]].

## Advanced study queue

Held back deliberately; these need more background or have open methods questions.

- [[P42134365 Nourredine 2026 ADHD medication dose-effect synthesis|Nourredine 2026, ADHD dose-effect synthesis]] - aggregate dose models; read after Stage 5, and do not extract titration numbers.
- [[P41949043 Lee 2026 Phase-based versus non-phase-based trauma therapy|Lee 2026, phase-based therapy comparison]] - read alongside a methods text; the certainty limits are the point.
- [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder|O'Connell 2025, bipolar genomics]] - needs comfort with polygenic reasoning and ascertainment.
- [[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing|Satterstrom 2020, autism exome sequencing]] - read after [[Excitation and inhibition balance]].
- Synaptic plasticity (long-term potentiation and depression) - not taught in this library yet because no verified source covers it; add one to `Sources/` before treating it as established, see [[Synapses and plasticity]].

The queue is a plan, not a record: nothing here is marked read until it is actually read.
