# Bipolar II treatment evidence

**Module.** M07 Bipolar Disorders · **Topic.** [[Bipolar II treatment evidence]]

## Why this matters

Most confident-sounding statements about treating bipolar II lean on evidence collected in bipolar I. Knowing how thin the bipolar-II-specific base actually is changes how much weight any particular recommendation deserves.

## The core model

Bipolar II is under-recognised and often misdiagnosed, so treatment studies that enrol it specifically are few - which means bipolar I results are routinely applied to bipolar II without direct evidence behind that step. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-extrapolation|Swartz 2023]] [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]]

Where bipolar-II-specific trials do exist, the certainty is strikingly low. A 2025 systematic review found ten randomised trials of lithium or lamotrigine in 645 patients with bipolar II, and every outcome across them was graded very low certainty: whether lithium lowers the risk of new mood episodes compared with placebo was very uncertain, lamotrigine-versus-placebo trials could not even be pooled, and lithium against lamotrigine for depressive severity was also very uncertain. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-certainty|Fredskild 2025]] [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-lithium|Fredskild 2025]] [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-lamotrigine|Fredskild 2025]] Very low certainty is not the same as evidence of no effect - it means the trials are too few or too flawed to show benefit or its absence either way. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-caution-absence|Appraisal: Fredskild 2025]]

Bipolar II is also where the antidepressant question is most contested, and the direct evidence is small. In two small double-blind trials of acute bipolar II depression, venlafaxine or sertraline alone did not differ significantly from lithium in response or in switching, and fewer patients stopped them for side effects. [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-response|Park 2022]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-switch|Park 2022]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-tolerability|Park 2022]] But 223 patients across both trials cannot rule out a meaningful difference in switching risk - an encouraging result is not the same as a settled one. [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-caution-power|Appraisal: Park 2022]]

## Worked example (hypothetical)

A hypothetical guideline states: "Lithium is first-line for bipolar II maintenance, exactly as it is for bipolar I." Before accepting that as settled, ask where the evidence for the bipolar II half of that sentence comes from. Checking the direct trial base: the 2025 review of bipolar-II-specific lithium and lamotrigine trials rated recurrence-prevention evidence very low certainty. So any confidence behind that guideline sentence is coming mostly from bipolar I data, extrapolated across to bipolar II - not from bipolar II trials themselves. The honest version separates the two: "Lithium is well-supported for bipolar I maintenance; for bipolar II, the direct trial evidence is currently too uncertain to say either way."

## Common confusions

- "The same guideline confidence applies whether it's bipolar I or bipolar II." Much bipolar II guidance is extrapolated from bipolar I rather than tested directly. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-extrapolation|Swartz 2023]]
- "Very low certainty evidence means these drugs don't work." It means the trials cannot show benefit or its absence, not that an effect was ruled out. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-caution-absence|Appraisal: Fredskild 2025]]
- "The antidepressant-monotherapy trials proved these drugs are safe in bipolar II." With only 223 patients, a meaningful switching difference cannot be ruled out. [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-caution-power|Appraisal: Park 2022]]
- "Bipolar II is well-studied because bipolar disorder generally is." Specific treatment studies remain few, and misdiagnosis compounds the gap. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]]

## What the sources do not establish

Whether lithium or lamotrigine genuinely prevents recurrence in bipolar II is not resolved by current trials. Whether switch risk on antidepressant monotherapy is truly lower in bipolar II than bipolar I, as some consensus suggests, awaits larger trials than the two summarised here.
