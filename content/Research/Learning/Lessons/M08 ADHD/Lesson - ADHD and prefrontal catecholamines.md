# ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines

**Module.** M08 ADHD · **Topic.** [[ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines]]

## Why this matters

This is the mechanism story that people carry around about ADHD medication: stimulants increase catecholamines,
which help the prefrontal cortex focus. The model is real and more interesting than the slogan, and it comes with
an inverted U that the slogan ignores.

## The core model

The prefrontal cortex is critical for regulating attention, screening distractions, inhibiting impulses and
planning.
[[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

Prefrontal function depends on noradrenaline and dopamine released according to arousal state, with too little
or too much impairing function.
[[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

The proposed receptor mechanisms are specific: noradrenaline benefit is attributed to postsynaptic alpha-2A
receptors, and moderate dopamine benefit to D1 receptors, with different optimal levels.
[[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

Imaging studies in ADHD are described as showing underactive prefrontal regions with weakened connections, and
animal studies show that the same manipulations can help or harm depending on baseline state and dose.
[[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-evidence|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

The evidence is layered: human imaging, genetic linkage and animal pharmacology, not a single experiment. That
matters for how strongly you can hold the mechanism, and the review itself predates later treatment literature.
[[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-limit|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
Mechanistic plausibility does not reduce ADHD to one transmitter deficit.
[[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-caution-transfer|AI synthesis: Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

## Worked example (hypothetical)

This scenario is invented for practice. A study reports an inverted-U relationship between a working-memory
score and an arousal measure — heart rate variability — in adults with ADHD.

Read the measurement before the curve. HRV indexes cardiac autonomic modulation with metrics that depend on
recording length, posture, breathing and individual factors; it is not a validated direct measure of
catecholamine level and not a general index of arousal.
[[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart Rate Variability Metrics and Norms]];
[[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-context|Heart Rate Variability Metrics and Norms]]
So the study can show that performance was associated with this autonomic measure; it cannot locate any
participant on a catecholamine inverted U, and group averages cannot capture individual optima.

The defensible statement: "in this sample, performance was associated with an intermediate level of the HRV
measure; that pattern is compatible with the inverted-U model as a hypothesis about arousal, but the design
does not measure catecholamines or establish anyone's position on the curve."

## Common confusions

- "ADHD is a dopamine deficit." The reviewed model is about optimal levels and receptor-specific effects across
  two transmitters, not a simple deficiency.
- "Arousal is good up to a point, so more is better." The model says exactly the opposite past the optimum.
- "Animal findings give doses." They give mechanisms; clinical evidence and prescribing are separate.
- "HRV measures arousal or catecholamines." It measures cardiac autonomic modulation, and its values depend on
  how and when they were recorded.

## What the sources do not establish

The review is a mechanistic synthesis combining animal and human evidence and predates later clinical
literature; it provides no dosing guidance and no individual prediction, and this vault reproduces none of its
numerical detail.
