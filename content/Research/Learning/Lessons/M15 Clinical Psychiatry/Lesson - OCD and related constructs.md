# OCD and related constructs

**Module.** M15 Clinical Psychiatry and Psychopathology · **Topic.** [[OCD and related constructs]]

## Why this matters

Obsessive-compulsive disorder is a clean case study in a habit this whole module builds: a formulation that
makes sense of a pattern is not the same as proof of its cause. The construct also connects learning theory
directly to why a specific treatment is designed the way it is.

## The core model

Obsessive-compulsive disorder is characterised by obsessions - unwanted, repeated thoughts or urges - and
compulsions, repetitive behaviours or mental acts performed in response to them.
[[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-pattern|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]] Symptoms may begin in
childhood or adulthood, fluctuate over time, and frequently co-occur with anxiety, depression and other
conditions.
[[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-course|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]]

A cognitive-behavioural interpretation offers one explanation for why the pattern persists: compulsions can
briefly relieve anxiety, and that relief can negatively reinforce the response, helping to maintain the ritual.
This applies a learning framework to the symptoms; it is not presented as a complete account of every person's
experience.
[[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-cycle|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]]
[[F10 Noba Conditioning and learning#^f10-operant|Operant learning]] Exposure and response prevention, the
best-established psychological treatment for the construct, applies this same extinction logic deliberately.

A cycle of intrusion, anxiety, ritual and relief is a useful formulation, but a formulation is not a diagnostic
test or proof of a single cause. Genetics, biology and environmental factors are all named as contributors, and
causes are described as not fully understood; research can examine learning, neural circuitry and clinical
symptoms at different levels without assuming one description replaces the others. Repetitive behaviour also
occurs in other conditions, so its form alone does not settle its function or meaning.
[[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-limit|Scope of the publication]]

Insight - how much a person recognises that an obsessional belief may not be accurate - ranges from good to
absent, and it is clinically informative: it predicts presentation and shapes how the cognitive part of treatment
is delivered, without changing the underlying cycle structure. This is a measurement description along a
continuum, not a softening of the construct.
[[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-cycle|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]]

## Worked example (hypothetical)

This is an invented exercise in tracing a formulation, not a description of any real person.

Picture a fictional pattern: a repeated urge to check a locked door, followed by checking, followed by a brief
drop in anxiety. Trace the reinforcement account step by step - the urge creates discomfort, checking reduces
the discomfort, and the reduction makes checking more likely next time. That is a coherent explanation for why
the pattern could persist. Now ask what it does not explain: why the urge arose in the first place, why one
person develops this pattern and another does not, and whether relief is the only thing keeping the ritual
going. Naming what the formulation explains and what it leaves open is the actual exercise - not settling on a
single cause.

## Common confusions

- "The intrusion-anxiety-ritual-relief cycle proves the cause of OCD." It is a formulation that supports
  treatment logic, not a demonstrated single cause.
- "Because exposure and response prevention helps, reinforcement must be the only mechanism." Clinical
  improvement does not establish that reinforcement is the only process involved.
- "Repetitive behaviour always signals OCD." The same behavioural form occurs in other conditions and contexts.

## What the sources do not establish

The reinforcement account explains why relief can maintain a ritual; it does not establish that reinforcement is
the sole mechanism of OCD or the sole target of successful treatment. The publication describing the cycle is a
public overview, not a mechanism study, and it explicitly states that causes are not fully understood.

## Answer notes

1. The compulsive behaviour gets reinforced by the brief relief from anxiety it produces.
2. Because a formulation is not a diagnostic test or a demonstrated single cause, and genetics, biology and
   environment are all named as contributors whose relative roles are not fully understood.
3. Insight affects presentation and how the cognitive part of treatment is delivered; it does not change the
   underlying cycle structure.
