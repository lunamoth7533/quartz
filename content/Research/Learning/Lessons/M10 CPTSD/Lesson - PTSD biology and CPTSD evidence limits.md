# PTSD biology and CPTSD evidence limits

**Module.** M10 CPTSD · **Topic.** [[PTSD biology and CPTSD evidence limits]]

## Why this matters

This lesson teaches a skill that applies across the whole vault: recognising when findings from one construct
are being relabelled as findings about another. The gap between PTSD biology and complex PTSD evidence is the
clearest example here, and naming it honestly is more useful than filling it.

## The core model

PTSD research spans psychophysiological, neuroimaging, endocrinological, genetic and molecular studies in humans
and animals.
[[P23047775 Pitman 2012 Biological studies of PTSD#^p23047775-layers|Biological studies of PTSD]]

The review's framing is that PTSD's cause is regarded as known — a threatening event — while the biological
literature searches for mechanisms and markers.
[[P23047775 Pitman 2012 Biological studies of PTSD#^p23047775-layers|Biological studies of PTSD]]

The limits are specific. This is a 2012 review covering PTSD rather than complex PTSD.
[[P23047775 Pitman 2012 Biological studies of PTSD#^p23047775-limit|Biological studies of PTSD]]

PTSD findings cannot be relabelled as complex PTSD specific mechanisms, and no single biological marker
explains PTSD, so these findings describe mechanisms under study rather than a diagnostic test.
[[P23047775 Pitman 2012 Biological studies of PTSD#^p23047775-caution-cptsd|AI synthesis: Biological studies of PTSD]]

The construct adds further distance: current definitions are symptom-based, and complex PTSD adds
self-organization domains that have no established biological signature in this vault.
[[F17 VA National Center for PTSD Complex PTSD history and definitions#^f17-definition|Complex PTSD: History and Definitions]]

The stress physiology that does appear in the vault — appraisal, the fast sympathetic response and the slower
HPA axis — is general physiology, not a complex-PTSD-specific finding.
[[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-axis|What Is Stress?]]

## Worked example (hypothetical)

This scenario is invented for practice. A study reports that a group with PTSD shows a difference in an
endocrine measure, and a commentary says it "explains the biology of complex PTSD".

Audit the leap. The sample was defined by DSM-based PTSD criteria, not ICD-11 complex PTSD, and the two groups
overlap only partly. The measure is a group difference, usually with overlap between groups and confounds such
as trauma type, medication and comorbidity. The study design is cross-sectional, so direction is open.

The defensible sentence: "in this PTSD sample, the measure differed between groups on average; whether the
finding extends to ICD-11 complex PTSD, and whether it is a cause or consequence, has not been established."
The relabelling is the error, not the original study.

## Common confusions

- "Complex PTSD is a more severe PTSD, so the biology carries over." The constructs overlap partly, and the
  additional domains have their own (unestablished here) biology.
- "A biological difference is a biomarker." Biomarkers require validation and predictive performance.
- "The absence of CPTSD-specific biology means there is none." It means this vault has no source for it; the
  gap is real and recorded.

## What the sources do not establish

The PTSD review is from 2012 and the vault holds no complex-PTSD-specific biological study; the stress
physiology source is a general educational overview. Nothing here supports a biological test or explanation for
any individual.

## Check yourself

1. What layers does the PTSD biology review cover?
2. Why can PTSD findings not be relabelled as complex PTSD mechanisms?
3. What minimum evidence would justify calling a finding CPTSD-specific?

## Answer notes

1. Psychophysiological, neuroimaging, endocrinological, genetic and molecular studies.
2. Because the constructs are defined differently and the evidence comes from PTSD samples, so the finding is
   about a partly different group.
3. Samples meeting ICD-11 complex PTSD criteria, contrast with PTSD and controls, replicated convergent measures
   and designs that address direction.
