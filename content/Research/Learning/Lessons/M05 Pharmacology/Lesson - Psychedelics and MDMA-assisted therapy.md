# Psychedelics and MDMA-assisted therapy

**Module.** M05 Pharmacology · **Topic.** [[Psychedelics and MDMA-assisted therapy]]

## Why this matters

Psilocybin for depression and MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD are two of the most publicised treatments in
psychiatry, described in places as fast, strong and durable. Both are also unapproved for these uses in the
United States, and both were tested in trials that are almost impossible to blind. Learning to read this
evidence well means separating a large reported effect from how much of that effect the trial design can
actually support.

## The core model

Psilocybin is a psychedelic, a drug that alters perception; MDMA is an entactogen, given not alone but as
MDMA-assisted therapy, a drug delivered inside structured psychotherapy sessions.
[[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-limit|Metaxa 2024]] Because MDMA is paired with
therapy, a trial result describes the combined package, not the molecule by itself.
[[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-caution-population|Appraisal: Fares-Otero 2026]]

Mechanistically, psychedelics are grouped among the rapid-acting antidepressant candidates that begin by
activating serotonin receptors.
[[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-limit|Brown 2024]]

In pooled trials, psilocybin given as a standalone treatment improved depression scores more than comparators,
with a pooled Hedges' g of 0.66 and moderate-certainty evidence.
[[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-effect|Metaxa 2024]] But the effect was larger in
exactly the groups you would expect if participants and raters could guess who received the drug: people with
prior psychedelic experience, older participants, and self-report rather than clinician-rated scales.
[[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-moderators|Metaxa 2024]] A drug that produces a
distinctive altered state is hard to hide from the person taking it, so those patterns are a caution about
unblinding rather than a bonus finding.
[[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-caution-unblinding|Appraisal: Metaxa 2024]]

MDMA-assisted therapy shows a similar shape. Pooled trials in PTSD found a large reduction in symptom severity
(a standardised mean difference of about -1.2), but the certainty of that estimate was rated very low, because
most trials carried a high risk of bias in how outcomes were measured, few used an active comparison, and
blinding was likely compromised by MDMA's distinctive effects.
[[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-ptsd|Fares-Otero 2026]]
[[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-certainty|Fares-Otero 2026]]

Both treatments also sit inside a live regulatory story. Early trials led the US Food and Drug Administration to
grant MDMA-assisted therapy breakthrough status, and later phase 3 results reported that close to seven in ten
treated participants no longer met the criteria for PTSD.
[[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-history|Singh 2025]] In 2024, the
FDA still declined to approve it, citing problems with trial design, including failed blinding and missing
safety assessments for heart rhythm and abuse potential, alongside allegations of possible misconduct.
[[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-rejection|Singh 2025]] A rejection
like this weighs trial conduct and safety data together with effect size, so it is a statement about the
evidence package, not a finding that the treatment does not work.
[[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-caution-regulatory|Appraisal: Singh 2025]]

**Reading rule.** Whenever you meet a psychedelic or MDMA result, ask three questions in order: what was the
certainty rating, not just the effect size; could the participant and rater tell which arm they were in; and is
the result describing the drug alone or the drug plus a therapy package.

## Worked example (hypothetical)

This scenario is invented for practice. Imagine a magazine article claims: "A new trial shows psilocybin
outperforms every standard depression treatment, curing patients others could not help." Before accepting that
framing, find the comparator (a standard treatment, an inactive pill, or a low-dose control believed inactive),
check whether the article reports a certainty rating alongside the effect size, and check whether participants
who had used psychedelics before did unusually well, since that would point to unblinding rather than to a
stronger drug effect.

Working through those three checks turns "cured" into something more bounded: "psilocybin outperformed the
comparator on this scale, with moderate-certainty evidence, in a trial that could not fully blind
participants" — a real finding, but not a cure.

## Common confusions

- "A large effect size means strong evidence." Effect size and certainty are different questions; MDMA-assisted
  therapy shows both a large effect and very low certainty at once.
- "MDMA-assisted therapy is approved for PTSD." It was not approved in the United States as of the 2024
  decision.
- "Psilocybin's benefit is proven to come from the drug itself." Expectancy and unblinding are live alternative
  explanations the reviewers themselves flagged.

## What the sources do not establish

Neither source shows how much benefit would remain under better blinding or an active control, and the PTSD
trials enrolled people with standard PTSD rather than the more complex presentation covered elsewhere in this
vault, so transfer to complex PTSD is untested.
[[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-caution-population|Appraisal: Fares-Otero 2026]]
Long-term safety, including abuse liability, is also unmeasured here.
[[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-path|Singh 2025]]

## Check yourself

1. Why is blinding especially hard to achieve in a psilocybin or MDMA trial?
2. What did the 2024 FDA decision on MDMA-assisted therapy actually weigh, besides effect size?
3. Why does a large effect size not guarantee strong evidence?

## Answer notes

1. Both drugs produce distinctive subjective effects, so participants and raters can often guess who received
   the active drug rather than placebo.
2. Trial design problems including failed blinding, missing cardiac and abuse-liability assessments, and
   allegations of possible misconduct.
3. Effect size describes the estimated size of a difference; certainty describes how much confidence the
   evidence supports in that estimate, and the two can point in different directions.
