# Tricyclic antidepressants

**Module.** M05 Pharmacology · **Topic.** [[Tricyclic antidepressants]]

## Why this matters

Tricyclics are usually introduced as an older, weaker class that SSRIs replaced. The evidence does not support
"weaker" - it supports "riskier." This lesson uses that distinction to teach an important reading skill: a
class's rank in prescribing guidelines can track safety rather than efficacy, and the two need to be checked
separately.

## The core model

Tricyclic antidepressants block reuptake of both serotonin and noradrenaline, and individual members are
approved for different specific indications.
[[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-mechanism|Moraczewski 2023]]
Amitriptyline and clomipramine are the tricyclics in the largest network meta-analysis of adult depression, and
desipramine and nortriptyline are the tricyclics tested in children with ADHD.
[[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-drugs|Cipriani 2018]] Reuptake
block raises serotonin and noradrenaline signalling, a mechanism the class shares with the SNRIs, but
anticholinergic activity - action on a different receptor system entirely - is what sets the tricyclics apart in
tolerability.
[[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-mechanism|Moraczewski 2023]];
[[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-secondline|Moraczewski 2023]]

On efficacy, amitriptyline had the largest odds ratio against placebo of all 21 drugs compared in the largest
depression network, while clomipramine had more dropouts than placebo, and the class is described in reference
literature as highly effective in severe or treatment-refractory depression.
[[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-placebo|Cipriani 2018]];
[[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-severe|Moraczewski 2023]] That is the
part "second-line" obscures if read as an efficacy verdict: anticholinergic effects and a lower threshold for
dangerous overdose, not a weaker treatment effect, are what define the class's safety profile and place it
behind newer drugs in guidelines.
[[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-secondline|Moraczewski 2023]] The
toxicity literature describes SSRIs as less toxic than the tricyclics they largely displaced, and amitriptyline
and clomipramine were among the drugs with the highest dropout in head-to-head depression trials.
[[P30521236 Bruggeman 2023 Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor toxicity#^p30521236-uses|Bruggeman 2023]];
[[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-acceptability|Cipriani 2018]]
That overdose margin is a population-level property of the class, not an individual risk estimate for any one
person taking it.
[[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-caution-toxicity|Appraisal: Moraczewski 2023]]

The ADHD evidence shows the same efficacy-versus-safety split at smaller scale. In six small trials in young
people, tricyclics beat placebo on predefined improvement in core symptoms, with desipramine helping across
parent, teacher and clinician ratings - but the trials raised diastolic blood pressure and pulse slightly, and
the reviewing authors rated the evidence low to very low quality while singling out desipramine's cardiovascular
effects as an important concern.
[[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-efficacy|Otasowie 2014]];
[[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-conclusion|Otasowie 2014]] That evidence
is also dated, centred on one drug, and silent about adults.
[[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-caution-age|Appraisal: Otasowie 2014]]
In pooled autism trials, clomipramine outperformed SSRIs on restricted and repetitive behaviours, a contrast
drawn from few small trials compared across studies rather than tested head-to-head.
[[P35948343 Liang 2022 Antidepressants for autism symptoms#^p35948343-moderators|Liang 2022]] And as with the
other reuptake-blocking classes, an international task force named tricyclic and tetracyclic drugs among those
that may carry a higher risk of switching mood into hypomania, mania or mixed states in bipolar disorder.
[[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]]

## Worked example (hypothetical)

This scenario is invented for practice. A trivia quiz states: "Tricyclics were replaced by SSRIs because
tricyclics don't work as well." Check the claim against the evidence rather than the folk explanation. In the
largest head-to-head depression network, amitriptyline had the largest effect against placebo of all 21 drugs
compared - that is not a weaker treatment effect. What differs is tolerability and overdose risk: the
anticholinergic burden and the lower threshold for dangerous overdose are what guidelines weigh when they rank
tricyclics behind newer classes. The quiz's causal story swaps a safety ranking for an efficacy ranking, which
is exactly the confusion this lesson exists to prevent.

## Common confusions

- "Second-line means less effective." Here it tracks tolerability and overdose risk, not treatment effect size.
- "An old class means an outdated mechanism." The reuptake-blocking mechanism is shared with the newer SNRIs;
  what is different is the added anticholinergic activity.
- "A cross-study contrast, like clomipramine outperforming SSRIs in autism, is as strong as a head-to-head
  trial." It is drawn from comparing separate small trials, not from testing the drugs directly against each
  other in the same trial.

## What the sources do not establish

The cardiac mechanism behind tricyclic overdose toxicity is not described by a source checked here, the ADHD
evidence says nothing about adults, and whether clomipramine's apparent autism advantage would survive a direct
head-to-head trial against SSRIs is untested.

## Check yourself

1. What single receptor-system difference sets tricyclics apart from the SNRIs in tolerability?
2. Why is "second-line" not the same claim as "less effective"?
3. What cardiovascular concern did reviewers single out in the ADHD trials, and for which drug?

## Answer notes

1. Anticholinergic activity, which the SNRIs do not share.
2. Because amitriptyline had the largest effect against placebo of all 21 antidepressants compared in the same
   network; "second-line" here reflects the anticholinergic burden and overdose margin, not a smaller treatment
   effect.
3. Cardiovascular effects, particularly raised diastolic blood pressure and pulse, singled out for desipramine.
