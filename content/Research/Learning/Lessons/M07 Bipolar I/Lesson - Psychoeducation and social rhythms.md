# Psychoeducation and social rhythms

**Module.** M07 Bipolar I · **Topic.** [[Psychoeducation and social rhythms]]

## Why this matters

This lesson joins the two halves of the module: the trial evidence for psychoeducation and the circadian model
that explains why routines are targeted in the first place. It is also a case study in how a plausible mechanism
can be oversold when the trial outcome does not isolate it.

## The core model

Family or group psychoeducation with supervised skills practice was linked to fewer recurrences than individual
delivery in a component analysis across 39 randomised trials.
[[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]

Retention was higher for family or conjoint therapy and for brief psychoeducation, which matters because
engagement is part of effectiveness.
[[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-retention|Adjunctive psychotherapy component network meta-analysis]]

The mechanism story comes from sleep physiology: sleep timing is regulated by circadian rhythms interacting
with homeostatic sleep drive, so timing and duration are separate questions.
[[F14 NINDS Brain basics understanding sleep#^f14-rhythm|Brain Basics: Understanding Sleep]]

Put together, the rationale for social rhythm work is that stabilising daily anchors supports circadian
alignment, which in turn supports mood stability. That rationale is coherent and it is not the same as having
demonstrated that rhythm change is the active ingredient; the component analysis cannot separate rhythm work
from the other elements delivered alongside it.

## Worked example (hypothetical)

This scenario is invented for practice. A service wants to know whether a group programme that teaches routine
planning actually changes sleep timing, beyond what ordinary support achieves.

Design the comparison rather than the routine. Record sleep timing and its variability for two weeks before and
after the programme using a diary or actigraphy, and compare with a group that receives the same amount of
contact without rhythm content. Pre-specify the outcomes: timing variability rather than average duration
alone, symptom or mood measures, and goals the participants themselves name.

Then test the mechanism claim carefully. The two-process model predicts that timing regularity is the relevant
variable, so the analysis asks whether the rhythm measure changed more in one arm and whether that change
travels with outcome change. A randomised intervention does not randomise the mediator, so a positive mediation
result is specific evidence for the story and a null result is a failure of evidence rather than proof that the
story is wrong; both arms may move the mediator, and measurement limits remain.

## Common confusions

- "Sleep duration is the target." Timing and regularity are separate variables in the two-process model.
- "Psychoeducation is just information." The evidence describes structured programmes with skills practice,
  not leaflets.
- "Rhythm stability is easy to sustain." It is easy to recommend and hard to maintain, which the retention and
  heterogeneity findings reflect.

## What the sources do not establish

This vault has no trial that isolates a rhythm intervention's effect, and the sleep source is a general
educational overview without clinical measurement protocols. The active-ingredient question stays open in the
coverage matrix.

## Check yourself

1. Which therapy format was linked to fewer recurrences in the component analysis?
2. What two processes regulate sleep timing, and why should a study record variability rather than only duration?
3. How would you test whether rhythm change is the active ingredient, and what would a null mediation result mean?

## Answer notes

1. Family or group psychoeducation with supervised skills practice, compared with individual delivery.
2. A circadian rhythm and homeostatic sleep drive; the same duration at different clock times differs in
   circadian alignment, so variability is a separate variable.
3. Measure the mediator directly, compare arms with equal contact, and test whether mediator change tracks
   outcome change; a null result fails to provide evidence for the mechanism rather than disproving it.
