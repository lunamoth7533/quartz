# Module 10 - CPTSD

**Lessons.** 6 · **Canvas.** [[Learning Map - CPTSD.canvas]] · **Entry point.** [[Learning Hub]]

## What this module is for

The ICD-11 construct, classification differences, psychological intervention evidence, the phase-based sequencing debate and the limits of PTSD biology for complex PTSD.

CPTSD applies the foundations to a construct that exists in ICD-11 and not in DSM-5, where the treatment evidence is newer and the biological evidence is largely inherited from PTSD research. The module is the curriculum's clearest case study in classification affecting evidence.

## Learning objectives

1. Describe the ICD-11 complex PTSD construct and its self-organization domains.
2. Explain how classification choices change who is included in research.
3. Summarise the pooled evidence for psychological interventions and its moderators.
4. Explain what the phase-based sequencing debate tests and what it does not test.
5. State the limits of PTSD biology for complex PTSD specifically.

## Common confusions

- Complex trauma exposure is not the same category as an ICD-11 diagnosis.
- Phase-based versus non-phase-based compares packaged protocols, not a randomised test of sequencing.
- PTSD biology cannot be relabelled as complex PTSD biology.

## Module assessment

Answer from memory first; the parent lesson holds the supporting detail.

1. Why does classification matter for reading trauma treatment evidence?
   - *Working answer.* Different systems select different samples, so a trial's result describes the population its diagnostic instrument defined, and studies are not directly comparable without that information.
   - *Answer sources.* [[F17 VA National Center for PTSD Complex PTSD history and definitions#^f17-systems|Complex PTSD: History and Definitions]]

2. What is the honest summary of the phase-based debate?
   - *Working answer.* Most contrasts found no significant difference between phase-based and non-phase-based packages, with some outcomes favouring structure; the trial base is small and the comparison is between protocols rather than a within-protocol sequence test.
   - *Answer sources.* [[P41949043 Lee 2026 Phase-based versus non-phase-based trauma therapy#^p41949043-conclusion|Phase-based versus non-phase-based trauma therapy]]

3. What would justify calling a biological finding specific to complex PTSD?
   - *Working answer.* Studies that recruit ICD-11 complex PTSD samples, compare them with PTSD and appropriate controls, and replicate the finding with convergent measures.
   - *Answer sources.* [[P23047775 Pitman 2012 Biological studies of PTSD#^p23047775-caution-cptsd|AI synthesis: biological studies of PTSD]]
