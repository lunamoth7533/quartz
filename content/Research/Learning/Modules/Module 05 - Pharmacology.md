# Module 05 - Pharmacology

**Lessons.** 28 · **Canvas.** [[Learning Map - Pharmacology.canvas]] · **Entry point.** [[Learning Path]]

## What this module is for

Exposure, targets, half-life and steady state, the therapeutic window, and how efficacy and tolerability are measured. No dosing or titration content.

Pharmacology explains how exposure, targets and measurement interact. It is written for understanding evidence rather than for using medicines: no doses, no titration and no individual treatment decisions appear anywhere in the module.

## Learning objectives

1. Describe absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion, and what bioavailability means.
2. Explain half-life and steady state as models of exposure over time.
3. Explain pharmacodynamics as target-mediated action, and why one target appears in many tissues.
4. Distinguish efficacy from tolerability and explain how trials operationalize each.
5. Explain the therapeutic window and why some drugs need monitoring, without reproducing clinical instructions.

## Common confusions

- A concentration is a proxy for exposure, not a measure of response.
- A drug's mechanism story is not the same as its clinical evidence.
- Tolerability measured by dropout undercounts side effects people endure.

## Module assessment

Answer from memory first; the parent lesson holds the supporting detail.

1. Why can two people taking the same labelled dose have different blood concentrations?
   - *Working answer.* Bioavailability, metabolism, interactions, body composition and adherence all vary between people, so the label describes an average rather than an individual.
   - *Answer sources.* [[F12 NIGMS What happens to medicine in your body#^f12-bioavailability|What Happens to Medicine in Your Body?]]

2. What is the difference between efficacy and tolerability in one sentence?
   - *Working answer.* Efficacy is how much the treatment changes the target outcome, and tolerability is the burden it imposes, including adverse effects and how often people stop.
   - *Answer sources.* [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|ADHD medication network meta-analysis]]

3. Why can 'we do not know exactly how it works' coexist with strong evidence that it works?
   - *Working answer.* Clinical trials can establish benefit without resolving mechanism; the mechanism question and the effect question are answered by different kinds of evidence.
   - *Answer sources.* [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-unknown|Lithium pharmacology review]]
