# Learning Hub

This is the entry point to the detailed learning layer. The vault keeps its original shape: `Sources/` holds one record per source with anchored blocks, `Topics/` holds the atomic claims, and `Arguments/` holds open questions. This layer adds the teaching that the atomic notes deliberately skip: a plain-language model, mechanisms, worked examples, misconceptions, source boundaries and retrieval practice.

**Coverage.** 70 lessons, 10 module guides, 70 topics (each with a lesson), 60 source records, a glossary, a study workflow and a coverage matrix that names what is still missing.

## How to use this layer

1. Start with [[Module 01 - Research Methods and Evidence Literacy|Research Methods]] unless you already read studies comfortably; every later module assumes it.
2. Read one lesson, then close it and answer its *Check yourself* questions from memory before reading the answer notes.
3. Open the topic note and the source block behind any claim you want to keep; the lesson is teaching, the topic is the claim, the source block decides.
4. Schedule the lesson again using [[Study Workflow]] rather than re-reading it.

## The ten modules

- [[Module 01 - Research Methods and Evidence Literacy|Research Methods and Evidence Literacy]] - How designs, measurement, statistics and synthesis shape what a study can show. Start here: every later module assumes these tools.
- [[Module 02 - Neurobiology|Neurobiology]] - Cells, membranes, spikes, synapses, plasticity, networks and the genetic background that the chemistry and clinical modules build on.
- [[Module 03 - Neurochemistry|Neurochemistry]] - Transmitters, receptors, balance, stress physiology and autonomic regulation, with the chemical-imbalance framing kept as a misconception to reason about rather than a model to teach.
- [[Module 04 - Psychology|Psychology]] - Attention, learning, memory, emotion regulation and the evidence base for psychological treatment in general.
- [[Module 05 - Pharmacology|Pharmacology]] - Exposure, targets, half-life and steady state, the therapeutic window, and how efficacy and tolerability are measured. No dosing or titration content.
- [[Module 06 - Neurology|Neurology]] - Organisation of the nervous system, the clinical examination, and how structural and functional measures differ in what they can answer.
- [[Module 07 - Bipolar I|Bipolar I]] - Course and phases, treatment evidence by phase, lithium's mechanism and uncertainty, the dopamine hypothesis, genomic findings and imaging limits.
- [[Module 08 - ADHD|ADHD]] - Lifespan course, measurement, prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms, medication evidence and what short-term trials can and cannot say about long-term outcomes.
- [[Module 09 - Autism|Autism]] - Heterogeneity and support needs, rare-variant genetics and the limits of genetic inference, and how support and services are organised and evaluated.
- [[Module 10 - CPTSD|CPTSD]] - The ICD-11 construct, classification differences, psychological intervention evidence, the phase-based sequencing debate and the limits of PTSD biology for complex PTSD.

## What this layer is not

It is not a systematic review and not a clinical resource. Lessons summarise the sources currently in the vault, including abstract-only sources, and they say so where it matters. Nothing here is a diagnosis, a dose, a titration or an individual treatment decision, and no canvas edge is evidence of causation. Where the vault has no verified source, the lesson names the gap instead of filling it.
