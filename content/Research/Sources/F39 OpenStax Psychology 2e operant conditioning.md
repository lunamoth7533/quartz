## Key points (as published)

Operant conditioning changes behaviour through consequences: reinforcement increases and punishment decreases the likelihood of a behaviour.
^f39-consequences

Reinforcement schedules produce characteristic patterns of responding, which is how partial reinforcement effects are taught.
^f39-schedules

Shaping builds complex behaviour by reinforcing successive approximations, and punishment is described as less effective and more variable than reinforcement.
^f39-shaping
