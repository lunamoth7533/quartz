# The International Trauma Questionnaire: development of a self-report measure of ICD-11 PTSD and complex PTSD

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The ITQ was developed as a self-report measure of ICD-11 PTSD and complex PTSD, with symptom indicators selected through item response theory in trauma-exposed community and clinical samples.
^p30178492-development

The optimised 12-item version showed a latent structure consistent with prior findings, supporting the ICD-11 distinction in the studied samples.
^p30178492-structure

The authors report diagnostic rates against earlier validation work, which frames the instrument as an evolving standard with sample-dependent properties.
^p30178492-limits

## Scope as reported

A psychometric development study. Access in this build was abstract-level; instrument performance in a new population must be established rather than assumed.
