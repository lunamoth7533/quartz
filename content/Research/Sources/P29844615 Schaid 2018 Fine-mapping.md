## Reported findings (checked full text)

A genome-wide association signal marks a region in linkage disequilibrium, so identifying which variant is causal requires additional statistical and functional work.
^p29844615-problem

The review covers fine-mapping approaches that construct credible sets of variants consistent with the data and can be combined with functional annotation.
^p29844615-methods

Fine-mapping narrows candidates; it does not by itself establish the mechanism by which a variant acts, and results depend on sample size and ancestry.
^p29844615-limits
