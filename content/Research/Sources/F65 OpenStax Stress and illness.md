## Key points (as published)

The section distinguishes psychophysiological disorders, where stress and emotion contribute to physical symptoms, from purely infectious explanations.
^f65-psychophys

Chronic stressors are linked with altered immune function in the reviewed studies, and the text frames this as bidirectional rather than a one-way causal path.
^f65-immune

Social status, control and chronic strain are described as conditions that shape stress exposure and health outcomes, not as personality traits.
^f65-social

## Scope and limitations

An introductory psychology textbook section. It summarises correlational and experimental literature at a teaching level and carries no clinical guidance.
^f65-limit
