# Clinical Descriptions and Diagnostic Requirements for ICD-11 Mental, Behavioural and Neurodevelopmental Disorders

## Key points (as published)

The CDDR is the WHO's diagnostic manual for the ICD-11 mental, behavioural and neurodevelopmental disorders chapter, providing descriptions and required features for each category.
^f93-purpose

Categories are numbered (for example 6A60 for bipolar type I disorder) and each entry gives essential features, boundaries with other categories and course qualifiers.
^f93-structure

It is written to support reliable clinical identification and to accompany, not replace, the ICD-11 statistical classification itself.
^f93-use

## Scope and limitations

An official WHO manual. It defines categories; it is not evidence about mechanisms or treatment effects, and its rules differ from DSM-5-TR in places.
^f93-limit
