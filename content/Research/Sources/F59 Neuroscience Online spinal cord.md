## Key points (as published)

The cord is organised into segments, each innervating a dermatome, and nerve roots exit in relation to the vertebrae differently in the cervical region than lower down.
^f59-segments

Cross-sections show sensory afferents entering dorsally and motor neurons leaving ventrally, giving the dorsal and ventral horns their functional associations.
^f59-horns

Ascending and descending tracts occupy characteristic positions, which is why the level and pattern of a deficit carries localising information.
^f59-tracts
