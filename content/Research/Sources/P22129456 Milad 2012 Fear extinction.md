## Reported findings (checked full text)

Extinction is laboratory-learned reduction of a conditioned fear response, and it is used as a tractable model for exposure-based treatment mechanisms.
^p22129456-model

Extinction is new learning that competes with the original fear memory, which can return through spontaneous recovery, reinstatement or renewal.
^p22129456-newlearning

The review examines how findings such as consolidation and reconsolidation windows might inform treatment design, while noting translation is incomplete.
^p22129456-translation
