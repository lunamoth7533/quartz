## Key points (as published)

Classical conditioning pairs a neutral cue with a meaningful stimulus so that the cue comes to evoke the response on its own.
^f38-pairing

Extinction reduces the conditioned response by repeated presentation without the outcome, and the response can return - evidence that extinction is new learning rather than erasure.
^f38-extinction

The section describes applications including conditioned emotional responses, which is the model used in fear-learning research.
^f38-application
