## Key points (as published)

Sensory pathways are described as three-neuron chains: a primary afferent, a second-order neuron that crosses the midline, and a thalamic neuron projecting to the cortex.
^f52-pathway

The postcentral gyrus is where discriminative touch and proprioception reach cortex, which is why it is treated as the primary somatosensory area.
^f52-cortex

The section also covers brain-stem and spinal processing, where reflexive and autonomic integrations happen before information reaches the cortex.
^f52-brainstem

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section. Pathway diagrams are simplified: parallel channels, feedback and convergence are not represented.
^f52-limit
