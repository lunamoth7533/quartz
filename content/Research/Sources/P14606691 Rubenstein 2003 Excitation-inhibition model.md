## Reported findings (checked full text)

The paper proposes that an increased ratio of excitation to inhibition in key neural systems could contribute to autism features, including sensory and social differences.
^p14606691-proposal

It links the proposal to candidate mechanisms such as altered GABAergic or glutamatergic signalling and to conditions that share seizure vulnerability.
^p14606691-mechanisms

It is a mechanistic hypothesis paper that has framed later research; it is not a demonstration that autism is caused by an E/I imbalance.
^p14606691-status

## Scope as reported

A widely cited hypothesis paper in Genes, Brain and Behavior. The model is proposed as a research framework and remains contested in detail.
