## Key points (as published)

A stroke occurs when blood flow to part of the brain is interrupted, either by a blockage or by bleeding, and brain cells begin to die within minutes.
^f74-mechanism

Risk factors named include high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, smoking, and higher risk in some population groups, with age as a strong factor.
^f74-risk

The page emphasises immediate emergency care and rehabilitation afterwards, and directs individual decisions to clinicians.
^f74-urgency

## Scope and limitations
