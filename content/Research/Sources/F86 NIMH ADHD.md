## Key points (as published)

ADHD is described as a developmental disorder with patterns of inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity that interfere with functioning or development.
^f86-definition

The page covers presentation across childhood, adolescence and adulthood, and notes that symptoms can change in form while difficulties persist.
^f86-lifespan

Treatment described includes behaviour therapy, medication, and combinations, with the page noting that evidence and recommended approaches differ by age.
^f86-treatment
