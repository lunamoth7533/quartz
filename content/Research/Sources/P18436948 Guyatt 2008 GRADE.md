## Reported findings (checked full text)

GRADE separates the certainty of evidence from the strength of a recommendation, so a strong recommendation can rest on low-certainty evidence when values and context justify it.
^p18436948-certainty

Certainty is rated down for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias, and up for large effects and dose-response.
^p18436948-domains

The approach is designed for guideline panels making judgements across outcomes, not for grading a single study.
^p18436948-use
