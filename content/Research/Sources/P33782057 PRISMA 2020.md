## Reported findings (checked abstract)

PRISMA 2020 is a reporting guideline: it specifies what a systematic review should report so readers can assess what was done.
^p33782057-purpose

It includes a checklist covering the review's rationale, methods, search strategy, selection process, synthesis, certainty assessment and funding.
^p33782057-items

It recommends a flow diagram documenting how records were identified, screened, excluded and included, so the selection path is auditable.
^p33782057-flow

## Scope as reported

A reporting standard, not evidence about any intervention. It governs completeness of reporting, not the quality of the underlying studies.
^p33782057-limit
