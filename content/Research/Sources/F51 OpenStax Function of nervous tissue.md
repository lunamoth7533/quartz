## Key points (as published)

Graded potentials are local changes in membrane potential whose size depends on the stimulus; they decrease with distance and are the receiving end of neural signalling.
^f51-graded

Graded potentials summate in space and time, and their combined effect at the trigger zone determines whether a neuron reaches threshold and fires.
^f51-summation

Because graded potentials vary in amplitude, they carry information about stimulus strength that action potentials, being all-or-none, encode by their pattern instead.
^f51-coding

An introductory textbook section. It describes the classical model of summation and does not cover dendritic computation in detail.
