## Reported findings (checked abstract)

In primary visual cortex, activity-dependent development is triggered by functional maturation of local inhibitory connections, including a late-developing subset of interneurons.
^p16261181-trigger

Structural consolidation of competing sensory inputs involves proteolytic reorganisation of the extracellular matrix that occurs during the critical period.
^p16261181-consolidation

The review argues that reactivating this process could support recovery of function in conditions such as amblyopia, framed as a research strategy.
^p16261181-reactivation
