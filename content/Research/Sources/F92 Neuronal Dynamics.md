# Neuronal Dynamics: From Single Neurons to Networks and Models of Cognition

**Publisher.** Gerstner, Kistler, Naud and Paninski

**Source.** <https://neuronaldynamics.epfl.ch/online/index.html>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F92.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The book develops formal models of neurons, from integrate-and-fire to conductance-based descriptions, and connects model parameters to measured quantities.
^f92-models

It extends the treatment to populations and networks, where dynamics and coding properties emerge from connectivity rather than from single cells.
^f92-networks

Methods for analysing and fitting models are part of the text, which makes explicit that a model's usefulness depends on whether its parameters can be identified from data.
^f92-analysis

## Scope and limitations

An open advanced textbook. It presents mathematical methods rather than empirical claims about specific disorders.
^f92-limit
