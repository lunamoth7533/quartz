# Cells of the Nervous System

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Psychology 2e, section 3.2

## Key points (as published)

The chapter lists acetylcholine with muscle action and memory, dopamine with mood, sleep and learning, serotonin with mood and sleep, noradrenaline with alertness, GABA with brain function and sleep, and glutamate with learning and memory.
^f06-transmitters

Agonists mimic a neurotransmitter at the receptor while antagonists block it, and reuptake inhibitors keep transmitters active in the cleft for longer.
^f06-receptor

Some neurons communicate through electrical synapses formed by gap junctions; they are much less common than chemical synapses and much faster.
^f06-electrical
