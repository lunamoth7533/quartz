## Key points (as published)

Pharmacodynamics describes what a drug does to the body, usually by acting on receptors, enzymes, transporters or ion channels.
^f13-targets

Agonists mimic or enhance a signal while antagonists block it, and the same target can appear in many tissues, which is why effects are rarely single.
^f13-agonism
