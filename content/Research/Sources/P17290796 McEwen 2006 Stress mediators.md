## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The paper develops the concept of allostasis: stability through change, where mediators adapt to demand rather than holding a constant set point.
^p17290796-allostasis

It describes allostatic load as the cumulative cost of repeated or poorly managed stress responses, with the brain as both regulator and target.
^p17290796-load

The argument covers glucocorticoids, autonomic activity and other mediators, treating their effects as protective acutely and potentially damaging when dysregulated.
^p17290796-mediators
