# Sensation versus Perception

## Key points (as published)

Sensation is the detection of a stimulus by a sensory receptor, while perception is the organisation and interpretation of that information.
^f37-sensation

Detection thresholds describe the minimum stimulus that can be noticed, and signal detection theory separates sensitivity from response bias.
^f37-absolute

Illusions are used to show that perception is constructive: the same sensory input supports different interpreted scenes depending on context and expectation.
^f37-illusions

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section on sensation and perception. It teaches established psychophysics at a general level.
