# ILAE official report: a practical clinical definition of epilepsy (2014)

**Publisher.** International League Against Epilepsy; published in Epilepsia

## Key points (as published)

The task force proposed that epilepsy be considered a disease of the brain defined by any of three conditions: (1) at least two unprovoked (or reflex) seizures occurring >24 h apart; (2) one unprovoked (or reflex) seizure and a probability of further seizures similar to the general recurrence risk (at least 60%) after two unprovoked seizures, occurring over the next 10 years; (3) diagnosis of an epilepsy syndrome.
^f130-criteria

Epilepsy is considered resolved for individuals who either had an age-dependent epilepsy syndrome but are now past the applicable age, or who have remained seizure-free for the last 10 years and off antiseizure medicines for at least the last 5 years.
^f130-resolution

## Scope and limitations

Abstract-level access. Consensus definition for clinical use; not a treatment guideline.
