## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The study analysed motor cortex population activity as a dynamical system, finding that trial-to-trial activity occupies a low-dimensional space.
^p22722855-population

Preparatory and movement-related activity form distinct trajectories through that space, which the authors argue is not well captured by single-neuron response properties.
^p22722855-trajectory

The paper's claim is about the descriptive power of population-level models, not about a specific circuit mechanism.
^p22722855-claim

## Scope as reported

A primary analysis of motor-cortex recordings in primates, published in Nature. Population-level findings depend on the recorded population and task.
