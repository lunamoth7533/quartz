## Key points (as published)

Psychotherapy approaches are grouped by orientation - psychodynamic, behavioural, cognitive, humanistic - and the section distinguishes what each targets.
^f49-psychotherapy

Behavioural therapies apply conditioning principles directly, including exposure-based methods developed for fear and avoidance.
^f49-behavioural

Biomedical treatments are described in parallel, and the section notes that psychotherapy and medication are frequently combined rather than chosen exclusively.
^f49-biomedical

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section on treatment types. It describes approaches rather than comparing their effect sizes.
^f49-limit
