# What Is Stress?

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Psychology 2e, section 14.1

**Source.** <https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/14-1-what-is-stress>

**Verification.** Official page opened and checked on 2026-09-24; educational material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Stress responses depend on appraisal: the same demand can be experienced as a threat or a challenge, which changes the physiological response.
^f08-appraisal

The sympathetic response is fast and the HPA axis is slower, ending in cortisol release that mobilises energy and modulates many systems.
^f08-axis

Acute stress responses are adaptive; chronic activation is associated with wear on regulation, mood and health.
^f08-chronic
