## Key points (as published)

Observational learning describes acquiring behaviour by watching others, which the section presents alongside conditioning as a distinct route to learning.
^f99-modeling

Attention, retention, reproduction and motivation are described as the steps through which modelled behaviour becomes performance.
^f99-steps

The section discusses both prosocial and antisocial learning by observation, which is why media and modelling effects are studied in this framework.
