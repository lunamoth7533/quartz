## Reported findings (checked full text)

Misinterpretation of tests, intervals and power remains widespread, and no reading of them is simple and foolproof.
^p27209009-misinterpretation

Selecting analyses after seeing results can produce small P values when the hypothesis is true and large ones when it is false.
^p27209009-protocols

The essay lists 25 misinterpretations, including reading P as the probability that the hypothesis is true.
^p27209009-guide

Confidence intervals are compatibility ranges under the data and model, not probability distributions for the parameter.
^p27209009-ci

Power is a property of a test design under assumptions, not a probability that a result is correct.
^p27209009-power

## Scope as reported

A methodological essay about how statistical summaries are interpreted, aimed at researchers and readers rather than a study of any population. Its examples are illustrative rather than exhaustive.
^p27209009-limit
