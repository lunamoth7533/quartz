## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Converging pharmacological and imaging findings support a model in which elevated striatal D2/3 receptor availability and a hyperactive reward network accompany mania, while bipolar depression shows increased dopamine transporter levels and inconsistent changes elsewhere.
^p28289283-state

Dopamine agonists and antidopaminergic drugs have both improved bipolar depressive symptoms in pharmacological studies, which the authors suggest may reflect other receptors.
^p28289283-pharmacology

The receptor and transporter homeostasis model is presented as speculation, with treatment implications if confirmed.
^p28289283-speculative

## Scope as reported

Mechanistic review; the authors report reliance on pharmacological evidence, which can affect other monoamines, and scarce imaging evidence on dopaminergic function.
^p28289283-limit
