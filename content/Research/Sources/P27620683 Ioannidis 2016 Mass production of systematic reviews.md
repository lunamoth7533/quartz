## Reported findings (checked full text)

Systematic review output has escalated, with reviews of trials making up about half of published reviews.
^p27620683-volume

Many reviews are redundant, and overlapping meta-analyses of the same questions are documented.
^p27620683-redundancy

Industry ties are common, and results tend to align with sponsor interests where studied.
^p27620683-conflict

Misleading conclusions can be inherited from biased included studies or created by review-level choices.
^p27620683-misleading

Demand from clinicians and patients increases the supply of reviews regardless of quality.
^p27620683-capacity

## Scope as reported

An essay synthesizing published meta-research, not a systematic review itself, with a literature base through 2016. It argues about the population of reviews rather than estimating a frequency in a defined sample.
^p27620683-limit

## Analyst cautions (AI synthesis)

These paragraphs are this library's own appraisal, not statements from the essay.

A systematic review is a method, not a seal of quality. Because reviews inherit the biases of the studies they pool and add selection, synthesis and interpretation choices of their own, this library treats a review as a starting point that still has to be traced to the underlying studies.
^p27620683-caution-use
