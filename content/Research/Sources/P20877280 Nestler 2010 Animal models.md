## Reported findings (checked full text)

The paper reviews criteria for animal models, including construct, face and predictive validity, and applies them to depression, anxiety, addiction and psychosis models.
^p20877280-validity

It is explicit that no animal model reproduces a human psychiatric disorder, and that model value depends on the specific question being asked.
^p20877280-limits

Construct validity - whether the model's biology matches the human mechanism - is identified as the hardest and most important criterion.
^p20877280-construct

## Scope as reported

A review in Nature Neuroscience discussing how models should be judged. It is guidance on inference, not evidence about a disorder.
^p20877280-limit
