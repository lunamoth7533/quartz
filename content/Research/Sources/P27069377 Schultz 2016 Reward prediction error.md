## Reported findings (checked full text)

Midbrain dopamine neurons respond to reward-prediction error: activation when an outcome is better than predicted, and depression when it is worse.
^p27069377-rpe

This signal maps onto the teaching signal used in reinforcement learning, which is why the finding links cellular recording to a computational framework.
^p27069377-learning

The review notes that dopamine carries additional signals beyond reward prediction error, including salience and movement-related information.
^p27069377-scope
