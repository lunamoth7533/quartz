## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review pooled studies measuring endogenous cytokine concentrations in bipolar disorder, reporting results by affective state, with 13 studies and over 500 patients.
^p22749156-design

Levels of TNF-alpha, soluble TNF receptor type 1 and soluble interleukin-2 receptor were elevated in the included studies.
^p22749156-finding

Meta-analytic differences in circulating markers describe group averages; they do not identify a cause of bipolar disorder or a usable individual test.
^p22749156-caution
