# Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder

## Key points (as published)

PTSD is described as developing after exposure to a traumatic event, with intrusive memories, avoidance, negative changes in thinking and mood, and altered arousal.
^f88-pattern

The page notes that not everyone exposed develops PTSD and that symptoms, timing and severity vary between people.
^f88-variation

Treatment approaches described include trauma-focused psychotherapies and medication, with research continuing on prevention and early intervention.
^f88-treatment

## Scope and limitations

An official public education page. It orients readers to the construct and treatment landscape without presenting trial data.
