## Key points (as published)

Sleep cycles through rapid-eye-movement and non-REM stages, each associated with different patterns of brain activity and function.
^f14-stages

Sleep timing is regulated by circadian rhythms interacting with homeostatic sleep drive: how long you have been awake and what your body clock expects.
