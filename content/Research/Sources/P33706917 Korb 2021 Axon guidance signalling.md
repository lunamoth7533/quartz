## Reported findings (checked full text)

The review focuses on how guidance receptors are regulated rather than only which cue molecules exist, which is where much of the complexity sits.
^p33706917-regulation

Receptor trafficking, modification and local signalling change how a growth cone interprets the same cue in different contexts.
^p33706917-context

Misregulation of guidance signalling is discussed in relation to developmental and degenerative conditions, as a research direction rather than a mechanistic conclusion.
