# Systematic review of distributed practice and retrieval practice in health professions education

**Authors.** Trumble E, Lodge J, Mandrusiak A, Forbes R. (4 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615780/)

**Verification.** Open-access full text read through Europe PMC on 2026-09-24 (CC BY). Blocks are original paraphrases; the review's own numbers are reported as its findings.

## Reported findings (checked full text)

Distributed practice spaces study over time; retrieval practice recalls information from memory.
^p37615780-definition

From 1818 records, 56 studies and 63 experiments; 43 showed significant benefit and one a negative effect.
^p37615780-results

Studies averaged 12.23/18 on MERSQI and 4.55/6 on NOS-E, with heterogeneous designs.
^p37615780-quality

Heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis, and a single reviewer scored quality.
^p37615780-limits

Time on task and assessment stakes are flagged as confounders to track.
^p37615780-confounders

## Scope as reported

A systematic review of health professions education, with academic grades as the outcome. It reviews study strategies rather than clinical treatments, and its conclusions are about that educational setting.
^p37615780-scope

## Analyst cautions (AI synthesis)

These paragraphs are this library's own appraisal, not statements from the review.

The finding supports using retrieval and spacing as a personal study routine; it does not show that a particular schedule is optimal, and grade improvements in health professions students may not transfer unchanged to other learners, subjects or outcomes.
^p37615780-caution-transfer
