## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The paper describes a distinct interoceptive system carrying homeostatic afferent activity from all tissues, separate from cutaneous mechanoreception and proprioception.
^p12965300-system

Primary interoceptive representation is placed in the dorsal posterior insula, producing feelings such as pain, temperature, itch, hunger, thirst and air hunger.
^p12965300-insula

A re-representation in the right anterior insula is proposed as a basis for the subjective sense of the bodily self and emotion.
^p12965300-self
