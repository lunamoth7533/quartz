## Reported findings (checked full text)

Mendelian randomisation uses genetic variants as instruments for a modifiable exposure to estimate the effect of that exposure on an outcome, and its validity rests on stated assumptions: the instrument is associated with the exposure, is not associated with confounders of the exposure-outcome relationship, and affects the outcome only through the exposure.
^p30002074-assumptions

The guide sets out the checks and sensitivity analyses a reader should expect - including assessment of instrument strength, pleiotropy and the consistency of estimates across methods and populations - and warns that a genetic association alone does not establish the causal claim.
^p30002074-checks

## Scope as reported

A methodological guide written for clinicians reading published studies, illustrated with examples rather than reporting a new analysis. It describes when the design is informative and what its assumptions require.
