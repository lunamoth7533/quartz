## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The analysis identified 102 risk genes (false discovery rate 0.1 or less); most act on gene regulation or neuronal communication and are expressed early in development.
^p31981491-genes

Forty-nine genes were enriched in people ascertained for severe developmental delay and 53 in people ascertained for autism, with phenotypic differences between groups.
^p31981491-subgroups

Risk-gene expression was enriched in excitatory and inhibitory lineages, which the authors call consistent with multiple paths to an imbalance.
^p31981491-model

## Scope as reported

Genetic association study using rare de novo and case-control variation; it estimates population-level risk contributions.
