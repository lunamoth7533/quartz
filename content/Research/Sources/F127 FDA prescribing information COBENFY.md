# COBENFY (xanomeline and trospium chloride) prescribing information

**Publisher.** U.S. Food and Drug Administration (approved labelling for Karuna Therapeutics, Inc.)

**Source.** <https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/216158s000lbl.pdf>

**Verification.** official PDF retrieved and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F127.txt`. Approved labelling, so it states the sponsor's and the agency's position rather than reporting an experiment.

## Key points (as published)

COBENFY is indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia in adults.
^f127-indication

Section 12.1 states that the mechanism of action of xanomeline in schizophrenia is unclear, but that its efficacy is thought to be due to agonist activity at M1 and M4 muscarinic acetylcholine receptors in the central nervous system; trospium chloride is a muscarinic antagonist that antagonises muscarinic receptors primarily in peripheral tissues.
^f127-mechanism

Section 12.2 reports that xanomeline binds M1 to M5 with comparable affinity and shows higher agonist activity at M1 and M4, which is how a centrally acting agonist is combined with a peripherally restricted antagonist.
^f127-pharmacodynamics

## Scope and limitations

Approved labelling. The mechanism statement is hedged by the sponsor and the agency; it is a regulatory statement of what efficacy is thought to follow from, not a demonstrated causal pathway. This note carries no dosing guidance.
^f127-limit
