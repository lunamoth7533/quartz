## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The project replicated 100 experimental and correlational studies from three psychology journals, using high-powered designs and original materials where available.
^p26315443-design

Replication effect sizes averaged about half the original magnitudes, and 36 percent of replications reached significance compared with 97 percent of originals.
^p26315443-result

Results varied by journal and subfield, so the paper reports a distribution of outcomes rather than a single reproducibility rate for psychology.
^p26315443-scope
