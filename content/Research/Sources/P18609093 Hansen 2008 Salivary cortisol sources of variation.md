## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review concludes that sampling time must be registered and included in the analysis; that longitudinal designs collect at the same time of year; that food and vigorous exercise be avoided for at least two hours beforehand; and that results differ between laboratory techniques, so the same or comparable technique must be used.
^p18609093-recommendations

It recommends collecting and where possible controlling for alcohol use, medication such as oral contraceptives, and treatment for mental disease, while stating that this rests on judgement rather than hard evidence.
^p18609093-controls

## Scope as reported

A methodological review of field measurement of salivary cortisol in occupational settings. Its recommendations are measurement guidance, and several of its controls are stated to rest on judgement rather than evidence.
