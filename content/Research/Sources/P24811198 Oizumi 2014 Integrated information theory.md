## Reported findings (checked abstract)

IIT 3.0 begins with phenomenological axioms about experience and translates them into postulates that a physical system must satisfy to be conscious.
^p24811198-axioms

The theory defines quantities, notably integrated information, intended to measure how much a system's causal structure is irreducible to its parts.
^p24811198-phi

It is a mathematical theory with committed claims, including that feedforward systems cannot be conscious; those claims are what critics target.
^p24811198-claim

## Scope as reported

A theory paper in PLOS Computational Biology. It presents a formal framework and its predictions; it does not present a validated measure of consciousness in clinical use.
^p24811198-limit
