# Neuropeptides and Nitric Oxide

## Key points (as published)

Neuropeptides are organised into families, including the opioid peptides, with sequence relationships that predict receptor cross-reactivity.
^f107-families

Peptides are made from larger precursors and processed through successive enzymatic steps before release, so synthesis is slower than for small-molecule transmitters.
^f107-processing

Peptidergic and gaseous messengers are described as signalling in ways that extend beyond classical point-to-point synaptic transmission, including heterosynaptic effects.
^f107-transmission
