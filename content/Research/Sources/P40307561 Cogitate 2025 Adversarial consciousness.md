# Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness

**Publisher.** Springer Nature (Nature)

**Source.** <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1>

**Verification.** Article page opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/P40307561-cogitate-2025-adversarial-consciousness.txt`. Open-access article.

## Key points (as published)

Preregistered adversarial collaboration of integrated information theory (IIT) vs global neuronal workspace theory (GNWT) in 256 human participants (fMRI, MEG, intracranial EEG).
^p40307561-design

Results align with some predictions of each theory while substantially challenging key tenets of both: IIT by the absence of sustained posterior-cortex synchronisation; GNWT by the lack of ignition at stimulus offset and limited prefrontal representation of certain conscious dimensions.
^p40307561-results

Authors frame this as an alternative approach to advance cognitive neuroscience through principled, theory-driven collaborative research.
^p40307561-interpretation

## Scope and limitations

Human visual-paradigm scope; not all predictions of either theory are tested, and the authors frame results as challenges to specific predictions rather than definitive refutations of the theories.
