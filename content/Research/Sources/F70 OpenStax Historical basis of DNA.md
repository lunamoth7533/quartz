## Key points (as published)

Griffith's experiments showed that a substance transferred between bacteria could change their heritable properties, the observation later called the transforming principle.
^f70-transforming

The section follows the work that identified DNA as the molecule carrying that information, distinguishing it from protein-based explanations of inheritance.
^f70-identity

It presents these experiments as historical cases of inference from indirect evidence, which is how genetic reasoning still works.
^f70-frames
