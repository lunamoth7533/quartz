# Definitions of the RDoC Domains and Constructs

**Publisher.** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)

**Source.** <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/definitions-of-the-rdoc-domains-and-constructs>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F122.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The page names six domains: negative valence, positive valence, cognitive systems, systems for social processes, arousal and regulatory systems, and sensorimotor systems.
^f122-domains

Each domain is broken into constructs with working definitions, such as acute threat under negative valence or working memory under cognitive systems.
^f122-constructs

Constructs are intended to be measured across levels of analysis and to cut across conventional diagnoses, which is the framework's central methodological claim.
^f122-use

## Scope and limitations

An official definitions page. It provides working research definitions and explicitly does not present them as diagnostic entities.
^f122-limit
