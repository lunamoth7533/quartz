# What Is Cognition?

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Psychology 2e, section 7.1

## Key points (as published)

Cognition is described through concepts - mental categories built from prototypes and from defined features - which is how the section introduces categorisation.
^f97-concepts

Natural concepts are built from experience while artificial concepts are defined by rules, and the section uses examples to show that everyday categories are fuzzy.
^f97-types

Schemas are described as frameworks that organise knowledge and guide interpretation, which is why schemas support fast processing and can also produce systematic error.
^f97-schemas
