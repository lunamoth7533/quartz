## Reported findings (checked full text)

Network meta-analysis compares treatments that have not been compared head to head, by combining direct and indirect evidence through shared comparators.
^p27913917-indirect

Validity depends on the transitivity assumption, meaning that study populations and settings are similar enough for indirect comparisons to be meaningful.
^p27913917-assumption

Rankings are estimates with uncertainty, and the review advises reading them alongside confidence in the evidence rather than as a definitive ordering.
^p27913917-interpretation
