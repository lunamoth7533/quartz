## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The paper targets theories that identify consciousness with causal structure, such as IIT and recurrent processing theory.
^p31078047-target

Using results from computability theory, the authors argue such theories are either false or unfalsifiable, because any behaviour can be produced by candidate structures that differ in consciousness-relevant ways.
^p31078047-argument

It is a critique in an ongoing debate rather than a finding that settles which theory is correct; functionalist theories are treated differently by the argument.
^p31078047-debate
