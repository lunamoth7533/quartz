## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The study used an online survey with a large sample of autistic adults to characterise deliberate and less deliberate strategies for managing self-presentation.
^p28527095-method

Reported strategies grouped around masking, compensation and assimilation, and participants described both effort and perceived benefits and costs.
^p28527095-strategies

Camouflaging was described as demanding and linked by participants to exhaustion and reduced sense of self, which motivates research on assessment and support implications.
^p28527095-consequence

## Scope as reported

A survey study reporting self-reported experience. It documents what participants describe and does not establish prevalence or clinical causality.
^p28527095-limit
