## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Cortisol incorporated into growing hair is assumed to provide a retrospective reflection of integrated cortisol secretion over periods of several months, addressing limitations the authors attribute to blood, saliva and urine strategies for long-term assessment.
^p22366690-window

The review reports accumulating supportive evidence for hair cortisol concentrations as an index of long-term systemic cortisol levels, for reliability across repeated assessments, and for relative robustness to a range of potential confounding influences.
^p22366690-evidence

## Scope as reported

A methodological review of hair cortisol analysis. Its summary statements are about a measurement development and its accumulating validation evidence; the assumption language ('assumed to provide') and the review's scope are part of the finding.
