## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The prefrontal cortex is critical for regulating attention, screening distractions, inhibiting impulses and planning.
^p19621976-networks

Prefrontal function depends on noradrenaline and dopamine released according to arousal state, with too little or too much impairing function.
^p19621976-catecholamines

Noradrenaline benefit is attributed to postsynaptic alpha-2A receptors; moderate dopamine benefit to D1 receptors.
^p19621976-mechanisms

Imaging studies in ADHD show underactive prefrontal regions with weakened connections, and animal studies show alpha-2A blockade producing an ADHD-like profile.
^p19621976-evidence

## Scope as reported

Mechanistic review. Findings combine human imaging, genetic linkage and animal pharmacology rather than a single clinical trial.
^p19621976-limit
