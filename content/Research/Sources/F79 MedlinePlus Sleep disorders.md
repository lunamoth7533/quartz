## Key points (as published)

Sleep disorders are grouped as a family of problems with falling asleep, staying asleep, timing and unusual behaviours during sleep.
^f79-scope

Causes named include other conditions, medications, stress, shift work and circadian factors, which is why assessment looks beyond sleep itself.
^f79-causes

The page links sleep studies and other evaluations as tools rather than describing them as routine.
^f79-assessment

## Scope and limitations

A consumer health overview. It orients readers to categories and assessment without diagnostic criteria.
^f79-limit
