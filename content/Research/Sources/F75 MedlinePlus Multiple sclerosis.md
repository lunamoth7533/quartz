## Key points (as published)

Multiple sclerosis is described as an autoimmune process in which the immune system attacks myelin in the central nervous system, disrupting signalling.
^f75-mechanism

The page distinguishes relapsing-remitting from progressive courses, noting that most people start with relapsing-remitting disease.
^f75-course

There is no single specific test for MS. Diagnosis can combine history, physical and neurological examination, MRI, and laboratory tests to assess the condition and alternatives.
^f75-diagnosis

## Scope and limitations

A consumer health overview describing typical courses and diagnostic logic without reporting specific trial outcomes.
