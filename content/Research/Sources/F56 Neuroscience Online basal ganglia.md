# Basal Ganglia

## Key points (as published)

The chapter groups the caudate, putamen, nucleus accumbens and globus pallidus as forebrain components of the basal ganglia, with related midbrain and diencephalic structures.
^f56-structures

Signals are processed through direct and indirect pathways whose relative activity shapes cortical output, which is why the system is described as modulating rather than generating movement.
^f56-pathways

The chapter links basal ganglia dysfunction to movement disorders, showing how pathway logic is used to interpret clinical signs.
^f56-disorders

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter presenting the classical pathway model, which has been refined since by anatomical and physiological work.
^f56-limit
