## Key points (as published)

A psychological disorder is described through clinically significant disturbance in cognition, emotion regulation or behaviour, with the definition tied to dysfunction and distress rather than to difference alone.
^f47-definition

Cultural expectations shape what counts as disordered, and the section notes that some experiences are more common in particular cultural contexts - a reminder that criteria are not culture-free.
^f47-cultural

The harm-and-dysfunction framing is presented alongside the observation that unusual behaviour alone is not disorder, which is why diagnosis requires context.
^f47-harm
