# Drug Interactions: What You Should Know

**Publisher.** US Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

**Source.** <https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-drugs/drug-interactions-what-you-should-know>

**Verification.** Official page opened and checked on 2026-09-24; educational material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Interactions are grouped as drug-drug, drug-food and drug-condition interactions, and they can change how much of a drug reaches its target.
^f19-types

The FDA directs individual decisions to product labelling and qualified clinicians or pharmacists rather than general rules.
^f19-advice

## Scope and limitations

Educational overview written for a general audience; it simplifies and does not carry the qualifications of primary literature.
