## Reported findings (checked full text)

The review argues that inflammation is relevant to a subset of depressed patients, identified by markers, rather than to depression as a whole.
^p26711676-subset

It frames inflammatory responses as evolutionarily conserved and potentially adaptive in short bursts, with costs when sustained.
^p26711676-adaptive

It discusses anti-inflammatory strategies as experimental treatment directions that require targeting and patient selection, not as established practice.
^p26711676-treatment

## Scope as reported

A review written for immunology readers. Treatment implications are presented as research directions.
