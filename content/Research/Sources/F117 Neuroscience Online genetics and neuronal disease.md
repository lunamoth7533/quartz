# Genetics and Neuronal Disease

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 1 Chapter 15

**Source.** <https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/m/s1/chapter15.html>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F117.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The chapter outlines routes from a clinical phenotype to gene identification, including functional cloning, candidate genes, positional cloning and sequencing.
^f117-routes

Mapping and cloning examples include Duchenne muscular dystrophy and shaker-1, showing how gene identification changed understanding of the disease process.
^f117-disease

It presents gene identification as a step that enables diagnosis and mechanism research, not as a complete explanation of a condition.
^f117-inference

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on genetics in neurology, written from a 1990s-2000s vantage point; techniques have advanced since.
^f117-limit
