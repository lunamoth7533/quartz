## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The paper organises brain development into overlapping processes: neurogenesis, migration, differentiation, synaptogenesis, pruning and myelination.
^p21042938-processes

These processes overlap in time across regions rather than completing in sequence, which is why structure-function relations shift with age.
^p21042938-overlap

Early experience interacts with genetically guided development, so variation in outcome is expected rather than exceptional.
^p21042938-experience

## Scope as reported

A review written for a neuropsychology audience. It describes typical development; findings are generalisations across samples.
