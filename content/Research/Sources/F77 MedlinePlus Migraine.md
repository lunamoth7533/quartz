## Key points (as published)

Migraine is described as a recurring headache disorder with attacks that can include nausea, sensitivity to light and sound, and visual or sensory changes.
^f77-attack

The page distinguishes migraine with and without aura and notes that attack frequency and triggers vary between people.
^f77-patterns

Assessment may include imaging to exclude other causes in specific presentations; management is described as individualised and clinician-led.
^f77-care
