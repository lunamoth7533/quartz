# What Is Social Psychology?

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Psychology 2e, section 12.1

## Key points (as published)

Social psychology examines how situations and interpretations shape behaviour, contrasting this with explanations that locate causes inside the person.
^f46-situational

The fundamental attribution error describes the tendency to overweight dispositional explanations for others' behaviour, a finding with replication history worth noting.
^f46-attribution

Attitudes, persuasion and group influence are treated as core topics, showing that social context is a mechanism rather than background noise.
^f46-application
