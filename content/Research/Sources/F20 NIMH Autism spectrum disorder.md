## Key points (as published)

Autism is a developmental condition that varies widely between people in its sensory, social and behavioural features.
^f20-development

Support is individualized and can include behavioural, educational, developmental and family-focused approaches.
^f20-supports

Medication may be used to target specific associated symptoms or co-occurring conditions rather than autism itself.
^f20-medication
