## Key points (as published)

Stage theories describe development as a sequence of phases; the section also presents the criticisms that stages oversimplify continuous change.
^f42-stage

Erikson's psychosocial stages extend development across the lifespan rather than ending in childhood, which is why they remain widely cited.
^f42-psychosocial

Moral development theories are presented with the caveat that the evidence for stage-like progression is limited and culturally variable.
^f42-moral

## Scope and limitations
