## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The free-energy principle proposes that action, perception and learning minimise a single quantity related to surprise and prediction error.
^p20068583-claim

The paper argues that several existing brain theories can be expressed as optimisation of value or its complement, giving the principle unifying scope.
^p20068583-unification

Its breadth is also the basis of criticism, since a framework that accommodates many theories can be difficult to falsify in specific cases.
^p20068583-status
