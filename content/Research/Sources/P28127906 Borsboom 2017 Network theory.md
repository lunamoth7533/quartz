## Reported findings (checked full text)

The network approach treats symptoms as interacting components rather than as indicators of one latent disorder, so the disorder is the network.
^p28127906-claim

It predicts hysteresis and sudden transitions, and treats comorbidity as overlap between symptom networks rather than as distinct co-occurring entities.
^p28127906-consequences

The paper is a theoretical proposal with early empirical support, not a replacement for classification in clinical use.
^p28127906-status

## Scope as reported

A theory article in World Psychiatry. Its claims are programmatic and it discusses the approach's own limitations.
