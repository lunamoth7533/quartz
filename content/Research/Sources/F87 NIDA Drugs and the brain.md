# Drugs and the Brain

**Publisher.** National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA)

**Source.** <https://nida.nih.gov/publications/drugs-brains-behavior-science-addiction/drugs-brain>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F87.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The page describes how drugs can flood reward circuitry with dopamine, producing effects that are much larger or faster than natural rewards.
^f87-reward

Repeated exposure is described as changing brain circuits involved in reward, stress and self-control, which is why use can become compulsive.
^f87-adaptation

It notes that response varies with the drug, the route, the amount, genetics and environment, so no single pathway explains all substance-use problems.
^f87-variation

## Scope and limitations

An official public education page summarising addiction neuroscience in general terms. It is not a treatment guide.
^f87-limit
