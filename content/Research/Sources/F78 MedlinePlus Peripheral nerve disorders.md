# Peripheral Nerve Disorders

## Key points (as published)

Peripheral nerve disorders are described as a large group affecting the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord, with over a hundred recognised types.
^f78-list

Causes include diabetes, infections, nutritional deficiencies, toxins, trauma and inherited conditions, and many are illustrated by symptoms first.
^f78-causes

Symptoms described include weakness, aching, and tingling, numbness or pain that typically begins in the hands and feet.
^f78-symptoms

## Scope and limitations

A consumer health overview. It aggregates a heterogeneous group of conditions and does not rank causes by frequency.
^f78-limit
