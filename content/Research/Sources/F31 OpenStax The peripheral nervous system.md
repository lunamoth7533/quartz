# The Peripheral Nervous System

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, section 13.4

**Source.** <https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/13-4-the-peripheral-nervous-system>

## Key points (as published)

Peripheral nerves bundle axons with connective tissue; spinal nerves carry both sensory and motor fibres and reorganise in plexuses before reaching their targets.
^f31-nerves

Twelve cranial nerves are named and each is described as sensory, motor or mixed, with the specific functions it serves.
^f31-cranial

The section distinguishes somatic and autonomic peripheral structures, including the enteric nervous system as a largely self-organising peripheral network.
^f31-enteric

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section. Cranial-nerve functions are presented in summary form for teaching, not as a clinical localisation reference.
^f31-limit
