# Epilepsies in children, young people and adults (NG217)

**Publisher.** National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), UK

**Source.** <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng217/chapter/recommendations>

**Verification.** official page opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F95.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The guideline covers referral and diagnostic pathways, including the role of specialist assessment after a first suspected seizure.
^f95-diagnosis

It recommends specific antiseizure medicines by seizure type and syndrome and sets out when surgery or other interventions are considered.
^f95-treatment

It lists research recommendations where the evidence was insufficient, which marks the boundary of what the guideline can settle.
^f95-research

## Scope and limitations

UK national guidance. Recommendations are specific to the UK service model and to the evidence review used.
^f95-limit
