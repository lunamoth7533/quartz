# The Action Potential

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, section 12.4

**Source.** <https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-4-the-action-potential>

**Verification.** Official page opened and checked on 2026-09-24; educational material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Neurons maintain ion gradients across the membrane, producing a resting membrane potential that is negative inside relative to outside.
^f03-gradient

If depolarisation reaches threshold, voltage-gated ion channels open in sequence and produce an action potential with a characteristic rise and fall.
^f03-threshold

The action potential is an all-or-none event that propagates at full strength along the axon rather than fading with distance.
^f03-signals
