## Key points (as published)

The section states the central dogma: information flows from DNA to RNA to protein, with replication, transcription and translation as distinct steps.
^f71-dogma

Nucleotides are read in three-base codons, most amino acids are specified by more than one codon, and stop codons end translation.
^f71-codons

Ribosomes, tRNAs and the reading frame together determine which protein is produced, so the same sequence read differently gives a different product.
^f71-machinery
