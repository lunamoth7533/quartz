## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review describes several communication routes between gut microbiota and brain: immune signalling, tryptophan metabolism, the vagus nerve and the enteric nervous system.
^p31460832-routes

Microbial metabolites such as short-chain fatty acids are treated as candidate mediators of these effects.
^p31460832-metabolites

The authors present the field as rapidly growing and emphasise that many findings are correlational, with causal direction often unresolved.
^p31460832-status
