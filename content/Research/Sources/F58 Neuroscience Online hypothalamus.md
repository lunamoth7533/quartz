# Hypothalamus: Structural Organization

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 4 Chapter 1

## Key points (as published)

The hypothalamus is described in longitudinal zones, including a periventricular zone containing the arcuate and paraventricular nuclei, which are central to neuroendocrine and autonomic control.
^f58-zones

A table maps nuclei and zones to functions, showing that small structures carry many distinct regulatory roles.
^f58-nuclei

The chapter also covers biological timing, including circadian expression patterns, linking hypothalamic structure to daily rhythms.
^f58-timing

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on hypothalamic organisation. Functional assignments come from animal lesion, stimulation and tracing studies.
^f58-limit
