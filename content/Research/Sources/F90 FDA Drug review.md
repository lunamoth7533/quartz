# Step 4: FDA Drug Review

## Key points (as published)

A New Drug Application presents the full data package from development, including animal and human studies, for review as one submission.
^f90-nda

The review team can refuse to file an incomplete application, asks clarifying questions, and may convene an advisory committee when the evidence is not clear-cut.
^f90-process

Approval decisions weigh the submitted evidence against the standard the agency applies, and the page separates that regulatory judgement from the fact of testing.
^f90-decision

## Scope and limitations

An official process description. It explains review mechanics rather than the scientific strength of any particular evidence base.
^f90-limit
