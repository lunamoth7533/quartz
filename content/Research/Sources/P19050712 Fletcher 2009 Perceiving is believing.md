# Perceiving is believing: a Bayesian approach to explaining the positive symptoms of schizophrenia

**Authors.** Fletcher PC, Frith CD. · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19050712/)

**Published.** 2009.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1038/nrn2536](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2536) · PMID [19050712](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19050712/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors propose that hallucinations and delusions can be understood together as a disturbance in error-dependent updating of inferences and beliefs about the world, within a disturbed hierarchical Bayesian framework, rather than as separate abnormalities of perception and belief formation.
^p19050712-proposal

## Scope as reported

A theoretical review in a clinical-neuroscience journal. It proposes an explanatory framework for the positive symptoms of schizophrenia; a proposal of this kind is not itself evidence that the brain implements the framework, and the review reports no new experiment.
