## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Dose-effect patterns differed by class and age: median efficacy plateaued above study-specific doses for methylphenidate, amphetamines and guanfacine in children, and for amphetamines in adults, while adult methylphenidate kept rising on sparse data.
^p42134365-doseeffect

Discontinuation for adverse events rose with dose for amphetamines and methylphenidate above study-specific ranges; no dose pattern appeared for atomoxetine in fixed-dose studies or modafinil.
^p42134365-tolerability

The authors say the findings challenge both therapeutic inertia and uncritical dose escalation and should support shared decision making.
^p42134365-interpretation
