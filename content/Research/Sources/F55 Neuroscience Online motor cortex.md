## Key points (as published)

The motor cortex comprises the primary motor cortex, immediately anterior to the central sulcus, plus premotor cortex and the supplementary motor area.
^f55-areas

Premotor areas use external context to select movements, while the supplementary motor area is engaged in sequences and internally generated plans.
^f55-context

Stimulation and recording studies are used to map body representations and to show how cortical activity precedes and accompanies movement.
^f55-cortex

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter summarising cortical motor organisation from stimulation, recording and lesion work, largely in animals and patients.
^f55-limit
