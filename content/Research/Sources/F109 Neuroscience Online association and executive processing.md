# Higher Cortical Functions: Association and Executive Processing

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 4 Chapter 9

**Source.** <https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/m/s4/chapter09.html>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F109.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Association areas are grouped into anterior, posterior and limbic regions, each linking information from primary and unimodal sensory or motor areas.
^f109-areas

Posterior association cortex integrates sensory streams, and bilateral parietal damage is used as a case study of the resulting syndrome.
^f109-posterior

Anterior association cortex, including prefrontal regions, is linked with executive processing, which the chapter treats as a set of interacting functions rather than one faculty.
^f109-executive

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on association cortex that relies on lesion evidence and functional interpretation.
^f109-limit
