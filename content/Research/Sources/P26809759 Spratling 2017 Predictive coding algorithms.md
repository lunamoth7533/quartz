## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The term predictive coding covers several distinct algorithms, from signal-processing linear predictive coding to cortical models, which differ in their generative models and optimisation methods.
^p26809759-plural

All of them fit a generative model to sensory data, so they share the idea that perception involves inference about hidden causes.
^p26809759-common

Because the algorithms differ substantially, evidence for one is not automatically evidence for another, which is a common conflation in discussion.
^p26809759-difference
