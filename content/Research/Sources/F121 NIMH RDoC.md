# Research Domain Criteria (RDoC)

**Publisher.** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)

**Source.** <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F121.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

RDoC is described as a research framework that organises studies around dimensions of functioning rather than around existing diagnostic categories.
^f121-purpose

The framework combines units of analysis - genes, molecules, cells, circuits, physiology, behaviour and self-report - with domains of function, forming a matrix for study design.
^f121-matrix

NIMH frames it as an approach for research on mechanisms across conditions, not as a diagnostic system for clinical use.
^f121-scope

## Scope and limitations

An official research-framework page. It explains a funding and design approach, not clinical criteria.
^f121-limit
