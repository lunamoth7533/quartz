## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Cortical grey matter was thinner bilaterally in frontal, temporal and parietal regions; the largest effects were in left pars opercularis, fusiform gyrus and rostral middle frontal cortex.
^p28461699-thickness

Commonly prescribed drugs, including lithium, antiepileptics and antipsychotics, were associated with cortical measures even after accounting for multiple medications.
^p28461699-medication

Longer illness duration was associated with reduced cortical thickness; reduced surface area was associated with a history of psychosis, and mood state showed no association.
^p28461699-course

## Scope as reported

Observational cortical MRI analysis that the authors describe as an extensive analysis of potential confounding variables in bipolar neuroimaging.
