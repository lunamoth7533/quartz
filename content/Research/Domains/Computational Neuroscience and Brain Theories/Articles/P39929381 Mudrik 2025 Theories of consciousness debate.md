## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Proponents of five theories each set out what their theory aims to explain, its core mechanisms and its founding premises, followed by an open discussion of testability, evidence that could refute each theory, and points of agreement.
^p39929381-format

The authors conclude that, at this stage, controversy outweighs agreement on the most basic questions: what consciousness is, how conscious states are identified, and what any theory of consciousness must explain.
^p39929381-disagreement

## Scope as reported

A written account of a debate held at the 2022 meeting of the Association for the Scientific Study of Consciousness; it reports positions and discussion rather than new data.
^p39929381-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.
