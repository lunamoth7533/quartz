## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Model-based analysis of behaviour requires fitting candidate models to data and comparing them, with parameter recovery checks before interpreting parameters.
^p31769410-fitting

The paper recommends simulating data from the candidate models, using cross-validation or similar methods, and reporting model comparison uncertainty.
^p31769410-validation

Different models can produce similar behaviour, so a good fit is not evidence that the hypothesised mechanism is the true one.
^p31769410-identifiability
