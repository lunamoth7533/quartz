## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review proposes that excessive fear of trauma reminders in PTSD may reflect difficulty in learning and updating abstract representations of situations (latent states), rather than only fear attached to specific stimuli.
^p38212163-reframe

It cites recent behavioural and brain data showing biased latent-state and model-based learning in PTSD, and suggests these biases may be a shared target of common PTSD therapies.
^p38212163-evidence

## Scope as reported

Narrative review with no systematic search reported; the authors list open questions about the neurocircuitry involved and about tuning treatments to these processes.
^p38212163-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

The link to treatment is a proposal; testing it needs trials that measure latent-state learning before and after therapy and relate its change to symptom change.
^p38212163-caution-hypothesis
