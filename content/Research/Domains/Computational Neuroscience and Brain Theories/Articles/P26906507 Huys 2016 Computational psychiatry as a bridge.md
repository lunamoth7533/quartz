## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Computational psychiatry is presented as a field that joins computation at several levels with several kinds of data, aiming to improve how mental illness is understood, predicted and treated.
^p26906507-definition

Data-driven approaches use machine learning on high-dimensional data to classify disorders, predict outcomes or guide treatment choice without committing to a mechanism; theory-driven approaches build models that encode explicit hypotheses about mechanisms at one or more levels of analysis.
^p26906507-approaches

The authors treat the two approaches as complementary and argue that combining them is most useful.
^p26906507-combine

## Scope as reported

Narrative review of recent advances with an emphasis on clinical applications; it reports no new data and no systematic search.
