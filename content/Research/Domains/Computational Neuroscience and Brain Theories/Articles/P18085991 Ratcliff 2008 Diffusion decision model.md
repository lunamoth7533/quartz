## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The model converts the accuracy, mean response times and full response-time distributions of a two-choice task into estimates of separate components of processing.
^p18085991-translation

In the illustrative experiments, stimulus difficulty changed the quality of the evidence (drift rate), speed or accuracy instructions changed how much evidence was needed before responding (boundary separation), and the relative frequency of the two stimuli biased drift rate and starting point.
^p18085991-parameters

Because accuracy and the shapes of the response-time distributions must be fitted together, the model is tightly constrained and, the authors stress, empirically testable and potentially falsifiable.
^p18085991-constraints

## Scope as reported

Review of the model and its wide range of applications, including ageing and neurophysiology, illustrated by three experiments rather than a systematic synthesis.
^p18085991-limit
