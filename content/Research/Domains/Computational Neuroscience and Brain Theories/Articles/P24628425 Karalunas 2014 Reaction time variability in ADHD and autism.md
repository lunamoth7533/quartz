# Annual research review: Reaction time variability in ADHD and autism spectrum disorders: measurement and mechanisms of a proposed trans-diagnostic phenotype.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Reaction time variability was raised in autism only when the samples included children who also had ADHD.
^p24628425-autism

In ADHD, the extra variability was accounted for by moderate-to-large group differences in the ex-Gaussian tail parameter tau and in the diffusion-model drift rate (d = 0.63-0.99), and by a smaller difference in non-decision time (d = 0.32).
^p24628425-ddm

The authors read the tau and drift-rate differences as possible problems of arousal or state regulation and of picking signal out of noise, read the non-decision-time difference as a possible motor contribution, and recommend decomposing reaction times rather than only summarising their spread.
^p24628425-interpretation

## Scope as reported

Annual research review of child studies that combines narrative discussion of mechanisms and neuroimaging with two new meta-analyses; the abstract does not give the number of studies pooled.
^p24628425-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Parameter differences describe how a group's reaction times are distributed; the arousal and signal-detection readings are interpretations that fit the parameters but are not measured by them.
^p24628425-caution-interpretation
