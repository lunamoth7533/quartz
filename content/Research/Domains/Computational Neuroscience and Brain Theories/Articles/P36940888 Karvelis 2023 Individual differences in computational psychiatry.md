## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors describe computational assays, cognitive tasks paired with models that infer hidden patient-specific processes, as the main route to studying clinically relevant individual differences.
^p36940888-assays

Reviewing the emerging evidence, they find that many computational measures have poor reliability and construct validity, which threatens earlier findings on individual and even group differences.
^p36940888-psychometrics

They propose remedies and set them within the developments needed before computational assays can be used in clinical practice.
^p36940888-recommendations

## Scope as reported

Review of emerging empirical evidence on psychometric properties; the abstract gives no count of studies and no pooled reliability estimate.
^p36940888-limit
