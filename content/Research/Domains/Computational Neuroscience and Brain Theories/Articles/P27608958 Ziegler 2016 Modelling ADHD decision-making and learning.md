## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review works out what each neurobiological theory of ADHD predicts for drift-diffusion and basic reinforcement-learning parameters; theories often agree on single parameters, but each implies its own combination.
^p27608958-predictions

Empirical diffusion-model studies agreed on a lower drift rate in ADHD, while findings for boundary separation were less conclusive.
^p27608958-drift

The few studies that fitted reinforcement-learning models supported lower choice sensitivity in ADHD but not a changed learning rate.
^p27608958-rl

## Scope as reported

Narrative review of theories and of the modelling studies available by 2016; the abstract reports no systematic search or pooled estimate and closes with areas for theoretical refinement.
^p27608958-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A lower drift rate is predicted by several competing theories, so finding it does not decide between them; the combination of parameters, which few studies estimate together, is what could.
