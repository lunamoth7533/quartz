# Mood stabilizers and/or antipsychotics for bipolar disorder in the maintenance phase: a systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.

**Authors.** Kishi T, Ikuta T, et al. (7 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33177610/)

**Published.** 2021 in Molecular psychiatry (first online 2020-11-11).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1038/s41380-020-00946-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-020-00946-6) · PMID [33177610](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33177610/) · PMCID [PMC8550938](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8550938/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Almost every active maintenance treatment lowered recurrence of any mood episode compared with placebo; carbamazepine and paliperidone did not, and one combination had no data.
^p33177610-any

Protection depended on polarity: lamotrigine lowered depressive but not manic, hypomanic or mixed recurrence, valproate lowered manic but not depressive recurrence, and lithium lowered both.
^p33177610-polarity

Added to lithium or valproate, quetiapine and lurasidone lowered depressive recurrence, and aripiprazole and quetiapine manic recurrence.
^p33177610-adjunct

## Scope as reported

Two categorical network meta-analyses: one of monotherapies and specified pairs, and one of second-generation antipsychotics (aripiprazole, lurasidone, olanzapine, quetiapine, ziprasidone) added to lithium or valproate; outcomes included recurrence by polarity, discontinuation, mortality and adverse events, and the authors note that profiles differed across treatments.
^p33177610-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Maintenance trials often enrol people who first responded to the study drug during an acute episode, which tilts comparisons toward that drug; pooling in a network does not remove this design feature.
^p33177610-caution-enrichment

A treatment that failed to beat placebo on one polarity may have lacked trials or power rather than effect; a non-significant network comparison is not evidence of no benefit.
^p33177610-caution-null
