## Reported findings (checked abstract)

MAOIs are used for depression and, in some cases, for Parkinson disease, with a toxicity risk that is greatest in overdose.
^p30969670-uses

The non-selective, irreversible inhibitors phenelzine and tranylcypromine can provoke hypertensive crisis when taken with foods rich in tyramine, whereas selective MAO-B inhibitors such as selegiline carry a lower hypertensive risk.
^p30969670-selectivity

Toxicity can show as agitation, flushing, a fast heart, low or high blood pressure, palpitations, muscle twitching, exaggerated reflexes, seizures and high fever, and needs prompt treatment.
^p30969670-toxicity
