## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Citing a network meta-analysis, the review reports similar efficacy against placebo but different acceptability for SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclics, mirtazapine, agomelatine and bupropion, which it groups as a dopamine reuptake inhibitor.
^p40282849-classes

Dextromethorphan-bupropion, ketamine infusion and intranasal esketamine acted faster than monoamine antidepressants but with similar effect sizes; the neurosteroids brexanolone and zuranolone work in postpartum depression, while zuranolone's effect in major depression was very small.
^p40282849-rapid

Psychedelics, still investigational, showed rapid onset, large effects and durability, pending validation in phase III trials.
^p40282849-psychedelics

## Scope as reported

Narrative review drawing on the STAR*D trial, systematic reviews and trial registries, with priority given to systematic reviews for newer drugs; it reports no pooled analysis of its own.
^p40282849-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A narrative review's cross-class comparisons inherit the designs of the trials it cites; early rapid-acting and psychedelic results come from small trials that are hard to blind, so "large effect" is a provisional description.
^p40282849-caution-narrative
