## Reported findings (checked full text)

Benzodiazepines act as positive modulators at GABA-A receptors, enhancing inhibitory signalling rather than directly activating the receptor.
^p23789008-mechanism

The review covers sedative, anxiolytic, anticonvulsant and muscle-relaxant effects, and their dependence on receptor subtype and dose.
^p23789008-effects

It describes tolerance, dependence and withdrawal, together with adverse effects such as sedation and falls in older patients.
^p23789008-risks

## Scope as reported

A pharmacology review in The Ochsner Journal aimed at clinicians. It describes mechanisms and risks, not prescriptive advice.
