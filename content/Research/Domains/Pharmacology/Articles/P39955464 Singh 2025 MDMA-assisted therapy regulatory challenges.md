## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review notes that only two drugs, the SSRIs paroxetine and sertraline, hold US approval for PTSD.
^p39955464-approved

Encouraging early trials of MDMA, an entactogen, earned MDMA-assisted therapy FDA breakthrough status in 2017; in phase 3 trials, close to 70% of those treated no longer met the diagnosis.
^p39955464-history

In 2024 the FDA turned down approval, citing trial design, including failed blinding and missing QT-prolongation and abuse-liability assessments, and allegations of possible misconduct.
^p39955464-rejection

Blinding, long-term safety and variable accompanying psychotherapy are named as problems for future trials, now framed by recent FDA guidance on psychedelic research.
^p39955464-path

## Scope as reported

Single-author narrative review of the evidence and the regulatory decision; it reports no new data.
^p39955464-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A regulatory decision weighs trial conduct and safety data as well as effect sizes, so a rejection is not a finding that the treatment fails, and the phase 3 figures come from the same trial designs the decision criticised.
^p39955464-caution-regulatory
