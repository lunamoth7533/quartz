# Tardive Dyskinesia Prevalence in the Period of Second-Generation Antipsychotic Use: A Meta-Analysis.

**Authors.** Carbon M, Hsieh CH, et al. (4 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28146614/)

**Published.** 2017 in The Journal of clinical psychiatry.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.4088/JCP.16r10832](https://doi.org/10.4088/JCP.16r10832) · PMID [28146614](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28146614/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across 41 studies about a quarter of people taking antipsychotics met rating-scale criteria for tardive dyskinesia (25.3%), with wide variation.
^p28146614-prevalence

Prevalence was lower during current second-generation than first-generation treatment (20.7% against 30.0%), and the gap persisted after adjustment for moderators including age and region.
^p28146614-class

Treatment arms never exposed to first-generation drugs had particularly low rates (7.2%, four studies), compared with 23.4% in second-generation cohorts likely to have had earlier first-generation exposure.
^p28146614-naive

Older age, longer illness and more parkinsonism went with higher prevalence.
^p28146614-moderators

## Scope as reported

Random-effects meta-analysis of cross-sectional, rating-scale studies published 2000-2015; only ten studies reported severity, too poorly to pool, so the clinical weight of the detected cases could not be judged.
^p28146614-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Cross-sectional prevalence mixes current and past exposure: many people counted under second-generation treatment had earlier first-generation exposure, so the class comparison describes treatment eras as much as drug classes.
^p28146614-caution-crosssectional

A rating-scale threshold detects movements, not disability; without severity data, prevalence alone cannot show how much the condition burdens the people counted.
^p28146614-caution-severity
