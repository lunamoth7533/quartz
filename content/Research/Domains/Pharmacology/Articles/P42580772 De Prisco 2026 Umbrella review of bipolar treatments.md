# Evidence based interventions for bipolar disorder across phases and age groups: living umbrella review, evaluation, analysis, and communication hub (U-REACH) project.

**Authors.** De Prisco M, Oliva V, et al. (42 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42580772/)

**Published.** 2026 in BMJ (Clinical research ed.) (first online 2026-08-11).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1136/bmj-2026-100216](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2026-100216) · PMID [42580772](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42580772/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

In adults with bipolar depression, options judged effective included lamotrigine and valproate as well as quetiapine, lurasidone, cariprazine, lumateperone, olanzapine and olanzapine combined with fluoxetine.
^p42580772-depression

In adult mania they included lithium, valproate and carbamazepine, haloperidol, and risperidone, quetiapine, olanzapine, cariprazine and aripiprazole.
^p42580772-mania

For adult maintenance, treatments effective across outcomes included lithium, valproate, quetiapine, olanzapine, aripiprazole and group psychoeducation added to medication; lamotrigine was listed among treatments effective across phases but not in that maintenance list.
^p42580772-maintenance

## Scope as reported

Living umbrella review searched to November 2024, pooling 2510 meta-analyses of 133 outcomes, rated by GRADE as high (236), moderate (827), low (986) or very low (461) certainty; results are published on an open platform for shared decision-making.
^p42580772-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

'Effective across outcomes' compresses many meta-analyses of differing certainty into one list; appearing in it says nothing about the size of the effect, the harms, or which person a treatment suits, and each list is specific to a phase and an age group.
^p42580772-caution-lists

As a living review it may be updated, so its lists should be cited with their version date (August 2026 here).
^p42580772-caution-living
