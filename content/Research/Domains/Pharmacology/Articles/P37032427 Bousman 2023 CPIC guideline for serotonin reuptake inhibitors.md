# Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium (CPIC) Guideline for CYP2D6, CYP2C19, CYP2B6, SLC6A4, and HTR2A Genotypes and Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitor Antidepressants.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Variation in CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and CYP2B6 affects the metabolism of many serotonin reuptake inhibitor antidepressants, which may bear on dosing, efficacy and tolerability.
^p37032427-metabolism

Updating the 2015 guidance, the consortium sets out how genotype results for CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and CYP2B6 can inform the prescribing of these drugs.
^p37032427-recommendations

It finds the evidence on SLC6A4 (serotonin transporter) and HTR2A (serotonin-2A receptor) genotypes insufficient to justify using them when prescribing antidepressants.
^p37032427-pd

## Scope as reported

Guideline update that extends the 2015 CPIC guidance on CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and SSRIs to CYP2B6, SLC6A4 and HTR2A and to SNRIs and serotonin modulators; its dosing tables are not reproduced in this library.
^p37032427-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Guidance on how to act on a genotype result answers a different question from whether routine testing improves outcomes; the second needs outcome trials such as those pooled in the remission meta-analysis this library holds.
