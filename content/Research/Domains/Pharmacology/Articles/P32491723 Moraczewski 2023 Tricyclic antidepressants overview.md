## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Tricyclics act by blocking the reuptake of transmitters such as serotonin and noradrenaline, a route that can alter mood, attention and pain, and individual members hold US approval for different, specific indications.
^p32491723-mechanism

Guidelines place them second-line for major depression after SSRIs: efficacy is broadly similar, but their anticholinergic activity brings heavier adverse effects and overdose becomes dangerous at a lower threshold.
^p32491723-secondline

The chapter adds that they have proved highly effective in severe or treatment-refractory depression.
^p32491723-severe
