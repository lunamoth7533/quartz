# Second-generation versus first-generation antipsychotic drugs for schizophrenia: a meta-analysis.

**Authors.** Leucht S, Corves C, et al. (6 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19058842/)

**Published.** 2009 in Lancet (London, England) (first online 2008-12-06).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/S0140-6736(08)61764-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(08)61764-X) · PMID [19058842](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19058842/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Of nine second-generation drugs, only amisulpride, clozapine, olanzapine and risperidone beat first-generation comparators on overall efficacy, with small to medium effects; the rest did not, negative symptoms included.
^p19058842-efficacy

Against haloperidol, even at low doses, second-generation drugs produced fewer extrapyramidal effects; against low-potency first-generation drugs, only a few showed that advantage.
^p19058842-eps

Most second-generation drugs, aripiprazole and ziprasidone excepted, caused more weight gain than haloperidol but not more than low-potency first-generation drugs, and they differed in sedation.
^p19058842-weight

The authors read the newer drugs as varying on most properties rather than forming one uniform class.
^p19058842-heterogeneity

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of 150 double-blind, mostly short-term trials (21,533 participants); open-label studies were left out because they systematically favoured the newer drugs, and industry sponsorship, comparator dose and preventive antiparkinson medication did not consistently moderate results.
^p19058842-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Much of the extrapyramidal advantage is measured against haloperidol, a high-potency comparator; against low-potency first-generation drugs it largely disappears, so 'fewer movement effects' is a statement about the comparator as much as about the newer drugs.
^p19058842-caution-haloperidol

Published in 2009, the analysis predates the later partial agonists brexpiprazole and cariprazine and the 2024 muscarinic combination.
^p19058842-caution-age
