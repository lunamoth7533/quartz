## Reported findings (checked full text)

Tolerance is a reduced effect after repeated drug exposure, and it develops for some effects of a drug and not others.
^p25566076-tolerance

The review examines receptor-level processes that may contribute to opioid tolerance, especially desensitisation, described as a progressive reduction of signal transduction after receptor activation.
^p25566076-desensitization

Those processes include phosphorylation, uncoupling, internalisation and post-endocytic trafficking, and their speed and extent depend on the agonist, receptor type and signalling pathway studied.
^p25566076-mechanism

## Scope as reported

A narrative review of receptor pharmacology, synthesising cell-system and animal studies rather than testing a clinical intervention. The mechanisms are described for opioid receptors and are not presented as a general account of every drug's tolerance.
^p25566076-limit
