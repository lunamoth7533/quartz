## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Trazodone, a triazolopyridine used for major depression on its own or combined with other drugs or psychotherapy, belongs to the serotonin antagonist and reuptake inhibitor class.
^p29262060-class

It inhibits the serotonin transporter and blocks serotonin type 2, histamine and alpha-1 adrenergic receptors.
