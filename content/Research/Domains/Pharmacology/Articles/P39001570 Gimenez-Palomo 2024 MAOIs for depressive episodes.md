# Efficacy and tolerability of monoamine oxidase inhibitors for the treatment of depressive episodes in mood disorders: A systematic review and network meta-analysis.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Isocarboxazid, phenelzine, tranylcypromine and moclobemide each outperformed placebo, and as a group MAOIs matched other antidepressants in efficacy.
^p39001570-efficacy

Their tolerability and acceptability were comparable with those of other antidepressants.
^p39001570-tolerability

The authors describe MAOIs as third-line options for treatment-resistant depression that are underused in practice.
^p39001570-context

## Scope as reported

Network meta-analysis of double-blind randomised trials; the authors note that studies concentrate on moclobemide and phenelzine and that treatment-resistant, atypical and bipolar depression lack dedicated trials.
^p39001570-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Trials averaged roughly 115 participants each, and acceptability measured under trial conditions, with diet and co-medication controlled, does not describe the interaction burden of everyday use.
^p39001570-caution-trials
