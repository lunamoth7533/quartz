# Long-Term Safety and Efficacy of Xanomeline and Trospium Chloride in Schizophrenia: A 52-Week Open-Label Extension Trial.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Of 366 people who completed the two acute trials, 156 (42.6%) enrolled in the extension and 34 of them (21.8%) finished the 52 weeks.
^p41634905-uptake

About half of the 152 treated participants reported at least one treatment-emergent adverse event; the commonest were mild or moderate gastrointestinal effects that eased with continued use, and no clinically meaningful motor, prolactin, weight or metabolic effects were seen.
^p41634905-safety

Among those who stayed, symptom scores kept improving over the year, with similar gains from acute-trial baseline whether participants had started on the drug or on placebo.
^p41634905-symptoms

## Scope as reported

Single-arm open-label extension with no control group; the primary endpoint was the proportion reporting any treatment-emergent adverse event, and about one in five enrolled participants completed a year.
^p41634905-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

With about four in five enrolled participants leaving before 52 weeks, the long-term picture describes those who stayed; an uncontrolled extension cannot separate drug effects from selective retention and the passage of time.
^p41634905-caution-attrition
