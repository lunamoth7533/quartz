# How do antidepressants work? New perspectives for refining future treatment approaches

**Published.** 2017.

## Reported findings (checked full text)

The review notes that monoamine-based accounts explain acute pharmacological action poorly relative to the weeks-long clinical time course.
^p28153641-monoamine

It argues that antidepressants shift emotional and cognitive processing early in treatment, in ways that may interact with learning and environmental feedback.
^p28153641-processing

Cellular plasticity and stress-related signalling are presented as complementary mechanisms that could explain delayed clinical change.
^p28153641-plasticity

## Scope as reported

A review in Lancet Psychiatry proposing a mechanistic framework. The proposals are hypotheses under test, not established mechanisms.
^p28153641-limit
