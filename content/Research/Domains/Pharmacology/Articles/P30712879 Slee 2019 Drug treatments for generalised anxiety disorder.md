# Pharmacological treatments for generalised anxiety disorder: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.

**Authors.** Slee A, Nazareth I, et al. (6 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30712879/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Duloxetine, pregabalin, venlafaxine and escitalopram lowered Hamilton Anxiety Scale scores more than placebo and were relatively well accepted; mirtazapine, sertraline, fluoxetine, buspirone and agomelatine also looked effective and tolerable, but on small samples.
^p30712879-efficacy

Quetiapine had the largest effect on the scale but was poorly tolerated against placebo, and paroxetine and benzodiazepines were likewise effective but poorly tolerated.
^p30712879-tolerability

## Scope as reported

Network meta-analysis of placebo- and active-controlled trials in adult outpatients published from 1994 to 2017, with efficacy as change on the Hamilton Anxiety Scale and acceptability as discontinuation for any cause; the risk of reporting bias was judged low.
^p30712879-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

The abstract reports benzodiazepines as one group, and trials lasting weeks cannot show dependence or withdrawal, which weigh most on longer use.
^p30712879-caution-group

Pregabalin is an antiseizure drug, so its showing here is a psychiatric use of that class; the result does not transfer to antiseizure drugs with other mechanisms.
^p30712879-caution-pregabalin
