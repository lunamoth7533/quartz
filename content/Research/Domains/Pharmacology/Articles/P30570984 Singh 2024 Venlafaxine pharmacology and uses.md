## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The chapter classes venlafaxine as a serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor with US approval for unipolar major depression, social anxiety disorder and panic disorder, used alone or with other medicines.
^p30570984-class

Common off-label uses it lists include pain conditions such as fibromyalgia, diabetic neuropathy and migraine prevention, as well as hot flashes, premenstrual dysphoric disorder, obsessive-compulsive disorder, PTSD and attention-deficit disorder.
^p30570984-offlabel
