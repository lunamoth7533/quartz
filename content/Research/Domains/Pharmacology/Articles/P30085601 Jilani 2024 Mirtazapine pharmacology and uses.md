## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Mirtazapine, an atypical tetracyclic prescribed mainly for major depression, blocks presynaptic alpha-2 adrenergic receptors in the brain and so increases serotonin and noradrenaline release; the chapter ties this profile to its sedative, antiemetic, anxiolytic and appetite-stimulating effects.
^p30085601-mechanism

Off-label uses include insomnia, panic, PTSD, obsessive-compulsive disorder, generalised and social anxiety and migraine, and it is often considered when depression comes with insomnia or low body weight.
^p30085601-uses

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Sedation and appetite gain are wanted effects for some people and harms for others, so the same receptor property cannot be scored in general as benefit or as adverse effect.
