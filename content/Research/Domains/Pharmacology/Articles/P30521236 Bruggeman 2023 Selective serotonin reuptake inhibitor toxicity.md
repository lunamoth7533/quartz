## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Besides depression, the chapter lists anxiety, obsessive-compulsive, panic and eating disorders as indications, and it describes SSRIs as less toxic than the older monoamine oxidase inhibitors and tricyclics.
^p30521236-uses

Members differ in chemical structure and kinetics but all enhance the action of serotonin; close to therapeutic concentrations they can also block sodium, calcium and potassium channels, and citalopram and escitalopram lengthen the QTc interval.
^p30521236-mechanism

Listed adverse effects include low sodium, loss of orgasm, sedation or activation, stomach irritation, uncommon seizures and impaired platelet function.
^p30521236-adverse

Some authors prefer the term serotonin toxicity, a graded spectrum, to serotonin syndrome.
^p30521236-serotonin

## Scope as reported

A clinical reference chapter on adverse effects and poisoning, written for education; it reports no new data.
