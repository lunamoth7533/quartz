## Reported findings (checked abstract)

CYP2D6 alleles range from no to increased enzyme activity, and their frequencies differ considerably between major world populations.
^p27388693-alleles

Predicted poor metabolisers made up 0.4-5.4% of populations, intermediate metabolisers 0.4-11%, normal metabolisers 67-90% and ultrarapid metabolisers 1-21%.
^p27388693-phenotypes

There is no standard method for turning a person's pair of alleles into a predicted phenotype, and the authors discuss the difficulties of that step.
^p27388693-translation

## Scope as reported

Secondary analysis of published allele frequencies using activity-score assignments; phenotypes are predicted from genotype rather than measured.
^p27388693-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A predicted metaboliser category is a genotype-based estimate; other medicines and physiological factors can change actual enzyme activity, so the label is not a measured phenotype.
^p27388693-caution-predicted
