## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Lithium affects multiple steps in cellular signalling, usually enhancing basal and inhibiting stimulated activity; named nodes include GSK3, CREB and Na+/K+ ATPase.
^p25687772-signalling

The mechanism that prevents recurrences remains only partially understood: distinct hypotheses have not been conclusively supported or rejected, and research is limited by the absence of suitable animal models and reliance on in vitro studies of peripheral tissue.
^p25687772-unknown

Genetic and pharmacogenetic findings are described as promising but limited by small sample sizes, and the authors argue that lithium responders may be a distinct clinical population worth studying.
^p25687772-pharmacogenetics

## Scope as reported

Mechanistic review; no randomized trial data and no dose or monitoring guidance.
