# Comparative effects of 18 antipsychotics on metabolic function in patients with schizophrenia, predictors of metabolic dysregulation, and association with psychopathology: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.

**Authors.** Pillinger T, McCutcheon RA, et al. (11 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31860457/)

**Published.** 2020 in The lancet. Psychiatry (first online 2019-12-17).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/S2215-0366(19)30416-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(19)30416-X) · PMID [31860457](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31860457/) · PMCID [PMC7029416](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7029416/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Metabolic effects differed markedly: olanzapine and clozapine had the least favourable profiles, while aripiprazole, brexpiprazole, cariprazine, lurasidone and ziprasidone were the most benign.
^p31860457-ranking

Weight change relative to placebo ranged from slightly below zero with haloperidol to about 3 kg with clozapine.
^p31860457-weight

Higher baseline weight and male sex predicted larger glucose rises, and non-white ethnicity larger total-cholesterol rises.
^p31860457-predictors

Greater symptom improvement went with greater metabolic worsening, including gains in weight and BMI and rises in total and LDL cholesterol.
^p31860457-symptoms

## Scope as reported

Frequentist network meta-analyses with meta-regressions of short acute-treatment trials (median six weeks); outcomes were weight, BMI, lipids and glucose rather than clinical events.
^p31860457-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Six-week shifts in weight and lipids are early markers; they do not measure diabetes, cardiovascular events or mortality, which accumulate over years of treatment.
^p31860457-caution-horizon

The link between symptom improvement and metabolic worsening is estimated across trials, not within people, so it does not show that metabolic change is part of how the drugs work.
^p31860457-caution-correlation
