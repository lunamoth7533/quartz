## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review identifies striatal dopamine D2 receptor blockade as the shared mechanism behind clinical response, and also behind endocrine and motor side effects, implying a therapeutic window.
^p31299229-d2

Off-target binding at serotonergic, histaminergic, cholinergic and adrenergic receptors contributes to effects such as weight gain, sedation and dysphoria.
^p31299229-offtarget

The authors note that antipsychotics are ineffective for some patients and that response varies, so mechanism does not translate into uniform benefit.
^p31299229-limits
