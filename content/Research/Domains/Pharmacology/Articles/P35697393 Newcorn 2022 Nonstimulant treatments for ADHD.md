## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Four nonstimulant medications hold FDA approval for ADHD: the noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors atomoxetine and extended-release viloxazine, and the alpha-2 adrenergic agonists extended-release clonidine and extended-release guanfacine.
^p35697393-approved

Nonstimulants are described as important when stimulant response or tolerability is poor, when certain co-occurring conditions are present, or when patients prefer them.
^p35697393-role

Response and time course vary across and within these drug classes, which the authors see as room for tailoring treatment in complex presentations.
^p35697393-variability

## Scope as reported

Clinical narrative review of mechanism, pharmacokinetics, efficacy, tolerability and safety for approved, off-label and pipeline nonstimulants; it reports no pooled estimates, and its titration content is not reproduced here.
^p35697393-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Grouping alpha-2 agonists with reuptake inhibitors as 'nonstimulants' is a label by exclusion; the two groups act through different receptors, which matters when reading their harms.
^p35697393-caution-label
