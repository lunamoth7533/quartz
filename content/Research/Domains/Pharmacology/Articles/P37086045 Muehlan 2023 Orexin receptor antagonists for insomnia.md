# The orexin story and orexin receptor antagonists for the treatment of insomnia.

**Authors.** Muehlan C, Roch C, et al. (4 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37086045/)

**Published.** 2023 in Journal of sleep research (first online 2023-04-22).

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Insomnia is framed as a state of hyperarousal, with orexin identified as a key promoter of arousal and vigilance.
^p37086045-arousal

Unlike benzodiazepines and Z-drugs, orexin receptor antagonists do not act on GABA receptors; they temporarily block orexin signalling, which the authors link to fewer next-morning effects and less motor incoordination and cognitive impairment.
^p37086045-mechanism

The antagonists appear free of dependence and tolerance, making longer-term use feasible, and safety studies did not show worsening of existing breathing problems, though more real-world data are needed.
^p37086045-dependence

## Scope as reported

Narrative review of orexin biology, mechanism and the approved antagonists, including their pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic properties; it presents no pooled analysis.
^p37086045-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Fewer next-day effects and absent dependence are comparisons across separate studies of different drug classes; they need head-to-head trials and long-term pharmacovigilance, which the authors acknowledge for real-world safety.
^p37086045-caution-comparison
