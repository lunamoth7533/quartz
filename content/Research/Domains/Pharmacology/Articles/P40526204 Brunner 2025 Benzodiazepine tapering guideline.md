## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The guideline asks clinicians to keep reassessing the risks and benefits of continued benzodiazepine use and to decide about tapering together with patients.
^p40526204-review

It advises against stopping benzodiazepines abruptly in people likely to be physically dependent, because of the risk of withdrawal, and calls for tapers tailored and adjusted to each person's response.
^p40526204-abrupt

It recommends offering psychosocial support alongside tapering.
^p40526204-support

## Scope as reported

Guideline developed with modified GRADE, a systematic review, targeted supplementary searches, consensus and external stakeholder review; it addresses tapering once risks outweigh benefits, and this library reproduces none of its schedules.
^p40526204-limit
