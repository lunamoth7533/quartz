## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Longer cumulative use of ADHD medication was associated with higher risk of cardiovascular disease, each additional year of use being linked to about 4% higher odds across up to 14 years.
^p37991787-cumulative

The increase was steeper over the first three years of cumulative use and then stable; associations were clearest for hypertension and arterial disease and were similar in people under and over 25.
^p37991787-pattern

## Scope as reported

Case-control design with incidence density sampling in the Swedish inpatient and prescribed-drug registers, with exposure measured as dispensed medication; median follow-up was about four years.
^p37991787-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Duration-response associations in observational data are suggestive but not causal; people who stay on medication longer may differ in health, monitoring and diagnosis, and odds ratios near 1.2 describe small average shifts rather than an individual's risk.
^p37991787-caution-observational
