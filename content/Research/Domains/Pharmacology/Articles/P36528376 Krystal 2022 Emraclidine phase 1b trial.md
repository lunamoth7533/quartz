# Emraclidine, a novel positive allosteric modulator of cholinergic M4 receptors, for the treatment of schizophrenia: a two-part, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, phase 1b trial.

**Authors.** Krystal JH, Kane JM, et al. (14 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36528376/)

**Published.** 2022 in Lancet (London, England).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/S0140-6736(22)01990-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(22)01990-0) · PMID [36528376](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36528376/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Emraclidine is a selective, brain-penetrant positive allosteric modulator of the M4 muscarinic receptor, tested here as a schizophrenia treatment.
^p36528376-compound

In the acute part, adverse-event rates were similar in both emraclidine arms and on placebo (52-56% against 52%), headache was the most common event, weight change did not differ, and small early rises in blood pressure and heart rate faded by week six.
^p36528376-safety

## Scope as reported

Phase 1b study whose primary endpoint was safety and tolerability, with 27 participants per arm in the acute part; it was not designed or powered to show efficacy.
^p36528376-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A small safety-focused study cannot show that M4 modulation treats psychosis; that question belongs to larger efficacy trials, whose results this library does not yet hold.
^p36528376-caution-efficacy
