# Comparative cardiovascular safety of medications for attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder in children, adolescents, and adults: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.

**Authors.** Farhat LC, Lannes A, et al. (14 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40203844/)

**Published.** 2025 in The lancet. Psychiatry.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/S2215-0366(25)00062-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(25)00062-8) · PMID [40203844](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40203844/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Amphetamines, atomoxetine, lisdexamfetamine, methylphenidate and viloxazine raised blood pressure and pulse relative to placebo in children and adolescents, adults or both, whereas guanfacine lowered them.
^p40203844-haemodynamic

Stimulants were not associated with larger rises than atomoxetine or viloxazine, and the authors conclude that cardiovascular vigilance applies to non-stimulant medications as well as stimulants.
^p40203844-comparison

The 102 included trials had a median follow-up of about seven weeks; only four informed medium-term effects and none long-term.
^p40203844-horizon

## Scope as reported

Random-effects network meta-analyses of blood pressure, pulse and ECG outcomes at the time points closest to 12, 26 and 52 weeks, with certainty rated by CINeMA from high to very low; adult estimates were mostly of very low certainty.
^p40203844-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Average shifts of a few units in blood pressure or pulse over several weeks describe group-level physiology; they cannot show long-term cardiovascular outcomes, which trials of this length do not observe.
^p40203844-caution-horizon

Reading these results by molecular route rather than by the stimulant label is this library's interpretation; the review compared drugs, not mechanisms.
^p40203844-caution-route
