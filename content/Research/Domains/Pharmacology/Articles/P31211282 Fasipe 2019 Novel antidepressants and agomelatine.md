## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review describes agomelatine as an agonist at melatonergic MT1 and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at serotonin 5-HT2B and 5-HT2C receptors, and argues that it fits none of the existing antidepressant classes.
^p31211282-agomelatine

It classes vortioxetine as combining serotonin reuptake inhibition with serotonin-receptor antagonism, and vilazodone as combining reuptake inhibition with partial agonism at 5-HT1A autoreceptors.
^p31211282-vortioxetine

Its list of emerging drugs includes selective MAO inhibitors such as selegiline, newer SNRIs such as levomilnacipran, noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors such as reboxetine, viloxazine and atomoxetine, and NMDA blockers such as ketamine, which it proposes admitting as an antidepressant class.
^p31211282-classes

## Scope as reported

A single-author narrative review without a systematic search; it discusses classification and mechanism rather than pooled efficacy.
^p31211282-limit
