## Reported findings (checked abstract)

MAOIs raise synaptic monoamine concentrations by inhibiting the monoamine oxidase enzymes.
^p29083800-mechanism

Their prescribing fell once SSRIs and related drugs arrived, but they are still used for treatment-resistant depression and some anxiety disorders.
^p29083800-use

They interact in clinically important ways with many medicines and with foods containing tyramine; poisoning is now rarer but can be severe and life-threatening, can mimic other hyperthermic and hyperadrenergic syndromes, and its worst outcomes track uncontrolled neuromuscular overactivity and high body temperature.
^p29083800-interactions
