# Comparative effects of pharmacological interventions for the acute and long-term management of insomnia disorder in adults: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.

**Authors.** De Crescenzo F, D'Alò GL, et al. (17 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35843245/)

**Published.** 2022 in Lancet (London, England).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/S0140-6736(22)00878-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(22)00878-9) · PMID [35843245](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35843245/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

In acute treatment, zolpidem, zopiclone, eszopiclone, benzodiazepines, the orexin receptor antagonists lemborexant and seltorexant, and the antihistamine doxylamine improved self-rated sleep quality more than placebo (moderate-to-high certainty).
^p35843245-acute

Benzodiazepines, eszopiclone, zolpidem and zopiclone left more people with side-effects than placebo, doxepin, seltorexant or zaleplon, and zolpidem and zopiclone caused more adverse-event dropouts than placebo.
^p35843245-tolerability

Melatonin, the melatonin-receptor agonist ramelteon and unlicensed drugs showed no material overall benefit; benzodiazepines, the orexin receptor antagonists daridorexant and suvorexant, and trazodone helped acutely but were poorly tolerated or lacked long-term data.
^p35843245-limited

Only eszopiclone and lemborexant beat placebo long term, on very low-certainty evidence.
^p35843245-longterm

## Scope as reported

Network meta-analysis of monotherapy trials in adults with insomnia disorder; efficacy was self-rated sleep quality, safety was adverse events and dropouts, certainty was rated with CINeMA, and long-term comparisons rested on few trials.
^p35843245-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Efficacy here is self-rated sleep quality over trial periods; it does not capture next-day function, falls or dependence, which also matter when weighing hypnotic classes.
^p35843245-caution-outcomes

The melatonin result concerns adults with insomnia disorder; it does not address circadian rhythm disorders or children, where melatonin is used to shift timing rather than to sedate.
^p35843245-caution-melatonin
