# Pharmacogenomics

## Definition

Pharmacogenomics uses inherited variation to anticipate how a person will handle a drug: variants in CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and CYP2B6 change how many serotonin reuptake inhibitor antidepressants are metabolised, and consortium guidance sets out how such results can inform prescribing. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-metabolism|Bousman 2023]] [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-recommendations|Bousman 2023]] HLA genes supply a second kind of marker, tied to severe reactions such as carbamazepine-induced Stevens-Johnson syndrome. [[P38527170 Tham 2024 HLA-B and antiseizure drug skin reactions#^p38527170-association|Tham 2024]] How to act on a result and whether testing improves outcomes are separate questions with separate evidence. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-caution-question|Appraisal: Bousman 2023]]

## How it works

**Metabolic genes change exposure.** Pharmacokinetics covers absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion, and the CYP genes act at the metabolism step, so they change what the body does to a drug rather than what the drug does at its target. [[F12 NIGMS What happens to medicine in your body#^f12-adme|What happens to medicine in your body]] CYP2D6 alleles range from no to increased enzyme activity, so predicted phenotypes run from poor to ultrarapid metaboliser. [[P27388693 Gaedigk 2017 CYP2D6 phenotype prediction across populations#^p27388693-alleles|Gaedigk 2017]] [[P27388693 Gaedigk 2017 CYP2D6 phenotype prediction across populations#^p27388693-phenotypes|Gaedigk 2017]] Such variation may bear on dosing, efficacy and tolerability; this library reproduces none of the dosing tables. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-metabolism|Bousman 2023]] [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-limit|Bousman 2023]]

**Frequencies differ between populations.** Predicted poor metabolisers made up 0.4-5.4% of major world populations and ultrarapid metabolisers 1-21%, with allele frequencies differing considerably between groups. [[P27388693 Gaedigk 2017 CYP2D6 phenotype prediction across populations#^p27388693-phenotypes|Gaedigk 2017]] [[P27388693 Gaedigk 2017 CYP2D6 phenotype prediction across populations#^p27388693-alleles|Gaedigk 2017]] There is no standard method for turning a pair of alleles into a predicted phenotype. [[P27388693 Gaedigk 2017 CYP2D6 phenotype prediction across populations#^p27388693-translation|Gaedigk 2017]] A predicted category is a genotype-based estimate, and other medicines and physiology can change actual enzyme activity. [[P27388693 Gaedigk 2017 CYP2D6 phenotype prediction across populations#^p27388693-caution-predicted|Appraisal: Gaedigk 2017]]

**Target genes add little so far.** The consortium judged the evidence on the serotonin transporter gene SLC6A4 and the serotonin-2A receptor gene HTR2A insufficient for use in antidepressant prescribing. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-pd|Bousman 2023]] For lithium, genetic and pharmacogenetic findings remain limited by small samples, though responders may form a distinct group worth studying. [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-pharmacogenetics|Alda 2015]]

**HLA markers flag severe reactions.** HLA-B*15:02 is strongly associated with Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis on carbamazepine and oxcarbazepine and more weakly on lamotrigine and phenytoin, and the review's authors suggest that screening could reduce these reactions. [[P38527170 Tham 2024 HLA-B and antiseizure drug skin reactions#^p38527170-association|Tham 2024]] [[P38527170 Tham 2024 HLA-B and antiseizure drug skin reactions#^p38527170-screening|Tham 2024]] A strong association is not a strong individual prediction: the allele's frequency varies between ancestries, and a negative result does not remove the risk. [[P38527170 Tham 2024 HLA-B and antiseizure drug skin reactions#^p38527170-caution-prediction|Appraisal: Tham 2024]] [[P38527170 Tham 2024 HLA-B and antiseizure drug skin reactions#^p38527170-caution-negative|Appraisal: Tham 2024]]

**What testing does in trials.** In depression, a meta-analysis of 13 trials found that test-guided treatment raised remission (risk ratio 1.41), a modest gain whose uncertainty the authors trace to differences in the genes, alleles and advice covered. [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-remission|Brown 2022]] [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-modest|Brown 2022]] In the PRIME Care trial, test results cut prescriptions carrying a predicted drug-gene interaction, but remission rose only modestly over 24 weeks and not significantly at week 24. [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-prescribing|Oslin 2022]] [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-remission|Oslin 2022]] Across drug classes, a 12-gene panel used before first prescriptions lowered clinically relevant adverse reactions from 28.6% to 21.5% in a European implementation study. [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-design|Swen 2023]] [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-adr|Swen 2023]]

**What testing cannot predict.** Clinicians in guided arms see the report, so part of any benefit may come from closer medication review rather than from the genotype, and a small average gain changes the outcome for few of the people tested. [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-caution-blinding|Appraisal: Brown 2022]] [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-caution-outcome|Appraisal: Oslin 2022]] A metabolic genotype speaks to exposure and an HLA allele to the risk of a specific reaction; neither says whether a person's condition will respond to a drug. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-caution-question|Appraisal: Bousman 2023]] [[P38527170 Tham 2024 HLA-B and antiseizure drug skin reactions#^p38527170-caution-prediction|Appraisal: Tham 2024]]

**Reading rule.** Ask which gene, which drug and which outcome - exposure, a specific reaction or response - then check the ancestry of the population studied and whether clinicians could be blinded. [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-caution-ancestry|Appraisal: Swen 2023]] [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-caution-blinding|Appraisal: Brown 2022]]

## Evidence and status

Gene-drug guidance for CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and CYP2B6 exists at consortium level, while evidence for the serotonin-target genes is insufficient. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-recommendations|Bousman 2023]] [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-pd|Bousman 2023]] Outcome evidence comes from controlled trials in depression with modest effects on remission and from one large open-label study of adverse reactions. [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-limit|Brown 2022]] [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-summary|Oslin 2022]] [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-limit|Swen 2023]] The remission meta-analysis and the consortium guideline share an author, so they are related rather than independent lines of evidence. [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-caution-authorship|Appraisal: Brown 2022]] The trial populations were mostly of European ancestry or US veterans. [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-limit|Swen 2023]] [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-caution-population|Appraisal: Oslin 2022]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether testing improves outcomes enough to matter beyond changing what is prescribed, and for how long. [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-caution-outcome|Appraisal: Oslin 2022]]
- How results transfer to populations whose allele frequencies differ from those studied. [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-caution-ancestry|Appraisal: Swen 2023]]
- Whether any genetic marker will predict response to lithium or other mood stabilisers. [[P25687772 Alda 2015 Lithium pharmacology and pharmacogenetics#^p25687772-pharmacogenetics|Alda 2015]]
- How much of the benefit in guided-care trials comes from the genotype rather than from closer review. [[P36111494 Brown 2022 Pharmacogenomic testing and depression remission#^p36111494-caution-blinding|Appraisal: Brown 2022]]

## Recent research

- **2023 · Cluster-randomised crossover implementation study (Lancet).** Moves the question from single gene-drug pairs to a pre-emptive panel, which lowered clinically relevant adverse drug reactions across many drug classes. [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-adr|Swen 2023]] Open-label assessment may have shaped the result. [[P36739136 Swen 2023 Pharmacogenetic panel to prevent adverse reactions#^p36739136-caution-openlabel|Appraisal: Swen 2023]]
- **2023 · Clinical practice guideline (Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics).** Extends antidepressant guidance to CYP2B6 and rules the serotonin-target genes not yet usable. [[P37032427 Bousman 2023 CPIC guideline for serotonin reuptake inhibitors#^p37032427-pd|Bousman 2023]]
- **2022 · Pragmatic randomised clinical trial (JAMA).** Shows testing changes prescribing more than it changes remission, whose gain was small and not persistent. [[P35819423 Oslin 2022 PRIME Care trial of pharmacogenomic testing#^p35819423-summary|Oslin 2022]]
