# Tricyclic antidepressants

## Definition

Tricyclic antidepressants block the reuptake of serotonin and noradrenaline, a route that can alter mood, attention and pain, and individual members are approved for different specific indications. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-mechanism|Moraczewski 2023]] Their efficacy in major depression is broadly similar to that of SSRIs, but anticholinergic activity makes adverse effects heavier and overdose dangerous at a lower threshold, so guidelines place them second-line. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-secondline|Moraczewski 2023]]

## How it works

**Members.** Amitriptyline and clomipramine are the tricyclics in the 2018 network meta-analysis of adult depression, and desipramine and nortriptyline are the tricyclics tested in children with ADHD. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-drugs|Cipriani 2018]] [[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-limit|Otasowie 2014]]

**Mechanism.** Reuptake block raises serotonin and noradrenaline signalling, which the class shares with the SNRIs; the anticholinergic activity is what sets the tricyclics apart in tolerability. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-mechanism|Moraczewski 2023]] [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-secondline|Moraczewski 2023]] As for other monoamine antidepressants, the acute pharmacology explains the weeks-long clinical course in depression poorly. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-monoamine|Harmer 2017]] A drug that acts at several targets across many tissues is expected to produce several effects at once. [[F13 NIGMS How do medicines work#^f13-agonism|How Do Medicines Work?]]

**What it is used for.** In adult depression, amitriptyline had the largest odds ratio against placebo of the 21 drugs compared, while clomipramine had more dropouts than placebo. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-placebo|Cipriani 2018]] [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-acceptability|Cipriani 2018]] The reference literature describes the class as highly effective in severe or treatment-refractory depression. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-severe|Moraczewski 2023]] For chronic pain, the synthesis checked here found only low-certainty evidence for antidepressants other than duloxetine and milnacipran. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-others|Birkinshaw 2023]] A 2025 review groups tricyclics with SSRIs, SNRIs and newer drugs as similarly effective against placebo but differing in acceptability. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** Anticholinergic effects and a lower threshold for dangerous overdose define the class's safety profile. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-secondline|Moraczewski 2023]] The toxicity literature describes SSRIs as less toxic than the tricyclics they largely replaced. [[P30521236 Bruggeman 2023 Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor toxicity#^p30521236-uses|Bruggeman 2023]] In head-to-head depression trials amitriptyline and clomipramine were among the drugs with the highest dropout. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-acceptability|Cipriani 2018]] In child ADHD trials, tricyclics raised diastolic blood pressure and pulse slightly, desipramine suppressed appetite and nortriptyline increased weight. [[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-harms|Otasowie 2014]] Discontinuation symptoms apply to antidepressants as a group, at rates that differ by drug. [[P38851198 Henssler 2024 Antidepressant discontinuation symptom incidence#^p38851198-drugs|Henssler 2024]] The overdose point is a population-level property, not an individual risk estimate. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-caution-toxicity|Appraisal: Moraczewski 2023]]

**Interactions.** Interactions can be drug-drug, drug-food or drug-condition, and individual decisions belong to product labelling and clinicians. [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-types|Drug interactions: what you should know]] [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-advice|Drug interactions: what you should know]] Because tricyclics raise serotonergic signalling, the serotonin-toxicity spectrum described for SSRIs is relevant when serotonergic drugs are combined. [[P30521236 Bruggeman 2023 Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor toxicity#^p30521236-serotonin|Bruggeman 2023]] [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-mechanism|Moraczewski 2023]]

**ADHD: short-term benefit, cardiovascular concern.** In six small trials in young people, tricyclics beat placebo on predefined improvement in core ADHD symptoms, with desipramine helping on parent, teacher and clinician ratings. [[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-efficacy|Otasowie 2014]] The authors rated the evidence low to very low quality and singled out desipramine's cardiovascular effects as an important concern. [[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-conclusion|Otasowie 2014]] The evidence is dated, centred on one drug and silent about adults. [[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-caution-age|Appraisal: Otasowie 2014]] The current ADHD drug evidence is in [[ADHD medication evidence]].

**Autism: clomipramine stands out, provisionally.** Pooled placebo-controlled trials in autistic participants found that clomipramine outperformed SSRIs on restricted and repetitive behaviours and global improvement, and that adults responded better than children. [[P35948343 Liang 2022 Antidepressants for autism symptoms#^p35948343-moderators|Liang 2022]] Those contrasts come from few trials compared across studies. [[P35948343 Liang 2022 Antidepressants for autism symptoms#^p35948343-caution-subgroups|Appraisal: Liang 2022]]

**PTSD and bipolar depression.** In [[Post-traumatic stress disorder|PTSD]], one small trial suggested benefit from amitriptyline on low-certainty evidence. [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-others|Williams 2026]] [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-caution-cptsd|Appraisal: Williams 2026]] In bipolar disorder, the ISBD task force named tricyclic and tetracyclic drugs among those that may switch mood into [[Hypomania|hypomania]], [[Mania|mania]] or mixed states more often than SSRIs or bupropion. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]] See [[Bipolar depression and mixed features]].

**Reading rule.** "Second-line" is a safety ranking, not an efficacy ranking: tricyclics work about as well as newer drugs in depression, and it is the adverse-effect burden and overdose margin that place them lower. [[P32491723 Moraczewski 2023 Tricyclic antidepressants overview#^p32491723-secondline|Moraczewski 2023]]

## Evidence and status

Depression evidence comes from the large 2018 trial network, with certainty from moderate to very low. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-limit|Cipriani 2018]] The ADHD evidence is six small trials searched to 2013, the autism evidence rests on cross-study subgroup contrasts, and the PTSD signal is a single trial. [[P25238582 Otasowie 2014 Tricyclic antidepressants for ADHD#^p25238582-limit|Otasowie 2014]] [[P35948343 Liang 2022 Antidepressants for autism symptoms#^p35948343-limit|Liang 2022]] [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-others|Williams 2026]] The bipolar switch comparison is expert consensus. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Pacchiarotti 2013]]

## Uncertainties

- The cardiac mechanism behind tricyclic overdose toxicity is not described by a source checked in this library.
- Whether clomipramine's advantage over SSRIs in autism survives within-trial comparison is untested.
- The pain evidence for tricyclics specifically is not broken out in the pain synthesis checked here.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Cochrane systematic review (Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews).** Records a low-certainty amitriptyline signal in PTSD from a single trial, next to moderate-certainty evidence for SSRIs. [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-others|Williams 2026]] [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-ssri|Williams 2026]]
- **2025 · Narrative review (Medicina).** Places tricyclics among the classes with similar efficacy against placebo and different acceptability. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]]
- **2022 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Journal of Psychiatry and Neuroscience).** Finds clomipramine ahead of SSRIs in autism trials, a cross-study contrast that needs direct testing. [[P35948343 Liang 2022 Antidepressants for autism symptoms#^p35948343-moderators|Liang 2022]]
