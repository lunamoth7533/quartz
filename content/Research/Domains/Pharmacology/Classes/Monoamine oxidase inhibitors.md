# Monoamine oxidase inhibitors

## Definition

Monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) raise synaptic monoamine concentrations by inhibiting the monoamine oxidase enzymes rather than the reuptake transporters. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-mechanism|Easton-Carr 2026]] They are used for depression and, for some drugs, Parkinson disease, and their toxicity is greatest in overdose. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-uses|Patel 2025]] Their prescribing fell once SSRIs arrived, but they remain in use for treatment-resistant depression and some anxiety disorders. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-use|Easton-Carr 2026]]

## How it works

**Members.** Isocarboxazid, phenelzine, tranylcypromine and moclobemide are the MAOIs with trial evidence against placebo in depression. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-efficacy|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] Selegiline is the example of a selective inhibitor, listed among emerging antidepressants and used as an MAO-B inhibitor. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-classes|Fasipe 2019]] [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-selectivity|Patel 2025]]

**Mechanism.** Inhibiting the enzyme that breaks monoamines down increases the amount available for signalling, a different step from the transporter block of SSRIs, SNRIs and tricyclics. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-mechanism|Easton-Carr 2026]] Two drug properties change what that means in practice: whether a drug is selective for one enzyme form and whether it binds irreversibly. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-selectivity|Patel 2025]] Those properties belong to individual drugs, so risk statements attach to named drugs rather than to the class. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-caution-selectivity|Appraisal: Patel 2025]] As with other monoamine antidepressants, the acute pharmacology explains the weeks-long course of improvement in depression poorly. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-monoamine|Harmer 2017]]

**What it is used for.** In a 2024 network meta-analysis of 83 double-blind trials, the four tested MAOIs each outperformed placebo, and as a group they matched other antidepressants in efficacy. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-efficacy|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] Their tolerability and acceptability were comparable with other antidepressants under trial conditions. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-tolerability|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] The authors describe them as third-line options for treatment-resistant depression that are underused in practice. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-context|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] Trial acceptability, measured with diet and co-medication controlled, does not describe the interaction burden of everyday use. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-caution-trials|Appraisal: Giménez-Palomo 2024]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** The toxicity literature describes SSRIs as less toxic than the MAOIs that preceded them. [[P30521236 Bruggeman 2023 Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor toxicity#^p30521236-uses|Bruggeman 2023]] MAOI toxicity can show as agitation, flushing, a fast heart, low or high blood pressure, palpitations, muscle twitching, exaggerated reflexes, seizures and high fever, and needs prompt treatment. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-toxicity|Patel 2025]] Poisoning is now rarer but can be life-threatening, can resemble other hyperthermic and hyperadrenergic syndromes, and its worst outcomes track uncontrolled neuromuscular overactivity and high body temperature. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-interactions|Easton-Carr 2026]]

**Interactions: the class-defining issue.** MAOIs interact in clinically important ways with many medicines and with foods containing tyramine. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-interactions|Easton-Carr 2026]] The non-selective, irreversible drugs phenelzine and tranylcypromine can provoke hypertensive crisis with tyramine-rich foods, whereas selective MAO-B inhibitors such as selegiline carry a lower hypertensive risk. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-selectivity|Patel 2025]] Interactions can be drug-drug, drug-food or drug-condition, and individual decisions belong to product labelling and clinicians. [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-types|Drug interactions: what you should know]] [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-advice|Drug interactions: what you should know]] Interaction warnings are reasons for specialist oversight, and this library gives no instructions. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-caution-interactions|Appraisal: Easton-Carr 2026]]

**Bipolar depression: an evidence gap.** The 2024 network meta-analysis notes that bipolar depression, like treatment-resistant and atypical depression, lacks dedicated MAOI trials, so the class result cannot be carried across to bipolar depression. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-limit|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] The phase logic that such a claim would need is set out in [[Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence]].

**Parkinson disease: a second use.** Some MAOIs are also used in Parkinson disease, and the selective MAO-B inhibitor selegiline is the class's example of lower hypertensive risk. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-uses|Patel 2025]] [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-selectivity|Patel 2025]] See [[Movement disorders]].

**Reading rule.** Name the drug and its selectivity. A statement such as "MAOIs are dangerous with tyramine-rich food" is true of named non-selective, irreversible drugs and weaker for selective ones, so a class-level warning or efficacy claim must say which drugs it rests on. [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-selectivity|Patel 2025]] [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-limit|Giménez-Palomo 2024]]

## Evidence and status

Efficacy evidence in depression is a network of 83 double-blind trials that concentrates on moclobemide and phenelzine. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-limit|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] The toxicity and interaction statements come from clinical reference chapters, which describe hazards rather than measure their frequency. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-limit|Easton-Carr 2026]] [[P30969670 Patel 2025 Monoamine oxidase inhibitors overview#^p30969670-limit|Patel 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- No MAOI evidence specific to ADHD, autism or PTSD is sourced in this library.
- How often serious food or drug interactions occur in current practice is not measured by the sources checked here.
- Whether MAOIs help in bipolar or treatment-resistant depression specifically awaits dedicated trials. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-limit|Giménez-Palomo 2024]]

## Recent research

- **2024 · Systematic review and network meta-analysis (Acta Psychiatrica Scandinavica).** Finds MAOIs as effective as other antidepressants with comparable acceptability in trials, which questions their third-line, underused position without settling interaction risk outside trials. [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-efficacy|Giménez-Palomo 2024]] [[P39001570 Gimenez-Palomo 2024 MAOIs for depressive episodes#^p39001570-context|Giménez-Palomo 2024]]
- **2026 · Reference chapter update (StatPearls).** Notes that falling prescribing has made MAOI poisoning less common while it remains potentially life-threatening. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-interactions|Easton-Carr 2026]]
