# Antidepressant mechanisms

## Definition

Antidepressants act mainly by increasing monoamine availability through reuptake inhibition or enzymatic breakdown inhibition, though the pharmacological action is immediate while clinical change usually takes weeks.

## How it works

**Acute action, delayed effect.** Transporter or enzyme blockade raises synaptic monoamine availability within hours, while mood change typically appears over weeks. Monoamine-based accounts explain that acute pharmacology poorly relative to the clinical time course, and the review that sets out the mismatch treats it as the central theoretical problem - one of the main arguments that monoamine elevation alone is not an adequate mechanistic account. [[F12 NIGMS What happens to medicine in your body#^f12-adme|What happens to medicine in your body]] [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-monoamine|How do antidepressants work?]]

**Processing account.** Antidepressants are proposed to shift emotional and cognitive processing early in treatment - attention to and interpretation of emotional material change before mood does - and these early biases interact with learning, environmental feedback and ongoing experience, compounding into clinical change over weeks. On this account the drug changes the system that generates mood rather than adding mood directly, and "delay" becomes a compounding process rather than a mysterious lag. The account is attractive because it is testable at the timescale where the pharmacology operates, and it remains a proposal with partial evidence. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-processing|How do antidepressants work?]]

**Plasticity account.** Cellular plasticity and stress-related signalling are presented as complementary mechanisms for the delayed effect: the treated system must regain adaptive capacity, not just transmitter exposure. This connects the pharmacology to the HPA-axis, neurotrophic and stress material elsewhere in the library and predicts that environmental context should moderate treatment response - a research direction rather than an established moderator table. None of these accounts is established as the explanation, and they are not mutually exclusive. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-plasticity|How do antidepressants work?]] [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-axis|What is stress?]]

**Adaptation.** Receptor and transporter changes develop over days to weeks, which is also why discontinuation effects occur. [[P25566076 Allouche 2014 Opioid receptor desensitization and tolerance#^p25566076-tolerance|Opioid receptor desensitization and tolerance]]

**What the mechanism does not settle.** Mechanism plausibility does not tell a reader who will respond, how quickly or at what cost; those are empirical questions answered by trials with named outcomes and raters, and the tolerability picture differs between agents and age groups. The pharmacological time course in a review is a hypothesis-generating structure, not a treatment plan. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]]

**Reading rule.** Separate acute target engagement, early processing change, delayed clinical change and mechanism proposals. Each is a different claim with a different evidence base, and collapsing them is how a transporter story becomes a story about depression. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-monoamine|How do antidepressants work?]]

## Evidence and status

Mechanistic proposals are hypotheses under test; efficacy evidence comes from randomised trials and meta-analyses, with effect sizes depending on severity, outcome and comparator. [[P27620683 Ioannidis 2016 Mass production of systematic reviews#^p27620683-caution-use|AI appraisal: Mass production of systematic reviews]] The trials are numerous, small and sometimes duplicated in publications, so meta-analytic estimates carry publication and study-quality issues alongside clinical heterogeneity. That is not a reason to dismiss the evidence; it is why certainty language matters as much as effect sizes.
