# Psychedelics and MDMA-assisted therapy

## Definition

This note covers two investigational treatments: psilocybin, a psychedelic tested against placebo or non-psychoactive drugs in depression, and MDMA, an entactogen given as MDMA-assisted therapy in PTSD. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-limit|Metaxa 2024]] [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-history|Singh 2025]] Psychedelics are reported to act quickly, strongly and durably, but that picture awaits validation in phase III trials. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-psychedelics|Gao 2025]] Neither is approved in the United States for these uses: the only approved PTSD drugs there are the SSRIs paroxetine and sertraline. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-approved|Singh 2025]]

## How it works

**Members.** Psilocybin is the psychedelic in the pooled randomised trials of depression summarised here, and MDMA is the drug in MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-effect|Metaxa 2024]] [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-ptsd|Fares-Otero 2026]]

**Mechanism.** Among the starting points discussed for rapid-acting antidepressant effects, psychedelics are the ones that begin by activating serotonin receptors. [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-limit|Brown 2024]] One synthesis argues that these different starting points converge on stronger excitatory synapses and changed later plasticity, an account that rests mostly on preclinical work. [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-convergence|Brown 2024]] [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-caution-scope|Appraisal: Brown 2024]] In MDMA-assisted therapy the drug is given within structured therapy, so the effect measured belongs to a drug-plus-therapy package rather than to the molecule alone. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-caution-population|Appraisal: Fares-Otero 2026]]

**Psilocybin in depression.** Given as a standalone treatment, psilocybin improved depression scores more than comparators, with a pooled Hedges' g of 0.66 and moderate-certainty evidence. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-effect|Metaxa 2024]] Effects were larger in secondary depression, on self-report scales, in older participants and in people who had used psychedelics before. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-moderators|Metaxa 2024]] Those are the patterns that unblinding and expectancy would produce, so the pooled effect may overstate the drug's specific contribution. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-caution-unblinding|Appraisal: Metaxa 2024]] The authors themselves call for work on expectancy, moderators and delivery before the effect can be interpreted. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-expectancy|Metaxa 2024]]

**MDMA-assisted therapy in PTSD.** Pooled trials associate MDMA-assisted therapy with lower [[Post-traumatic stress disorder|PTSD]] severity than control (standardised mean difference about -1.2), a smaller reduction in dissociative symptoms and a possible gain in functioning. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-ptsd|Fares-Otero 2026]] No clear benefit appeared for depressive symptoms. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-depression|Fares-Otero 2026]] Certainty was very low, because most trials were at high risk of bias in outcome measurement, few used active controls and blinding was probably compromised. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-certainty|Fares-Otero 2026]] The trials enrolled PTSD rather than ICD-11 complex PTSD, so transfer to [[CPTSD and disturbances in self-organization|complex PTSD]] is untested. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-caution-population|Appraisal: Fares-Otero 2026]]

**Regulatory history.** Encouraging early trials led the FDA to grant MDMA-assisted therapy breakthrough status in 2017, and phase 3 trials reported that close to 70% of those treated no longer met the PTSD diagnosis. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-history|Singh 2025]] In 2024 the FDA turned down approval, citing trial design, including failed blinding and missing QT-prolongation and abuse-liability assessments, together with allegations of possible misconduct. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-rejection|Singh 2025]] Blinding, long-term safety and variation in the accompanying psychotherapy are named as the problems for future trials, which recent FDA guidance on psychedelic research now frames. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-path|Singh 2025]] A rejection weighs trial conduct and safety data as well as effect sizes, so it is not a finding that the treatment fails. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-caution-regulatory|Appraisal: Singh 2025]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** Safety is the thinnest part of the evidence: the 2024 decision pointed to missing assessments of heart-rhythm effects and abuse liability, and long-term safety is an open question. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-rejection|Singh 2025]] [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-path|Singh 2025]] Early results for psychedelics come from small trials that are hard to blind, so "large effect" is a provisional description. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-caution-narrative|Appraisal: Gao 2025]]

**How PTSD evidence compares.** SSRIs remain the first-line PTSD drug class in almost all guidelines, so MDMA-assisted therapy is measured against that benchmark as well as against trauma-focused psychotherapy. [[P34575284 Martin 2021 Treatment guidelines for PTSD#^p34575284-firstline|Martin 2021]]

**Reading rule.** Separate the molecule from the session and the rating. A result from these trials describes a drug given within therapy, often rated by scales that participants or raters could bias once they guessed allocation, so certainty ratings matter more than effect sizes. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-certainty|Fares-Otero 2026]] [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-caution-unblinding|Appraisal: Metaxa 2024]]

## Evidence and status

Psilocybin's short-term effect in depression carries moderate certainty from seven pooled trials, while MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD carries very low certainty from a small set of trials. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-effect|Metaxa 2024]] [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-limit|Fares-Otero 2026]] In the United States MDMA-assisted therapy was not approved in 2024, and psilocybin remains investigational. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-rejection|Singh 2025]] [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-psychedelics|Gao 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- How much of either effect survives better blinding and active controls. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-limit|Fares-Otero 2026]]
- Regulatory decisions outside the United States are not yet covered by a source checked in this library.
- No psychedelic evidence in bipolar disorder, ADHD or autism is sourced here.
- Long-term safety, including abuse liability, is unmeasured in the sources checked. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-path|Singh 2025]]

## Recent research

- **2026 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (European Neuropsychopharmacology).** Large pooled PTSD effects for MDMA-assisted therapy, rated very low certainty because of bias in outcome measurement and compromised blinding. [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-ptsd|Fares-Otero 2026]] [[P41825162 Fares-Otero 2026 MDMA-assisted therapy for PTSD#^p41825162-certainty|Fares-Otero 2026]]
- **2025 · Narrative review (CNS Drugs).** Sets out why the FDA turned down MDMA-assisted therapy in 2024 and what future trials must fix. [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-rejection|Singh 2025]] [[P39955464 Singh 2025 MDMA-assisted therapy regulatory challenges#^p39955464-path|Singh 2025]]
- **2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (BMJ).** A moderate-certainty psilocybin effect in depression whose moderators point to expectancy. [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-effect|Metaxa 2024]] [[P38692686 Metaxa 2024 Psilocybin for depression#^p38692686-moderators|Metaxa 2024]]
