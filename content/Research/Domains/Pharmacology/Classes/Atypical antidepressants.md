# Atypical antidepressants

## Definition

"Atypical antidepressants" is a residual grouping for drugs whose main actions fall outside the SSRI, SNRI, tricyclic and MAOI classes; one review argues that agomelatine fits none of the existing classes at all. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-agomelatine|Fasipe 2019]] Class acronyms for these drugs vary between sources, and receptor profiles rather than class names describe what each drug does. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-caution-labels|Appraisal: Fasipe 2019]] What the members share is a use: in depression they show efficacy against placebo similar to the older classes, with differing acceptability. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]]

## How it works

**Members.** Agomelatine, mirtazapine, trazodone and vortioxetine are among the 21 drugs of the 2018 network meta-analysis of adult depression, together with the noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor reboxetine. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-drugs|Cipriani 2018]] [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-classes|Fasipe 2019]] Bupropion and vilazodone belong here too. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]] [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-vortioxetine|Fasipe 2019]]

**Mechanism, drug by drug.** Bupropion is grouped as a dopamine reuptake inhibitor, which places it with the catecholamine drugs. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]] [[F62 Neuroscience Online biogenic amines#^f62-classes|Biogenic Amines]] Mirtazapine, an atypical tetracyclic, blocks presynaptic alpha-2 adrenergic receptors and so increases serotonin and noradrenaline release; the same profile is linked to its sedative, antiemetic, anxiolytic and appetite-stimulating effects. [[P30085601 Jilani 2024 Mirtazapine pharmacology and uses#^p30085601-mechanism|Jilani 2024]] Trazodone, a serotonin antagonist and reuptake inhibitor, inhibits the serotonin transporter and blocks serotonin type 2, histamine and alpha-1 adrenergic receptors. [[P29262060 Shin 2024 Trazodone pharmacology and uses#^p29262060-class|Shin 2024]] [[P29262060 Shin 2024 Trazodone pharmacology and uses#^p29262060-mechanism|Shin 2024]] Vortioxetine combines serotonin reuptake inhibition with serotonin-receptor antagonism, and vilazodone combines reuptake inhibition with partial agonism at 5-HT1A autoreceptors. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-vortioxetine|Fasipe 2019]] Agomelatine is an agonist at melatonergic MT1 and MT2 receptors and an antagonist at 5-HT2B and 5-HT2C receptors. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-agomelatine|Fasipe 2019]] Receptor lists of this kind rarely give relative strengths, so they do not show which action dominates at a given exposure. [[P29262060 Shin 2024 Trazodone pharmacology and uses#^p29262060-caution-receptors|Appraisal: Shin 2024]]

**What it is used for.** In adult depression every drug in the 2018 network beat placebo, and agomelatine was one of only two with fewer dropouts than placebo. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-placebo|Cipriani 2018]] [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-acceptability|Cipriani 2018]] Trazodone is used for depression on its own or combined with other drugs or psychotherapy. [[P29262060 Shin 2024 Trazodone pharmacology and uses#^p29262060-class|Shin 2024]] Mirtazapine is used off-label for insomnia, several anxiety disorders, PTSD and migraine, and is often considered when depression comes with insomnia or low body weight. [[P30085601 Jilani 2024 Mirtazapine pharmacology and uses#^p30085601-uses|Jilani 2024]] Bupropion is also one half of dextromethorphan-bupropion, which acted faster than monoamine antidepressants but with a similar effect size. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-rapid|Gao 2025]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** There is no single profile, because a drug acting at several targets across many tissues produces several effects. [[F13 NIGMS How do medicines work#^f13-agonism|How Do Medicines Work?]] Mirtazapine's sedation and appetite gain are wanted effects for some people and harms for others. [[P30085601 Jilani 2024 Mirtazapine pharmacology and uses#^p30085601-caution-effects|Appraisal: Jilani 2024]] In head-to-head depression trials trazodone was among the drugs with the highest dropout, while agomelatine and vortioxetine were among the better tolerated. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-acceptability|Cipriani 2018]] Discontinuation symptoms apply to antidepressants as a group, at rates that differ by drug. [[P38851198 Henssler 2024 Antidepressant discontinuation symptom incidence#^p38851198-drugs|Henssler 2024]]

**Interactions.** Members that inhibit the serotonin transporter, such as trazodone, vortioxetine and vilazodone, fall within the serotonin-toxicity spectrum described for serotonergic drugs. [[P30521236 Bruggeman 2023 Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor toxicity#^p30521236-serotonin|Bruggeman 2023]] [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-vortioxetine|Fasipe 2019]] Interactions can be drug-drug, drug-food or drug-condition, and individual decisions belong to product labelling and clinicians. [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-types|Drug interactions: what you should know]] [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-advice|Drug interactions: what you should know]]

**ADHD: bupropion is the exception.** In adults with ADHD, bupropion beat placebo on clinician ratings in the 2018 network meta-analysis of ADHD medications, the only drug in this group with ADHD trial evidence sourced here. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|Cortese 2018]] Group-level rankings are not prescribing guidance. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-caution-choice|Appraisal: Cortese 2018]] The catecholamine drugs it sits beside are covered in [[Stimulant and non-stimulant mechanisms]].

**Bipolar depression: members differ.** The ISBD task force suggested that bupropion, like SSRIs, may switch mood into [[Hypomania|hypomania]] or [[Mania|mania]] less often than tricyclic and tetracyclic drugs and SNRIs. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]] Mirtazapine is described as a tetracyclic, but whether the task force's tetracyclic grouping covers it is not stated in the abstract checked. [[P30085601 Jilani 2024 Mirtazapine pharmacology and uses#^p30085601-mechanism|Jilani 2024]] For bipolar I the task force confined antidepressants to use alongside mood stabilisers. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] See [[Bipolar depression and mixed features]].

**PTSD.** In [[Post-traumatic stress disorder|PTSD]], a single small trial suggested benefit from mirtazapine on low-certainty evidence, next to moderate-certainty evidence for SSRIs. [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-others|Williams 2026]] [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-ssri|Williams 2026]] That signal rests on one trial. [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-caution-cptsd|Appraisal: Williams 2026]]

**Reading rule.** "Atypical" names what a drug is not. Name the drug and its receptor profile before reading any efficacy or safety claim, because bupropion, mirtazapine, trazodone, vortioxetine and agomelatine share neither mechanism nor adverse effects. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-caution-labels|Appraisal: Fasipe 2019]]

## Evidence and status

Depression efficacy for most members rests on the same trial network as the other classes, with certainty from moderate to very low. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-limit|Cipriani 2018]] Mechanism descriptions come from reference chapters and a single-author narrative review rather than from comparative binding studies. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-limit|Fasipe 2019]] [[P30085601 Jilani 2024 Mirtazapine pharmacology and uses#^p30085601-limit|Jilani 2024]] The condition-specific statements rest on one ADHD network, a consensus statement and a single PTSD trial. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-limit|Cortese 2018]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-limit|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-others|Williams 2026]]

## Uncertainties

- Drug-specific serious risks described in product labels are not yet covered by a source checked in this library.
- No evidence on these drugs specific to autistic people is sourced here.
- Whether mirtazapine carries the higher switch concern attached to tetracyclics in bipolar disorder is unresolved.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Cochrane systematic review (Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews).** Records a low-certainty mirtazapine signal in PTSD from one trial. [[P42206608 Williams 2026 Pharmacotherapy for PTSD#^p42206608-others|Williams 2026]]
- **2025 · Narrative review (Medicina).** Groups bupropion as a dopamine reuptake inhibitor with efficacy similar to other classes, and reports that dextromethorphan-bupropion acts faster without a larger effect. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]] [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-rapid|Gao 2025]]
