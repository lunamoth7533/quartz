# Second-generation antipsychotics

## Definition

Second-generation, or atypical, antipsychotics are the drugs that followed the older dopamine blockers; each either blocks dopamine receptors or acts as a dopamine partial agonist, and together they do not form a uniform class. [[P39350614 Leucht 2024 Antipsychotic drugs concise review#^p39350614-mechanism|Leucht 2024]] [[P19058842 Leucht 2009 Second-generation versus first-generation antipsychotics#^p19058842-heterogeneity|Leucht 2009]] Aripiprazole, brexpiprazole and cariprazine stand apart within the group as partial agonists at D2 and D3 receptors. [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-pharmacology|Keks 2020]]

## How it works

**Members.** Named second-generation drugs include amisulpride, clozapine, olanzapine and risperidone, the four that outperformed older comparators on overall efficacy in a 2009 meta-analysis, together with aripiprazole, lurasidone, quetiapine and ziprasidone and the partial agonists brexpiprazole and cariprazine. [[P19058842 Leucht 2009 Second-generation versus first-generation antipsychotics#^p19058842-efficacy|Leucht 2009]] [[P33177610 Kishi 2021 Maintenance treatment of bipolar disorder#^p33177610-limit|Kishi 2021]] [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-pharmacology|Keks 2020]]

**Mechanism: two ways of acting at one receptor.** Most members block D2 receptors, as the older drugs do; the partial agonists occupy the same receptor but produce a submaximal signal, so the difference lies in what binding does rather than where it happens. [[P39350614 Leucht 2024 Antipsychotic drugs concise review#^p39350614-mechanism|Leucht 2024]] [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-pharmacology|Keks 2020]] Even that subgroup is not uniform: brexpiprazole has weaker intrinsic D2 activity, and cariprazine the strongest D3 affinity and longest half-life. [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-pharmacology|Keks 2020]] Binding at serotonergic, histaminergic, cholinergic and adrenergic receptors shapes much of the difference in weight gain and sedation between drugs. [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-offtarget|Kaar 2020]]

**What it is used for.** In acute schizophrenia every drug's estimate favoured it over placebo, with clozapine at the top of the range of effects. [[P31303314 Huhn 2019 Comparative efficacy of 32 antipsychotics#^p31303314-efficacy|Huhn 2019]] In bipolar disorder the uses are phase-specific: risperidone, quetiapine, olanzapine, cariprazine and aripiprazole are among the options judged effective for adult mania; quetiapine, lurasidone, cariprazine, lumateperone and olanzapine, alone or with fluoxetine, for adult bipolar depression; and quetiapine, olanzapine and aripiprazole for adult maintenance. [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-mania|De Prisco 2026]] [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-depression|De Prisco 2026]] [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-maintenance|De Prisco 2026]] Added to lithium or valproate, quetiapine and lurasidone lowered depressive recurrence, and aripiprazole and quetiapine lowered manic recurrence. [[P33177610 Kishi 2021 Maintenance treatment of bipolar disorder#^p33177610-adjunct|Kishi 2021]] Guidelines rank such options separately for each phase, weighing efficacy, safety and tolerability together. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-phases|Keramatian 2023]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** Metabolic harm varies most: in an 18-drug network, olanzapine and clozapine had the least favourable profiles and aripiprazole, brexpiprazole, cariprazine, lurasidone and ziprasidone the most benign, with weight change reaching about 3 kg with clozapine in trials of around six weeks. [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-ranking|Pillinger 2020]] [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-weight|Pillinger 2020]] [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-limit|Pillinger 2020]] Higher baseline weight and male sex predicted larger rises in glucose. [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-predictors|Pillinger 2020]] Movement effects are fewer than with haloperidol but not uniformly fewer than with low-potency older drugs, and tardive dyskinesia was less prevalent during second-generation treatment (20.7% against 30.0%). [[P19058842 Leucht 2009 Second-generation versus first-generation antipsychotics#^p19058842-eps|Leucht 2009]] [[P28146614 Carbon 2017 Tardive dyskinesia prevalence meta-analysis#^p28146614-class|Carbon 2017]] Among 32 drugs, paliperidone produced the largest prolactin rise and sertindole the longest QTc prolongation. [[P31303314 Huhn 2019 Comparative efficacy of 32 antipsychotics#^p31303314-sideeffects|Huhn 2019]] The partial agonists trade these harms for akathisia, mostly early and mild to moderate, with little sedation or prolactin rise, and aripiprazole has been linked to gambling and other impulse-control problems. [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-akathisia|Keks 2020]] [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-profile|Keks 2020]] That link is a safety signal whose frequency the source does not give. [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-caution-impulse|Appraisal: Keks 2020]]

**Reading rule.** Treat "atypical" as a list, not a mechanism: ask whether the drug blocks D2 or partially stimulates it, which harms it carries (metabolic, movement, prolactin, cardiac, akathisia), and which phase and population an efficacy claim came from. [[P19058842 Leucht 2009 Second-generation versus first-generation antipsychotics#^p19058842-heterogeneity|Leucht 2009]] [[P31303314 Huhn 2019 Comparative efficacy of 32 antipsychotics#^p31303314-caution-generations|Appraisal: Huhn 2019]]

## Evidence and status

Efficacy differences between these drugs are mostly gradual while harm differences are sharper, and confidence in network estimates is often low or very low. [[P31303314 Huhn 2019 Comparative efficacy of 32 antipsychotics#^p31303314-sideeffects|Huhn 2019]] [[P31303314 Huhn 2019 Comparative efficacy of 32 antipsychotics#^p31303314-interpretation|Huhn 2019]] The metabolic ranking comes from short trials measuring weight, lipids and glucose rather than clinical events. [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-limit|Pillinger 2020]] Comparisons among the partial agonists still need head-to-head trials, and their diabetes and tardive dyskinesia risks are unknown. [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-limit|Keks 2020]] In bipolar disorder, the phase lists rest on thousands of meta-analyses graded mostly moderate or low in certainty. [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-limit|De Prisco 2026]]

## Uncertainties

- Six-week metabolic shifts do not measure diabetes, cardiovascular events or mortality, which accumulate over years. [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-caution-horizon|Appraisal: Pillinger 2020]]
- Whether the across-trial link between symptom improvement and metabolic worsening reflects a shared mechanism; it was not measured within people. [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-symptoms|Pillinger 2020]] [[P31860457 Pillinger 2020 Metabolic effects of 18 antipsychotics#^p31860457-caution-correlation|Appraisal: Pillinger 2020]]
- How often impulse-control problems occur with aripiprazole. [[P32246399 Keks 2020 Tolerability of dopamine partial agonists#^p32246399-caution-impulse|Appraisal: Keks 2020]]
- Uses outside psychosis and bipolar disorder, such as irritability in autism or add-on treatment in depression, are not yet sourced in this library.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Living umbrella review (BMJ).** Sorts second-generation drugs by phase - several for mania, a partly different set for bipolar depression and a smaller set for maintenance - which is why a claim about one of these drugs must carry its phase. [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-depression|De Prisco 2026]] [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-maintenance|De Prisco 2026]] Each list compresses meta-analyses of differing certainty. [[P42580772 De Prisco 2026 Umbrella review of bipolar treatments#^p42580772-caution-lists|Appraisal: De Prisco 2026]]
- **2024 · Narrative review (The American Journal of Psychiatry).** Holds that every antipsychotic then available blocked dopamine receptors or was a partial agonist, that side-effect differences are clearer than efficacy differences, and that the generation labels should be dropped. [[P39350614 Leucht 2024 Antipsychotic drugs concise review#^p39350614-mechanism|Leucht 2024]] [[P39350614 Leucht 2024 Antipsychotic drugs concise review#^p39350614-effects|Leucht 2024]] [[P39350614 Leucht 2024 Antipsychotic drugs concise review#^p39350614-classification|Leucht 2024]]
