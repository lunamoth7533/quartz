# Muscarinic antipsychotics

## Definition

Muscarinic antipsychotics treat psychosis by stimulating muscarinic acetylcholine receptors rather than by blocking dopamine D2 receptors. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-mechanism|COBENFY prescribing information]] [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-efficacy|Kaul 2024]] The first approved example, xanomeline-trospium, was approved in the United States in September 2024 for schizophrenia in adults; until then every available antipsychotic had acted on dopamine receptors. [[F126 FDA Drug Trials Snapshot COBENFY#^f126-approval|Drug Trials Snapshot: COBENFY]] [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-indication|COBENFY prescribing information]] [[P39350614 Leucht 2024 Antipsychotic drugs concise review#^p39350614-mechanism|Leucht 2024]]

## How it works

**Members.** Xanomeline-trospium is the only approved member; emraclidine, a selective positive allosteric modulator of the M4 receptor, has been tested in a phase 1b trial. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-indication|COBENFY prescribing information]] [[P36528376 Krystal 2022 Emraclidine phase 1b trial#^p36528376-compound|Krystal 2022]]

**Mechanism.** The approved label says xanomeline's mechanism in schizophrenia is unclear, but that its efficacy is thought to follow from agonist activity at M1 and M4 receptors in the brain, while trospium blocks muscarinic receptors mainly in peripheral tissues. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-mechanism|COBENFY prescribing information]] Xanomeline binds all five muscarinic subtypes with similar affinity but activates M1 and M4 most, which is why a central agonist is paired with a peripherally restricted antagonist. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-pharmacodynamics|COBENFY prescribing information]] The combination has no direct D2-blocking activity. [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-efficacy|Kaul 2024]] Emraclidine takes a different route to the same receptor family: as a positive allosteric modulator it is meant to amplify acetylcholine's own signal at M4 rather than activate the receptor directly, the same logic benzodiazepines use at GABA-A receptors. [[P36528376 Krystal 2022 Emraclidine phase 1b trial#^p36528376-compound|Krystal 2022]] [[P23789008 Griffin 2013 Benzodiazepine pharmacology#^p23789008-mechanism|Griffin 2013]]

**What it is used for.** The label names one indication, schizophrenia in adults, and approval rested on two randomised trials with 470 adults in the efficacy analyses and 504 in the safety analyses. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-indication|COBENFY prescribing information]] [[F126 FDA Drug Trials Snapshot COBENFY#^f126-trials|Drug Trials Snapshot: COBENFY]] Pooling three five-week trials, xanomeline-trospium beat placebo on total PANSS (SMD -0.56) and on positive, negative and global-severity measures, roughly doubling the response rate, with moderate-certainty evidence. [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-efficacy|Fabiano 2025]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** The side-effect profile follows the muscarinic receptors rather than dopamine: vomiting, nausea, dyspepsia, constipation, dry mouth and hypertension were more frequent than on placebo, as were raised triglycerides and supine heart rate, but these effects were mostly mild to moderate and transient, and serious adverse events and discontinuation did not increase. [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-safety|Fabiano 2025]] Extrapyramidal symptoms, weight gain and somnolence were similar to placebo, and clinically relevant weight gain was, if anything, less likely. [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-safety|Kaul 2024]] [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-weight|Fabiano 2025]] Over a 52-week open-label extension, gastrointestinal effects were mild or moderate and eased with continued use, and no clinically meaningful motor, prolactin, weight or metabolic effects appeared. [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-safety|Kaul 2026]] The absence of D2-linked harms fits the account in which D2 blockade produces them. [[P31299229 Kaar 2020 Antipsychotics mechanisms#^p31299229-d2|Kaar 2020]]

**Reading rule.** Treat "muscarinic antipsychotic" as a dated mechanism claim resting on short evidence: the label itself calls the mechanism unclear, efficacy rests on five-week placebo comparisons of one design, and the one-year data come from an uncontrolled extension that most participants left. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-limit|COBENFY prescribing information]] [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-caution-design|Appraisal: Fabiano 2025]] [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-caution-attrition|Appraisal: Kaul 2026]]

## Evidence and status

Short-term efficacy against placebo in acute schizophrenia is supported by three low-risk-of-bias trials with moderate certainty. [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-efficacy|Fabiano 2025]] [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-limit|Fabiano 2025]] There is no head-to-head comparison with dopamine-blocking antipsychotics, so the drug's place relative to them is not established. [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-caution-comparison|Appraisal: Kaul 2024]] Long-term safety comes from an open-label extension in which 34 of 156 enrolled participants completed a year. [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-uptake|Kaul 2026]] [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-limit|Kaul 2026]] Evidence for selective M4 modulation is limited to a phase 1b safety study. [[P36528376 Krystal 2022 Emraclidine phase 1b trial#^p36528376-limit|Krystal 2022]]

## Uncertainties

- How xanomeline's antipsychotic effect arises - which receptor subtype and which circuit - since the label calls the mechanism unclear. [[F127 FDA prescribing information COBENFY#^f127-mechanism|COBENFY prescribing information]]
- How the combination compares with dopamine-blocking drugs, and whether it helps psychosis in other conditions. [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-caution-comparison|Appraisal: Kaul 2024]]
- Whether selective M4 modulation treats psychosis at all; a safety study cannot say. [[P36528376 Krystal 2022 Emraclidine phase 1b trial#^p36528376-caution-efficacy|Appraisal: Krystal 2022]]
- Whether the post hoc signals on negative and cognitive symptoms hold up in prospective tests. [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-limit|Fabiano 2025]]

## Recent research

- **2026 · Open-label extension trial (The American Journal of Psychiatry).** Adds the first year-long data: symptom gains persisted and no new safety problems appeared among those who stayed, but only about one in five enrolled participants completed. [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-symptoms|Kaul 2026]] [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-uptake|Kaul 2026]] Retention, not only the drug, shapes that picture. [[P41634905 Kaul 2026 Xanomeline-trospium 52-week extension#^p41634905-caution-attrition|Appraisal: Kaul 2026]]
- **2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (European Neuropsychopharmacology).** Pools the three placebo-controlled trials, confirming a moderate short-term effect with a cholinergic rather than dopaminergic side-effect profile. [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-efficacy|Fabiano 2025]] [[P39724748 Fabiano 2025 Xanomeline-trospium meta-analysis#^p39724748-safety|Fabiano 2025]]
- **2024 · Phase 3 randomised trial (JAMA Psychiatry).** EMERGENT-3 reproduced the effect of the earlier trials, with extrapyramidal, weight and somnolence rates similar to placebo. [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-limit|Kaul 2024]] [[P38691387 Kaul 2024 Xanomeline-trospium EMERGENT-3 trial#^p38691387-safety|Kaul 2024]]

- [[Autonomic regulation]] - explains the peripheral cholinergic effects on gut and heart rate, and why a peripheral antagonist is added to the central agonist.
- [[First-generation antipsychotics]] - the dopamine-blocking template this class departs from, and the source of the movement and prolactin harms its trials do not show.
- [[Second-generation antipsychotics]] - the current mainstay, whose metabolic and movement burdens set a comparison this class has not yet faced head to head.
- [[Psychosis constructs]] - the clinical target; a second mechanism that reduces psychosis is also evidence that dopamine blockade is not the only route.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** The muscarinic route links the neurochemistry domain's cholinergic system to the clinical domain's psychosis constructs without passing through dopamine blockade, and the autonomic material explains why the approved product pairs a central agonist with a peripheral antagonist.
