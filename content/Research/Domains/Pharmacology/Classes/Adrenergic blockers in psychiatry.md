# Adrenergic blockers in psychiatry

## Definition

The adrenergic drugs used in psychiatry act on noradrenaline's receptors: the alpha-1 antagonist prazosin, beta-blockers such as propranolol, and the alpha-2 agonists clonidine and guanfacine. [[P39828080 Mendes 2025 Prazosin for PTSD sleep meta-regression#^p39828080-framing|Mendes 2025]] [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-prescribing|Archer 2025]] [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-approved|Newcorn 2022]] Only prazosin and propranolol block a receptor; clonidine and guanfacine stimulate alpha-2 receptors, and clonidine is described as a centrally acting anti-adrenergic drug. [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-approved|Newcorn 2022]] [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-framing|Marchi 2024]] Their psychiatric targets sit on the arousal side of conditions: trauma-related nightmares and hyperarousal, the anxiety disorders, and ADHD. [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-framing|Marchi 2024]] [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-null|Archer 2025]] [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-approved|Newcorn 2022]]

## How it works

**Members.** Prazosin is an alpha-1 adrenoceptor antagonist and propranolol a beta-blocker. [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-rationale|Raskind 2018]] [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-prescribing|Archer 2025]] Extended-release clonidine and extended-release guanfacine are alpha-2 agonists approved for ADHD, alongside the noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors atomoxetine and viloxazine. [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-approved|Newcorn 2022]]

**Mechanism.** A transmitter's effect depends on the receptor it meets rather than on the transmitter alone, so drugs aimed at different adrenergic receptors do different things. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]] In PTSD, whose pattern includes altered arousal, the rationale is that hyperarousal and disturbed sleep reflect adrenergic involvement, which makes anti-adrenergic drugs a reasonable target. [[F88 NIMH Post-traumatic stress disorder#^f88-pattern|Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder]] [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-framing|Marchi 2024]] In ADHD the logic differs: prefrontal function depends on noradrenaline and dopamine along an inverted U, and noradrenaline's benefit is attributed to postsynaptic alpha-2A receptors, the mechanistic case for alpha-2 agonists. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Arnsten 2009]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Arnsten 2009]] That account combines imaging, genetics and animal pharmacology rather than trials. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-limit|Arnsten 2009]]

**What it is used for: trauma-related nightmares.** Earlier trials in veterans found prazosin effective against PTSD nightmares, but in a 304-veteran trial it did not beat placebo on distressing dreams, sleep quality or global change at 10 or 26 weeks. [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-rationale|Raskind 2018]] [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-null|Raskind 2018]] Pooled across ten trials with 648 patients, prazosin improved nightmares and insomnia but not overall PTSD symptoms. [[P39828080 Mendes 2025 Prazosin for PTSD sleep meta-regression#^p39828080-sleep|Mendes 2025]] Trials with more benzodiazepine co-prescription showed larger gains, a trial-level pattern that says nothing about combining the drugs in any person. [[P39828080 Mendes 2025 Prazosin for PTSD sleep meta-regression#^p39828080-moderator|Mendes 2025]] [[P39828080 Mendes 2025 Prazosin for PTSD sleep meta-regression#^p39828080-caution-ecological|Appraisal: Mendes 2025]] Guidelines seldom address nightmares, and those that do diverge. [[P34575284 Martin 2021 Treatment guidelines for PTSD#^p34575284-nightmares|Martin 2021]] Clonidine has been tried for the same targets, but its evidence is mostly case reports and observational studies, and two small comparisons with alpha-1 blockers on nightmares showed no difference. [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-evidence|Marchi 2024]] [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-effects|Marchi 2024]]

**What it is used for: anxiety and trauma prevention.** Beta-blocker prescribing for anxiety rose substantially between 2003 and 2018 without clinical guidance, yet pooled trials showed no benefit over placebo or benzodiazepines in social phobia or panic disorder. [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-prescribing|Archer 2025]] [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-null|Archer 2025]] Given within three months of trauma, propranolol did not prevent PTSD in pooled randomised trials, where only hydrocortisone outperformed placebo. [[P31819037 Astill Wright 2019 Early pharmacological prevention of PTSD#^p31819037-null|Astill Wright 2019]] [[P31819037 Astill Wright 2019 Early pharmacological prevention of PTSD#^p31819037-hydrocortisone|Astill Wright 2019]]

**What it is used for: ADHD.** In children and adolescents, alpha-2 agonists reduced ADHD symptoms in the short term with medium to large effects and moderate to high certainty. [[P41297970 Gosling 2025 Umbrella review of ADHD interventions#^p41297970-children|Gosling 2025]] A clinical review places non-stimulants where stimulant response or tolerability is poor, where certain conditions co-occur, or where people prefer them, and notes that response varies within and between the drug groups. [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-role|Newcorn 2022]] [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-variability|Newcorn 2022]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** The same receptors act on the heart and vessels: prazosin lowered supine systolic blood pressure by 6.7 mm Hg more than placebo, and guanfacine lowered blood pressure and pulse in ADHD trials in which stimulants and noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors raised them. [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-safety|Raskind 2018]] [[P40203844 Farhat 2025 Cardiovascular safety of ADHD medications#^p40203844-haemodynamic|Farhat 2025]] Guanfacine was less well tolerated than placebo in children, and in adults it had lower acceptability. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|Cortese 2018]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-acceptability|Ostinelli 2025]] In the prazosin trial, new or worsening suicidal ideation was not more common on the drug (8% against 15% on placebo). [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-safety|Raskind 2018]]

**Reading rule.** Name the receptor and the target: "adrenergic" covers blockade at alpha-1 and beta receptors and stimulation at alpha-2 receptors, and a result for nightmares, anxiety or ADHD symptoms does not carry over to the others or from veterans to civilian and complex trauma. [[P35697393 Newcorn 2022 Nonstimulant treatments for ADHD#^p35697393-caution-label|Appraisal: Newcorn 2022]] [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-caution-population|Appraisal: Raskind 2018]]

## Evidence and status

The firmest evidence is for alpha-2 agonists in childhood ADHD, graded in an umbrella review of meta-analyses against passive controls. [[P41297970 Gosling 2025 Umbrella review of ADHD interventions#^p41297970-limit|Gosling 2025]] Prazosin's evidence is discordant, a pooled benefit on nightmares beside a large null trial. [[P39828080 Mendes 2025 Prazosin for PTSD sleep meta-regression#^p39828080-caution-discordance|Appraisal: Mendes 2025]] [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-caution-discordance|Appraisal: Raskind 2018]] The beta-blocker evidence in anxiety rests on small studies at high or unclear risk of bias, the prevention trials after trauma were mostly of low quality, and clonidine's evidence in PTSD is low to very low quality. [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-limit|Archer 2025]] [[P31819037 Astill Wright 2019 Early pharmacological prevention of PTSD#^p31819037-quality|Astill Wright 2019]] [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-evidence|Marchi 2024]]

## Uncertainties

- Which trauma populations, if any, benefit from prazosin, given discordant trials. [[P29414272 Raskind 2018 Prazosin trial in veterans with PTSD#^p29414272-caution-population|Appraisal: Raskind 2018]]
- Whether propranolol has a modest preventive effect after trauma that small trials could not detect. [[P31819037 Astill Wright 2019 Early pharmacological prevention of PTSD#^p31819037-caution-power|Appraisal: Astill Wright 2019]]
- Whether beta-blockers help situational anxiety outside the diagnoses pooled so far. [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-caution-absence|Appraisal: Archer 2025]]
- Whether clonidine matches prazosin for nightmares; the small comparisons were not built to show equivalence. [[P38941125 Marchi 2024 Clonidine for PTSD systematic review#^p38941125-caution-equivalence|Appraisal: Marchi 2024]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review and meta-regression (Progress in Neuro-Psychopharmacology and Biological Psychiatry).** Finds a pooled benefit of prazosin on nightmares and insomnia despite the large null trial, but none on overall PTSD symptoms. [[P39828080 Mendes 2025 Prazosin for PTSD sleep meta-regression#^p39828080-sleep|Mendes 2025]]
- **2025 · Umbrella review (BMJ).** Grades the short-term effect of alpha-2 agonists in childhood ADHD as medium to large, with moderate to high certainty. [[P41297970 Gosling 2025 Umbrella review of ADHD interventions#^p41297970-children|Gosling 2025]]
- **2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Journal of Affective Disorders).** Finds no pooled benefit of beta-blockers in social phobia or panic disorder while their prescribing for anxiety rises. [[P39271062 Archer 2025 Beta-blockers for anxiety disorders#^p39271062-null|Archer 2025]]

## Connections

- [[Noradrenaline signalling]] - the transmitter system these drugs act on, whose alpha, beta and autoreceptor subtypes explain why receptor blockade and alpha-2 stimulation differ.
- [[Post-traumatic stress disorder]] - the diagnosis whose hyperarousal and nightmares prazosin and clonidine target, and whose guidelines rarely address nightmares.
- [[Stimulant and non-stimulant mechanisms]] - the ADHD medicines among which clonidine and guanfacine are the alpha-2 non-stimulants.
- [[ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines]] - the inverted-U, alpha-2A account that supplies the mechanistic case for guanfacine and clonidine in ADHD.
- [[Autonomic regulation]] - the sympathetic side of the same transmitter, which is why these drugs lower blood pressure and pulse.
- [[Hypnotics and sleep medicines]] - the sleep medicines beside which prazosin's nightmare evidence sits.
- [[Anxiety and fear constructs]] - the anxiety disorders in which beta-blockers are prescribed without supporting pooled evidence.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** Adrenergic drugs link the neurochemistry domain's noradrenaline receptors to two condition domains that seldom meet: trauma, where damping arousal is the aim, and ADHD, where alpha-2 stimulation is proposed to strengthen prefrontal function. The neuroanatomy domain's autonomic material explains the cardiovascular effects they share.
