# Serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors

## Definition

Serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs) are antidepressants that block reuptake of both serotonin and noradrenaline; venlafaxine, the reference member, is approved in the United States for major depression, social anxiety disorder and panic disorder. [[P30570984 Singh 2024 Venlafaxine pharmacology and uses#^p30570984-class|Singh 2024]] Blocking reuptake keeps a transmitter active in the synaptic cleft for longer. [[F06 OpenStax Cells of the nervous system#^f06-receptor|Cells of the Nervous System]] One member, duloxetine, is also the antidepressant with the clearest evidence in chronic pain. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-duloxetine|Birkinshaw 2023]]

## How it works

**Members.** Venlafaxine and duloxetine are the SNRIs in the 2018 network meta-analysis of acute treatment for adult depression, and milnacipran is the next-ranked drug in the chronic-pain analysis. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-drugs|Cipriani 2018]] [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-duloxetine|Birkinshaw 2023]] Newer SNRIs such as levomilnacipran appear in reviews of emerging antidepressants. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-classes|Fasipe 2019]]

**Mechanism.** The class adds noradrenaline transporter block to the serotonin transporter block of the SSRIs; drugs that block only the noradrenaline transporter, such as reboxetine, viloxazine and atomoxetine, form a separate group. [[P31211282 Fasipe 2019 Novel antidepressants and agomelatine#^p31211282-classes|Fasipe 2019]] As with every monoamine antidepressant, transporter block is immediate while clinical change in depression takes weeks, a gap that monoamine accounts explain poorly. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-monoamine|Harmer 2017]] Because one target sits in many tissues, the noradrenergic half of the mechanism also acts outside the brain. [[F13 NIGMS How do medicines work#^f13-agonism|How Do Medicines Work?]]

**What it is used for.** In adult depression, SNRIs were among the 21 drugs that all beat placebo, with small and uncertain differences between them. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-placebo|Cipriani 2018]] [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-comparisons|Cipriani 2018]] A 2025 review likewise reports similar efficacy against placebo but different acceptability across SSRIs, SNRIs and other classes. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]] In chronic pain, duloxetine ranked first on every efficacy outcome with moderate- to high-certainty evidence of a small to moderate benefit, milnacipran ranked next, and evidence for the other antidepressants was low certainty. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-duloxetine|Birkinshaw 2023]] [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-others|Birkinshaw 2023]] Off-label use of venlafaxine extends to fibromyalgia, neuropathy, migraine prevention, hot flashes, premenstrual dysphoric disorder and obsessive-compulsive disorder, but an off-label list records practice rather than trial support. [[P30570984 Singh 2024 Venlafaxine pharmacology and uses#^p30570984-offlabel|Singh 2024]] [[P30570984 Singh 2024 Venlafaxine pharmacology and uses#^p30570984-caution-offlabel|Appraisal: Singh 2024]]

**Class-level adverse effects and serious risks.** In head-to-head depression trials, duloxetine and venlafaxine were among the drugs with the highest dropout rates. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-acceptability|Cipriani 2018]] Discontinuation symptoms follow antidepressants as a group, with frequency and severity differing between drugs. [[P38851198 Henssler 2024 Antidepressant discontinuation symptom incidence#^p38851198-incidence|Henssler 2024]] [[P38851198 Henssler 2024 Antidepressant discontinuation symptom incidence#^p38851198-drugs|Henssler 2024]] In the pain trials, safety evidence was very low certainty for every antidepressant and none had reliable long-term data. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-gaps|Birkinshaw 2023]] The noradrenergic component has a measurable peripheral signature in a neighbouring group: atomoxetine and viloxazine raised blood pressure and pulse against placebo in ADHD trials. [[P40203844 Farhat 2025 Cardiovascular safety of ADHD medications#^p40203844-haemodynamic|Farhat 2025]] Carrying that result across to SNRIs by shared mechanism is this library's inference, not a finding of that review. [[P40203844 Farhat 2025 Cardiovascular safety of ADHD medications#^p40203844-caution-route|Appraisal: Farhat 2025]]

**Interactions.** SNRIs share the serotonergic interaction agenda of the SSRIs, whose extreme is described as serotonin toxicity, a graded spectrum rather than a single syndrome. [[P30521236 Bruggeman 2023 Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor toxicity#^p30521236-serotonin|Bruggeman 2023]] Monoamine oxidase inhibitors interact in clinically important ways with many medicines. [[P29083800 Easton-Carr 2026 Monoamine oxidase inhibitor toxicity#^p29083800-interactions|Easton-Carr 2026]] Interactions may be drug-drug, drug-food or drug-condition, and individual decisions belong to product labelling and clinicians. [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-types|Drug interactions: what you should know]] [[F19 FDA Drug interactions what you should know#^f19-advice|Drug interactions: what you should know]]

**Bipolar depression: a higher switch concern.** The ISBD task force judged the evidence for antidepressants in bipolar disorder weak, and it named SNRIs, with tricyclic and tetracyclic drugs, as possibly carrying higher rates of switching into [[Hypomania|hypomania]], [[Mania|mania]] or mixed states than SSRIs and bupropion. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-evidence|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]] For bipolar I the task force confined antidepressants to use alongside mood stabilisers, and its class comparison rests on expert judgement rather than randomised estimates. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Pacchiarotti 2013]] See [[Bipolar depression and mixed features]].

**ADHD: the neighbouring noradrenergic route.** Venlafaxine's off-label list includes attention-deficit disorder. [[P30570984 Singh 2024 Venlafaxine pharmacology and uses#^p30570984-offlabel|Singh 2024]] The trial-tested noradrenergic route in ADHD is instead the selective noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor atomoxetine, which beat placebo in children and in adults. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|Cortese 2018]] That route is covered in [[Stimulant and non-stimulant mechanisms]].

**PTSD.** Venlafaxine is used off-label for [[Post-traumatic stress disorder|PTSD]]. [[P30570984 Singh 2024 Venlafaxine pharmacology and uses#^p30570984-offlabel|Singh 2024]] PTSD guidelines, however, name SSRIs rather than SNRIs as the first-line drug class. [[P34575284 Martin 2021 Treatment guidelines for PTSD#^p34575284-firstline|Martin 2021]]

**Reading rule.** Name the indication. The pain evidence was gathered in people without clinically low mood, so it says nothing about antidepressant action, and a depression result says nothing about pain. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-caution-mood|Appraisal: Birkinshaw 2023]]

## Evidence and status

For adult depression the class rests on the same large trial network as other antidepressants, with certainty from moderate to very low. [[P29477251 Cipriani 2018 Comparative efficacy of 21 antidepressants#^p29477251-limit|Cipriani 2018]] For chronic pain, duloxetine's short-term benefit is the best-supported antidepressant result, while long-term efficacy and safety lack reliable evidence. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-duloxetine|Birkinshaw 2023]] [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-gaps|Birkinshaw 2023]] The bipolar switch comparison is consensus-level. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-limit|Pacchiarotti 2013]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether SNRIs raise blood pressure and pulse as the noradrenaline-selective ADHD drugs do is not measured by a source checked here.
- Evidence for SNRIs in anxiety disorders beyond venlafaxine's approvals is not yet sourced in this library.
- No SNRI evidence specific to autistic people is sourced here.

## Recent research

- **2025 · Narrative review (Medicina).** Places SNRIs alongside SSRIs, tricyclics and newer drugs as similarly effective against placebo but differing in acceptability. [[P40282849 Gao 2025 Pharmacological monotherapy for depressive disorders#^p40282849-classes|Gao 2025]]
- **2025 · Network meta-analysis (Lancet Psychiatry).** The noradrenaline-selective drugs atomoxetine and viloxazine raised blood pressure and pulse in ADHD trials, a signature this note flags as untested for SNRIs. [[P40203844 Farhat 2025 Cardiovascular safety of ADHD medications#^p40203844-haemodynamic|Farhat 2025]]
- **2023 · Cochrane network meta-analysis (Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews).** Across 176 trials in chronic pain, duloxetine was the only antidepressant with evidence certain enough to rely on, and trials excluded people with low mood. [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-duloxetine|Birkinshaw 2023]] [[P37160297 Birkinshaw 2023 Antidepressants for chronic pain#^p37160297-gaps|Birkinshaw 2023]]
