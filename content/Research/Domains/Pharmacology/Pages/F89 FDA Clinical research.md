# Step 3: Clinical Research

**Publisher.** U.S. Food and Drug Administration

**Source.** <https://www.fda.gov/patients/drug-development-process/step-3-clinical-research>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F89.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The page lays out clinical research phases: phase 1 for safety and dosage in small groups, phase 2 for efficacy and side effects, phase 3 for efficacy and monitoring in larger groups.
^f89-phases

It notes that phase 3 studies commonly randomise participants to the investigational product or a comparator such as placebo or standard treatment.
^f89-design

It states that only a minority of investigational drugs proceed through all phases, which frames later-phase evidence as a filtered sample of what was tested.
^f89-proportion

## Scope and limitations

An official process description aimed at patients and the public. It describes how studies are staged, not what any product achieves.
^f89-limit
