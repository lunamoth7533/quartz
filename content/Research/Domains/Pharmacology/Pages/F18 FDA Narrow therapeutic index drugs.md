# Setting and Implementing Standards for Narrow Therapeutic Index Drugs

**Publisher.** US Food and Drug Administration (CDER Conversations)

**Source.** <https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cder-conversations/setting-and-implementing-standards-narrow-therapeutic-index-drugs>

**Verification.** Official page opened and checked on 2026-09-24; educational material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Every medicine sits between a concentration that does too little and one that produces toxicity; that range is the therapeutic window.
^f18-window

Drugs with a narrow therapeutic index need tighter control of exposure and often biological monitoring, because small changes can move a person outside the useful range.
^f18-nti
