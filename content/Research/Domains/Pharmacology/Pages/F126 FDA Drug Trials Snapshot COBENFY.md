# Drug Trials Snapshot: COBENFY (xanomeline and trospium chloride)

**Publisher.** U.S. Food and Drug Administration

**Source.** <https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-trials-snapshots/drug-trials-snapshot-cobenfy>

**Verification.** official page opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F126.txt`. A regulatory summary of the trials that supported the original approval, not a research article.

## Key points (as published)

COBENFY is a combination of xanomeline, a muscarinic agonist, and trospium chloride, a muscarinic antagonist, and is used for the treatment of schizophrenia in adults. The original approval date is September 26, 2024.
^f126-approval

Approval rested on two randomised, double-blind trials: efficacy was evaluated in 470 adults with schizophrenia and safety in 504 who received at least one dose, at 39 sites in the United States and Ukraine, with symptoms measured on a clinician-administered scale.
^f126-trials

## Scope and limitations

A snapshot of who participated in the pivotal trials and how they were designed. It reports no mechanism, no comparative effectiveness and no long-term outcome, and it states that it does not cover later approvals or additional uses.
^f126-limit
