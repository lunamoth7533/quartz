# Lithium carbonate labelling: elimination and monitoring

**Publisher.** DailyMed (US National Library of Medicine), label for Hikma Pharmaceuticals USA · [Product label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b839ff4b-f62d-41ab-a823-550a756d58ec)

**Verification.** Official label XML retrieved from DailyMed on 2026-09-24; the blocks below paraphrase the clinical pharmacology and monitoring statements. No dosage or titration detail is reproduced.

## Key points (as published)

Lithium is eliminated in urine proportionally to serum concentration; it is filtered by the glomerulus and about 80 percent is reabsorbed in the proximal tubule.
^f27-clearance

The elimination half-life is approximately 18 to 36 hours, and renal clearance falls when kidney function is impaired.
^f27-half-life

Serum concentrations should be sampled when levels are relatively stable, and total reliance must not be placed on concentrations alone: clinical and laboratory evaluation are both required.
^f27-monitoring

## Scope and limitations

Official U.S. product labelling for one manufacturer's lithium carbonate product, describing its approved uses, pharmacology, monitoring and safety information. It documents exposure and safety rather than explaining the mechanism of the mood-stabilising effect.
^f27-limit
