# NUPLAZID (pimavanserin) approved product label

**Publisher.** ACADIA Pharmaceuticals Inc.; label hosted on National Library of Medicine DailyMed

## Key points (as published)

NUPLAZID is indicated for the treatment of hallucinations and delusions associated with Parkinson's disease psychosis; the original approval date is April 29, 2016.
^f128-indication

Section 12.1 describes in vitro inverse agonist and antagonist activity at serotonin 5-HT2A receptors, and to a lesser extent 5-HT2C receptors, with no appreciable affinity for dopaminergic (including D2), muscarinic, histaminergic or adrenergic receptors or calcium channels.
^f128-mechanism

## Scope and limitations

Approved labelling states the indication and the sponsor-reported pharmacology for one named medicine. It is not a class-level statement about antipsychotic mechanisms, it covers no comparative effectiveness, and the label's mechanism section reports binding and activity findings rather than a demonstration of how the drug works clinically.
