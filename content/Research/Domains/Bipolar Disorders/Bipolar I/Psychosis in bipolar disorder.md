# Psychosis in bipolar disorder

## Definition

Psychotic features in bipolar disorder are delusions, hallucinations or disordered thinking that occur within mood episodes; they are common in mania and most frequent in bipolar I. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-psychosis|Dailey 2023]] [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-type|Shao 2026]] Their content is described as mood-congruent or mood-incongruent according to whether it fits the prevailing mood, and incongruent content turns out to be common. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-incongruent|Shao 2026]] [[P29167880 Allardyce 2018 Schizophrenia polygenic liability and mood-incongruent psychosis#^p29167880-incongruent|Allardyce 2018]] When psychosis is confined to mood episodes, DSM-5 keeps the diagnosis within the mood disorder; psychosis that persists without prominent mood symptoms points toward schizoaffective disorder or schizophrenia. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-rule|Malaspina 2013]]

## How it works

**How common it is.** A meta-analysis of 285 studies found psychotic symptoms frequent in bipolar disorder, above all in bipolar I and during mania, with delusions more common than hallucinations or thought disorder. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-type|Shao 2026]] The typical manic delusions are grandiose or persecutory. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-psychosis|Dailey 2023]] Head-to-head comparisons find fewer psychotic features in bipolar II than in bipolar I. [[P37437249 Hernandorena 2023 Bipolar II versus bipolar I#^p37437249-less|Hernandorena 2023]]

**Congruent and incongruent content.** Broadly defined mood-incongruent psychosis was pooled at about 47% of bipolar samples, while a co-occurring schizophrenia diagnosis was uncommon at about 8%. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-incongruent|Shao 2026]] [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-schizophrenia|Shao 2026]] The pooled literature mixes classification systems and definitions of incongruence, so the 47% signals that incongruent content is common rather than giving a precise rate. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-caution-definitions|Appraisal: Shao 2026]]

**A genetic gradient rather than a wall.** In 4436 UK bipolar cases, polygenic liability for schizophrenia was highest in schizophrenia, then in schizoaffective bipolar disorder and in bipolar I, with no clear association for bipolar II. [[P29167880 Allardyce 2018 Schizophrenia polygenic liability and mood-incongruent psychosis#^p29167880-gradient|Allardyce 2018]] Within bipolar disorder the same liability rose from no psychosis to mood-congruent psychosis to prominent mood-incongruent features. [[P29167880 Allardyce 2018 Schizophrenia polygenic liability and mood-incongruent psychosis#^p29167880-incongruent|Allardyce 2018]] This fits the wider picture of bipolar I sitting genetically closer to schizophrenia and bipolar II closer to major depression, and of genetic architecture that varies with subtype. [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-genetics|McIntyre 2020]] [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-ascertainment|O'Connell 2025]] Shifts of this size describe populations and cannot assign an individual to a diagnosis. [[P29167880 Allardyce 2018 Schizophrenia polygenic liability and mood-incongruent psychosis#^p29167880-caution-scores|Appraisal: Allardyce 2018]]

**The boundary with schizoaffective disorder and schizophrenia.** DSM-IV schizoaffective disorder was unreliable, unstable over time and used far more often than intended. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-problem|Malaspina 2013]] DSM-5 therefore judges the boundary over the whole illness: psychosis only during mood episodes, manic or depressive, indicates a mood disorder with psychotic features; two weeks or more of psychosis without prominent mood symptoms indicates schizoaffective disorder or schizophrenia; and schizoaffective disorder requires full mood episodes for most of the illness since psychosis began. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-rule|Malaspina 2013]] [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-lifecourse|Malaspina 2013]] Schizophrenia is also described through negative and cognitive symptom domains, not psychosis alone. [[F83 NIMH Schizophrenia#^f83-features|NIMH schizophrenia]] Measuring how much of an illness was spent in mood episodes depends on recall and records. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-caution-duration|Appraisal: Malaspina 2013]]

**What psychosis marks.** In a large cortical MRI analysis, a history of psychosis went with smaller cortical surface area, a group-level difference that cannot diagnose a person. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-course|Hibar 2018]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|Appraisal: Hibar 2018]] The authors of the 285-study meta-analysis read the overlap with schizophrenia as a reason to compare patients by shared symptoms rather than by diagnosis alone. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-overlap|Shao 2026]]

**Reading rule.** Before comparing bipolar and schizophrenia samples, state the episode in which psychosis occurred, whether its content was congruent, and which system's boundary rule set the diagnosis. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-rule|Malaspina 2013]] [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-caution-definitions|Appraisal: Shao 2026]]

## Evidence and status

Prevalence rests on a very large but heterogeneous pooled literature built from title and abstract searches. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-limit|Shao 2026]] The genetic gradient comes from one large UK case-control sample using scores from an earlier schizophrenia genome-wide study. [[P29167880 Allardyce 2018 Schizophrenia polygenic liability and mood-incongruent psychosis#^p29167880-limit|Allardyce 2018]] The DSM-5 boundary rule is a criteria decision whose promised gains in reliability and stability were expectations at publication. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-limit|Malaspina 2013]] The imaging association is observational and entangled with medication effects. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|Hibar 2018]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether mood-incongruent psychosis marks a distinct subgroup or the severe end of liability shared with schizophrenia. [[P29167880 Allardyce 2018 Schizophrenia polygenic liability and mood-incongruent psychosis#^p29167880-gradient|Allardyce 2018]] [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-overlap|Shao 2026]]
- How reliably the whole-course schizoaffective rule is applied, given that it depends on retrospective timing. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-caution-duration|Appraisal: Malaspina 2013]]
- Evidence specific to psychotic features in bipolar depression, as distinct from mania, is not yet sourced in this library.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Meta-analysis (Bipolar Disorders).** Across 285 studies, schizophrenia diagnoses were uncommon (8%) but broadly defined mood-incongruent psychosis common (47%), sharpening the overlap argument without settling the boundary. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-schizophrenia|Shao 2026]] [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-incongruent|Shao 2026]] [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-caution-definitions|Appraisal: Shao 2026]]
- **2025 · Multi-ancestry genome-wide study (Nature).** Genetic architecture differed by bipolar subtype and by how patients were ascertained, so psychosis-rich bipolar I samples and bipolar II samples are not genetically interchangeable. [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-ascertainment|O'Connell 2025]] [[P39843750 O'Connell 2025 Genomics of bipolar disorder#^p39843750-caution-prediction|Appraisal: O'Connell 2025]]
- **2023 · Systematic review with meta-analyses (Harvard Review of Psychiatry).** Bipolar II had fewer psychotic features and hospitalisations than bipolar I across about 90,000 patients. [[P37437249 Hernandorena 2023 Bipolar II versus bipolar I#^p37437249-less|Hernandorena 2023]]
