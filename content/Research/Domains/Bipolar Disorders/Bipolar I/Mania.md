# Mania

## Definition

Mania is a period of elevated or irritable mood with increased activity that lasts at least a week, or any length if hospital care is needed, and severely disrupts functioning. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-criteria|Dailey 2023]] [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-features|Dailey 2023]] One manic episode is enough for a diagnosis of bipolar I disorder, and depressive episodes, though common, are not required. [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-mania|NIMH]] [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-definitions|McIntyre 2020]] Mania is also what separates the two main types, because bipolar II is defined by hypomania and major depression instead. [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-definitions|McIntyre 2020]] [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-types|NIMH]]

## How it works

**What an episode looks like.** The core picture is elevated or irritable mood with less need for sleep, rapid speech, racing thoughts, distractibility and more goal-directed activity, often with poor insight into the change. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-features|Dailey 2023]] Activation caused by substances or medical illness has to be excluded before an episode is called mania. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-exclusion|Dailey 2023]] At group level, imaging and pharmacology link mania to raised striatal D2/3 receptor availability and an overactive reward network, a state-dependent finding rather than a test for the episode. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-state|Ashok 2017]] [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-caution-framework|Appraisal: Ashok 2017]]

**Where the threshold sits.** Under the DSM criteria summarised in a clinical reference, mania needs a week and marked impairment, hypomania needs four days without major impairment, and hospitalisation makes any episode manic regardless of length. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-criteria|Dailey 2023]] DSM-5 and the ICD-11 draft both require increased activity as well as the change in mood, which made the threshold stricter than before. [[P25450512 de Dios 2014 Bipolar disorders in DSM-5 and ICD-11#^p25450512-activity|de Dios 2014]] The systems still diverge: DSM-5 removed the mixed episode in favour of a specifier while ICD-11 kept it, and the WHO manual notes that its rules differ from DSM-5-TR in places. [[P25450512 de Dios 2014 Bipolar disorders in DSM-5 and ICD-11#^p25450512-dsm5|de Dios 2014]] [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-mixed|Chakrabarti 2022]] [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-limit|ICD-11 CDDR]] Because both thresholds are conventions, a sample recruited under one system is not interchangeable with one recruited under the other. [[P25450512 de Dios 2014 Bipolar disorders in DSM-5 and ICD-11#^p25450512-caution-draft|Appraisal: de Dios 2014]]

**Psychotic features.** Delusions, most often grandiose or persecutory, are common in mania. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-psychosis|Dailey 2023]] Across 285 studies, psychotic symptoms were most frequent in bipolar I and during manic episodes, and delusions outnumbered hallucinations and thought disorder. [[P41847966 Shao 2026 Psychotic symptoms in bipolar disorder#^p41847966-type|Shao 2026]] When psychosis stays within mood episodes, DSM-5 keeps the diagnosis a mood disorder with psychotic features rather than schizophrenia. [[P23707642 Malaspina 2013 Schizoaffective disorder in DSM-5#^p23707642-rule|Malaspina 2013]]

**Onset and course.** Bipolar symptoms usually begin in late adolescence or early adulthood. [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-onset|NIMH]] Although mania defines bipolar I, depressive episodes and symptoms fill more of the long-term course and a disproportionate share of illness and deaths. [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-course|McIntyre 2020]] In a ten-year staging cohort, more than three lifetime episodes and a predominantly elevated polarity went with progression to the latest stage, hazards that depend on the staging rules used. [[P38944295 Cremaschi 2024 Ten-year staging trajectory of bipolar disorder#^p38944295-predictors|Cremaschi 2024]] [[P38944295 Cremaschi 2024 Ten-year staging trajectory of bipolar disorder#^p38944295-caution-staging|Appraisal: Cremaschi 2024]]

**What precedes and provokes episodes.** Sleep change often comes first: most patients in one sample recalled sleep disturbance before their current episode, beginning about five weeks before mania against three months before depression. [[P38653574 Basquin 2024 Prodromal sleep disturbances in bipolar disorder#^p38653574-prevalence|Basquin 2024]] [[P38653574 Basquin 2024 Prodromal sleep disturbances in bipolar disorder#^p38653574-timing|Basquin 2024]] Passive-sensing studies associate mania and hypomania with more, and more variable, activity and with shorter or advanced sleep, and modelled circadian-phase disturbance preceded mood symptoms in bipolar I. [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-patterns|Kutcher 2026]] [[P38579366 Song 2024 Circadian phase and mood symptom dynamics#^p38579366-direction|Song 2024]] Recall bias and unmeasured common causes keep these as warning associations rather than proven triggers. [[P38653574 Basquin 2024 Prodromal sleep disturbances in bipolar disorder#^p38653574-caution-recall|Appraisal: Basquin 2024]] [[P38579366 Song 2024 Circadian phase and mood symptom dynamics#^p38579366-caution-precedence|Appraisal: Song 2024]] Antidepressant exposure is the best-studied drug trigger: in trials, antidepressants as a class carried more manic switch than antipsychotics, and in a Swedish register, antidepressant-only maintenance periods went with more mania-related admissions. [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-antidepressants|Yildiz 2023]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-mania|Ermis 2025]] Among people with ADHD prescribed stimulants, about 3.7% later received a bipolar diagnosis, but no pooled study had an unexposed comparison group, so causation is not shown. [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-occurrence|Salazar de Pablo 2025]] [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-causality|Salazar de Pablo 2025]]

**Acute treatment at class level.** In a network meta-analysis of 72 double-blind trials in adults, nine antipsychotics, lithium, the anticonvulsants carbamazepine and valproate, and tamoxifen produced more responders than placebo, but only four of the antipsychotics also had fewer dropouts than placebo. [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-efficacy|Kishi 2022]] [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-acceptability|Kishi 2022]] A Lancet seminar summarises the same classes for acute mania and treats lithium as the reference mood stabiliser. [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-treatment|McIntyre 2020]] Guidelines rank acute-phase options by efficacy, safety and tolerability together. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-phases|Keramatian 2023]] Trials averaged about four weeks, so they answer the acute question only and carry no choice for an individual. [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-limit|Kishi 2022]] [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-caution-short|Appraisal: Kishi 2022]]

**Reading rule.** Before comparing mania findings, name the classification and threshold, whether psychotic features were present, and whether the result concerns the acute episode or the longer course; read triggers as group-level associations and treatment ranks as acute-phase evidence. [[P25450512 de Dios 2014 Bipolar disorders in DSM-5 and ICD-11#^p25450512-caution-draft|Appraisal: de Dios 2014]] [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-caution-short|Appraisal: Kishi 2022]]

## Evidence and status

The episode is described consistently across the sources, but its thresholds are conventions that differ between systems, and ICD-11's revised episode requirements are still debated. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-caution-dsm|Appraisal: Dailey 2023]] [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-episodes|Chakrabarti 2022]] Acute antimanic efficacy rests on many short double-blind trials, the strongest treatment evidence in this condition. [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-limit|Kishi 2022]] Evidence on triggers is observational: a retrospective prodrome sample, small monitoring cohorts, a register and uncontrolled stimulant cohorts. [[P38653574 Basquin 2024 Prodromal sleep disturbances in bipolar disorder#^p38653574-limit|Basquin 2024]] [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-gaps|Kutcher 2026]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-limit|Ermis 2025]] [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-limit|Salazar de Pablo 2025]] The dopamine model of the manic state is presented by its authors as speculation. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-speculative|Ashok 2017]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether sleep loss triggers mania or is its first symptom: sleep changes appear in every mood state, and the direction of the sleep-mood link is still open. [[P39210713 Tonon 2024 Sleep and circadian disruption in bipolar disorders#^p39210713-allstates|Tonon 2024]] [[P39210713 Tonon 2024 Sleep and circadian disruption in bipolar disorders#^p39210713-caution-bidirectional|Appraisal: Tonon 2024]]
- How much stimulant exposure adds to the background risk of bipolar disorder in ADHD, which cannot be judged without unexposed comparison groups. [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-caution-baseline|Appraisal: Salazar de Pablo 2025]]
- Whether ICD-11's revised requirements balance sensitivity and specificity better than the DSM thresholds. [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-episodes|Chakrabarti 2022]]
- How acute response relates to later course, which trials of about four weeks cannot show. [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-caution-short|Appraisal: Kishi 2022]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (JAMA Psychiatry).** Among 391,043 people with ADHD prescribed stimulants, about 3.7% later received a bipolar diagnosis and psychosis was more frequent after amphetamines than methylphenidate, but no pooled study had an unexposed comparison group. [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-occurrence|Salazar de Pablo 2025]] [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-moderators|Salazar de Pablo 2025]] [[P40900605 Salazar de Pablo 2025 Psychosis and bipolar disorder after stimulants#^p40900605-caution-baseline|Appraisal: Salazar de Pablo 2025]]
- **2025 · Within-individual register cohort (The Lancet Psychiatry).** Antidepressant-only maintenance periods were associated with more mania-related hospitalisation, a severe-relapse signal open to confounding by the reasons treatment starts. [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-mania|Ermis 2025]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-caution-register|Appraisal: Ermis 2025]]
- **2024 · Prodrome study (Bipolar Disorders).** Structured questions raised reported pre-episode sleep disturbance from 59% to 83%, with a shorter lead before mania than before depression. [[P38653574 Basquin 2024 Prodromal sleep disturbances in bipolar disorder#^p38653574-prevalence|Basquin 2024]] [[P38653574 Basquin 2024 Prodromal sleep disturbances in bipolar disorder#^p38653574-timing|Basquin 2024]]
- **2022 · Network meta-analysis (Molecular Psychiatry).** Placed antipsychotics, lithium and two anticonvulsants ahead of placebo for acute mania, with only four antipsychotics better tolerated than placebo. [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-efficacy|Kishi 2022]] [[P34642461 Kishi 2022 Drug treatment for acute mania#^p34642461-acceptability|Kishi 2022]]
