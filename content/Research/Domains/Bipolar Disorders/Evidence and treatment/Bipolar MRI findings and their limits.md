# Bipolar MRI findings and their limits

**Definition.** Bipolar neuroimaging research reports group-level structural associations, including cortical thickness differences and medication-related signals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]

## Supported claims

- Cortical grey matter was thinner in frontal, temporal and parietal regions across 6503 individuals. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]
- Lithium, antiepileptic and antipsychotic treatment showed associations with cortical measures. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]
- Medication exposure confounds simple case-control readings of these differences. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|Appraisal: ENIGMA cortical MRI findings in bipolar disorder]]

## How it works

**The finding.** Large consortium analyses report group differences in cortical thickness and surface area between people with bipolar disorder and controls. In the multi-site cortical analysis, grey matter was thinner bilaterally in frontal, temporal and parietal regions, with the largest effects in left pars opercularis, fusiform gyrus and rostral middle frontal cortex. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]]

**Why consortium design changed the inference available.** Pre-consortium imaging studies were typically small enough that between-group differences were unstable and effect sizes inflated; the ENIGMA-scale aggregation of thousands of scans across sites is what made a cortical-thickness finding robust enough to be worth interpreting at all - and even then only as a group-level association with named confounds. The design upgrade, not a new analysis trick, is what moved the evidence. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-power|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

**Confounds were studied rather than assumed away.** Commonly prescribed drugs including lithium, antiepileptics and antipsychotics were associated with cortical measures even after accounting for multiple medications; longer illness duration was associated with reduced cortical thickness, reduced surface area was associated with a history of psychosis, and mood state showed no association. Medication exposure, illness duration and comorbidity are therefore associated with structural measures, which makes attribution to the condition itself difficult. The authors describe the study as an extensive analysis of potential confounding variables, which is the correct framing - the associations with medication and course remain confounds for the diagnosis-based comparison. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-course|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-limit|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]]

**Medication-associated structural signals are findings in their own right.** The lithium, antiepileptic and antipsychotic associations with cortical measures - even after mutual adjustment - are clinically interesting and scientifically complicating: they mean the case-control contrast mixes illness with its treatment, and they open treatment-effects questions that diagnosis-based designs cannot separate. This is the article's central interpretive fact and the reason the finding cannot be converted into an illness marker without more design. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[Phase-specific bipolar treatment evidence]]

**The method's own caveats apply.** Neuroimaging has documented analytic flexibility - analysis choice affects imaging results - and low-power problems that exaggerate effect sizes in brain-behaviour correlations; reproducibility requires large samples and preregistration, with data sharing and multiverse analyses also proposed as responses. Consortium work addresses power; it does not remove the interpretive limits. [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-power|Neuroimaging reproducibility]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-practice|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

**What it does not license.** Group averages describe samples rather than individuals, and group distributions overlap substantially, so a difference in means does not classify individuals: a thinner cortex on average cannot establish causation, cannot diagnose an individual, and does not identify a mechanism. The modality measures magnetic tissue properties rather than function. The findings support research on pathophysiology; they are not diagnostic tests or prognoses. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|Appraisal: ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-mri|Brain Imaging]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-limit|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]

**Reading rule.** Report the sample, the contrast, the covariates examined and the effect size, and state explicitly that individual-level inference is out of reach. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-caution-modality|Appraisal: Brain Imaging]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-limit|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Observational group designs generate hypotheses rather than personal diagnostic information.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Multisite mediation study (JAMA Psychiatry).** Across 19 ENIGMA cohorts, brain morphology carried less than 1% of the association between reported childhood trauma and a bipolar diagnosis, through hippocampal volume and two frontal thickness measures - a measure of how little of a risk association structural differences account for. [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-association|Tozzi 2026]] [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-mediation|Tozzi 2026]] Cross-sectional mediation cannot fix the order of trauma, brain change and illness. [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-caution-mediation|Appraisal: Tozzi 2026]]
- **2023 · Multisite mega-analysis (Psychological Medicine).** In 2832 ENIGMA participants, higher body mass index and bipolar disorder were each associated with thinner cortex in overlapping regions, and medication load stayed associated with thinner cortex after controlling for BMI - adding body mass to the confounds this note lists. [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-additive|McWhinney 2023]] [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-medication|McWhinney 2023]] [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-caution-confounds|Appraisal: McWhinney 2023]]
