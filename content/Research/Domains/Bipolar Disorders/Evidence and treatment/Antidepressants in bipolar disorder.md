# Antidepressants in bipolar disorder

## Definition

Antidepressants are widely used in bipolar disorder, although the evidence for their efficacy and safety there is weak: long-term prevention trials are few, and adding them to mood stabilisers has insufficient support. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-evidence|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-treatment|McIntyre 2020]] The main safety concern is switching from depression into hypomania, mania or a mixed state during treatment, with a further worry that longer use destabilises mood. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-treatment|McIntyre 2020]] The question matters because depression, not mania, fills most of the long-term course. [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-course|McIntyre 2020]]

## How it works

**Switch risk in acute trials.** Across masked trials of 2-16 weeks in acute bipolar depression, antidepressants as a class appeared efficacious but carried more manic switch than antipsychotics. [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-limit|Yildiz 2023]] [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-antidepressants|Yildiz 2023]] A 2025 network meta-analysis found no single antidepressant with a switch rate significantly above placebo, with data mostly from add-on designs and rated low confidence. [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-placebo|Oliva 2025]] [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-regimen|Oliva 2025]] Few switches over a few weeks leave a real risk possible, and a class average gives no individual's risk. [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-caution-acute|Appraisal: Oliva 2025]] [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-caution-class|Appraisal: Yildiz 2023]]

**Drug classes may differ.** The International Society for Bipolar Disorders (ISBD) task force suggested that SSRIs and bupropion switch mood less often than tricyclic and tetracyclic drugs and SNRIs, a consensus ranking rather than a randomised estimate. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Pacchiarotti 2013]] In the later trial network only venlafaxine, a [[Serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitors|serotonin-noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor]], showed consistent switch signals across its studies. [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-venlafaxine|Oliva 2025]]

**Mixed features: the untested case.** No adequate trial has tested antidepressants alone in depression with mixed features, whereas several atypical antipsychotics reduced depressive symptoms there, mostly in post hoc analyses, without more worsening of hypomania or switching than placebo. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antidepressants|Xiao 2025]] [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antipsychotics|Xiao 2025]] [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-switch|Xiao 2025]] CANMAT and ISBD guidance on mixed presentations found no first-line treatment for DSM-5 episodes with mixed features and, leaning on expert opinion, named two antipsychotics as second-line options for mixed-features depression. [[P34599629 Yatham 2021 CANMAT and ISBD mixed presentations#^p34599629-firstline|Yatham 2021]] [[P34599629 Yatham 2021 CANMAT and ISBD mixed presentations#^p34599629-depression|Yatham 2021]] [[P34599629 Yatham 2021 CANMAT and ISBD mixed presentations#^p34599629-limit|Yatham 2021]] Missing trials are an evidence gap, not proof of harm. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-caution-posthoc|Appraisal: Xiao 2025]] [[P34599629 Yatham 2021 CANMAT and ISBD mixed presentations#^p34599629-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Yatham 2021]]

**Beyond the acute episode.** In a double-blind trial in bipolar I, continuing adjunctive escitalopram or bupropion XL for 52 weeks after remission, rather than replacing it with placebo at 8 weeks, did not significantly lower the risk of a new mood episode. [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-limit|Yatham 2023]] [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-primary|Yatham 2023]] Depressive recurrence was less frequent with continuation (17% against 40%) and mania or hypomania more frequent (12% against 6%, not significant). [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-polarity|Yatham 2023]] The trial stopped early, so it is underpowered and its secondary signals point in opposite directions. [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-limit|Yatham 2023]] [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-caution-power|Appraisal: Yatham 2023]] In Swedish registers, antidepressant-only periods went with more depression- and mania-related hospitalisation, a comparison biased against any medicine started when symptoms return. [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-classes|Ermis 2025]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-mania|Ermis 2025]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-limit|Ermis 2025]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-caution-register|Appraisal: Ermis 2025]] In a small randomised discontinuation trial, rapid-cycling patients who stayed on an antidepressant had about three times as many episodes a year as continuing patients without rapid cycling, a difference absent among those who stopped. [[P26142612 El-Mallakh 2015 Antidepressants and rapid cycling in STEP-BD#^p26142612-episodes|El-Mallakh 2015]] [[P26142612 El-Mallakh 2015 Antidepressants and rapid cycling in STEP-BD#^p26142612-discontinue|El-Mallakh 2015]] [[P26142612 El-Mallakh 2015 Antidepressants and rapid cycling in STEP-BD#^p26142612-caution-subgroup|Appraisal: El-Mallakh 2015]]

**What guidelines conclude.** The ISBD task force could not broadly endorse antidepressants, accepted that some individuals may benefit, and for bipolar I limited them to use alongside mood-stabilising medication. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-limit|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] The 2018 CANMAT and ISBD guidelines weigh switch risk when ranking treatments, list no antidepressant among first-line options for bipolar I depression, and name antidepressants as an exception to carrying acute treatment into maintenance. [[P29536616 Yatham 2018 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar guidelines#^p29536616-lines|Yatham 2018]] [[P29536616 Yatham 2018 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar guidelines#^p29536616-depression|Yatham 2018]] [[P29536616 Yatham 2018 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar guidelines#^p29536616-maintenance|Yatham 2018]] Guidelines diverge with method, timing and health policy over few high-quality trials, and their rankings mix judgements about harm with efficacy data. [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-sources|Park 2024]] [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-context|Park 2024]] [[P29536616 Yatham 2018 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar guidelines#^p29536616-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Yatham 2018]]

**The bipolar II question.** The ISBD task force judged antidepressant-associated mood elevations more frequent and severe in bipolar I than in bipolar II, so its add-on-only advice covers bipolar I. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] In two small double-blind trials in bipolar II depression, venlafaxine or sertraline alone did not differ significantly from lithium in response or switching, though 223 patients cannot exclude a meaningful switching difference. [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-response|Park 2022]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-switch|Park 2022]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-caution-power|Appraisal: Park 2022]] People with bipolar II receive antidepressants more often than people with bipolar I, and a narrative review links misdiagnosed bipolar II to antidepressant overuse. [[P37437249 Hernandorena 2023 Bipolar II versus bipolar I#^p37437249-more|Hernandorena 2023]] [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]] Treatment is also one way it comes to light: about 6.7% of patients diagnosed with major depression became hypomanic or manic on antidepressants and were rediagnosed, about three-quarters with bipolar II, though drug and course cannot be separated. [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-rate|Tondo 2024]] [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-caution-attribution|Appraisal: Tondo 2024]] The maintenance trial enrolled bipolar I only. [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-limit|Yatham 2023]]

**Reading rule.** For any antidepressant claim in bipolar disorder, name the bipolar type, the phase, whether the drug was given alone or as an add-on, how switching was defined and over what period, and whether exposure was randomised. [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-limit|Oliva 2025]] [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-caution-class|Appraisal: Yildiz 2023]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-caution-register|Appraisal: Ermis 2025]]

## Evidence and status

Acute evidence rests on two network meta-analyses of randomised trials: one across all treatments for bipolar depression, with manic switch as a co-primary outcome, and one of antidepressants alone, rated low confidence. [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-limit|Yildiz 2023]] [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-regimen|Oliva 2025]] Maintenance evidence is one trial stopped early, one register study and one small subgroup analysis. [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-limit|Yatham 2023]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-limit|Ermis 2025]] [[P26142612 El-Mallakh 2015 Antidepressants and rapid cycling in STEP-BD#^p26142612-limit|El-Mallakh 2015]] Guidance leans on consensus and clinical experience. [[P29536616 Yatham 2018 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar guidelines#^p29536616-limit|Yatham 2018]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Pacchiarotti 2013]] Mixed features and bipolar II have the least evidence: no antidepressant-alone trial in the first and two small monotherapy trials in the second. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-limit|Xiao 2025]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-limit|Park 2022]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether use over months raises switch or cycling risk; acute trials cannot show it and the one maintenance trial was too small. [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-caution-acute|Appraisal: Oliva 2025]] [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-caution-power|Appraisal: Yatham 2023]]
- Whether switch risk is truly lower in bipolar II, as consensus suggests. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-caution-power|Appraisal: Park 2022]]
- Whether antidepressants alone help or harm in depression with mixed features. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antidepressants|Xiao 2025]]
- Whether classes differ in switch risk as consensus proposes, when trials show a consistent signal only for venlafaxine. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-switch|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-venlafaxine|Oliva 2025]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review and network meta-analysis (EClinicalMedicine).** No antidepressant raised switching significantly above placebo in acute trials, with low confidence overall. [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-placebo|Oliva 2025]] [[P40823496 Oliva 2025 Switch to mania after antidepressants#^p40823496-caution-acute|Appraisal: Oliva 2025]]
- **2025 · Systematic review (Bipolar Disorders).** Antidepressants alone remain untested in depression with mixed features. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antidepressants|Xiao 2025]]
- **2025 · Within-individual register cohort (The Lancet Psychiatry).** Antidepressant-only periods went with more mania-related admissions, open to confounding by why treatment starts. [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-mania|Ermis 2025]] [[P39922213 Ermis 2025 Real-world maintenance treatment of bipolar depression#^p39922213-caution-register|Appraisal: Ermis 2025]]
- **2023 · Randomised controlled trial (New England Journal of Medicine).** A year's continuation in bipolar I did not significantly reduce new mood episodes; depressive recurrences fell while mania or hypomania rose non-significantly. [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-primary|Yatham 2023]] [[P37530824 Yatham 2023 Duration of adjunctive antidepressant maintenance#^p37530824-polarity|Yatham 2023]]
- **2023 · Systematic review and network meta-analysis (The Lancet Psychiatry).** Antidepressants as a class appeared efficacious but carried more manic switch than antipsychotics. [[P37595997 Yildiz 2023 Drug treatment for bipolar depression#^p37595997-antidepressants|Yildiz 2023]]
