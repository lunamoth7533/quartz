# Hypomania

## Definition

Hypomania is an elevated or irritable mood state with increased activity that lasts at least four days and changes behaviour without the marked impairment or need for hospital care that would make it mania. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-criteria|Dailey 2023]] [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-features|Dailey 2023]] Together with a major depressive episode it defines bipolar II disorder, whose hypomania is less severe than the mania of bipolar I. [[P33278937 McIntyre 2020 Bipolar disorders seminar#^p33278937-definitions|McIntyre 2020]] [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-types|NIMH]] It is often missed, so bipolar disorder can be treated for years as depression alone. [[P30933689 Daveney 2019 Unrecognised bipolar disorder in primary care#^p30933689-prevalence|Daveney 2019]] [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]]

## How it works

**The line with mania.** In the DSM thresholds, hypomania needs four days and no major impairment, while mania needs a week or a hospital admission. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-criteria|Dailey 2023]] DSM-5 and the ICD-11 draft both require increased activity as well as mood change, which tightened the threshold for both states. [[P25450512 de Dios 2014 Bipolar disorders in DSM-5 and ICD-11#^p25450512-activity|de Dios 2014]] ICD-11's revised requirements for manic and hypomanic episodes were much debated, and whether they balance sensitivity and specificity is still unclear. [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-episodes|Chakrabarti 2022]] A duration rule is a convention of each system, not a natural boundary, so moving it moves people across the line without changing them. [[P29630220 Dailey 2023 Mania reference chapter#^p29630220-caution-dsm|Appraisal: Dailey 2023]] [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-caution-utility|Appraisal: Chakrabarti 2022]]

**Why it goes unrecognised.** Across ten primary-care studies, about 17% of people treated for depression had bipolar disorder that had not been recognised. [[P30933689 Daveney 2019 Unrecognised bipolar disorder in primary care#^p30933689-prevalence|Daveney 2019]] People with bipolar II typically have symptoms for more than a decade before the correct diagnosis. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]] In a clinic cohort, about 7% of patients first diagnosed with major depression became hypomanic or manic during antidepressant treatment and were rediagnosed, most often with bipolar II. [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-rate|Tondo 2024]] Self-report is part of the problem: in a prospective cohort, daily self-rated mood fell before depressive episodes but did not change before hypomanic or manic ones. [[P38284217 Ludwig 2024 Autocorrelation and upcoming affective episodes#^p38284217-selfreport|Ludwig 2024]] Passive sensing records more, and more variable, activity and shorter sleep in elevated states, but its models have rarely been tested on new people. [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-patterns|Kutcher 2026]] [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-caution-validation|Appraisal: Kutcher 2026]]

**What screening questionnaires can do.** In 53 accuracy studies against structured interviews, the Hypomania Checklist (HCL-32) had summary sensitivity of 81% and specificity of 67% in psychiatric services, the Mood Disorder Questionnaire (MDQ) 66% and 79%, and the HCL-32 detected bipolar II more accurately than the MDQ. [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-accuracy|Carvalho 2015]] [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-type2|Carvalho 2015]] In primary care and community samples, the MDQ at a cut-off of 7 missed most cases but rarely flagged people without the disorder. [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-primary|Carvalho 2015]] With specificity this low in clinics, a positive screen should open an interview about past hypomania rather than settle the diagnosis. [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-caution-screen|Appraisal: Carvalho 2015]] In primary-care studies, questionnaire and interview methods gave estimates of unrecognised bipolar disorder that did not differ significantly. [[P30933689 Daveney 2019 Unrecognised bipolar disorder in primary care#^p30933689-method|Daveney 2019]]

**Below the threshold.** Hypomanic symptoms also occur below full criteria: in community surveys, subthreshold bipolar disorder was more common over a lifetime than bipolar II (1.4% against 0.4%). [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-prevalence|Merikangas 2011]] Symptom severity and suicidal behaviour rose from subthreshold states to bipolar I, while role impairment was similar across them. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-gradient|Merikangas 2011]]

**Reading rule.** Ask how hypomania was established - a structured interview about past episodes, a screening questionnaire or current self-rating - and which duration rule applied; each yields a different count of who has bipolar II. [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-caution-screen|Appraisal: Carvalho 2015]] [[P38284217 Ludwig 2024 Autocorrelation and upcoming affective episodes#^p38284217-selfreport|Ludwig 2024]]

## Evidence and status

Screening accuracy rests on a meta-analysis of 53 studies, most from mental health settings and several at high risk of bias. [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-limit|Carvalho 2015]] The primary-care estimate of unrecognised bipolar disorder rests on ten cross-sectional studies. [[P30933689 Daveney 2019 Unrecognised bipolar disorder in primary care#^p30933689-limit|Daveney 2019]] [[P30933689 Daveney 2019 Unrecognised bipolar disorder in primary care#^p30933689-caution-case|Appraisal: Daveney 2019]] The self-report finding comes from a small exploratory cohort, and the ICD-11 episode requirements remain under debate. [[P38284217 Ludwig 2024 Autocorrelation and upcoming affective episodes#^p38284217-caution-signal|Appraisal: Ludwig 2024]] [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-episodes|Chakrabarti 2022]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether four days is the right minimum; the ICD-11 revision has not settled the trade-off between catching cases and over-diagnosis. [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-episodes|Chakrabarti 2022]]
- How screening performs in primary care, where few accuracy studies were done. [[P25451435 Carvalho 2015 Screening for bipolar spectrum disorders#^p25451435-limit|Carvalho 2015]]
- Whether passive sensing can detect approaching hypomania in individuals rather than in group averages. [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-gaps|Kutcher 2026]]

## Recent research

- **2026 · Systematic review (JMIR Mental Health).** Across 57 passive-sensing studies, mania and hypomania went with more, and more variable, activity and shorter or advanced sleep, but external validation was usually absent. [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-patterns|Kutcher 2026]] [[P42594348 Kutcher 2026 Passive digital markers in bipolar disorder#^p42594348-gaps|Kutcher 2026]]
- **2024 · Intensive longitudinal cohort (Psychological Medicine).** Self-rated mood did not change before hypomanic or manic episodes, one reason self-report under-detects elevated states. [[P38284217 Ludwig 2024 Autocorrelation and upcoming affective episodes#^p38284217-selfreport|Ludwig 2024]] [[P38284217 Ludwig 2024 Autocorrelation and upcoming affective episodes#^p38284217-caution-signal|Appraisal: Ludwig 2024]]
- **2024 · Clinical cohort comparison (Acta Psychiatrica Scandinavica).** About 7% of patients first diagnosed with major depression were rediagnosed after hypomania or mania emerged on antidepressants, mostly with bipolar II. [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-rate|Tondo 2024]] [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-caution-attribution|Appraisal: Tondo 2024]]
- **2023 · Narrative review (Focus).** Bipolar II typically goes more than ten years before correct diagnosis. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]] [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-caution-narrative|Appraisal: Swartz 2023]]
