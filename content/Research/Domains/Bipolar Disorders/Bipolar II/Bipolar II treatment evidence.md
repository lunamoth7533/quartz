# Bipolar II treatment evidence

## Definition

Bipolar II treatment evidence means trials and guidance tested in people with bipolar II specifically, as distinct from results carried over from bipolar I or from mixed bipolar samples. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-extrapolation|Swartz 2023]] That evidence is small: a 2025 review found ten randomised trials of lithium or lamotrigine in 645 patients with bipolar II, all graded very low certainty. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-certainty|Fredskild 2025]] This note summarises the structure of the research and carries no prescribing, dose or individual guidance. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-caution-individual|Appraisal: Keramatian 2023]]

## How it works

**Why the evidence is thin.** Bipolar II is under-recognised and often misdiagnosed, treatment studies specific to it are few, and bipolar I results are often applied to bipolar II without direct evidence. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-delay|Swartz 2023]] [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-extrapolation|Swartz 2023]] Guidelines translate evidence into phase-specific, ranked recommendations that weigh efficacy, safety and tolerability, and guidelines from different sources diverge because of method, timing, study selection and health policy, with few high-quality trials underneath. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-phases|Keramatian 2023]] [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-sources|Park 2024]] [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-context|Park 2024]] A ranking resting on bipolar I or pooled samples should therefore be read as extrapolation when it is applied to bipolar II. [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Park 2024]]

**Mood stabilisers.** Whether lithium lowers the risk of new mood episodes in bipolar II compared with placebo was very uncertain; lamotrigine-placebo trials could not be pooled; and lithium against lamotrigine for depressive severity was also very uncertain. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-lithium|Fredskild 2025]] [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-lamotrigine|Fredskild 2025]] Very low certainty means these trials can show neither benefit nor its absence. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-caution-absence|Appraisal: Fredskild 2025]] In clinical samples, people with bipolar II receive lithium or antipsychotics less often, and antidepressants more often, than people with bipolar I. [[P37437249 Hernandorena 2023 Bipolar II versus bipolar I#^p37437249-less|Hernandorena 2023]] [[P37437249 Hernandorena 2023 Bipolar II versus bipolar I#^p37437249-more|Hernandorena 2023]]

**The antidepressant question.** Bipolar II is where antidepressant use is most contested. An international consensus judged antidepressant-associated mood elevations more frequent and severe in bipolar I than in bipolar II, and confined its add-on-only advice to bipolar I. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] In two small double-blind trials in acute bipolar II depression, venlafaxine or sertraline alone did not differ significantly from lithium in response or in switching, and fewer patients stopped them for side effects. [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-response|Park 2022]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-switch|Park 2022]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-tolerability|Park 2022]] Two trials and 223 patients cannot rule out a meaningful difference in switching. [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-caution-power|Appraisal: Park 2022]] The bipolar II review literature names antidepressant response as one point where the types differ. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-similar|Swartz 2023]] Rapid cycling, more common in bipolar II, is the setting in which continuing an antidepressant was linked to more episodes in a randomised comparison. [[P37437249 Hernandorena 2023 Bipolar II versus bipolar I#^p37437249-more|Hernandorena 2023]] [[P26142612 El-Mallakh 2015 Antidepressants and rapid cycling in STEP-BD#^p26142612-episodes|El-Mallakh 2015]]

**Mixed features.** In depressive episodes with mixed features, several atypical antipsychotics reduced symptoms, and lumateperone was the only drug tested with mixed-features efficacy as a primary outcome across bipolar I, bipolar II and major depression; antidepressants alone have not been adequately tested. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antipsychotics|Xiao 2025]] [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antidepressants|Xiao 2025]]

**Psychological treatment.** In a randomised trial of group CBT against supportive group therapy for relapse prevention, bipolar II predicted more new episodes, especially in the CBT arm, and the trial had no usual-care comparison. [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-relapse|Hautzinger 2024]] [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-caution-comparator|Appraisal: Hautzinger 2024]]

**Reading rule.** For any bipolar II treatment claim, check whether the trial enrolled bipolar II separately, which phase it tested, the comparator and the certainty grade; a recommendation extrapolated from bipolar I should say so. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-extrapolation|Swartz 2023]] [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-certainty|Fredskild 2025]]

## Evidence and status

Mood-stabiliser evidence in bipolar II is of very low certainty; antidepressant monotherapy evidence comes from two small trials; and guidance leans on extrapolation from bipolar I and on consensus. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-certainty|Fredskild 2025]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-limit|Park 2022]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-limit|Pacchiarotti 2013]] The review behind the extrapolation concern is narrative, and the consensus predates later trial syntheses. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-caution-narrative|Appraisal: Swartz 2023]] [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-caution-consensus|Appraisal: Pacchiarotti 2013]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether switch risk on antidepressants is lower in bipolar II, as consensus suggests, once larger trials are run. [[P24030475 Pacchiarotti 2013 ISBD antidepressants in bipolar disorders#^p24030475-bipolar-i|Pacchiarotti 2013]] [[P35721812 Park 2022 Antidepressant monotherapy in bipolar II depression#^p35721812-caution-power|Appraisal: Park 2022]]
- Whether lithium or lamotrigine prevents recurrence in bipolar II, which the current trials cannot show. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-lithium|Fredskild 2025]]
- How far bipolar I evidence transfers to bipolar II. [[P38694998 Swartz 2023 Bipolar II understudied and underdiagnosed#^p38694998-extrapolation|Swartz 2023]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Journal of Affective Disorders).** Ten randomised trials of lithium or lamotrigine in bipolar II gave very low certainty for every outcome. [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-certainty|Fredskild 2025]] [[P40288449 Fredskild 2025 Lithium and lamotrigine in bipolar II#^p40288449-caution-absence|Appraisal: Fredskild 2025]]
- **2025 · Systematic review (Bipolar Disorders).** In mixed-features depression, antipsychotic evidence is mostly post hoc and antidepressants alone are untested. [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antipsychotics|Xiao 2025]] [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-antidepressants|Xiao 2025]] [[P40808264 Xiao 2025 Treating depression with mixed features#^p40808264-caution-posthoc|Appraisal: Xiao 2025]]
- **2024 · Randomised trial (JAMA Psychiatry).** Bipolar II predicted relapse in group psychotherapy for relapse prevention. [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-relapse|Hautzinger 2024]]
- **2024 · Comparative review of guidelines (BJPsych Open).** Differences between guidelines trace to method and context over a thin base of high-quality trials. [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-sources|Park 2024]] [[P38586960 Park 2024 International bipolar guideline comparison#^p38586960-context|Park 2024]]
