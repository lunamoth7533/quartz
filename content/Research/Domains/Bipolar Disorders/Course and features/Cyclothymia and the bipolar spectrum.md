# Cyclothymia and the bipolar spectrum

## Definition

Cyclothymic disorder is defined by repeated hypomanic and depressive symptoms too mild or too brief to count as hypomanic or depressive episodes. [[F16 NIMH Bipolar disorder#^f16-types|NIMH]] The bipolar spectrum places bipolar I, bipolar II and such subthreshold presentations on one continuum; in surveys of 61,392 adults in 11 countries, about 2.4% fell somewhere on it over their lifetime. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-prevalence|Merikangas 2011]] Whether cyclothymia is a disorder, a temperament or a risk state is still argued. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-overlap|Van Meter 2012]] [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-temperament|Perugi 2015]]

## How it works

**A neglected category.** Cyclothymic disorder has been in DSM since 1980 but is rarely diagnosed and little studied, although evidence suggests it may be the most common bipolar form. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-neglect|Van Meter 2012]] Its overlap in wording and symptoms with temperament and personality disorders leaves its standing as a mood disorder ambiguous, and ICD-11's guidance on cyclothymic disorder and bipolar subtypes still leaves uncertainties. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-overlap|Van Meter 2012]] [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-subtypes|Chakrabarti 2022]]

**What population data show.** Lifetime prevalence in the World Mental Health surveys was 0.6% for bipolar I, 0.4% for bipolar II and 1.4% for subthreshold bipolar disorder. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-prevalence|Merikangas 2011]] Symptom severity and suicidal behaviour rose step by step from subthreshold disorder to bipolar I, while role impairment was similar across subtypes, and the authors read these consistent gradients as support for the spectrum concept. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-gradient|Merikangas 2011]] [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-validity|Merikangas 2011]] Three-quarters of people on the spectrum had another disorder, most often anxiety, and fewer than half had received mental health treatment. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-comorbidity|Merikangas 2011]] Lay-administered interviews can miscount past hypomania, and a severity gradient across categories drawn by severity is partly built in. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-caution-lay|Appraisal: Merikangas 2011]]

**Distinct or milder?** One clinical school defines cyclothymia as an exaggerated cyclothymic temperament - early-onset mood instability with marked reactivity, interpersonal and separation sensitivity, mixed features in depression, several comorbidities and a raised risk of impulsive and suicidal acts - and argues that it is a distinct form of bipolarity rather than a softer one. [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-temperament|Perugi 2015]] [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-distinct|Perugi 2015]] The same authors report that 20-50% of people seeking help for mood, anxiety, impulsive or addictive problems in their settings prove cyclothymic, more under broader rules. [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-prevalence|Perugi 2015]] Those figures come from specialist settings and a widened definition, and the claim that antidepressant exposure worsens the course rests on clinical observation. [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-caution-advocacy|Appraisal: Perugi 2015]]

**A developmental link.** Subthreshold bipolar disorder is more common in young people than was thought and runs anywhere from remission to escalation, which is why cyclothymic disorder is proposed as a possible diathesis for major mood disorder. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-youth|Van Meter 2012]] In a clinic cohort, cyclothymic temperament was among the predictors of a diagnostic change from major depression to bipolar disorder during antidepressant treatment. [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-predictors|Tondo 2024]] [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-caution-attribution|Appraisal: Tondo 2024]]

**Instability within the spectrum.** Within established bipolar disorder, greater affect intensity and affective lability went with markers of more severe illness, including rapid cycling, suicide attempts and anxiety disorders. [[P40715078 Etain 2025 Polygenic scores and bipolar dimensions#^p40715078-lability|Etain 2025]] Because the analysis was cross-sectional, it shows co-variation rather than a pathway. [[P40715078 Etain 2025 Polygenic scores and bipolar dimensions#^p40715078-caution-mediation|Appraisal: Etain 2025]]

**What widening the spectrum changes.** DSM-5 brought subthreshold states within the bipolar spectrum into its framework, alongside dimensional symptom ratings. [[P25450512 de Dios 2014 Bipolar disorders in DSM-5 and ICD-11#^p25450512-dsm5|de Dios 2014]] A broader definition raises prevalence and draws in people currently described through anxiety, impulsive or personality problems, which changes who enters research samples. [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-prevalence|Perugi 2015]] [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-overlap|Van Meter 2012]]

**Reading rule.** Ask which definition of cyclothymia or subthreshold bipolar disorder a study used and where its sample came from: estimates run from 1.4% for subthreshold disorder in community surveys to as much as half of some specialist clinics' attenders, depending on both. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-prevalence|Merikangas 2011]] [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-prevalence|Perugi 2015]] [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-caution-advocacy|Appraisal: Perugi 2015]]

## Evidence and status

Community prevalence and the severity gradient rest on large cross-sectional surveys that used a fully structured lay interview and DSM-IV criteria. [[P21383262 Merikangas 2011 Bipolar spectrum in World Mental Health surveys#^p21383262-limit|Merikangas 2011]] Evidence specific to cyclothymic disorder is mostly narrative review and clinical experience, one side of it written as an expert position. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-limit|Van Meter 2012]] [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-limit|Perugi 2015]] The critical review predates DSM-5 and ICD-11. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-caution-age|Appraisal: Van Meter 2012]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether cyclothymia is best treated as a temperament, a disorder or a risk state for major mood disorder. [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-overlap|Van Meter 2012]] [[P22459786 Van Meter 2012 Cyclothymic disorder critical review#^p22459786-youth|Van Meter 2012]]
- How ICD-11's guidance on cyclothymic disorder performs in practice. [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-subtypes|Chakrabarti 2022]]
- Whether antidepressant exposure worsens cyclothymia, which has not been tested in a controlled comparison. [[P26005206 Perugi 2015 Cyclothymia as misconceived affective disorder#^p26005206-caution-advocacy|Appraisal: Perugi 2015]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Cross-sectional cohort analysis (Translational Psychiatry).** Affective lability tracked rapid cycling, suicide attempts and anxiety disorders in 852 people with bipolar disorder. [[P40715078 Etain 2025 Polygenic scores and bipolar dimensions#^p40715078-lability|Etain 2025]] [[P40715078 Etain 2025 Polygenic scores and bipolar dimensions#^p40715078-caution-mediation|Appraisal: Etain 2025]]
- **2024 · Clinical cohort comparison (Acta Psychiatrica Scandinavica).** Cyclothymic temperament independently predicted diagnostic change from major depression to bipolar disorder. [[P38922810 Tondo 2024 Diagnostic change from depression to bipolar disorder#^p38922810-predictors|Tondo 2024]]
- **2022 · Narrative review (World Journal of Psychiatry).** ICD-11's guidance on bipolar subtypes and cyclothymic disorder still leaves uncertainties. [[P36579354 Chakrabarti 2022 Bipolar disorder in ICD-11#^p36579354-subtypes|Chakrabarti 2022]]
