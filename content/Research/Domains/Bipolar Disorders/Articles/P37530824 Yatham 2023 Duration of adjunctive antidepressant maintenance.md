# Duration of Adjunctive Antidepressant Maintenance in Bipolar I Depression.

**Authors.** Yatham LN, Arumugham SS, Kesavan M, Ramachandran K, Murthy NS, Saraf G, et al. (24 authors and the BEAM-BD Trial Group) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37530824/)

**Published.** 2023 in The New England journal of medicine.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1056/NEJMoa2300184](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2300184) · PMID [37530824](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37530824/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Over the year, 31% of the continuation group and 46% of the group moved to placebo at 8 weeks had a new mood episode; the difference in time to an episode was not significant (hazard ratio 0.68, interval including no effect).
^p37530824-primary

Depressive recurrence was less common with continuation (17% against 40%, hazard ratio 0.43), while mania or hypomania was more common (12% against 6%), a difference whose wide interval included no effect (hazard ratio 2.28).
^p37530824-polarity

Adverse events were about as frequent in both groups.
^p37530824-safety

## Scope as reported

Multisite double-blind trial of adjunctive escitalopram or bupropion XL in bipolar I only: after an open-label phase (209 entrants, 150 remitted) plus 27 direct enrolments, patients were randomised 1:1, and the composite primary outcome counted any mood episode, added treatment or admission for mood symptoms, and suicide attempts or deaths. The trial was halted before reaching its planned sample because enrolment was slow and funds ran short.
^p37530824-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A trial stopped short of its target is underpowered, so a non-significant primary result neither shows nor excludes a modest benefit; the fewer depressive recurrences and the extra manic episodes are secondary outcomes pointing in opposite directions, and they describe two drugs given as add-ons in bipolar I only.
^p37530824-caution-power
