## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Bipolar I is defined by a syndromal manic episode; bipolar II by a syndromal hypomanic episode plus a major depressive episode.
^p33278937-definitions

Mania defines bipolar I, yet depressive episodes and symptoms fill more of the long-term course and a disproportionate share of illness and deaths.
^p33278937-course

Life expectancy falls by roughly 10-20 years, mainly through cardiovascular disease and suicide.
^p33278937-mortality

Bipolar I is genetically closer to schizophrenia, bipolar II closer to major depressive disorder.
^p33278937-genetics

Antipsychotics, divalproex and carbamazepine treat acute mania and lithium is the reference mood stabiliser; antidepressants are widely used on weak evidence and can destabilise mood, notably in maintenance.
^p33278937-treatment
