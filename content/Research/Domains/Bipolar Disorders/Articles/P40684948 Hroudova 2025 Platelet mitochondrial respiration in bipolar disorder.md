# Mitochondrial respiration in patients with bipolar disorder: Can mitochondrial respiratory control ratios serve as state and/or trait markers?

**Authors.** Hroudová J, Kališová L, Kitzlerová E, Zvěřová M, Lambertová A, Fišar Z · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40684948/)

**Published.** 2025 in Journal of affective disorders.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/j.jad.2025.119948](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2025.119948) · PMID [40684948](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40684948/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

In remission, intact platelets from patients showed lower complex II-linked and reserve respiration than controls, and permeabilised platelets showed lower physiological respiration and electron-transfer capacity.
^p40684948-remission

Changes between episode and remission were more pronounced after treatment of depression than of mania, and the authors propose some respiratory ratios as candidate trait markers and others as state markers.
^p40684948-markers

## Scope as reported

High-resolution respirometry of intact and permeabilised blood platelets, with clinical assessment by diagnostic scales in episode and after remission under treatment.
^p40684948-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Platelet respiration is a peripheral proxy measured under medication, so the trait and state labels are proposals needing replication and cannot stand in for brain energy metabolism.
^p40684948-caution-peripheral
