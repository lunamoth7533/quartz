# Adjuvant Psychotherapies to Prevent Relapse in Bipolar Disorder: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Added to usual psychiatric care, a structured, skill-oriented CBT group (with social rhythm and mindfulness elements) and a supportive, emotion-focused group were equally effective at preventing a new affective episode over 18 months.
^p38837133-equivalence

Relapse was common in both arms (49% and 46%); bipolar II, comorbidity and attending all sessions predicted more new episodes, and patients with bipolar II relapsed most, especially in the CBT arm.
^p38837133-relapse

## Scope as reported

Observer-blind randomised trial at 9 outpatient units: 24 hours of group therapy in four full-day sessions over 5 months, follow-up to 18 months, and recurrence judged by blinded structured interview.
^p38837133-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

With no usual-care-only arm, equal relapse rates show only that the two active formats did not differ; they cannot show whether either beats care as usual.
