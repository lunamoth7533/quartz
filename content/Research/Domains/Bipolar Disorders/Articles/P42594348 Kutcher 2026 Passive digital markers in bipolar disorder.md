# Digital Markers for Passive Remote Monitoring of Bipolar Disorder: Systematic Review.

**Authors.** Kutcher TP, Chakraborty I, Kostick-Quenet K, Sano A, Moukaddam N, Herron JA, Goodman WK, Sheth SA, Sabharwal A, Provenza NR · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42594348/)

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.2196/94819](https://doi.org/10.2196/94819) · PMID [42594348](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42594348/) · PMCID [PMC13472530](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13472530/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across 57 studies, depression went with reduced mobility and social contact, later or more variable sleep and less daytime light; mania and hypomania went with more and more variable activity, shorter or advanced sleep and more communication.
^p42594348-patterns

Circadian features from sleep and activity repeatedly aided prediction, and several machine-learning studies reported strong classification.
^p42594348-circadian

External validation was usually absent, samples small, monitoring windows short relative to episodes, clinical labels sparse or misaligned, and missing data rarely modelled.
^p42594348-gaps

The authors call for longer within-person monitoring, label timing matched to sensing, preregistration, external validation and minimal data collection to protect privacy.
^p42594348-priorities

## Scope as reported

PRISMA-guided review with two independent reviewers; studies relying only on active measures such as ecological momentary assessment were excluded, 60% of included studies enrolled 50 or fewer participants and 81% monitored for a year or less.
^p42594348-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

High accuracy without external validation is an in-sample estimate; until models are tested on new people and new periods, passive monitoring describes group patterns rather than a validated personal alarm.
^p42594348-caution-validation
