## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Differences in the medicines each guideline recommends trace to how it was built - expert consensus or meta-analysis - and to when its analyses were done and which studies were selected.
^p38586960-sources

Population differences in drug-metabolising enzymes and national health policies also shape recommendations, and the authors identify the scarcity of high-quality research as the main obstacle to consistent conclusions.
^p38586960-context

## Scope as reported

A comparative review of guideline content from five sources updated after 2014; it analyses recommendations rather than patient outcomes.
^p38586960-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Divergent rankings for the same phase show that a guideline's order partly reflects its method, date and setting, so a ranking should be read with its evidence grades rather than as a fixed fact.
