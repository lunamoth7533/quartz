**Authors.** Ventriglio A, Ricci F, Giallanella D, Gaggiano C, Torales J, Castaldelli-Maia JM, Smith A, Liebrenz M, Caycho-Rodríguez T, Bellomo A · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40411321/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across the international guidelines reviewed, psychosocial interventions are endorsed as supportive treatments delivered alongside pharmacological or psychotherapeutic care, with recommendations examined across illness phases.
^p40411321-endorse

The authors conclude that more research is needed to confirm the effect of psychosocial interventions on long-term outcomes.
^p40411321-gap

## Scope as reported

A review of recommendation content on efficacy and implementation across nine guideline sources; it does not pool trial outcomes.
^p40411321-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Guideline endorsements rest on overlapping trial evidence and committee judgement, so agreement across guidelines is not independent confirmation of long-term benefit.
^p40411321-caution-endorsement
