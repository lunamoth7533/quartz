## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Despite many documented effects on cellular pathways, the review states that the precise mechanism by which lithium stabilises mood remains unresolved.
^p38697859-elusive

It reports strengthened support for GSK3 inhibition, for changes in homeostatic synaptic plasticity and for regulation of microRNA expression as key candidate mechanisms, and links them to efforts to model lithium response.
^p38697859-candidates

## Scope as reported

A narrative review drawing on cellular and animal models of psychiatric and neurodegenerative disorders plus translational human work; it also covers toxicology and repurposing beyond bipolar disorder.
^p38697859-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The mechanistic support comes largely from cellular and animal models, so 'strengthened' means more convergent laboratory evidence, not a demonstrated chain from those actions to fewer episodes in people.
^p38697859-caution-models
