## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The revised requirements for manic and hypomanic episodes were much debated, and it is still unclear whether they strike the right balance between sensitivity and specificity.
^p36579354-episodes

Unlike DSM-5, ICD-11 kept a mixed-episode category; the review judges it imperfect but more inclusive than the DSM-5 approach.
^p36579354-mixed

ICD-11's depressive-episode definition changed substantially with little empirical support, and the review rates the reliability and utility of bipolar depression as relatively low.
^p36579354-depression

Guidance on bipolar subtypes and cyclothymic disorder leaves uncertainties, while early findings on the reliability and clinical utility of bipolar disorder are promising.
^p36579354-subtypes

## Scope as reported

Single-author review of the ICD-11 chapter, weighing each change against its scientific basis and clinical utility; no systematic search is described.
^p36579354-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Reliability and utility studies show that clinicians can apply the categories consistently; they do not show that the episode thresholds mark natural boundaries, and a threshold change moves people across category lines without changing them.
^p36579354-caution-utility
