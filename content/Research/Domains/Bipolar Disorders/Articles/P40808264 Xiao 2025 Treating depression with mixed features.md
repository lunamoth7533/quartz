# The Efficacy of Pharmacological Interventions in the Treatment of Major Depressive Disorder and Bipolar Depression With Mixed Features: A Systematic Review.

**Authors.** Xiao N, Yin L, Lee S, Teopiz KM, Wong S, Le GH, et al. (13 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40808264/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Six atypical antipsychotics reduced depressive symptoms in episodes with mixed features, but much of this evidence came from post hoc analyses; only lumateperone was tested with mixed-features efficacy as the prespecified primary outcome across bipolar I, bipolar II and major depression.
^p40808264-antipsychotics

Hypomanic symptoms that co-occurred usually improved, with no difference from placebo in worsening hypomania or in switching.
^p40808264-switch

No adequate trials have tested antidepressants alone in mixed-features depression, and lithium or valproate have minimal evidence there.
^p40808264-antidepressants

The authors call for a consistent definition of mixed presentations.
^p40808264-definition

## Scope as reported

PubMed and OVID searched to July 2024; risk of bias assessed with the Cochrane RoB2 tool; all included trials were short-term acute studies.
^p40808264-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Post hoc subgroups of trials designed for other questions can show that a drug does not fail in mixed features, but not that it was tested fairly against alternatives; the lack of antidepressant trials is an evidence gap rather than evidence of harm.
^p40808264-caution-posthoc
