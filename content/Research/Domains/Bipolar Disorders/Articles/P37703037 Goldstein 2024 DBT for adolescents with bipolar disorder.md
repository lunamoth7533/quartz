# Dialectical Behavior Therapy for Adolescents With Bipolar Disorder: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

**Authors.** Goldstein TR, Merranko J, Rode N, Sylvester R, Hotkowski N, Fersch-Podrat R, et al. (11 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37703037/)

**Published.** 2024 in JAMA psychiatry.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2023.3399](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2023.3399) · PMID [37703037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37703037/) · PMCID [PMC10500432](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10500432/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Over one year, adolescents receiving dialectical behavior therapy reported significantly fewer suicide attempts than those in standard-of-care psychotherapy, on two different interview measures.
^p37703037-suicide

Mood symptoms and episodes improved similarly in both arms; the fall in attempts was larger in those with a prior attempt and was partly mediated by reduced emotion dysregulation.
^p37703037-mood

## Scope as reported

Single-site trial with quarterly independent assessments over a mean of 47 weeks; 85% of participants were female and all received medication management.
^p37703037-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

A single-site trial in a mostly female bipolar-spectrum sample tests one outcome, suicide attempts; it does not show that the therapy changes the mood course, which improved equally in both arms.
^p37703037-caution-scope
