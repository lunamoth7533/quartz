## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Cyclothymic disorder has been in DSM since 1980 yet is rarely diagnosed and little researched, although evidence suggests it may be the most common form of bipolar disorder.
^p22459786-neglect

Because its vocabulary and its symptoms overlap with those used for temperament and for personality disorders, its standing as a mood disorder is ambiguous.
^p22459786-overlap

Subthreshold bipolar disorder appears more common among young people than was thought and follows courses ranging from remission to escalation; the authors propose cyclothymic disorder as a possible diathesis for major mood disorders and a bridge to dimensional models of mood.
^p22459786-youth
