## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Mania lasts at least a week and severely disrupts functioning; hypomania needs four days without major impairment, and needing hospital care makes an episode manic whatever its length.
^p29630220-criteria

Hallmarks include elevated or irritable mood, less need for sleep, rapid speech, racing thoughts, distractibility and more goal-directed activity, often with poor insight.
^p29630220-features

Psychotic features, often grandiose or persecutory delusions, are common.
^p29630220-psychosis

Rapid cycling means four or more full episodes in 12 months, separated by two months of remission or a switch of polarity, and responds less well to medication.
^p29630220-rapid

Activation caused by substances or medical conditions must be excluded.
^p29630220-exclusion
