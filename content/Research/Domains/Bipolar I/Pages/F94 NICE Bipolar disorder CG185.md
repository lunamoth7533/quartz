# Bipolar disorder: assessment and management (CG185)

**Publisher.** National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), UK

**Source.** <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg185/chapter/recommendations>

**Verification.** official page opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F94.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The guideline sets out assessment expectations for people with suspected or diagnosed bipolar disorder, including risk assessment and attention to coexisting conditions.
^f94-assessment

It recommends long-term pharmacological management and structured psychological approaches, with explicit cautions around particular medicines in pregnancy and for younger people.
^f94-longterm

Recommendations are graded and tied to the evidence review the committee used, which is why the guideline text distinguishes stronger and weaker recommendations.
^f94-framing

## Scope and limitations

UK national guidance for health services. It reflects the evidence base and service context at publication and update, and does not transfer automatically to other systems.
^f94-limit
