# The social zeitgeber theory, circadian rhythms, and mood disorders: review and evaluation

**Published.** 2006.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The social zeitgeber theory proposes that life events trigger mood episodes by disrupting social routines, which in turn disrupt biological circadian rhythms.
^p16904251-theory

The review evaluates the evidence and reports that direct support for the theory is limited, and it considers an alternative internal-pacemaker account.
^p16904251-evaluation

Circadian disruption is treated as a plausible contributing process rather than an established cause of mood episodes.
^p16904251-status
