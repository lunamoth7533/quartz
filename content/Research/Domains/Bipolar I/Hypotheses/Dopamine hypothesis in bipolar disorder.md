# Dopamine hypothesis in bipolar disorder

**Definition.** The dopamine hypothesis proposes that altered dopamine signalling contributes to bipolar episodes, with different patterns in mania and depression. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-state|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]

## Supported claims

- Elevated D2/3 receptor availability and a hyperactive reward network accompany mania, while increased dopamine transporter levels accompany depression. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-state|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]
- Both dopamine agonists and antidopaminergics have improved bipolar depression in pharmacological studies. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-pharmacology|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]
- The receptor and transporter homeostasis model is presented as speculation, not an established mechanism. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-speculative|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]
- A hypothesis that organizes research questions is not a diagnostic test. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-caution-framework|AI synthesis: Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]

## How it works

**What the hypothesis claims.** Converging pharmacological and imaging findings support a model in which elevated striatal D2/3 receptor availability and a hyperactive reward network accompany mania, while bipolar depression shows increased dopamine transporter availability, with inconsistent findings elsewhere. The direction of the finding therefore differs between states rather than being a single abnormality: the pattern is state-dependent rather than uniform. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-state|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]

**What the imaging measure actually reports.** The mania findings come from positron emission tomography with D2/3 ligands, so the measured quantity is receptor availability - how much binding is detectable - rather than dopamine concentration. Availability shifts can reflect receptor levels, occupancy by endogenous transmitter, or both, which is why the imaging evidence constrains the hypothesis without photographing transmitter release. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-state|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]

**Why the pharmacological paradox is informative.** Dopamine agonists and antidopaminergic drugs have both improved bipolar depressive symptoms in pharmacological studies, which is difficult to reconcile with a single direction of dopamine change. If bipolar depression were simply "too little dopamine", dopamine agonists should help and blockers should worsen it; the trial literature shows both classes improving depressive symptoms in different studies. The review's suggested resolution - that some agents act on other monoamines, receptors or receptor families - keeps the hypothesis alive while demonstrating that a one-transmitter reading of it is untenable: evidence that opposite manipulations help the same state is a signal that the receptor-level story is incomplete. This is the bipolar-domain sibling of the chemical-imbalance critique in the mood-disorder literature. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-pharmacology|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]] [[Chemical imbalance framing]]

**Dopamine is not one signal.** Mesostriatal, mesolimbocortical and periventricular systems support different functions, so 'dopamine in bipolar disorder' refers to several systems at once. [[F62 Neuroscience Online biogenic amines#^f62-dopamine|Biogenic amines]] Midbrain dopamine neurons carry salience and movement-related information in addition to reward-prediction error, and different populations project to different targets, so 'dopamine activity' in one paradigm is not interchangeable with another. [[P27069377 Schultz 2016 Reward prediction error#^p27069377-scope|Reward prediction error]] [[P29760524 Berke 2018 What does dopamine mean#^p29760524-heterogeneity|What does dopamine mean?]]

**Prediction-error framing.** Dopamine signalling also relates to reward prediction error, which connects the hypothesis to reward-processing accounts of mood episodes rather than to a simple level deficit. [[P27069377 Schultz 2016 Reward prediction error#^p27069377-rpe|Reward prediction error]]

**The speculative layer is labelled.** The receptor and transporter homeostasis model is presented as speculation, with treatment implications if confirmed, and the review notes reliance on pharmacological evidence that can affect other monoamines plus scarce imaging evidence on dopaminergic function. Reading the paper means reading those qualifiers. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-speculative|Dopamine hypothesis in bipolar disorder]] [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-limit|Dopamine hypothesis in bipolar disorder]]

**Interpretive boundary.** These are group-level pharmacological and imaging observations; they do not identify a cause or provide a test for any individual, and a hypothesis that organises research questions is not a chemical test for the disorder. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-caution-framework|AI appraisal: Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]

**Reading rule.** State the mood state, the measure and the direction of the finding. In this hypothesis the sign of the dopamine finding flips between mania and depression, so a state-free summary is guaranteed to be wrong about half the literature. [[P28289283 Ashok 2017 Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder#^p28289283-state|Dopamine hypothesis of bipolar disorder]]

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** The reward-prediction-error formalism from computational neuroscience gives this hypothesis its most precise current vocabulary: mania's hyperactive reward network is a statement about valuation circuitry, not about a hormone level, and formal models are what make that statement testable. [[Reward prediction error]] [[Bipolar depression and mixed features]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Imaging evidence on dopamine function is scarce, and drug studies can affect other monoamines.
