# Circadian and social rhythm hypotheses

## Definition

The social zeitgeber theory proposes that life events trigger mood episodes by disrupting social routines, which then disrupt biological circadian rhythms. [[P16904251 Grandin 2006 Social zeitgeber theory#^p16904251-theory|The social zeitgeber theory]]

## How it works

**The proposal.** Social zeitgeber theory proposes that life events trigger mood episodes by disrupting social routines, which in turn disrupt biological circadian rhythms. Regular social cues entrain circadian timing; when they are lost - through shift work, travel, bereavement or a change in routine - sleep-wake timing and the other rhythms coupled to it that influence mood are proposed to destabilise in a way that raises episode risk. [[P16904251 Grandin 2006 Social zeitgeber theory#^p16904251-theory|The social zeitgeber theory]]

**The clock biology the hypothesis invokes.** The core molecular clock is a transcriptional-translational feedback loop in which clock proteins repress their own transcription over roughly 24 hours. Most tissues contain their own clocks, coordinated centrally by the suprachiasmatic nucleus, so circadian organisation is distributed rather than located in one structure; this is the biological machinery the hypothesis invokes. Clock output influences metabolism, physiology and behaviour, which is why disrupted timing has consequences beyond sleep. [[P27990019 Takahashi 2017 Circadian clock architecture#^p27990019-loop|Circadian clock architecture]] [[P27990019 Takahashi 2017 Circadian clock architecture#^p27990019-tissue|Circadian clock architecture]] [[P27990019 Takahashi 2017 Circadian clock architecture#^p27990019-outputs|Circadian clock architecture]]

**How sleep timing is actually regulated.** Sleep timing reflects the interaction of circadian rhythms with a homeostatic sleep drive: how long the person has been awake and what the body clock expects. Sleep cycles through rapid-eye-movement and non-REM stages with different associated activity patterns, so 'sleep disruption' is not one variable - timing, duration, continuity and stage composition are distinct measurements. [[F14 NINDS Brain basics understanding sleep#^f14-rhythm|Brain Basics: Understanding Sleep]] [[F14 NINDS Brain basics understanding sleep#^f14-stages|Brain Basics: Understanding Sleep]]

**Where the hypothesis has practical expression.** Structured routines and sleep regularity are components of manualised psychosocial approaches in bipolar disorder. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]]

**Reading rule.** Keep three claims apart: that circadian biology exists and is distributed, that rhythm disruption is associated with episodes, and that stabilising rhythms prevents episodes. The first is well established, the second is supported with unresolved direction, and the third is what the intervention literature tries to test. [[P38695002 Keramatian 2023 CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines#^p38695002-phases|CANMAT and ISBD bipolar treatment guidelines]]

## Evidence and status

The evidence is weaker than the theory's currency suggests. The review that evaluates the model reports that direct support is limited and considers an alternative internal-pacemaker account in which the rhythm disturbance is not primarily a social-entrainment failure; its conclusion treats circadian disruption as a plausible contributing process rather than an established cause of mood episodes. [[P16904251 Grandin 2006 Social zeitgeber theory#^p16904251-evaluation|The social zeitgeber theory]] [[P16904251 Grandin 2006 Social zeitgeber theory#^p16904251-status|The social zeitgeber theory]]

Observational evidence links rhythm disruption to episode onset, but the direction-of-effect problem is structural: disturbed sleep can be an early symptom of an episode, a consequence of one, or a trigger, and observational designs rarely separate the three. [[P16904251 Grandin 2006 Social zeitgeber theory#^p16904251-limit|The social zeitgeber theory]] The clock architecture the hypothesis invokes is described primarily from animal models. [[P27990019 Takahashi 2017 Circadian clock architecture#^p27990019-limit|Circadian clock architecture]]

Intervention studies on rhythm-focused therapy show benefits with modest effect sizes and heterogeneous designs. A systematic review and network meta-analysis of 39 randomised trials in outpatients found that adding psychotherapy to medication was associated with lower recurrence, and its component analysis linked psychoeducation with supervised skills practice delivered in family or group sessions to fewer recurrences than the same content delivered individually. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-recurrence|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-components|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] Component analyses estimate average contributions across heterogeneous protocols; they do not identify which ingredient produced one person's improvement, and the authors temper their conclusions for heterogeneity in populations, duration and follow-up. [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-limit|Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]] [[P33052390 Miklowitz 2021 Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder#^p33052390-caution-ingredients|AI appraisal: Adjunctive psychotherapy for bipolar disorder]]
