# Inflammation and immune hypotheses in bipolar disorder

## Definition

Immune hypotheses propose that inflammatory signalling contributes to mood episodes in a subset of people. They generalise from work on inflammation and depression to bipolar presentations.

## How it works

**The mechanism comes from depression research.** Peripheral immune signals reach the brain and produce sickness behaviour - a coordinated set of changes in activity, appetite, sleep and mood. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-sickness|Inflammation to sickness and depression]] The review that established this framework proposes that prolonged inflammatory signalling can contribute to depressive symptoms in vulnerable individuals, with cytokine signalling, altered tryptophan metabolism and vagal transmission as proposed routes, presented as interacting rather than exclusive. Immune hypotheses for bipolar disorder generalise that proposal to a different disorder. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-depression|From inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-mechanism|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]

**The subset argument, imported with its caveats.** The depression literature argues that inflammation is relevant to a marker-identified subset of patients rather than to depression as a whole - targeting a subgroup rather than treating inflammation as a general mechanism - and frames inflammatory responses as evolutionarily conserved and potentially adaptive in short bursts, with costs when sustained. [[P26711676 Miller 2016 Inflammation in depression#^p26711676-subset|The role of inflammation in depression]] [[P26711676 Miller 2016 Inflammation in depression#^p26711676-adaptive|The role of inflammation in depression]] It discusses anti-inflammatory strategies as experimental directions requiring targeting and patient selection, so carrying the argument into bipolar disorder inherits both its promise and its unresolved selection problem. [[P26711676 Miller 2016 Inflammation in depression#^p26711676-treatment|The role of inflammation in depression]]

## Evidence and status

Group-level marker differences are reproducible in some studies; causal direction and treatment implications remain experimental. [[P26711676 Miller 2016 Inflammation in depression#^p26711676-treatment|The role of inflammation in depression]]

**What the meta-analytic evidence shows.** A systematic review pooled 13 studies with more than 500 patients measuring endogenous cytokine concentrations in bipolar disorder, reporting results by affective state; TNF-alpha, soluble TNF receptor type 1 and soluble interleukin-2 receptor were elevated across affective states in the included studies. Meta-analytic differences in circulating markers describe group averages; they do not identify a cause of bipolar disorder or supply a usable individual test. [[P22749156 Munkholm 2013 Cytokines in bipolar disorder#^p22749156-design|Cytokines in bipolar disorder]] [[P22749156 Munkholm 2013 Cytokines in bipolar disorder#^p22749156-finding|Cytokines in bipolar disorder]] [[P22749156 Munkholm 2013 Cytokines in bipolar disorder#^p22749156-caution|Cytokines in bipolar disorder]]

**The mechanism is a model.** The route from inflammation to depressive symptoms is a model advanced by a mechanistic review rather than an established mechanism for depression generally, and it is being extended here to a different disorder. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-limit|Inflammation to sickness and depression]]

**Why cross-sectional marker studies cannot settle direction.** Circulating markers are affected by infection, sleep, medication, body composition and illness state, so a difference between groups can be a consequence of the episode, its treatment or associated health factors rather than a contributor, and group differences are not usable individual tests. [[P22749156 Munkholm 2013 Cytokines in bipolar disorder#^p22749156-caution|Cytokines in bipolar disorder]] Direction of causation is exactly what these designs are weakest at answering, and the tool that could help - genetic instruments used for causal inference - is a method rather than a result. [[P28686856 Visscher 2017 10 years of GWAS discovery#^p28686856-translation|10 years of GWAS discovery]]

**Where the marker findings sit relative to the disorder.** The bipolar imaging literature shows how much of a group difference can be accounted for by treatment and course variables; marker studies rarely have comparable covariate depth. A defensible summary is therefore that reproducible group-level marker elevations exist in some samples, that a mechanistic route from inflammation to mood symptoms is proposed and plausible, and that neither establishes inflammation as a cause or a treatment target in bipolar I disorder. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI appraisal: ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder]] [[P26711676 Miller 2016 Inflammation in depression#^p26711676-limit|The role of inflammation in depression]]
