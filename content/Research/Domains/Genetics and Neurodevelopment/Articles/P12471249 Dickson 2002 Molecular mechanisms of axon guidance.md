## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Axons are guided by attractive and repulsive cues in the extracellular environment, and conserved families of guidance molecules - netrins, Slits, semaphorins and ephrins - carry those signals.
^p12471249-cues

Cues act by regulating cytoskeletal dynamics in the growth cone, through signalling pathways the review describes as still incompletely understood.
^p12471249-growthcone

Regulatory mechanisms ensure the right axon responds to the right cue at the right time, so a modest number of guidance factors produces complex wiring.
^p12471249-precision
