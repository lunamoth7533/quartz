## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Evidence from families, twins and adoptees shows ADHD clustering in families, and the review gives its heritability as 74%.
^p29892054-heritability

Linkage studies imply that each risk variant has a very small effect; genome-wide studies have identified significant loci and assign about a third of the heritability to a polygenic component of many common variants.
^p29892054-common

Rare copy-number insertions and deletions account for another part of the heritability, and genetic factors also contribute to ADHD's comorbidity with other disorders.
^p29892054-rare

## Scope as reported

A two-author narrative review of family, twin, adoption, linkage, genome-wide association and copy-number evidence, first published online in 2018; it reports no new pooled analysis.
^p29892054-limit
