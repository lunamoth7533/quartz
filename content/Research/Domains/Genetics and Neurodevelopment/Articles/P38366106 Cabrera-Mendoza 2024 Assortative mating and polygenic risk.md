# The impact of assortative mating, participation bias and socioeconomic status on the polygenic risk of behavioural and psychiatric traits.

**Authors.** Cabrera-Mendoza B, Wendt FR, et al. (5 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38366106/)

**Published.** 2024 in Nature human behaviour.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1038/s41562-024-01828-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-01828-5) · PMID [38366106](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38366106/) · PMCID [PMC11161911](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11161911/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Combining gametic phase disequilibrium with within-spouse and within-sibling polygenic score correlations, the authors found genetic signatures of assortative mating for bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, depression, Tourette syndrome and several alcohol-related phenotypes.
^p38366106-mating

Conditioning on socioeconomic status changed the signatures for some alcohol-related traits and for Tourette syndrome.
^p38366106-ses

People who did and did not answer the UK Biobank mental health questionnaire differed in gametic phase disequilibrium for depression and for alcohol use disorder, a sign of participation bias.
^p38366106-participation

## Scope as reported

Cross-method meta-analysis of assortative mating signatures from gametic phase disequilibrium and spouse and sibling polygenic score correlations, with an analysis conditioned on socioeconomic status.
^p38366106-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Assortative mating and selective participation break the random-sampling assumptions under which score weights are estimated, so a score can carry mating and sampling structure alongside direct genetic effects. That is a further reason to read score associations as properties of the sampled population rather than of individuals.
^p38366106-caution-assumptions
