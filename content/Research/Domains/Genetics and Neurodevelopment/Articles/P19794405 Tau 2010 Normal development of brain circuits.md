## Reported findings (checked full text)

The review describes brain circuit development as an ordered sequence of proliferation, migration, differentiation, synapse formation and experience-dependent refinement.
^p19794405-sequence

Overproduced synapses and connections are subsequently eliminated, so normal development involves loss as well as growth.
^p19794405-pruning

Different systems mature on different schedules, which is why age of onset and sensitive windows differ across functions.
^p19794405-timing

## Scope as reported

A review of normal circuit development in animals and humans. It synthesises developmental neuroscience; it does not test a clinical hypothesis.
^p19794405-limit
