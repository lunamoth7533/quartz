# Childhood Maltreatment and Mental Health Problems: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Quasi-Experimental Studies.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Without adjustment, maltreatment in childhood showed a moderate link with later mental health problems (Cohen's d = 0.56); once quasi-experimental methods accounted for confounding, a smaller link remained (d = 0.31).
^p36628513-attenuation

The adjusted estimate held up across different quasi-experimental approaches - including twin and sibling differences, children-of-twins, adoption and fixed-effects designs - and across different psychiatric outcomes.
^p36628513-designs

The authors read this as a small causal contribution of maltreatment, with part of the raw association reflecting wider genetic and environmental risk factors.
^p36628513-interpretation

## Scope as reported

Systematic search of PubMed, PsycINFO and Embase to 1 January 2022 for English-language, peer-reviewed quasi-experimental studies; 34 studies with 54,646 independent participants were included, using designs such as natural experiments, cross-lagged panel models, propensity score matching and inverse probability weighting.
^p36628513-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Each quasi-experimental design removes some confounding under its own assumptions, so agreement across designs strengthens a causal reading without proving it. The pooled outcome is broad mental health problems, not complex PTSD specifically.
^p36628513-caution-designs
