## Key points (as published)

Genomics is the study of the whole set of genetic material rather than single genes, which changes the kinds of question that can be asked about a trait.
^f66-scope

The fact sheet connects genomics to areas including pharmacogenomics, stem-cell therapy and drug development, each with its own evidence base and time frame.
^f66-applications

It is maintained as a public educational page by the institute that funds genomics research, so content is revised as the field moves.
^f66-updates

## Scope and limitations

A government fact sheet written for a general audience. It explains concepts and programmes rather than reporting specific effect estimates.
