# Polygenic Risk Scores

**Publisher.** National Human Genome Research Institute (NIH)

**Source.** <https://www.genome.gov/health/Genomics-and-Medicine/Polygenic-risk-scores>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F68.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

A polygenic risk score aggregates many small variant associations into one number intended to summarise genetic liability for a trait.
^f68-definition

The page notes that scores are population statistics built from specific study samples, so their meaning can shift across ancestry groups and study designs.
^f68-limits

Possible uses include stratifying research samples or health screening, but the page frames these as areas under investigation rather than settled clinical practice.
^f68-use

## Scope and limitations

An official explanatory page written for the public. It describes the method's promise and cautions rather than a validated clinical instrument.
^f68-limit
