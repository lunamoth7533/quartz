# Epigenomics Fact Sheet

**Publisher.** National Human Genome Research Institute (NIH)

**Source.** <https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/fact-sheets/Epigenomics-Fact-Sheet>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F69.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Epigenomic marks change how cells use DNA rather than altering the sequence, and they are part of how different cell types maintain different states.
^f69-definition

The page describes mapping marks such as DNA methylation and chromatin features, including large reference projects that catalogue them across tissues.
^f69-methods

Epigenomic changes are studied in disease, but the page presents that work as ongoing rather than as established mechanisms for specific disorders.
^f69-disease

## Scope and limitations

An official explanatory page. It defines epigenomic marks and research programmes without quantifying exposure effects.
^f69-limit
