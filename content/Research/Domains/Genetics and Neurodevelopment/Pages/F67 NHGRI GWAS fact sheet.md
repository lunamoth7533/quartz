# Genome-Wide Association Studies Fact Sheet

**Publisher.** National Human Genome Research Institute (NIH)

## Key points (as published)

A genome-wide association study compares variants across a large sample to find markers that are statistically associated with a trait, without assuming which gene is involved.
^f67-design

Association identifies a region of the genome associated with a trait in a population; it does not by itself identify the causal variant or show how the variant acts.
^f67-meaning

Credible signals require very large samples and replication, because most single-variant effects on complex traits are small.
^f67-scale

## Scope and limitations

An official explanatory page. It describes the method's logic and limitations but reports no specific study results.
