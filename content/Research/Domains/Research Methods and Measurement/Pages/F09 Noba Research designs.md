# Research Designs

**Publisher.** Noba Project

**Source.** <https://nobaproject.com/modules/research-designs>

**Verification.** Official page opened and checked on 2026-09-24; educational material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Observational designs describe what co-occurs, while experimental designs manipulate a variable and can support causal inference when assignment and control are sound.
^f09-designs

Correlation between two variables does not establish that one causes the other: a third variable or reverse direction can explain it.
^f09-correlation

Confounding and selection effects distort comparisons, which is why random assignment and preregistered outcomes matter.
^f09-confounds
