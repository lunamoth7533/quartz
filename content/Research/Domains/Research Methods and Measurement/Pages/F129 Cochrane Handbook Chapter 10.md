# Cochrane Handbook Chapter 10: Analysing data and undertaking meta-analyses

**Publisher.** Cochrane

**Source.** <https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-10>

**Verification.** Chapter page opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F129-cochrane-chapter-10.txt`. Official methodological handbook chapter.

## Key points (as published)

Common-effect methods assume a common intervention effect; random-effects methods model different but related effects across studies.
^f129-fixed-effect

A random-effects confidence interval describes uncertainty about the mean effect, not the full spread of study effects. Model choice should not rely only on a heterogeneity test.
^f129-rand-effects

The chapter discusses small-study differences arising from publication bias or within-study bias and the complications funnel-plot asymmetry creates for both fixed- and random-effects analyses.
^f129-pub-bias

The chapter explains effect measures, pooling methods, heterogeneity and sensitivity analyses.
^f129-overview

## Scope and limitations

A methodological handbook; it specifies how to conduct meta-analysis, not what any particular clinical finding means.
