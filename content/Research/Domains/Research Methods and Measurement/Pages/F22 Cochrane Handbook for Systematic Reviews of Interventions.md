# Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, version 6.5

**Publisher.** Cochrane · [Handbook contents](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current)

**Source.** <https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current> and chapter 15, <https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-15>

**Verification.** Official handbook pages opened and checked on 2026-09-24. Version 6.5 (2024) with chapter-level update dates; key points below are verified from the published chapter pages.

## Key points (as published)

The handbook is Cochrane's official methods guidance for systematic reviews of interventions; chapter 15 covers relative and absolute effects, the number needed to treat and standardized mean differences.
^f22-effects

It directs authors not to describe results as statistically significant or non-significant and to report the confidence interval with the exact P value rather than relying on P-value thresholds.
^f22-thresholds

Reviews rate the certainty of the evidence with summary-of-findings tables, weighing risk of bias, imprecision, inconsistency, indirectness and publication bias.
^f22-certainty

The handbook advises against healthcare recommendations from reviews, while allowing actions to be highlighted against values and preferences.
^f22-conclusions

## Scope and limitations

Official methodological guidance rather than a study or a finding. Chapters are updated on their own dates within the handbook, and following a method does not guarantee that the resulting review's conclusions are correct.
^f22-limit

## Analyst cautions (AI synthesis)

These paragraphs are this library's own appraisal, not statements from Cochrane.

A handbook defines a standard of practice; it is not itself evidence about any treatment. In this vault it is used to mark where a claim sits relative to review method and certainty, and to keep "a review says" from being treated as "the evidence is settled".
