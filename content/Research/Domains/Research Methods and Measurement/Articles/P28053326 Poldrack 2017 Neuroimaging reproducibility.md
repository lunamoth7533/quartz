## Reported findings (checked full text)

The paper identifies analytic flexibility - many defensible choices in preprocessing and modelling - as a central source of irreproducibility in neuroimaging.
^p28053326-flexibility

It discusses low statistical power and the resulting exaggeration of effect sizes in published brain-behaviour correlations.
^p28053326-power

Proposed responses include data and code sharing, preregistration, larger samples and multiverse or specification-curve analyses.
^p28053326-practice

## Scope as reported

A methodological review in Nature Reviews Neuroscience. It concerns research practice and makes no claims about any disorder.
