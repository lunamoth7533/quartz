## Reported findings (checked full text)

Preregistration separates prediction from postdiction by recording hypotheses, methods and analysis plans before data collection.
^p29531091-distinction

It makes the difference between confirmatory and exploratory work visible, which is what allows readers to weight a claim appropriately.
^p29531091-purpose

The paper describes practical workflows, including registering analysis plans and reporting deviations, rather than treating preregistration as a single document.
