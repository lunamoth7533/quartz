## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Of 176 replications, 49% were judged successful; of the 136 whose effect sizes could be compared numerically, 46% of replication estimates fell inside the original study's prediction interval, where 95% would be expected.
^p38026006-rate

Larger original effect sizes and within-participants designs were the features most associated with replication success.
^p38026006-predictors

## Scope as reported

A dataset of student-run replications from one graduate methods course, analysed for correlates of success; the authors read the result as consistent with earlier large-scale replication reports.
^p38026006-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The original studies were not sampled at random from the literature, and student replications may differ from the originals in resources and setting, so the success rate describes this collection rather than psychology as a whole.
^p38026006-caution-sample
