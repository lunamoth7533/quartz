## Reported findings (checked full text)

Scale development runs through three phases and nine steps: item generation with content validity, scale construction, then evaluation of dimensionality, reliability and validity.
^p29942800-phases

Content validity is assessed before administration, against the defined domain.
^p29942800-content

Reliability uses statistics such as Cronbach's alpha, ordinal alpha, omega and test-retest coefficients.
^p29942800-reliability

Criterion and construct validity are assessed after administration through relationships with other measures and groups.
^p29942800-validity

Common sample-size guidance is about ten respondents per item and/or 200-300 observations.
^p29942800-sample

## Scope as reported

Methodological guidance synthesising psychometric conventions rather than a single empirical study. The recommendations are heuristics for health and behavioural research that vary by instrument purpose, population and analysis method.
^p29942800-limit

## Analyst cautions (AI synthesis)

These paragraphs are this library's own appraisal, not statements from the primer.

Reliability is not validity. An instrument can produce consistent scores that consistently miss the construct, and a scale validated in one population or purpose can lose those properties when it is moved. In this library, "measured" always invites the follow-up question: measured how, in whom, and against what criterion.
^p29942800-caution-score
