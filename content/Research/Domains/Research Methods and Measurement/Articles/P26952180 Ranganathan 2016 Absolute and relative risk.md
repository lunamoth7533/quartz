## Reported findings (checked full text)

Intervention effects are reported as absolute risk reduction, relative risk reduction and number needed to treat.
^p26952180-measures

Absolute risk reduction is the risk difference and must be read against baseline risk.
^p26952180-arr

Relative risk reduction is the absolute reduction divided by baseline risk.
^p26952180-rrr

The number needed to treat is one divided by the absolute risk reduction.
^p26952180-nnt

Each trial outcome carries its own absolute, relative and person-based measures.
^p26952180-outcomes

## Scope as reported

A short teaching article built on a hypothetical comparison, designed to explain risk measures rather than to establish the effectiveness of any treatment. Time-to-event and competing-risk settings need additional methods that the tutorial does not cover.
^p26952180-limit

## Analyst cautions (AI synthesis)

These paragraphs are this library's own appraisal, not statements from the tutorial.

Reporting only a relative reduction can flatter a small absolute benefit, which is why this library's clinical lessons ask for both numbers, the baseline risk, and the outcome being counted.
