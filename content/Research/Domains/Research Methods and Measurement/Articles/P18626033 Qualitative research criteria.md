## Reported findings (checked full text)

The paper reviews disagreement about how qualitative research should be evaluated, including whether quantitative criteria such as generalisability transfer at all.
^p18626033-controversy

It recommends assessment through transparency, coherence between question and method, reflexivity, and attention to how findings were generated.
^p18626033-criteria

The practical implication is that a qualitative claim is judged by the quality of its reasoning and evidence, not by statistical significance.
^p18626033-reading
