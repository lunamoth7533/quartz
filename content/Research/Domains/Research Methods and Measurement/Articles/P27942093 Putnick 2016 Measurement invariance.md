## Reported findings (checked full text)

Measurement invariance asks whether an instrument measures the same construct in the same way across groups or time points, which is a precondition for comparing scores.
^p27942093-definition

The paper distinguishes configural, metric and scalar invariance, each imposing progressively stronger constraints on model parameters.
^p27942093-levels

It argues that invariance should be tested and reported routinely, because partial or absent invariance changes what a group comparison means.
^p27942093-practice
