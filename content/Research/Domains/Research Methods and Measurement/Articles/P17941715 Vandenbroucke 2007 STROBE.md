## Reported findings (checked abstract)

STROBE provides reporting guidance for observational studies - cohort, case-control and cross-sectional - so that design, selection and analysis are transparent.
^p17941715-purpose

The explanation and elaboration paper documents why each item matters, often with examples of adequate and inadequate reporting.
^p17941715-elaboration

Reporting quality is not the same as study quality: a fully reported study can still be biased, which is why checklists are not risk-of-bias tools.
^p17941715-limits

## Scope as reported

A reporting guideline with commentary. It improves transparency and does not validate causal claims.
^p17941715-limit
