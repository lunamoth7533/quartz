# Efficacy of 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA)-assisted therapy for posttraumatic stress disorder: A systematic review and meta-analysis of clinical and functional outcomes.

**Authors.** Fares-Otero NE, Furukawa Y, et al. (8 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41825162/)

**Published.** 2026 in European neuropsychopharmacology : the journal of the European College of Neuropsychopharmacology (first online 2026-03-12).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/j.euroneuro.2026.112802](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroneuro.2026.112802) · PMID [41825162](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41825162/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

MDMA-assisted therapy was associated with lower PTSD severity than control (standardised mean difference about -1.2), with a smaller reduction in dissociative symptoms and a possible gain in functioning.
^p41825162-ptsd

No clear benefit appeared for depressive symptoms.
^p41825162-depression

Certainty was very low: most trials were at high risk of bias in how outcomes were measured, few used active controls, and blinding was probably compromised.
^p41825162-certainty

## Scope as reported

PRISMA-based review with RoB2 and GRADE; trials were few and small with considerable heterogeneity, and the authors call for larger trials with active controls and long follow-up.
^p41825162-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Trials enrolled adults with PTSD, not ICD-11 complex PTSD, and the treatment tested is a drug plus structured therapy, so an effect cannot be credited to MDMA alone or assumed for complex presentations.
^p41825162-caution-population
