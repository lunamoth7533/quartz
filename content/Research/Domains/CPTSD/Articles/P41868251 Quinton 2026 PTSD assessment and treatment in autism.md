## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across the included studies, PTSD symptoms were more severe in autistic children and adults than in non-autistic peers, and PTSD was at least as common.
^p41868251-severity

The 18 new studies reflect growing research interest in PTSD in autistic people.
^p41868251-growth

The authors call for validation of PTSD assessment tools and treatment strategies for autistic people, and for study of autism-specific expressions of trauma-related symptoms.
^p41868251-gaps

## Scope as reported

Narrative systematic review update; the abstract reports no pooled estimates.
^p41868251-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The call to validate assessment tools and treatments indicates that adapted treatments are not yet established for autistic people; effectiveness should not be assumed from trials in non-autistic samples.
^p41868251-caution-treatment
