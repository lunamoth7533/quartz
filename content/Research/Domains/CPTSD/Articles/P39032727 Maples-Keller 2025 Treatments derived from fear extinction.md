## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review presents conditioning and extinction as a translational model that links animal research to treatments such as prolonged exposure, including work on consolidation and reconsolidation.
^p39032727-model

It surveys pharmacological, behavioural (such as physical exercise) and device-based augmentation strategies drawn from basic science, for prevention and for chronic PTSD, with the evidence from clinical samples.
^p39032727-augmentation

The authors set out limitations and challenges of this literature and future directions.
^p39032727-limits

## Scope as reported

Brief narrative review; the abstract reports no systematic search or pooled estimates.
^p39032727-limit
