# Neuroimaging of posttraumatic stress disorder in adults and youth: progress over the last decade on three leading questions of the field.

**Authors.** Hinojosa CA, George GC, Ben-Zion Z · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38632413/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review organises a decade of adult and youth imaging around three questions: which alterations are pre-existing risk factors and which are acquired after trauma, which predict or track treatment response, and whether imaging can define brain-based PTSD biotypes.
^p38632413-questions

It concludes that there has been progress on all three but that the findings are not ready for clinical practice.
^p38632413-status

The authors call for prospective designs with scans before trauma, repeated scans around treatment, and replication of proposed biotypes.
^p38632413-designs

## Scope as reported

Narrative review; no systematic search or meta-analysis is reported.
^p38632413-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Group-level imaging differences and proposed biotypes are research findings; until biotypes replicate, a scan cannot diagnose PTSD or complex PTSD or choose a treatment.
^p38632413-caution-clinical
