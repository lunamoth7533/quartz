## Reported findings (checked abstract)

A dissociative subtype of PTSD was proposed around symptoms of depersonalisation and derealisation, supported by latent class analyses in veteran and civilian samples.
^p22431063-definition

The review assembles antecedent, concurrent and predictive evidence, which is the standard basis for arguing a subtype is clinically meaningful.
^p22431063-validators

It argues the distinction may matter for treatment, while acknowledging that evidence on differential response was developing.
