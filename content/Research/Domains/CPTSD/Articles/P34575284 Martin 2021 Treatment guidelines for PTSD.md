## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Of 14 guidelines published between 2004 and 2020, only five were under five years old, and three scored highly on every AGREE II domain.
^p34575284-guidelines

Most guidelines count both psychological and pharmacological therapies as first-line; all but one name cognitive behavioural therapy as the first-line psychological approach and selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors as the first-line drug class.
^p34575284-firstline

Recommendations for core PTSD symptoms varied little between guidelines, whereas targeted treatment of nightmares was seldom covered and recommendations on it diverged.
^p34575284-nightmares

## Scope as reported

Guidelines were found through bibliographic databases, guideline repositories and professional and targeted web searches; recommendations were extracted and quality rated with AGREE II, with particular attention to nightmares.
^p34575284-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Guideline agreement concerns first-line treatment classes for populations and reflects the evidence available when each guideline was written; it does not rank individual drugs or therapies for a particular person, and several of the guidelines were already dated.
