# The effect of multiple adverse childhood experiences on health: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review treats adverse childhood experiences as adversities in childhood or adolescence, such as maltreatment or exposure to domestic violence, which often occur together.
^p29253477-definition

Adults reporting four or more ACEs had higher risk than those reporting none on every one of 23 health outcomes studied.
^p29253477-dose

Associations were weakest (odds ratios under 2) for inactivity, overweight and diabetes, strong for mental ill health and problematic drinking, and strongest (above 7) for problematic drug use and for violence against others or oneself; almost half the outcomes showed considerable heterogeneity.
^p29253477-gradient

## Scope as reported

Random-effects meta-analysis of studies published up to May 2016 with adult samples of at least 100, comparing four or more ACEs with none for outcomes reported in at least four populations.
^p29253477-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

The included designs are observational and mostly rest on adults' recall of childhood, so the pooled odds ratios describe association rather than the causal effect of adversity; contrasting four or more ACEs with none also says nothing about which adversities, what severity or what timing matter.
^p29253477-caution-association
