## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors note substantial support for the construct validity of ICD-11 complex PTSD but no account of how it develops, and list what a theory must explain: repeated trauma, the functional independence of PTSD and self-organisation symptoms, and diagnostic heterogeneity.
^p37338431-gap

The theory proposes that trauma exposure interacts with individual vulnerability to produce intrusive, sensation-based trauma memories and negative identities, which together generate the PTSD and self-organisation symptoms.
^p37338431-model

Both processes are held to range from pre-reflective experience to full self-awareness, and the paper derives implications for assessment and treatment.
^p37338431-continuum

## Scope as reported

Theory article proposing testable relations rather than reporting data.

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

A theory proposed by developers of the diagnosis and its main questionnaire organises existing findings; its causal claims need prospective and independent tests, which a single cross-sectional survey cannot supply.
