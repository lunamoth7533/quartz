## Reported findings (checked abstract)

12.7% met ADHD criteria on the Wender Utah Rating Scale.
^p39607449-prevalence

Agreement between ADHD measures was poor: the Wender Utah scale had low sensitivity against a historical ADHD diagnosis, and sensitivity and specificity against the adult self-report screener were both about 61%.
^p39607449-measures

ADHD was about 2.5 times as prevalent in veterans with lifetime PTSD and about twice as prevalent in those with current PTSD.
^p39607449-ptsd

## Scope as reported

Single VA cohort of combat-deployed veterans; ADHD status was defined by a retrospective self-report criterion standard.
^p39607449-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

When ADHD is inferred from self-report in a trauma-exposed population, overlapping symptoms such as poor concentration make it hard to tell co-occurrence from misattribution; the prevalence ratios describe association in this sample only.
^p39607449-caution-overlap
