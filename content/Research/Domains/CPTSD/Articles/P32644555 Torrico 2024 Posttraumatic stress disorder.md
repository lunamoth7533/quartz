## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The chapter presents PTSD as a common disorder that can follow a traumatic event, with symptoms affecting thinking, mood, bodily experience and behaviour, and with risks of chronic impairment, co-occurring psychiatric illness and suicide.
^p32644555-definition

It traces the diagnosis to the third edition of the DSM in 1980 and summarises the DSM criteria as a traumatic exposure plus intrusion symptoms such as memories or nightmares, avoidance, negative shifts in mood and thinking, and heightened arousal.
^p32644555-dsm

Management is described as individualised, drawing on psychological interventions and medication for both prevention and treatment.
^p32644555-management

## Scope as reported

A clinical reference chapter that synthesises existing literature for education; it reports no new data.
