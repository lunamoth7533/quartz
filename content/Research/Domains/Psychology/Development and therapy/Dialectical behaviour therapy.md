# Dialectical behaviour therapy

## Definition

Dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT) is a cognitive-behavioural treatment that combines behavioural and cognitive techniques to teach skills for managing emotions, tolerating distress and keeping relationships working. [[P39536143 Opland 2024 Behavioral therapy reference chapter#^p39536143-dbt|Opland 2024]] It is the most frequently trialled psychotherapy for borderline personality disorder, a condition described by long-standing instability of mood, self-image and relationships. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-dbt|Storebø 2020]] [[F84 NIMH Borderline personality disorder#^f84-pattern|NIMH: borderline personality disorder]] Adapted versions have been tested in complex presentations of PTSD after childhood abuse and in adolescents with bipolar spectrum disorder. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-design|Bohus 2020]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]]

## How it works

**What it targets.** DBT is aimed at emotional escalation and the dangerous behaviour that can follow it, above all self-harm and suicide attempts; the outcomes its trials count first are self-harm, suicide-related behaviour and borderline severity. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-dbt|Storebø 2020]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] In the adolescent bipolar trial the fall in suicide attempts was partly carried by reduced emotion dysregulation, which is the clearest sourced evidence that the therapy works through its intended target. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-mood|Goldstein 2024]]

**What it teaches.** The skills cover three areas - regulating emotion, tolerating distress and handling relationships - and the textbook account credits the therapy with lower self-rated emotional and behavioural dysregulation and fewer dysfunctional coping strategies. [[P39536143 Opland 2024 Behavioral therapy reference chapter#^p39536143-dbt|Opland 2024]] [[P39536143 Opland 2024 Behavioral therapy reference chapter#^p39536143-dbt-outcomes|Opland 2024]] Those outcome statements carry no effect sizes, so they describe what DBT is for rather than how well it works. [[P39536143 Opland 2024 Behavioral therapy reference chapter#^p39536143-caution-level|Appraisal: Opland 2024]]

**How it is delivered and adapted.** The full programme is long: the bipolar adolescent protocol ran for a year alongside medication management. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-limit|Goldstein 2024]] DBT-PTSD is a phase-based version built for women with PTSD after childhood abuse who also have borderline features, delivered in up to 45 individual sessions over a year plus three further sessions. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-limit|Bohus 2020]] [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-design|Bohus 2020]]

**Borderline personality disorder: the main evidence base.** Across 75 trials of tailored psychotherapies, DBT accounted for about one-third, and against usual care it reduced borderline severity and self-harm and improved functioning. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-dbt|Storebø 2020]] Each of those DBT estimates came from three to seven small trials rated low quality, and tailored therapies as a group did not differ clearly from one another on severity or functioning. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-models|Storebø 2020]] [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-caution-certainty|Appraisal: Storebø 2020]]

**Complex PTSD presentations.** In 193 women with abuse-related PTSD and borderline features, both DBT-PTSD and cognitive processing therapy reduced clinician-rated PTSD severity, with a small advantage for DBT-PTSD. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-ptsd|Bohus 2020]] Fewer participants dropped out of DBT-PTSD, and more reached remission and reliable recovery. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-remission|Bohus 2020]] The sample was defined by DSM-5 PTSD plus borderline criteria rather than an ICD-11 complex PTSD diagnosis, and one trial of a new programme cannot rule out allegiance effects. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-caution-sample|Appraisal: Bohus 2020]]

**Bipolar disorder.** In 100 adolescents with bipolar spectrum disorder, a year of DBT reduced suicide attempts compared with standard psychotherapy, while mood symptoms and episodes improved equally in both arms. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-mood|Goldstein 2024]] That is a target-specific effect from one mostly female, single-site sample, not evidence that DBT changes the course of bipolar disorder. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-caution-scope|Appraisal: Goldstein 2024]]

**Reading rule.** Ask which DBT was tested - the full programme, a skills format or an adaptation such as DBT-PTSD - against which comparator, and which outcome moved: the sourced effects are on self-harm, suicide attempts, borderline severity and PTSD severity, not on the course of mood episodes. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-dbt|Storebø 2020]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-caution-scope|Appraisal: Goldstein 2024]]

## Evidence and status

The evidence base is broad but thin. DBT is the most trialled therapy in borderline personality disorder, yet its estimates against usual care rest on small trials rated low quality, and among tailored therapies only the change in overall borderline severity passed the review's threshold for a clinically relevant difference. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-tau|Storebø 2020]] [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-caution-certainty|Appraisal: Storebø 2020]] Outside borderline personality disorder the sourced evidence is one randomised trial per condition: women with abuse-related PTSD and borderline features, and adolescents with bipolar spectrum disorder. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-limit|Bohus 2020]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-limit|Goldstein 2024]] Harms could not be judged in the borderline trials because too few reported them. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-models|Storebø 2020]] Public guidance on borderline personality disorder names evidence-based psychotherapy as the primary approach, which is the service context in which these trials matter. [[F84 NIMH Borderline personality disorder#^f84-treatment|NIMH: borderline personality disorder]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether the benefit comes from DBT's specific skills or from the structure and contact it shares with other tailored therapies, since subgroup comparisons found no clear difference between models. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-models|Storebø 2020]]
- Whether DBT-PTSD's advantage holds in men, outside the original German clinics and in people who meet ICD-11 complex PTSD criteria. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-caution-sample|Appraisal: Bohus 2020]]
- Whether the fall in suicide attempts in bipolar adolescents replicates across sites and in adults. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-caution-scope|Appraisal: Goldstein 2024]]
- No DBT trial in ADHD or in autism is sourced in this library yet, so this note cannot say how skills formats perform in those groups.

## Recent research

- **2024 · Randomised clinical trial (JAMA psychiatry).** The first sourced trial of DBT in bipolar disorder found fewer suicide attempts with DBT and equal mood improvement in both arms, locating the effect in suicidal behaviour rather than mood course. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-mood|Goldstein 2024]]
- **2020 · Randomised clinical trial (JAMA psychiatry).** DBT-PTSD edged out an established trauma therapy at matched dose in women with abuse-related PTSD and borderline features, with lower dropout. [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-ptsd|Bohus 2020]] [[P32697288 Bohus 2020 DBT-PTSD versus cognitive processing therapy#^p32697288-remission|Bohus 2020]]
- **2020 · Cochrane systematic review (The Cochrane database of systematic reviews).** Confirmed DBT as the most studied borderline therapy while grading its effects against usual care as low-certainty. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-dbt|Storebø 2020]]
