# Mindfulness-based interventions

## Definition

Mindfulness-based interventions (MBIs) are structured programmes that train mindfulness, ranging from programmes with established protocols to broader, mindfulness-informed therapies in which mindfulness is one element among others. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-effect|Jovanovic 2024]] [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-limit|Burgos-Julián 2022]] An overview of the review literature judges them effective for common problems such as depression, anxiety and stress, but describes the evidence for PTSD, ADHD and autism as inconclusive or preliminary. [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-agreement|Zhang 2021]] [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-controversy|Zhang 2021]]

## How it works

**What they target.** The sourced evidence treats MBIs as general-purpose programmes rather than treatments aimed at one diagnosis: the overall review lists benefits across depression, anxiety, stress, insomnia, addiction and pain, in clinical, school and workplace settings. [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-agreement|Zhang 2021]] That breadth is also a limit, because one verdict then covers very different programmes and populations. [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-caution-breadth|Appraisal: Zhang 2021]]

**Components and forms.** In laboratory component studies, mixed mindfulness components and values combined with mindfulness produced reliable short-term effects against inactive comparisons. [[P23046777 Levin 2012 Psychological flexibility component studies#^p23046777-effects|Levin 2012]] In clinical use, mindfulness appears in two forms: as the core of an established protocol, and as one element inside another package, such as the skill-oriented CBT group with social-rhythm and mindfulness elements tested in bipolar disorder. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-effect|Jovanovic 2024]] [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-equivalence|Hautzinger 2024]] Pooled PTSD effects did not differ between the established and the broader types. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-effect|Jovanovic 2024]]

**PTSD and trauma.** An umbrella review of 69 systematic reviews found a medium pooled reduction in PTSD symptoms, consistent across control conditions and comparable, the authors say, with second-line treatments. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-effect|Jovanovic 2024]] The same review rated about two-thirds of those reviews non-rigorous, so the pooled figure inherits their weaknesses, and PTSD samples are not complex PTSD samples. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-quality|Jovanovic 2024]] [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-caution-inherited|Appraisal: Jovanovic 2024]]

**Bipolar disorder.** Across 13 studies, mindfulness-based and mindfulness-informed interventions had no significant effect on depression or mania but a moderate effect on anxiety. [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-mood|Burgos-Julián 2022]] [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-anxiety|Burgos-Julián 2022]] Imputed comparisons and short windows make even the anxiety result provisional. [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-caution-design|Appraisal: Burgos-Julián 2022]] In a relapse-prevention trial, a CBT group containing mindfulness elements did no better than a supportive, emotion-focused group, and without a usual-care arm the trial cannot show whether either beats standard care. [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-equivalence|Hautzinger 2024]] [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-caution-comparator|Appraisal: Hautzinger 2024]]

**ADHD and autism.** In adult ADHD, mindfulness - like CBT, cognitive remediation and psychoeducation - beat placebo only on clinician-reported measures, with confidence in the estimates ranging from very low to moderate. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Ostinelli 2025]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-limit|Ostinelli 2025]] For autism the overall review found the evidence inconclusive or preliminary, and no autism-specific trial is sourced here. [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-controversy|Zhang 2021]]

**Reading rule.** Ask whether the programme was an established protocol or mindfulness-informed, who rated the outcome, whether the review behind a pooled figure was itself rigorous, and - in mood disorders - whether mood, anxiety or relapse was the outcome that moved. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-quality|Jovanovic 2024]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Ostinelli 2025]] [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-mood|Burgos-Julián 2022]]

## Evidence and status

Across the conditions in this library the evidence is modest and uneven. PTSD has the largest pooled estimate, a medium effect, but it rests on mostly non-rigorous reviews. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-effect|Jovanovic 2024]] [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-quality|Jovanovic 2024]] In bipolar disorder the only pooled benefit is on anxiety, and in adult ADHD benefit appears only when clinicians rate the outcome. [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-anxiety|Burgos-Julián 2022]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Ostinelli 2025]] The overall review notes that many mindfulness reviews report low-quality primary studies and calls MBIs relatively safe while flagging ethical questions. [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-quality|Zhang 2021]] [[P33884400 Zhang 2021 Mindfulness-based interventions overall review#^p33884400-agreement|Zhang 2021]] A missing benefit on an outcome that was not studied is absence of evidence, not evidence of no effect. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-caution-absence|Appraisal: Ostinelli 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether the medium PTSD effect survives in rigorous reviews and in samples defined by complex PTSD. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-caution-inherited|Appraisal: Jovanovic 2024]]
- Whether mindfulness adds anything to relapse prevention in bipolar disorder, an outcome the bipolar meta-analysis did not report. [[P35102640 Burgos-Julian 2022 Mindfulness interventions in bipolar disorder#^p35102640-caution-design|Appraisal: Burgos-Julián 2022]]
- Whether clinician-rated gains in adult ADHD reflect change that people with ADHD would also report. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Ostinelli 2025]]
- How often mindfulness practice causes harm, particularly after trauma; the sources here give no rates.

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review and component network meta-analysis (The lancet. Psychiatry).** In adult ADHD, mindfulness joined CBT and psychoeducation in beating placebo only on clinician-reported scales. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Ostinelli 2025]]
- **2024 · Umbrella review (Journal of anxiety disorders).** Graded the review literature on mindfulness for PTSD and found it mostly non-rigorous, while pooling a medium effect. [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-quality|Jovanovic 2024]] [[P38761551 Jovanovic 2024 Mindfulness-based interventions for PTSD#^p38761551-effect|Jovanovic 2024]]
- **2024 · Randomised clinical trial (JAMA psychiatry).** A CBT group with mindfulness elements and a supportive group prevented bipolar relapse equally, with nearly half relapsing in each arm. [[P38837133 Hautzinger 2024 Group psychotherapies for bipolar relapse prevention#^p38837133-relapse|Hautzinger 2024]]

## Connections

- [[Acceptance and commitment therapy]] - shares mindfulness and present-moment components; ACT embeds them in a values-based model, whereas MBIs make them the main content.
- [[Attention]] - the construct family mindfulness training is usually said to work on; whether trained attention changes ADHD symptoms is what the rater-dependent trials leave open.
- [[CBT for adult ADHD]] - the same network meta-analysis found CBT and mindfulness ahead of placebo only on clinician ratings, so both face one rater question.
- [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]] - explains why psychological treatments in adult ADHD can show clinician-rated but not self-rated gains, the pattern mindfulness shares.
- [[Bipolar psychotherapy evidence]] - the adjunctive-therapy evidence in which a CBT group with mindfulness elements matched a supportive group for relapse prevention.
- [[Rumination and repetitive negative thinking]] - the repetitive-thinking process many psychological treatments aim at; the meta-analysis sourced there tests CBT rather than mindfulness.
- [[Psychological interventions for complex trauma]] - the complex-trauma therapy evidence against which mindfulness's PTSD effect has to be read, with its trial-category cautions.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** Mindfulness-based interventions connect the psychology domain's attention and emotion-regulation constructs to trial evidence in bipolar disorder, ADHD and trauma, and the research-methods domain's review-quality tools, such as AMSTAR 2, decide how much weight their pooled figures can bear.
