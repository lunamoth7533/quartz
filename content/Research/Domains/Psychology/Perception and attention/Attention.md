# Attention

## Definition

Attention is the selection of some information for further processing at the expense of other information. It has several forms - selective, sustained, divided - that are related but not identical.

## How it works

**Detection limits.** Detection thresholds describe the minimum stimulus that can be noticed, and signal detection theory separates sensory sensitivity from response bias - the measurement foundation for attention research, because a 'miss' can reflect either the sensory limit or the criterion the person applied. [[F37 OpenStax Psychology 2e sensation versus perception#^f37-absolute|Sensation versus Perception]]

**Selection and capacity.** Limits on processing appear as interference between concurrent tasks and as costs of switching between them, which is why divided attention degrades performance. [[F37 OpenStax Psychology 2e sensation versus perception#^f37-absolute|Sensation versus Perception]]

**Sustained attention.** Vigilance declines over time, and the rate of decline depends on task demands, reward structure and arousal. The vigilance decrement - declining detection over sustained task time - is therefore a measurement model and partly a motivational effect rather than a pure resource drain. That is why vigilance claims in clinical contexts name the task and the reinforcement conditions, and why "poor sustained attention" from one task does not automatically generalise. [[F43 OpenStax Psychology 2e motivation#^f43-theories|Motivation]] [[Attention and executive function]]

**Control sits in association cortex.** Attention involves posterior association cortex and prefrontal control regions. Association areas are grouped into anterior, posterior and limbic regions, each linking information from primary and unimodal sensory or motor areas, and anterior association cortex including prefrontal regions is linked with executive processing, which the source treats as a set of interacting functions rather than one faculty. Attention is therefore a family of control operations rather than a single spotlight. [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-areas|Association and executive processing]] [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-executive|Association and Executive Processing]]

**Arousal and neuromodulation.** Modulatory input from noradrenaline and acetylcholine shapes the gain of processing. Prefrontal function depends on noradrenaline and dopamine released according to arousal state, with an inverted-U relationship to performance, and long-axon cholinergic pathways including the nucleus basalis projection to cortex provide a separate modulatory route. That is why alertness, motivation and attention are difficult to separate experimentally. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]] [[F108 Neuroscience Online acetylcholine#^f108-pathways|Acetylcholine transmission]]

**Precision weighting and constructive perception.** Computational accounts treat attention as precision weighting of prediction errors, a description that connects attention to learning and perception. Predictive-coding algorithms share the idea that perception involves inference about hidden causes, and such accounts predict that what is attended changes what is perceived rather than only what is reported - a claim that requires a specific algorithm to test. [[P26809759 Spratling 2017 Predictive coding algorithms#^p26809759-common|Predictive coding algorithms]] [[P26809759 Spratling 2017 Predictive coding algorithms#^p26809759-difference|Predictive coding algorithms]]

**Reading rule.** Name the task, the manipulation, the outcome measure and the control condition; in this literature a difference in performance is often a difference in strategy or criterion rather than in capacity. [[F09 Noba Research designs#^f09-correlation|Research Designs]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

## Evidence and status

Behavioural attention effects are robust; how many separable systems exist and whether they map onto distinct networks remains debated. [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

Selection has an early and a late version, and the debate is empirical: whether unattended input is filtered early in processing or processed semantically before being dropped was settled neither way, because findings depend on task and load - which itself supports the family-of-operations framing. The practical residue is that attention claims must specify what selection acts on - location, feature, object or stream - because the operations dissociate. [[F37 OpenStax Psychology 2e sensation versus perception#^f37-absolute|Sensation versus Perception]]
