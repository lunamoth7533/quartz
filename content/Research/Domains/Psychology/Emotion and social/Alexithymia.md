# Alexithymia

## Definition

Alexithymia names difficulty identifying one's own feelings and difficulty describing them; the standard questionnaires score these two facets together with an externally oriented thinking style. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-facets|Van Bael 2024]] [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-dif|Preece 2024]] It is a questionnaire-defined construct that is common but not universal in autistic samples and is studied as a risk factor for posttraumatic symptoms. [[P30399531 Kinnaird 2019 Alexithymia in autism meta-analysis#^p30399531-prevalence|Kinnaird 2019]] [[P37987839 Putica 2024 Alexithymia distress and PTSD over time#^p37987839-risk|Putica 2024]]

## How it works

**The facets behave differently.** Across 32 studies, associations with self-reported interoception were stronger for difficulty identifying and difficulty describing feelings than for externally oriented thinking. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-facets|Van Bael 2024]] In a community sample, factor analysis separated an alexithymia factor from a general-distress factor; the difficulty-identifying-feelings facet of the 20-item Toronto Alexithymia Scale (TAS-20) loaded heavily on distress, while Perth Alexithymia Questionnaire scores did not. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-factors|Preece 2024]] [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-dif|Preece 2024]] A total score therefore mixes components that relate differently to the body and to current distress. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Preece 2024]]

**Why measurement is the live debate.** If part of the TAS-20 tracks current distress, its associations with anxiety, depression or PTSD are partly built into the instrument, so studies relating TAS-20 totals to distress-heavy outcomes need that overlap controlled. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Preece 2024]] Group comparisons add a second requirement: the scale must measure the same construct in the same way in each group, a testable property that should be tested and reported rather than assumed. [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-definition|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-practice|Measurement invariance]] Prevalence figures inherit both problems, because they rest on a questionnaire cut-off. [[P30399531 Kinnaird 2019 Alexithymia in autism meta-analysis#^p30399531-caution-cutoff|Appraisal: Kinnaird 2019]]

**Interoception: related, not identical.** Interoception is the sensing of the body's physiological condition through a dedicated afferent system, and a re-representation in the right anterior insula is proposed as a basis for the felt sense of the bodily self and emotion. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Interoception]] Alexithymia is linked to self-reported interoception, but the direction depends on the questionnaire: higher alexithymia goes with more interoceptive confusion, reactivity and attention, and with less self-reported accuracy and adaptive interoception such as trusting and self-regulation. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-direction|Van Bael 2024]] Because both sides are self-reported, part of the pattern may reflect shared method rather than a shared mechanism. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-caution-method|Appraisal: Van Bael 2024]]

**Autism: common, not universal.** In a meta-analysis of 15 studies using the Toronto scale, autistic groups scored higher than neurotypical groups on every score, and 49.93% of autistic participants against 4.89% of neurotypical participants fell in the alexithymic range. [[P30399531 Kinnaird 2019 Alexithymia in autism meta-analysis#^p30399531-scores|Kinnaird 2019]] [[P30399531 Kinnaird 2019 Alexithymia in autism meta-analysis#^p30399531-prevalence|Kinnaird 2019]] Because alexithymia varies within autism, it can be tested as an explanation: the alexithymia hypothesis proposes that some emotion-processing difficulties attributed to autism reflect co-occurring alexithymia instead. [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-limit|Kirsch 2025]] [[P36872641 Gehdu 2023 Facial expression recognition autism alexithymia#^p36872641-limit|Gehdu 2023]] The tests disagree. In one experiment, autistic participants with high alexithymia misclassified more facial expressions than controls while those with low alexithymia did not, with or without the lower face masked. [[P36872641 Gehdu 2023 Facial expression recognition autism alexithymia#^p36872641-subgroups|Gehdu 2023]] [[P36872641 Gehdu 2023 Facial expression recognition autism alexithymia#^p36872641-masks|Gehdu 2023]] In an fMRI study of 120 adults, autistic participants recognised facial affect less accurately regardless of alexithymia status, and alexithymia instead modulated brain activation during the task. [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-behaviour|Kirsch 2025]] [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-brain|Kirsch 2025]] In non-clinical adults, higher alexithymia goes with poorer emotion recognition and empathy, with mixed results for theory of mind. [[P39309025 Di Tella 2024 Alexithymia and social cognition#^p39309025-recognition|Di Tella 2024]] [[P39309025 Di Tella 2024 Alexithymia and social cognition#^p39309025-tom|Di Tella 2024]] That association does not by itself show that alexithymia explains differences within a diagnostic group. [[P39309025 Di Tella 2024 Alexithymia and social cognition#^p39309025-caution-transfer|Appraisal: Di Tella 2024]]

**Trauma.** In a military cohort of 1,871 people followed from before deployment to 3-4 years after return, alexithymia predicted more severe PTSD symptoms and hyperarousal at every assessment; general distress partly mediated these links and fully mediated those with re-experiencing and avoidance. [[P37987839 Putica 2024 Alexithymia distress and PTSD over time#^p37987839-risk|Putica 2024]] [[P37987839 Putica 2024 Alexithymia distress and PTSD over time#^p37987839-distress|Putica 2024]] Because the same questionnaire overlaps with distress, the mediation and the measurement problem are hard to separate, and a military sample limits transfer to complex trauma. [[P37987839 Putica 2024 Alexithymia distress and PTSD over time#^p37987839-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Putica 2024]] [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-dif|Preece 2024]]

**ADHD.** The evidence sourced here is thinner. In 248 non-clinical young adults, self-reported ADHD and autism symptoms correlated, and alexithymia, impulsivity and negative mood fully mediated both that correlation and the link from ADHD symptoms to alcohol use severity. [[P38552929 Lyvers 2024 ASD and ADHD symptoms and alcohol use#^p38552929-mediation|Lyvers 2024]] [[P38552929 Lyvers 2024 ASD and ADHD symptoms and alcohol use#^p38552929-alcohol|Lyvers 2024]] Symptom scales in a non-clinical sample are not diagnoses, so this shows co-variation of traits rather than alexithymia in diagnosed ADHD. [[P38552929 Lyvers 2024 ASD and ADHD symptoms and alcohol use#^p38552929-caution-design|Appraisal: Lyvers 2024]]

**Reading rule.** Name the questionnaire and facet, check whether current distress was measured and controlled, and ask whether a group comparison assumed the scale works the same way in both groups before crediting an emotion-processing difference to alexithymia or to a diagnosis. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Preece 2024]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-practice|Measurement invariance]]

## Evidence and status

As a questionnaire score, alexithymia is consistently higher in autistic than in neurotypical groups and prospectively associated with PTSD severity in a large cohort. [[P30399531 Kinnaird 2019 Alexithymia in autism meta-analysis#^p30399531-scores|Kinnaird 2019]] [[P37987839 Putica 2024 Alexithymia distress and PTSD over time#^p37987839-risk|Putica 2024]] What the score measures is contested, because the TAS-20 difficulty-identifying-feelings facet shares variance with current distress. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-dif|Preece 2024]] Tests of the alexithymia hypothesis in autism disagree between tasks and between behavioural and brain measures, and the ADHD evidence sourced here is cross-sectional and non-clinical. [[P36872641 Gehdu 2023 Facial expression recognition autism alexithymia#^p36872641-subgroups|Gehdu 2023]] [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-behaviour|Kirsch 2025]] [[P38552929 Lyvers 2024 ASD and ADHD symptoms and alcohol use#^p38552929-limit|Lyvers 2024]] The interoception synthesis rests on cross-sectional, self-report studies on both sides. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-limit|Van Bael 2024]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether TAS-20 difficulty-identifying-feelings scores index alexithymia or current distress, and whether alternative questionnaires hold up in clinical samples. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-limit|Preece 2024]]
- Whether alexithymia accounts for emotion-recognition differences in autism: recent tests disagree depending on the task and on whether behaviour or brain activity is measured. [[P36872641 Gehdu 2023 Facial expression recognition autism alexithymia#^p36872641-subgroups|Gehdu 2023]] [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-behaviour|Kirsch 2025]]
- No systematic review of alexithymia in diagnosed ADHD is sourced in this library yet.
- How interoceptive differences and alexithymia relate causally stays open while both are measured mainly by self-report. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-caution-method|Appraisal: Van Bael 2024]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Case-control fMRI study (Biological Psychiatry: Cognitive Neuroscience and Neuroimaging).** Autistic adults recognised facial affect less accurately whether or not they had alexithymia, which weighs against the alexithymia hypothesis at the behavioural level while showing that alexithymia still shapes brain responses. [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-behaviour|Kirsch 2025]] [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-brain|Kirsch 2025]] Subgroup contrasts within 60 autistic participants narrow the hypothesis rather than settle it. [[P39827966 Kirsch 2025 Alexithymia hypothesis of autism revisited#^p39827966-caution-subgroup|Appraisal: Kirsch 2025]]
- **2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (PLoS One).** The alexithymia-interoception association changed direction with the interoception questionnaire used, turning an apparently simple link into a construct-definition problem. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-direction|Van Bael 2024]] [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-limit|Van Bael 2024]]
- **2024 · Psychometric study (Journal of Affective Disorders).** In 508 community adults, one TAS-20 facet loaded heavily on a distress factor while Perth questionnaire scores did not, the clearest recent statement of the measurement debate. [[P38320659 Preece 2024 Discriminant validity of alexithymia questionnaires#^p38320659-dif|Preece 2024]]

## Connections

- [[Emotion regulation]] - regulation acts on emotions once they arise and are appraised; alexithymia concerns the earlier step of identifying and describing them, and both rely heavily on self-report.
- [[Interoception and autonomic pathways]] - the bodily signals proposed to underlie felt emotion; alexithymia's association with interoception changes with the interoceptive measure used.
- [[Social cognition]] - emotion recognition and empathy are the social-cognitive abilities most consistently linked to alexithymia in non-clinical adults; theory-of-mind findings are mixed.
- [[Double empathy]] - a different locus for autistic social difficulty: alexithymia places some differences in a person's own emotional awareness, double empathy places them between people.
- [[Measurement validity and reliability]] - the discipline this construct needs most: the main scale's discriminant validity is contested, and group comparisons assume measurement invariance.
- [[Social cognition accounts]] - the autism accounts that the alexithymia hypothesis partly competes with, since emotion-recognition differences are tested against co-occurring alexithymia.
- [[Insula]] - the region proposed to turn bodily signals into felt emotion; the alexithymia evidence gathered here is self-report and has not been tied to insular measurements.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** Alexithymia sits where the psychology domain's emotion constructs meet the neuroanatomy domain's interoceptive pathways and the autism and trauma domains' questions about emotional awareness; the research-methods domain supplies the invariance and discriminant-validity checks that decide whether a group difference in a questionnaire score is a difference in the trait.
