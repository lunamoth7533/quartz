# Shame and guilt

## Definition

Shame is a negative view of the self, held either through one's own eyes (internal shame) or through the imagined eyes of others (external shame), while guilt concerns responsibility for events and becomes maladaptive when that responsibility is exaggerated or detached from any situation. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-external|Kim 2011]] [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-guilt-variants|Kim 2011]] Both are studied as correlates of symptoms across conditions: shame with depression, general psychopathology and trauma-related distress, and trauma-related guilt with PTSD. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-shame|Kim 2011]] [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-correlations|DeCou 2023]] [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-association|Kip 2022]]

## How it works

**Why the distinction matters.** Across 108 studies, shame was more closely tied to depressive symptoms than guilt was. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-shame|Kim 2011]] The gap closed for maladaptive guilt: exaggerated responsibility for uncontrollable events and free-floating guilt were as closely tied to depressive symptoms as shame, so the useful line runs between shame and maladaptive guilt on one side and ordinary guilt on the other. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-guilt-variants|Kim 2011]] External shame showed the largest associations of all, and depression scales that mention guilt inflated guilt's apparent link with depression. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-external|Kim 2011]] Part of the guilt-depression association is therefore shared item content. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-caution-content|Appraisal: Kim 2011]]

**Shame after trauma.** Across 25 studies, trauma-related shame correlated moderately with overall psychopathology, trauma-related distress and depression, and the links did not depend on study quality or sample type. [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-correlations|DeCou 2023]] [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-heterogeneity|DeCou 2023]] The authors recommend asking about trauma-related shame routinely, but the data cannot show whether shame drives symptoms or symptoms deepen shame. [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-heterogeneity|DeCou 2023]] [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-caution-direction|Appraisal: DeCou 2023]]

**Guilt after trauma.** In 163 studies of adult trauma survivors, trauma-related guilt correlated with PTSD symptoms at the same time point and, more weakly, with later symptoms, and every symptom cluster was involved. [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-association|Kip 2022]] The association was smaller in higher-quality studies, strongest after war-related trauma and weakest after motor-vehicle accidents. [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-moderators|Kip 2022]] The predictive estimate rests on 19 longitudinal data points, which makes guilt a candidate maintaining factor rather than an established cause. [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-caution-longitudinal|Appraisal: Kip 2022]]

**Autism.** In a survey of trauma-exposed adults, autistic participants reported more severe PTSD symptoms and more negative post-trauma appraisals, especially of alienation, shame and fear, and those appraisals statistically mediated the link between autism and symptom severity. [[P41454656 Prosser 2026 Trauma appraisals in autistic adults#^p41454656-severity|Prosser 2026]] Mediation in cross-sectional data is a decomposition, not evidence that shame appraisals cause the symptoms. [[P41454656 Prosser 2026 Trauma appraisals in autistic adults#^p41454656-caution-mediation|Appraisal: Prosser 2026]]

**Help-seeking.** In a Dutch population survey, only about half of people with probable lifetime PTSD had sought professional help, and shame was among the common reasons for not doing so, with lack of knowledge and avoidance. [[P39808949 Hoeboer 2025 PTSD and CPTSD epidemiology Netherlands#^p39808949-care|Hoeboer 2025]] Help-seeking was self-reported, so the size of that gap is approximate. [[P39808949 Hoeboer 2025 PTSD and CPTSD epidemiology Netherlands#^p39808949-caution-systems|Appraisal: Hoeboer 2025]]

**Reading rule.** Name which emotion and which form was measured - internal or external shame, ordinary or maladaptive guilt, trauma-related or general - and check whether the outcome scale already contains guilt or shame items before reading an association as a finding about mechanism. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-external|Kim 2011]] [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-caution-content|Appraisal: Kim 2011]]

## Evidence and status

Three meta-analyses agree on moderate associations: shame with depressive symptoms, trauma-related shame with psychopathology and distress, and trauma-related guilt with PTSD. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-shame|Kim 2011]] [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-correlations|DeCou 2023]] [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-association|Kip 2022]] Nearly all of those data are correlational and self-reported, heterogeneity is high, and the only prospective estimate - guilt predicting later PTSD symptoms - is small. [[P21219057 Kim 2011 Shame guilt and depressive symptoms#^p21219057-limit|Kim 2011]] [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-limit|Kip 2022]] [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-limit|DeCou 2023]] The autism and help-seeking findings each come from single cross-sectional surveys. [[P41454656 Prosser 2026 Trauma appraisals in autistic adults#^p41454656-limit|Prosser 2026]] [[P39808949 Hoeboer 2025 PTSD and CPTSD epidemiology Netherlands#^p39808949-limit|Hoeboer 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether shame and guilt maintain PTSD and depression or follow from them. [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-caution-direction|Appraisal: DeCou 2023]] [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-caution-longitudinal|Appraisal: Kip 2022]]
- What drives the higher shame appraisals reported by autistic adults after trauma, which a cross-sectional survey cannot establish. [[P41454656 Prosser 2026 Trauma appraisals in autistic adults#^p41454656-caution-mediation|Appraisal: Prosser 2026]]
- No trial of a shame-focused treatment is sourced in this library yet.
- No source on shame or guilt in ADHD or bipolar disorder is in this library yet.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Cross-sectional online survey (Autism : the international journal of research and practice).** Put shame among the appraisals that statistically carried autistic adults' higher PTSD severity after trauma. [[P41454656 Prosser 2026 Trauma appraisals in autistic adults#^p41454656-severity|Prosser 2026]]
- **2025 · Population survey (Journal of anxiety disorders).** Named shame among the reasons people with probable PTSD did not seek help. [[P39808949 Hoeboer 2025 PTSD and CPTSD epidemiology Netherlands#^p39808949-care|Hoeboer 2025]]
- **2023 · Meta-analysis (Trauma, violence & abuse).** Pooled trauma-related shame against symptoms and argued for assessing it routinely in trauma care. [[P34715765 DeCou 2023 Trauma-related shame and psychopathology#^p34715765-heterogeneity|DeCou 2023]]
- **2022 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Psychological medicine).** The largest synthesis of trauma-related guilt and PTSD, with a small prospective association. [[P35781354 Kip 2022 Trauma-related guilt and PTSD#^p35781354-association|Kip 2022]]
