## Key points (as published)

Motivation is described through several traditions: instinct and drive theories, arousal theory, and expectancy-based accounts of goal pursuit.
^f43-theories

Self-efficacy and goal-setting research links beliefs about capability to persistence and performance, which is why expectancy constructs recur across psychology.
^f43-efficacy

Social motives such as belonging and achievement are treated as organised patterns rather than as separate drives, which matters when motivation is described in clinical terms.
^f43-social
