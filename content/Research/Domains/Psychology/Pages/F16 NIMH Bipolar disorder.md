# Bipolar Disorder

**Publisher.** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)

**Source.** <https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/bipolar-disorder>

**Verification.** Official page opened and checked on 2026-09-24; educational material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Bipolar I disorder is defined by the occurrence of mania; depressive episodes are common but not required for the diagnosis.
^f16-mania

The clinical picture combines episodes with a lifetime course, so treatment planning considers acute symptoms and long-term stability together.
^f16-course

Treatment approaches include medication and psychotherapy, and biological and psychological factors are both treated as relevant.
^f16-treatment
