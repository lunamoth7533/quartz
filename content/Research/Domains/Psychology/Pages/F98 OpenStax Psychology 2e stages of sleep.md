# Stages of Sleep

## Key points (as published)

Sleep is described in cycles of non-REM and REM stages distinguished by brain-wave patterns, with slow-wave sleep early in the night and longer REM periods later.
^f98-stages

During REM sleep voluntary muscle activity is suppressed while brain activity resembles waking, which is why it is described as paradoxical sleep.
^f98-rem

The section reviews sleep deprivation effects on attention, mood and performance, and notes individual variation in how much sleep is needed.
^f98-deprivation

## Scope and limitations

An introductory psychology textbook section on sleep stages and deprivation. It teaches the standard sleep architecture and does not cover sleep disorders clinically.
^f98-limit
