## Key points (as published)

Historical perspectives on personality are traced from temperament ideas through trait and psychodynamic traditions.
^f45-historical

Trait approaches describe personality through dimensions rather than types, which is why modern work uses continuous scores rather than categories.
^f45-traits

The section presents enduring disagreements about consistency across situations, which is the same question raised by dimensional and contextual accounts of psychopathology.
^f45-debate
