## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across 20 two-group trials, adding manualized psychotherapy to medication was associated with lower recurrence than control (OR 0.56).
^p33052390-recurrence

A component analysis of 13 therapy elements found that psychoeducation with supervised skills practice in family or group sessions was linked to fewer recurrences than the same content delivered individually (OR 0.12).
^p33052390-components

CBT was linked to stabilizing depressive symptoms (SMD -0.32), with family and interpersonal therapy pointing the same way less certainly.
^p33052390-symptoms

Retention was higher for family or conjoint therapy and for brief psychoeducation.
^p33052390-retention

## Scope as reported

Systematic review and network meta-analysis of 39 randomized trials in outpatients, whose conclusions the authors temper for heterogeneity in populations, duration and follow-up.
^p33052390-limit
