# Cognitive behavior therapy vs. control conditions, other psychotherapies, pharmacotherapies and combined treatment for depression: a comprehensive meta-analysis including 409 trials with 52,702 patients.

**Authors.** Cuijpers P, Miguel C, Harrer M, Plessen CY, Ciharova M, Ebert D, Karyotaki E (7 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36640411/)

**Published.** 2023 in World psychiatry.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1002/wps.21069](https://doi.org/10.1002/wps.21069) · PMID [36640411](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36640411/) · PMCID [PMC9840507](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9840507/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Against control conditions such as care as usual and waiting list, CBT had moderate-to-large effects (g 0.79) that held in sensitivity analyses and remained significant at 6-12 months.
^p36640411-control

CBT's advantage over other psychotherapies was small (g 0.06) and lost significance in most sensitivity analyses; it matched pharmacotherapy in the short term and exceeded it at 6-12 months in a small number of trials; combined treatment beat medication alone but not CBT alone.
^p36640411-comparators

Effects were also found for unguided self-help, institutional settings and children and adolescents, and trial quality improved over time.
^p36640411-formats

## Scope as reported

Random-effects meta-analyses run on separate subsets of trials; the long-term comparison with pharmacotherapy rests on few trials and was not significant in every sensitivity analysis.
^p36640411-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The size of the CBT effect is set by the comparator - large against waiting list or usual care, close to zero against other active psychotherapies - so the evidence supports structured psychotherapy for depression more than CBT's specific ingredients; depression is not one of this library's four conditions, so transfer needs its own evidence.
^p36640411-caution-comparator
