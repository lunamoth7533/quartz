## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Autism is described as a construct covering early-emerging differences in social communication together with repetitive behaviour, restricted interests or sensory features; worldwide prevalence is just under 1%.
^p31949163-construct

Gross brain pathology is not characteristic, but subtle anatomical and functional differences appear in post-mortem, imaging and electrophysiology studies, and genetic findings mainly apply to heterogeneous groups.
^p31949163-biology

Psychosocial interventions in children can improve behaviours such as joint attention, language and social engagement; the authors call for long-term research, note that families often provide the main support, and include autistic perspectives.
^p31949163-support
