# Multimodal Assessment of Emotion Dysregulation in Children with and without ADHD and Disruptive Behavior Disorders.

**Authors.** Hernandez ML, Garcia AM, Spiegel JA, Dick AS, Graziano PA (5 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38270592/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Parent and teacher reports of emotion regulation, emotional lability and callous-unemotional behaviour differed across all three groups, whereas observed measures separated only the ADHD-plus-disruptive-behaviour group from typically developing children.
^p38270592-informants

Informant reports were associated with diagnostic status beyond the observed measures; the model classified the ADHD-plus-disruptive-behaviour group (91.3%) and typically developing children (95.9%) well, but identified the ADHD-only group correctly only 45.7% of the time.
^p38270592-classification

## Scope as reported

Young children (68.7% boys, 81.4% Latinx) assessed with parent and teacher reports plus behavioural observation of four domains of emotion dysregulation; the authors conclude the measures help identify ADHD with disruptive behaviour disorders more than ADHD alone.
^p38270592-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Informant and observed measures diverged, so whether a child "shows" dysregulation depends on the channel measured; informant reports may also share variance with the reports used to reach the diagnosis, which can inflate their apparent incremental value.
^p38270592-caution-channel
