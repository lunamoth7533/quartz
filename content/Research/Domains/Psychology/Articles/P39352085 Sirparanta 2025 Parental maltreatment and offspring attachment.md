**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

A parent's history of childhood maltreatment had a weak but significant combined association with offspring attachment insecurity (r = .06).
^p39352085-insecurity

The association with disorganised attachment was not significant (r = .03); in that analysis newer studies showed smaller effects, and a trim-and-fill check pointed to publication bias.
^p39352085-disorganization

## Scope as reported

Parental maltreatment was self-reported, offspring ages ranged from 12 to 79 months, and too few studies allowed conclusions about specific maltreatment types; the authors call for more objective measures of parental exposure.
^p39352085-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Small pooled associations describe populations of parent-child dyads, not a given family; they argue against treating a parent's history as decisive while leaving open effects that self-report and early-childhood follow-up cannot capture.
^p39352085-caution-population
