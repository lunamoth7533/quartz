## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors list areas of agreement: mindfulness-based interventions help with depression, anxiety, stress, insomnia, addiction and pain among other conditions, and are relatively safe, although ethical aspects need attention.
^p33884400-agreement

Evidence for PTSD, ADHD and autism is described as inconclusive or only preliminary, and some benefits were not confirmed in subgroups.
^p33884400-controversy

Many of the underlying reviews report low-quality primary studies, and the authors call for adequately powered trials with longer follow-up and for work on long-term adherence.
^p33884400-quality

## Scope as reported

Narrative overall review built from keyword searches of seven databases; the abstract reports no pooled estimate or quality grading of its own.
^p33884400-limit
