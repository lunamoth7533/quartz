## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The proposed symptom structures - three factors for PTSD, six for complex PTSD - and the distinction between the disorders found general support.
^p29029837-structure

Estimates from DSM-based items suggest lower ICD-11 PTSD prevalence in adults than older systems, with a caveat about re-experiencing items; child and adolescent community estimates looked similar to DSM-IV and DSM-5.
^p29029837-prevalence

ICD-11 PTSD detects some impaired people missed by DSM-IV or DSM-5, and complex PTSD identifies a group with more multiple and sustained traumas and greater functional impairment.
^p29029837-impairment

## Scope as reported

Review of studies conducted to test the ICD-11 diagnostic proposals, written before the final release of ICD-11.
^p29029837-limit
