# Dialectical Behavior Therapy for Posttraumatic Stress Disorder (DBT-PTSD) Compared With Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) in Complex Presentations of PTSD in Women Survivors of Childhood Abuse: A Randomized Clinical Trial.

**Authors.** Bohus M, Kleindienst N, et al. (12 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32697288/)

**Published.** 2020 in JAMA psychiatry.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2020.2148](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2020.2148) · PMID [32697288](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32697288/) · PMCID [PMC7376475](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7376475/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Clinician-rated PTSD severity (CAPS-5) fell markedly in both arms, and DBT-PTSD held a small but statistically significant advantage over cognitive processing therapy (between-group d 0.33).
^p32697288-ptsd

Early dropout was lower with DBT-PTSD (25.5% against 39.0%), and more DBT-PTSD participants reached symptomatic remission, reliable improvement and reliable recovery.
^p32697288-remission

Both arms received the same dose and frequency: up to 45 individual sessions over one year and three further sessions in the following three months.
^p32697288-design

## Scope as reported

Women only, randomised between 2014 and 2016; entry required childhood-abuse PTSD plus at least three borderline criteria including affective instability. DBT-PTSD is described as a new phase-based programme designed for this group.
^p32697288-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

One trial supports DBT-PTSD for women with abuse-related PTSD and borderline features. The sample was defined by DSM-5 PTSD plus borderline criteria rather than an ICD-11 complex PTSD diagnosis, and allegiance effects cannot be excluded when a new programme is tested against an established one.
^p32697288-caution-sample
