# Psychological therapies for people with borderline personality disorder.

**Authors.** Storebø OJ, Stoffers-Winterling JM, et al. (12 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32368793/)

**Published.** 2020 in The Cochrane database of systematic reviews (first online 2020-05-04).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1002/14651858.CD012955.pub2](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD012955.pub2) · PMID [32368793](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32368793/) · PMCID [PMC7199382](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7199382/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Against treatment as usual, therapies tailored to borderline personality disorder lowered symptom severity (SMD -0.52; 22 trials; moderate-quality evidence). Smaller gains in self-harm, suicide-related outcomes and functioning rested on low-quality evidence; only the severity change reached the authors' threshold for clinical relevance.
^p32368793-tau

Dialectical behaviour therapy was the most studied model, about one-third of all trials. Compared with usual care it reduced borderline severity and self-harm and improved functioning, each estimate coming from three to seven small trials rated low quality.
^p32368793-dbt

Therapy models did not clearly differ on severity or functioning, and too few trials reported harms to judge them.
^p32368793-models

## Scope as reported

Searches to March 2019 without date or language limits; randomised trials in any age group and setting comparing therapy with usual care, waiting list, no treatment or another active treatment over one to 36 months, graded with GRADE and checked with trial sequential analysis.
^p32368793-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

The direction of the DBT estimates is consistent while their size is uncertain, because each rests on a handful of small, low-quality trials; results from borderline personality disorder samples do not transfer automatically to bipolar, ADHD, autistic or complex-trauma populations.
^p32368793-caution-certainty
