## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Pooled effects of psychotherapy compared with control were significant for PTSD (k = 27), depression (k = 23), anxiety (k = 13) and dissociation (k = 7) outcomes.
^p40154799-improvement

At follow-up the effect sizes decreased slightly but remained significant, except for anxiety and dissociation symptoms.
^p40154799-followup

Participants with childhood trauma showed lower effects than those with other trauma types; risk of bias and the proportion of female participants moderated results, and the authors report some indications of publication bias.
^p40154799-moderators

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of psychological therapies in adults with complex trauma; the authors call for work on how interventions can be tailored.
^p40154799-limit

## Analyst cautions (AI synthesis)
