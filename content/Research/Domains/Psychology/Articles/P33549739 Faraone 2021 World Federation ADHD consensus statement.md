## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The statement curates 208 empirically supported assertions about ADHD, endorsed by 80 authors from 27 countries and read by 366 endorsing readers.
^p33549739-scope

Included evidence came from studies with more than 2000 participants or from meta-analyses of five or more studies with at least 2000 participants; meta-analyses without a publication-bias assessment were excluded except for prevalence, and treatment studies with waiting-list or treatment-as-usual controls were excluded.
^p33549739-methods

The authors conclude that many ADHD findings are supported by meta-analysis, allowing firm statements about the nature, course, outcome, causes and treatment of the disorder.
^p33549739-conclusion
