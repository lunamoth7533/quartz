# Systematic Review of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy in Individuals with Neurodevelopmental Disorders, Caregivers, and Staff.

**Authors.** Garcia Y, Keller-Collins A, et al. (7 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34154376/)

**Published.** 2022 in Behavior modification (first online 2021-06-21).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1177/01454455211027301](https://doi.org/10.1177/01454455211027301) · PMID [34154376](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34154376/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Twenty of the 30 studies used group designs that relied on process and outcome questionnaires; the other ten were single-case designs that also recorded behaviour directly.
^p34154376-designs

Improvements were mixed on both questionnaire and direct measures. In the group studies risk of bias was rated moderate or serious, and two single-case studies fell short of What Works Clearinghouse standards.
^p34154376-results

## Scope as reported

Studies of people with neurodevelopmental conditions were reviewed together with studies of their caregivers and staff, and quality was rated with WWC standards, RoB 2 and ROBINS-I; the abstract reports no pooled effect.
^p34154376-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Mixing caregiver and staff studies with studies of autistic and ADHD people themselves, at low quality, shows that ACT has been tried in these groups rather than that it works for them.
^p34154376-caution-mixed
