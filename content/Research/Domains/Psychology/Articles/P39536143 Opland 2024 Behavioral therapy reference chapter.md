**Authors.** Opland C, Torrico TJ (2 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39536143/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The chapter presents behavioural therapy as action-focused treatment that treats problem behaviour as learned and teaches new behaviour in its place, with cognitive behavioural therapy as a later generation that added work on thoughts.
^p39536143-behavioural

Among the behavioural therapies it lists, dialectical behaviour therapy combines behavioural and cognitive techniques to help people manage emotions, tolerate distress and improve their relationships.
^p39536143-dbt

It cites studies in which self-rated depression, emotional and behavioural dysregulation and psychological inflexibility fell within the first six months of DBT, and inpatients used fewer dysfunctional coping strategies.
^p39536143-dbt-outcomes

## Scope as reported

Educational study-guide chapter surveying several behavioural therapies; its outcome statements summarise selected studies rather than a systematic review.
^p39536143-limit
