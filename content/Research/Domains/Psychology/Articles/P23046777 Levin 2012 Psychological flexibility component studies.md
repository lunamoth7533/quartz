# The impact of treatment components suggested by the psychological flexibility model: a meta-analysis of laboratory-based component studies.

**Authors.** Levin ME, Hildebrandt MJ, et al. (4 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23046777/)

**Published.** 2012 in Behavior therapy (first online 2012-05-14).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/j.beth.2012.05.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.beth.2012.05.003) · PMID [23046777](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23046777/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The psychological flexibility model behind acceptance and commitment therapy names six components: acceptance, defusion, self as context, committed action, values and present-moment contact. This meta-analysis tested components one at a time in laboratory settings.
^p23046777-components

Acceptance, defusion, present-moment, values and mixed mindfulness components each produced significant positive effects against inactive comparisons, as did values combined with mindfulness.
^p23046777-effects

Effects were larger on the outcomes the model specifies, and components taught through experiential methods such as metaphors or exercises did better than a rationale alone; distressed and convenience samples did not differ.
^p23046777-experiential

## Scope as reported

Laboratory component studies rather than clinical treatment trials; the pool also touched other contextual forms of cognitive behavioural therapy, and the abstract gives no separate significant effect for self as context or committed action.
^p23046777-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Brief laboratory manipulations show that single components can shift the processes they target; they do not show that those components drive change across a full course of treatment in a clinical condition.
^p23046777-caution-lab
