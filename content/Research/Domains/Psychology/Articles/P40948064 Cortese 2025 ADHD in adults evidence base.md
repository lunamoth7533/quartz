## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Estimated adult prevalence is around 2.5%. Up to 70% of people with childhood-onset ADHD keep having impairing symptoms as adults, sometimes without meeting full criteria, and descriptive, predictive and concurrent validity are described as supported.
^p40948064-base

Open questions include late-onset ADHD, emotional dysregulation as a core symptom, defining functional impairment, comorbidity persistence, executive dysfunction, objective diagnostic measures, long-term treatment effects and non-pharmacological interventions.
^p40948064-open

The paper includes lived-experience perspectives on unmet needs and priorities for care.
