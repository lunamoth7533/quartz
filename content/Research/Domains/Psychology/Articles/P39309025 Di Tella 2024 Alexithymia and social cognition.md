## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Higher alexithymia was associated with reduced emotion recognition and reduced empathy, and results for these two abilities were relatively consistent across studies.
^p39309025-recognition

Results for alexithymia and theory of mind were mixed.
^p39309025-tom

## Scope as reported

Narrative synthesis limited to the general adult non-clinical population, searched in PubMed, PsycINFO and Scopus; the authors call for confirmation and outline theoretical and methodological problems in the literature.
^p39309025-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Associations in non-clinical adults do not show that alexithymia accounts for emotion-recognition differences within a diagnostic group; that needs designs that separate the two within the clinical group itself.
^p39309025-caution-transfer
