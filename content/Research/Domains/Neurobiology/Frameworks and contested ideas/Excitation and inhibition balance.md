# Excitation and inhibition balance

**Definition.** Whether a neuron fires depends on the summed excitatory and inhibitory influence reaching the point where the spike begins. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-initial-segment|Communication Between Neurons]]

## Supported claims

- Graded potentials summate at the initial segment, where voltage-gated sodium channels are dense. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-initial-segment|Communication Between Neurons]]
- Excitatory and inhibitory postsynaptic potentials move the membrane toward or away from threshold respectively. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-initial-segment|Communication Between Neurons]]
- In autism genetics, imbalance appears as one path among several rather than a universal explanation. [[P31981491 Satterstrom 2020 Autism exome sequencing#^p31981491-model|Autism exome sequencing study]]

## How it works

**The two channels.** Excitatory transmission, producing EPSPs, is mediated largely by the acidic amino acid glutamate; inhibitory transmission is mediated primarily by GABA, a glutamate metabolite, in the brain and largely by glycine in the spinal cord. Cortical excitability is therefore described as a balance between these opposing inputs. [[F106 Neuroscience Online amino acid transmitters#^f106-excitatory|Amino Acid Neurotransmitters]] [[F106 Neuroscience Online amino acid transmitters#^f106-inhibitory|Amino Acid Neurotransmitters]] [[P12467378 Petroff 2002 GABA and glutamate#^p12467378-balance|GABA and glutamate]] Because the same transmitter can act through receptors of opposite sign elsewhere, 'excitatory' and 'inhibitory' describe the effect on the postsynaptic membrane in a given cell, not a chemical property of the molecule. [[F04 OpenStax Communication between neurons#^f04-receptor|Communication Between Neurons]]

**Why the metabolic link matters.** Glutamate is the precursor of GABA and the two are linked through the tricarboxylic acid cycle, so synthesis and recycling couple them. [[P12467378 Petroff 2002 GABA and glutamate#^p12467378-metabolism|GABA and glutamate]] Treating the two systems as independent levers is therefore a modelling convenience.

**Location changes the arithmetic.** Excitatory and inhibitory synapses differ in their morphology and in where they sit on the target cell, and position determines how much an input can influence the decision to fire: an inhibitory contact near the soma or on the initial segment can veto a large dendritic excitation with a small conductance, while a distal inhibitory contact mainly subtracts locally. Any claim that inhibition is 'weak' or 'strong' therefore depends on where it lands, and 'balance' is a statement about summed current at a particular site, not a global quantity. [[F106 Neuroscience Online amino acid transmitters#^f106-spatial|Amino Acid Transmitters]] [[F102 Neuroscience Online cell types#^f102-compartments|Organization of Cell Types]]

**Four distinct things 'E/I balance' can mean.** In practice the phrase is used for at least four measurements that are not interchangeable: the ratio of excitatory to inhibitory synaptic conductances on a single cell; the relative numbers or densities of the two synapse types in a region; the ratio of network-level excitation to inhibition estimated from population activity; and the developmental sequence in which inhibitory maturation gates plasticity. A result expressed in one of these terms does not automatically transfer to the others, and much of the disagreement in the literature follows from that slippage. [[F106 Neuroscience Online amino acid transmitters#^f106-spatial|Amino Acid Transmitters]] [[F100 Neuroscience Online synaptic plasticity#^f100-lasting|Synaptic Plasticity]] Because E/I can be estimated in different ways - receptor expression, synaptic currents, imaging proxies, seizures - findings from these different measurements should not be treated as one quantity.

**Balance is dynamic, not a set point.** Because inhibition onto a cell can be recruited in proportion to its own recent activity, and because neuromodulators change the properties of both excitatory and inhibitory synapses, the operating point moves with state. This is why the same circuit can be described as balanced during one behavioural state and unbalanced in another without contradiction. [[P23040802 Marder 2012 Neuromodulation#^p23040802-state|Neuromodulation]] [[P23040802 Marder 2012 Neuromodulation#^p23040802-reconfigure|Neuromodulation]]

**Where the concept is used and overused.** Increased excitation-to-inhibition ratio has been advanced as a hypothesis for autism features and for seizure vulnerability, and it remains a model under test rather than an established cause. [[P14606691 Rubenstein 2003 Excitation-inhibition model#^p14606691-proposal|Excitation-inhibition model]] [[P14606691 Rubenstein 2003 Excitation-inhibition model#^p14606691-status|Excitation-inhibition model]] The widely cited version of the framing - that transmitter systems are 'out of balance' in psychiatric conditions - is presented in the introductory sources as one psychological perspective among others, and the same chapter documents receptor-dependent mixed effects that undercut a one-directional reading. Where E/I imbalance is proposed as a mechanism for a condition, it is a hypothesis with candidate molecular routes, not a finding that an individual's brain is out of balance in a measurable way. [[F06 OpenStax Cells of the nervous system#^f06-caution-framing|AI synthesis: Cells of the Nervous System]] [[P14606691 Rubenstein 2003 Excitation-inhibition model#^p14606691-status|Excitation-inhibition model]]

**Reading rule.** Treat every E/I claim as three questions: which level (synapse, cell, region, network), which measure, and which state. Without those, 'balance' is a metaphor that can be made to fit almost any dataset.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** The four meanings of E/I balance described here are also the vocabulary that the computational-brain-theories and neuroanatomy domains use, so a claim in one domain has to say which measure it is using before a result in another domain can be read as confirming or contradicting it. [[Network and developmental formulations]] [[Neurons and glia]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Balance is usually measured indirectly, and a difference in one circuit says little about the whole brain.
