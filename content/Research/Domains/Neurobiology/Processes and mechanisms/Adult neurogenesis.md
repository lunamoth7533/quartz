# Adult neurogenesis

## Definition

Adult neurogenesis is the production of new neurons after development, carrying into adult life the first of the overlapping processes that build the brain. [[P21042938 Stiles 2010 Basics of brain development#^p21042938-processes|Stiles 2010]] Its best-studied site is the dentate gyrus of the hippocampus, where rodents generate neurons throughout life; whether adult humans do so to any meaningful extent is disputed. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-background|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-decline|Sorrells 2021]]

## How it works

**Where new neurons would be added.** The dentate gyrus is the first station of the hippocampal circuit: input from the entorhinal cortex arrives on its granule cells, which pass it on to CA3 and then CA1. [[F61 Neuroscience Online hippocampus#^f61-circuit|Hippocampus]] Adult-born neurons would therefore join the circuit at its entry point, which is why the dentate gyrus is where both sides of the human debate look. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-persistence|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-markers|Sorrells 2021]]

**Two readings of human tissue.** Because direct evidence is hard to obtain in people, the argument turns on post-mortem markers of young neurons and on a few earlier studies that birth-dated new cells. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-background|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] One group reads its data as showing that adult hippocampal neurogenesis continues into the tenth decade of life and falls markedly in Alzheimer's disease. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-persistence|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] The other finds dividing progenitors beside immature doublecortin-positive cells easily in infants, less often in children, and few or none in adults. [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-decline|Sorrells 2021]]

**Why the tissue disagrees.** Proponents report that the way post-mortem tissue is processed and analysed can suppress markers of new neurons until they cannot be detected, which would make negative studies miss them. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-methods|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] Sceptics reply that most doublecortin-stained cells in the adult dentate gyrus look mature, that doublecortin also marks adult neurons in the amygdala and nearby cortex, that detecting it needs no special antigen-retrieval step, and that adults show no germinal zone of dividing precursors; they read RNA-sequencing data as consistent with rare or absent adult neurogenesis. [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-markers|Sorrells 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-rnaseq|Sorrells 2021]] Each argument limits the other side's evidence without settling the count: tissue handling could explain negative findings, and doubtful marker specificity could explain positive ones. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-caution-debate|Appraisal: Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-caution-absence|Appraisal: Sorrells 2021]] The sceptical side calls for direct imaging of dividing precursors or single-cell methods that date a neuron's birth. [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-rnaseq|Sorrells 2021]]

**The antidepressant hypothesis.** A neurogenic account of antidepressant action grew from the finding that prolonged antidepressant treatment increases the birth of new neurons in the adult hippocampus, and it was tied to neurotrophic and synaptic-plasticity ideas about depression. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-increase|Malberg 2021]] Antidepressants are described as counteracting, and sometimes reversing, what stress does to the brain and to newly formed hippocampal cells, perhaps through neurotrophic factors. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-stress|Malberg 2021]] Preclinical ablation studies suggest that neurogenesis may be needed for some antidepressant effects, and recently approved antidepressants are reported to act on neurogenesis and plasticity as well. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-ablation|Malberg 2021]]

**Why the link to mood stays contested.** The hypothesis answers a real puzzle: monoamine accounts fit the weeks-long clinical time course poorly, and cellular plasticity and stress signalling are candidate explanations for the delay. [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-monoamine|How do antidepressants work?]] [[P28153641 Harmer 2017 How do antidepressants work#^p28153641-plasticity|How do antidepressants work?]] But the evidence that neurogenesis is necessary comes from animal ablation, while the human side rests on post-mortem counts whose sensitivity is itself disputed. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-caution-translation|Appraisal: Malberg 2021]] No animal model reproduces a human psychiatric disorder, and whether a model's biology matches the human mechanism is the hardest criterion to meet. [[P20877280 Nestler 2010 Animal models#^p20877280-limits|Animal models]] [[P20877280 Nestler 2010 Animal models#^p20877280-construct|Animal models]] A competing plasticity account centres on synapses rather than new cells: rapid-acting compounds such as ketamine are argued to converge on stronger excitatory synapses and an altered capacity for later plasticity. [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-rapid|Brown 2024]] [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-convergence|Brown 2024]]

**Reading rule.** Keep four claims apart: that adult humans make new hippocampal neurons, how many they make, whether those neurons matter for behaviour, and whether antidepressants work through them. The first is disputed on method, the second is unknown, and the last two rest mainly on animal experiments, so a statement that a treatment helps people by growing new neurons goes beyond the evidence here. [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-caution-absence|Appraisal: Sorrells 2021]] [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-caution-translation|Appraisal: Malberg 2021]]

## Evidence and status

Lifelong hippocampal neurogenesis in rodents is an experimental finding, and so is the increase with prolonged antidepressant treatment on which the neurogenic hypothesis was built. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-background|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-increase|Malberg 2021]] In humans the question is unresolved and is argued in reviews that each present one side of a published debate, drawing on post-mortem tissue. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-limit|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-limit|Sorrells 2021]] The link to mood and antidepressant response is a hypothesis supported mainly by preclinical ablation work and a commemorative review of one research programme. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-ablation|Malberg 2021]] [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-limit|Malberg 2021]]

## Uncertainties

- How many new neurons, if any, the adult human dentate gyrus produces, and at what ages. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-persistence|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-decline|Sorrells 2021]]
- Which markers identify a young neuron in post-mortem human tissue, and how much tissue handling changes detection. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-methods|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-markers|Sorrells 2021]]
- Whether adult-born neurons contribute to antidepressant response in people. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-caution-translation|Appraisal: Malberg 2021]]
- Whether hippocampal neurogenesis differs in bipolar disorder, PTSD or the other conditions this library covers has not been sourced here.

## Recent research

- **2024 · Narrative review (Molecular Psychiatry).** Proposes that rapid-acting antidepressants converge on synaptic strengthening and altered later plasticity, a synaptic rather than neurogenic route that is largely preclinical and does not show conventional drugs work the same way. [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-convergence|Brown 2024]] [[P38177353 Brown 2024 Metaplasticity and sustained antidepressant action#^p38177353-caution-scope|Appraisal: Brown 2024]]
- **2021 · Dual Perspectives review (The Journal of Neuroscience).** Argues that adult human hippocampal neurogenesis persists into old age and that tissue processing explains negative findings. [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-persistence|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]] [[P33762406 Moreno-Jiménez 2021 Evidence for adult hippocampal neurogenesis#^p33762406-methods|Moreno-Jiménez 2021]]
- **2021 · Dual Perspectives review with new data (The Journal of Neuroscience).** Argues that very few, if any, new neurons are born in the adult human hippocampus and that doublecortin is not specific to new neurons. [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-markers|Sorrells 2021]] [[P33762407 Sorrells 2021 Few if any new adult hippocampal neurons#^p33762407-rnaseq|Sorrells 2021]]
- **2021 · Narrative review (Biological Psychiatry).** Looks back on two decades of the neurogenic hypothesis of antidepressant action, which rests on animal ablation evidence. [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-ablation|Malberg 2021]] [[P33771348 Malberg 2021 Adult neurogenesis and antidepressant treatment#^p33771348-caution-translation|Appraisal: Malberg 2021]]
