# The Embryologic Perspective

## Key points (as published)

The section traces how the neural plate folds into the neural tube, which becomes the central nervous system while the ventricular system derives from its hollow centre.
^f29-tube

Development is organised through primary and then secondary vesicles: the prosencephalon, mesencephalon and rhombencephalon divide into the adult forebrain, midbrain and hindbrain regions.
^f29-vesicles

Spinal cord development is described separately from brain vesicles, with the adult cord retaining a regional organisation tied to this early patterning.
^f29-spinal

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section. It presents established embryology at a level suitable for teaching, without the qualifications of a developmental-biology review.
^f29-limit
