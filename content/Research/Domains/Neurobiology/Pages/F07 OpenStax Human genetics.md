## Key points (as published)

Genes are expressed in interaction with environment and experience, so a genotype is a set of instructions read differently in different conditions rather than a fixed destiny.
^f07-expression

Heritability estimates describe how much of the variation in a population is associated with genetic differences, not how strongly genes determine one individual.
^f07-heritability
