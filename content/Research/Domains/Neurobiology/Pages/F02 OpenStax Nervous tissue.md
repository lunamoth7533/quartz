# Nervous Tissue

## Key points (as published)

A prototypical neuron has dendrites that serve as input sites, a cell body containing the nucleus, and an axon that carries output to terminals.
^f02-neuron

Glial cells provide scaffolding, insulation, nutrient transport, waste handling and immune mediation, and recent work suggests a roughly 1:1 ratio of glia to neurons rather than a large excess of glia.
^f02-glia

In some axons glial cells form a myelin sheath that insulates the axon and increases conduction speed, interrupted by the nodes of Ranvier.
^f02-myelin
