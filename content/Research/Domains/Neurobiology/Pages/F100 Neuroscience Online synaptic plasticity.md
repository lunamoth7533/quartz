## Key points (as published)

Short-term facilitation is illustrated by paired pulses: residual calcium from the first action potential increases release on the second, an effect that decays within a short interval.
^f100-facilitation

Temporal summation allows successive inputs to build on one another, changing the postsynaptic response without any lasting structural change.
^f100-summation

Second-messenger cascades can produce enduring enhancement of transmission, which is the cellular basis for describing some forms of plasticity as long-lasting.
^f100-lasting

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on synaptic plasticity using classical experimental preparations. It describes cellular mechanisms rather than learning in intact humans.
