## Key points (as published)

The central nervous system is described region by region: cerebral cortex, thalamus and hypothalamus, basal nuclei, brainstem, cerebellum and spinal cord.
^f05-parts

These regions work in loops and networks, so naming a structure identifies a hub of processing rather than a single function centre.
^f05-networks
