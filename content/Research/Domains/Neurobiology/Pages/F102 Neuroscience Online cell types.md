## Key points (as published)

Dendrites branch extensively and are often covered with spines, which increase the surface available for synaptic contact.
^f102-dendrites

Spines contain cytoskeletal elements responsible for the shape changes observed in some forms of synaptic plasticity, linking structure to function.
^f102-spines

The chapter distinguishes cell-body, dendritic and axonal compartments and notes that some classical distinctions do not hold for every neuron type.
^f102-compartments
