# Basic Structure and Function of the Nervous System

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, section 12.1

## Key points (as published)

The nervous system is described through a central division (brain and spinal cord) and a peripheral division, and functionally through sensory input, integration and motor output.
^f01-divisions

Grey matter is associated with cell bodies and local processing while white matter carries long-range connections, though real circuits do not observe a neat split.
^f01-matter

Autonomic function splits into sympathetic and parasympathetic branches that often oppose each other while cooperating in normal regulation.
^f01-autonomic
