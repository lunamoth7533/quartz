## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The multi-ancestry meta-analysis identified 298 genome-wide significant loci and 36 credible genes, a fourfold increase over previous findings, plus one ancestry-specific association in the East Asian cohort.
^p39843750-loci

The authors report differences in genetic architecture depending on the source of patient ascertainment and on bipolar subtype (type I or type II).
^p39843750-ascertainment

Cell-type analyses implicate GABAergic interneurons and medium spiny neurons, and common and rare variant signals converge on the same genes.
^p39843750-cells

## Scope as reported

Case-control genomic association study. The authors note high heritability (60-80%) while stating that most underlying determinants remained unknown before this analysis.
^p39843750-limit
