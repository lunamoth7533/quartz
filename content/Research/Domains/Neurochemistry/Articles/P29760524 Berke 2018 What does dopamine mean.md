## Reported findings (checked full text)

The review argues that dopamine neurons are not a uniform reward channel: their firing reflects several variables, which makes a single label such as 'reward' or 'prediction error' an approximation.
^p29760524-complexity

Different dopamine neuron populations project to different targets and can carry different signals, so population identity matters for interpretation.
^p29760524-heterogeneity

It cautions against inferring what dopamine does for an individual from group-level reward experiments, and calls for care in transferring computational labels to psychiatric claims.
^p29760524-caution

## Scope as reported

A critical review by a dopamine researcher, published in Nature Neuroscience. It evaluates the interpretation of dopamine signals rather than reporting new experiments.
