## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review notes that the locus coeruleus, the source of most central noradrenaline, was long treated as a uniform nucleus because its widely branching neurons seemed to release noradrenaline across many regions at once.
^p37172640-homogeneous

It summarises newer evidence that the nucleus varies in developmental origin, projection targets, topography, cell morphology and molecular make-up, electrophysiology and sex differences, and proposes this heterogeneity as a basis for its varied behavioural effects.
^p37172640-heterogeneity

## Scope as reported

Narrative review drawing largely on rodent work; no systematic search or quality appraisal is reported.

## Library appraisal
