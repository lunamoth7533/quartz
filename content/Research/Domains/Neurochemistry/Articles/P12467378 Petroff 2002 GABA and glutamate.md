## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review frames cortical excitability as a balance between glutamate's excitatory and GABA's inhibitory action, which is why the pair is treated as one system.
^p12467378-balance

Glutamate is the metabolic precursor of GABA and the two are linked through the tricarboxylic acid cycle, so synthesis and recycling connect them metabolically.
^p12467378-metabolism

GABA metabolites such as homocarnosine and pyrrolidinone occur at unusually high concentration in human brain and have anticonvulsant properties, which makes human GABA metabolism distinctive.
^p12467378-human
