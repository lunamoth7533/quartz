## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Pooling 38 studies, striatal D2/3 receptor availability and combined dopamine synthesis and release capacity did not differ between people with major depressive disorder and controls.
^p37811803-receptors

Dopamine transporter availability was lower in depression only when transporter-selective tracers were pooled; with tracers that also bind the serotonin transporter the difference was not significant. A subgroup analysis suggested greater dopamine release in depression.
^p37811803-transporter

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of in vivo imaging case-control studies; several comparisons rest on few studies, and the authors discuss discrepancies with preclinical findings and call for further work.
^p37811803-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Whether a group difference appears here depends on tracer choice, which is a property of the measurement rather than of the illness, and a pooled group difference says nothing about dopamine in one person.
^p37811803-caution-tracer
