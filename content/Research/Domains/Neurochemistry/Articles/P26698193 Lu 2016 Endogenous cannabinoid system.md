## Reported findings (checked full text)

Endocannabinoids are synthesised on demand rather than stored in vesicles, which makes their release a property of postsynaptic activity rather than of a presynaptic terminal.
^p26698193-synthesis

They commonly act as retrograde messengers, travelling from the postsynaptic cell to presynaptic CB1 receptors to reduce transmitter release.
^p26698193-retrograde

The review covers the system's role across the brain and body, including its involvement in pain, appetite and stress-related processes.
^p26698193-scope
