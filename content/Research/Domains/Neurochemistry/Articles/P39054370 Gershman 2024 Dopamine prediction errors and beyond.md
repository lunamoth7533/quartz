## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors treat reward prediction error as the most influential reading of phasic dopamine but judge its original form too simple, singling out three observations it struggles with: dopamine ramps as reward approaches, responses to sensory and motor features, and influence on action selection.
^p39054370-challenges

They argue that a suitably modified and generalised prediction-error concept can answer each of the three, while other findings seem to need different explanations, so a unifying theory would have to respect the diversity of dopamine signals and the circuitry around them.
^p39054370-generalised

## Scope as reported

A Perspective in Nature Neuroscience built on animal recordings and computational models; it reports no new data and does not address clinical populations.

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.
