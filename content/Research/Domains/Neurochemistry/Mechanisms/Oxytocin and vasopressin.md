# Oxytocin and vasopressin

## Definition

Oxytocin and vasopressin (antidiuretic hormone) are peptides made in the hypothalamus and released from the posterior pituitary into the circulation. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-lobes|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[F119 Neuroscience Online pituitary control#^f119-tract|Hypothalamic control of pituitary hormone]] Besides their bodily actions on birth, nursing, blood pressure and urine concentration, both are also secreted inside the brain, where they act on social behaviour. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-roles|Rigney 2022]]

## How it works

**Made as precursors, released along a dedicated tract.** Both peptides are cut from larger prohormones and carried down the hypothalamo-neurohypophyseal tract with their carrier peptides before release. [[F119 Neuroscience Online pituitary control#^f119-tract|Hypothalamic control of pituitary hormone]] This is the general peptide pattern: synthesis from precursors through successive enzymatic steps makes supply slower to change than for small-molecule transmitters. [[F107 Neuroscience Online neuropeptides and nitric oxide#^f107-processing|Neuropeptides and nitric oxide]] Release of the circulating hormones answers specific physiological signals, with plasma osmolality and angiotensin sensed in the subfornical organ as the textbook examples, so the peripheral hormones sit under homeostatic control. [[F119 Neuroscience Online pituitary control#^f119-release|Hypothalamic control of pituitary hormone]]

**Two routes, two kinds of signal.** The hypothalamus sends neural projections to the posterior pituitary, and the same peptides are also released within the brain, where peptidergic signalling reaches beyond point-to-point synaptic transmission to neighbouring contacts. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-connection|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-roles|Rigney 2022]] [[F107 Neuroscience Online neuropeptides and nitric oxide#^f107-transmission|Neuropeptides and nitric oxide]] Because the routes are distinct, a blood level reflects pituitary secretion and is not a direct measure of signalling inside the brain. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-caution-routes|Appraisal: Rigney 2022]]

**Family resemblance and cross-talk.** Neuropeptides come in families whose sequence relationships predict which receptors a given peptide can activate, so an effect attributed to one peptide is read with possible cross-activation in mind. [[F107 Neuroscience Online neuropeptides and nitric oxide#^f107-families|Neuropeptides and nitric oxide]]

**Social behaviour: what the animal work shows.** In the research reviewed by Rigney and colleagues, oxytocin supports maternal care and bonding and makes social cues more rewarding and salient, while vasopressin influences social communication, investigation, territoriality and aggression, mainly in males. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-effects|Rigney 2022]] Both aid social memory and, in monogamous species, pair bonding, and the effects differ between species. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-species|Rigney 2022]] The mechanism is best established where circuits can be mapped, monitored and manipulated with genetic tools, which is largely animal work. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-limit|Rigney 2022]] A role shown in an animal model is therefore a hypothesis for people, and the "social hormone" label overstates what transfers. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-caution-species|Appraisal: Rigney 2022]]

**The autism trial record.** Trials were motivated by laboratory studies and small trials that suggested social benefit, at a time when many autistic children were already receiving oxytocin. [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-rationale|Sikich 2021]] In the largest paediatric trial, 290 participants aged 3-17 were randomised for 24 weeks; change on the prespecified social-withdrawal outcome was almost identical with oxytocin and placebo, secondary social and cognitive measures generally did not differ, and adverse events were similar. [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-limit|Sikich 2021]] [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-primary|Sikich 2021]] [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-secondary|Sikich 2021]] A 2026 meta-analysis of 42 double-blind trials across mental disorders found a small, non-significant overall effect that shrank to g = 0.05 once two outlying substance-use trials were removed, and only schizophrenia-spectrum trials showed a small significant effect. [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-overall|Bonnieux 2026]] [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-outliers|Bonnieux 2026]] [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-moderators|Bonnieux 2026]]

**Why the trials were largely negative.** Three explanations fit the record, and they are not mutually exclusive. The early positive signal came from small studies, the setting in which false-positive findings are most likely. [[P16060722 Ioannidis 2005 Why most findings are false#^p16060722-conditions|Why most published research findings are false]] The mechanism rests mainly on animals whose peptide systems vary between species. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-species|Rigney 2022]] And trial methods rarely matched what meta-analysts judged optimal, so some null results test delivery and design as much as the peptide. [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-methods|Bonnieux 2026]] A large null result on a prespecified outcome still weighs against a general social benefit, while subgroup, outcome-measure and delivery questions remain hypotheses for new trials. [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-caution-null|Appraisal: Sikich 2021]]

**Reading rule.** Before calling either peptide a "social" molecule, ask which route the claim concerns (blood hormone or brain peptide), which species supplied the mechanism, and whether a human effect comes from a small experiment or a prespecified trial outcome. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-caution-routes|Appraisal: Rigney 2022]] [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-caution-species|Appraisal: Rigney 2022]] [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-caution-null|Appraisal: Sikich 2021]]

## Evidence and status

Synthesis and pituitary release are textbook material. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-lobes|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[F119 Neuroscience Online pituitary control#^f119-tract|Hypothalamic control of pituitary hormone]] Roles in social behaviour are well supported in animal circuit work but vary by species. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-species|Rigney 2022]] For autism, the largest paediatric trial was null on its primary outcome and a broad meta-analysis of double-blind trials found no reliable overall benefit, so intranasal oxytocin is not an established treatment for core autistic features on current evidence. [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-primary|Sikich 2021]] [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-overall|Bonnieux 2026]] The meta-analysis abstract gives no separate autism estimate, so that figure needs checking in the full text. [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-caution-autism|Appraisal: Bonnieux 2026]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether intranasal delivery changes brain peptide signalling enough to test the mechanism in people; trial protocols varied and rarely matched what reviewers judged optimal. [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-methods|Bonnieux 2026]]
- Whether effects differ by sex: trials with more women showed larger effects, a between-trial pattern rather than a within-trial comparison. [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-moderators|Bonnieux 2026]]
- How far vasopressin findings, gathered mostly in male animals, apply to humans. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-effects|Rigney 2022]]
- Receptor distribution and signalling for each peptide in the human brain are not yet sourced in this library.

## Recent research

- **2026 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Neuroscience and Biobehavioral Reviews).** Across 42 double-blind trials the overall effect was small and non-significant, heterogeneity vanished when two outlier trials were dropped, and only schizophrenia-spectrum trials showed a small effect; this extends the negative autism record to the field as a whole. [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-overall|Bonnieux 2026]] [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-outliers|Bonnieux 2026]] [[P42134427 Bonnieux 2026 Intranasal oxytocin across mental disorders#^p42134427-moderators|Bonnieux 2026]]
- **2022 · Narrative review (Endocrinology).** Maps both peptide systems with modern genetic tools and sets their social functions against species diversity, which frames why animal mechanisms have not translated cleanly. [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-limit|Rigney 2022]] [[P35863332 Rigney 2022 Oxytocin vasopressin and social behaviour#^p35863332-species|Rigney 2022]]
- **2021 · Randomised controlled trial (New England Journal of Medicine).** No difference from placebo on social withdrawal at 24 weeks in 290 children and adolescents, the result that most changed the field's expectations. [[P34644471 Sikich 2021 Intranasal oxytocin in autistic children#^p34644471-primary|Sikich 2021]]
