# Acetylcholine Neurotransmission

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 1 Chapter 11

## Key points (as published)

Long-axon cholinergic pathways include a projection from the nucleus basalis of Meynert in the basal forebrain to the neocortex and limbic structures.
^f108-pathways

Degeneration of that pathway is one of the pathologies associated with Alzheimer's disease, which is why cholinergic drugs were pursued in its treatment.
^f108-alzheimer

Acetylcholine is inactivated by enzymatic breakdown in the synapse, and choline is recovered for re-synthesis, so supply of precursor constrains transmission.
^f108-inactivation
