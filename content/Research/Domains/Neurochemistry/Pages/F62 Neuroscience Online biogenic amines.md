# Biogenic Amine Neurotransmitters

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 1 Chapter 12

**Source.** <https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/m/s1/chapter12.html>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F62.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Biogenic amines are grouped into catecholamines - dopamine, noradrenaline and adrenaline - and indolamines, principally serotonin, each with distinct synthetic pathways.
^f62-classes

Dopamine systems are described by projection: mesostriatal, mesolimbocortical and periventricular pathways, with different behavioural associations by target.
^f62-dopamine

Histamine is presented as a recognised neurotransmitter in the nervous system, alongside its roles elsewhere in the body.
^f62-histamine

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter presenting transmitter systems by anatomy and synthesis. Functional labels such as 'reward' or 'mood' are teaching summaries of heterogeneous systems.
^f62-limit
