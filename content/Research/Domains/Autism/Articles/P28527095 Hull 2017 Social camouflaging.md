## Reported findings (checked abstract)

This qualitative study used thematic analysis of 92 autistic adults' accounts of the nature, motivations and consequences of camouflaging to build a three-stage model of the process.
^p28527095-method

Motivations included fitting in and increasing connection with others, camouflaging itself combined masking and compensation techniques, and participants described both effort and perceived benefits and costs.
^p28527095-strategies

Camouflaging was described as demanding and linked by participants to exhaustion and reduced sense of self, which motivates research on assessment and support implications.
^p28527095-consequence

## Scope as reported

A qualitative study of self-reported experience. It documents what participants describe and does not establish prevalence or clinical causality.
^p28527095-limit
