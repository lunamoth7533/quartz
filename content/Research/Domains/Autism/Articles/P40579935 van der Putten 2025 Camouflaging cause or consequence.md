# Camouflaging in autism: A cause or a consequence of mental health difficulties?

**Authors.** van der Putten WJ, Mol AJ, Radhoe TA, et al. (7 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40579935/)

**Published.** 2025 in Autism : the international journal of research and practice (volume 29, issue 10; first online 2025-06-28).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1177/13623613251347104](https://doi.org/10.1177/13623613251347104) · PMID [40579935](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40579935/) · PMCID [PMC12417600](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12417600/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Higher initial camouflaging did not predict worsening mental health over about two years; a small interaction ran the opposite way, with high camouflagers improving slightly and low camouflagers worsening slightly.
^p40579935-camouflage-first

Initial mental health difficulties did not predict later change in total camouflaging, although the assimilation subscale gave a less consistent picture.
^p40579935-mh-first

The authors conclude that their data give no strong support for camouflaging driving the development of mental health difficulties.
^p40579935-conclusion

## Scope as reported

Preregistered multilevel analyses of two measurement waves using the Dutch Camouflaging Autistic Traits Questionnaire, the Symptom Checklist-90 Revised and the Autism Spectrum Quotient. The authors describe the camouflaging effect as small and say more research is needed before firm conclusions about direction and causality.
^p40579935-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Two self-report waves two years apart can test temporal ordering over that interval only; they cannot exclude effects that act over shorter periods, and a middle-aged and older sample may differ from younger adults in how much, and why, they camouflage.
^p40579935-caution-interval
