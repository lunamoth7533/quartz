# Therapeutic effects of antidepressants for global improvement and subdomain symptoms of autism spectrum disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

**Authors.** Liang SC, Sun CK, et al. (9 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35948343/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Pooled against placebo, antidepressants improved restricted and repetitive behaviours with a small effect and global symptoms with a large one, and they also reduced irritability and hyperactivity.
^p35948343-effects

Dropout and stopping because of adverse events did not differ from placebo.
^p35948343-tolerability

In subgroup analyses clomipramine outperformed SSRIs and adults responded better than children or adolescents; milder symptoms and older age went with larger effects.
^p35948343-moderators

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of 16 randomised trials searched to June 2021; the authors name small effect sizes and variable response as limits needing further study.
^p35948343-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Drug-type and age contrasts come from few trials compared across studies rather than within them, and a large global effect from small trials may shrink as better trials accrue; none of this is a treatment recommendation for an autistic person.
^p35948343-caution-subgroups
