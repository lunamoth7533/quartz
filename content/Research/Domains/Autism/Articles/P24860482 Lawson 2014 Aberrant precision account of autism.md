## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Responding to the hypo-prior proposal, the authors ground Bayesian inference in predictive coding and attribute many features of autistic perception to aberrant precision: an imbalance between the precision given to sensory evidence and that given to prior beliefs.
^p24860482-precision

They review direct and indirect empirical findings, including neurobiological ones, that they consider consistent with aberrant encoding of precision.
^p24860482-evidence

## Scope as reported

A theoretical paper that collects findings consistent with the account rather than testing it.
^p24860482-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Because an imbalance can arise from weaker priors, stronger sensory precision or both, the account needs tasks that separate the two terms before a result counts for or against it.
^p24860482-caution-imbalance
