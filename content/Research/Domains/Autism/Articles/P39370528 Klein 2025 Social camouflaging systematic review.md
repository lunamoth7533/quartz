# A systematic review of social camouflaging in autistic adults and youth: Implications and theory.

**Authors.** Klein J, Krahn R, Howe S, et al. (6 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39370528/)

**Published.** 2025 in Development and psychopathology (volume 37, issue 3; first online 2024-10-07).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1017/S0954579424001159](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0954579424001159) · PMID [39370528](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39370528/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Integrating quantitative and qualitative studies, the review found that autistic people camouflage both to protect themselves and to connect with others, using compensation, masking and assimilation behaviours.
^p39370528-motives

Females were more likely to camouflage, and autistic youth and adults showed similar motives, behaviours and after-effects.
^p39370528-gender

The review adds contextual, relational and identity-related consequences and describes possibly two-way links between camouflaging and mental health, cognition and age at diagnosis, whose direction it says still needs testing.
^p39370528-direction

## Scope as reported

A PRISMA-guided narrative synthesis that deliberately combined quantitative and qualitative designs, all age groups and all gender identities. The abstract reports no number of included studies and no risk-of-bias grading, and it names the direction between predictors and consequences, and possible moderators such as stigma and gender identity, as open questions.
^p39370528-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

A synthesis of mostly cross-sectional, self-report studies can map which factors travel with camouflaging; it cannot show that camouflaging causes the mental health outcomes it is associated with, which the review itself leaves open.
^p39370528-caution-direction
