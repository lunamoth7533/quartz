## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors propose that autistic perception reflects attenuated Bayesian priors, termed hypo-priors, so that perception follows incoming sensory information more closely and is less shaped by prior experience.
^p22959875-hypopriors

They argue the idea could explain many sensory and other non-social characteristics of autism, as well as differences in how autistic perception is experienced.
^p22959875-scope

## Scope as reported

A theoretical proposal applying Bayesian models of perception to autism; it presents no new data.
