## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Reviewing studies that compared diagnostic groups or people differing in autistic traits, the authors found highly mixed results, with a slight majority reporting no difference in how priors were integrated.
^p36581168-mixed

Priors formed within an experiment showed reduced influence more often than priors acquired beforehand, and several studies suggested learning differences between groups.
^p36581168-learned

Many studies had low statistical power and inconsistent methods, and the authors propose guidelines for future research.
^p36581168-methods

## Scope as reported

A systematic review that sorts studies by the kind of prior investigated; the abstract does not give the number of included studies.
^p36581168-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Tallying significant and non-significant results across underpowered studies cannot estimate an effect size; the review's value lies in showing where the account holds up better (priors learned in the task) and where it is weaker (long-standing priors).
