# Community views of neurodiversity, models of disability and autism intervention: Mixed methods reveal shared goals and key tensions.

**Authors.** Dwyer P, Gurba AN, et al. (8 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39291753/)

**Published.** 2025 in Autism : the international journal of research and practice (first online 2024-09-18).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1177/13623613241273029](https://doi.org/10.1177/13623613241273029) · PMID [39291753](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39291753/) · PMCID [PMC12332218](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12332218/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Participants who backed the neurodiversity movement favoured changing society and making environments more accommodating, and gave less backing to normalising interventions and to adaptive-skills training, although most participants still endorsed teaching adaptive skills.
^p39291753-support

The view that society alone causes disability was common but not universal, and even people holding it endorsed some help aimed at the individual, such as adaptive skills or treating depression and epilepsy.
^p39291753-social-model

Support for autistic leadership and for reforming society was strong, and the authors conclude that oversimplified rhetoric obscures what advocates actually believe.
^p39291753-leadership

## Scope as reported

A cross-sectional survey combining ratings of the neurodiversity movement, social and medical models and intervention goals with open-ended answers; it measures stated attitudes, not outcomes.
^p39291753-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A self-selected survey sample may over-represent people engaged in the neurodiversity debate and under-represent autistic people who cannot answer a questionnaire, the group at the centre of the main critique.
^p39291753-caution-sample
