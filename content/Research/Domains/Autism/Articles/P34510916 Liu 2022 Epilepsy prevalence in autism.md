## Reported findings (checked abstract)

About one in ten autistic people also had epilepsy, a higher share than in the general population.
^p34510916-prevalence

Prevalence was higher in clinical than in population-based samples and in adolescents and adults than in school-age children, rose with the proportion of female participants and of participants with intellectual disability, and was lower in countries with a higher human development index.
^p34510916-moderators

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of clinical, cross-sectional and cohort studies published up to 2020; the abstract gives no pooled confidence interval or heterogeneity statistic.
^p34510916-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Because epilepsy is more common where intellectual disability is present, any pooled figure depends on the mix of samples; the lower estimate in high-development countries may reflect detection and services as much as risk.
^p34510916-caution-mix
