## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The paper argues that confusion over what neurodiversity and the neurodiversity approaches to disability mean fuels much of the conflict around them.
^p36158596-meaning

It rejects the reading that the approaches locate disability in society alone, which it calls a controversial position, and joins literature holding that both individual characteristics and society contribute to disability.
^p36158596-social-model

It also addresses disputes about whom the approaches cover and what they imply for diagnostic categories, and asks researchers to study people and their contexts, weigh strengths and weaknesses, examine their own biases and learn from neurodivergent people.
^p36158596-scope

## Scope as reported

A single-author conceptual paper drawing on existing literature; its recommendations are argued rather than tested.
^p36158596-limit
