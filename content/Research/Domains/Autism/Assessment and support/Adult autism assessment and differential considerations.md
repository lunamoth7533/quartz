# Adult autism assessment and differential considerations

**Definition.** Adult autism assessment combines developmental history, multi-source information and clinical judgement; screening instruments support referral rather than diagnosis. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-comprehensive|NICE CG142]]

## Supported claims

- Assessment is considered when persistent social-communication difficulties or restricted, repetitive behaviours co-occur with functional, social or service-contact criteria. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-identification|F24]]
- For adults without moderate or severe learning disability the AQ-10 may screen; a score of six or more, or clinical judgement, leads to a comprehensive assessment. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-screening|F24]]
- Comprehensive assessment is team-based and uses informant or documentary evidence of early development and current behaviour. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-comprehensive|F24]]
- It covers features present since childhood, functioning, physical and mental disorders, and other neurodevelopmental conditions. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-domains|F24]]
- Differential assessment includes mental, neurological and physical conditions, communication difficulties and sensory sensitivities; biological, genetic and neuroimaging tests are not used routinely for diagnosis. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-differential|F24]]
- ADHD and autism frequently co-occur, and the earlier exclusion of a dual diagnosis limited research until the criteria were revised. [[P24808851 Leitner 2014 Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD#^p24808851-history|P24808851]]
- Autism varies widely between people in features and support needs, so assessment describes a profile rather than a position on one severity line. [[P31949163 Lord 2020 Autism spectrum disorder primer#^p31949163-construct|Lord 2020 Autism spectrum disorder primer]]
- Screens can produce false positives and false negatives; a score alone neither confirms nor excludes autism, so it leads to assessment rather than diagnosis. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-caution|Appraisal: F24]]

## How it works

**When to assess.** Assessment is considered when autistic features accompany functional difficulties or a relevant service history, rather than on the basis of a trait count alone. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-identification|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]]

**Screening and diagnosis are different acts.** A screening instrument sorts who should be assessed: an AQ-10 score of six or more leads to assessment, not diagnosis, because screen properties such as sensitivity and specificity are population-dependent and a screen optimises for not missing cases at the cost of false positives. The guideline's structure - screen, then comprehensive multi-source assessment, then formulation - exists because no single instrument can carry the differential weight of an adult assessment. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-screening|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]] [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-caution|Appraisal: Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]] [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management]]

**Comprehensive assessment.** Team-based assessment uses informants or documentary evidence where possible and covers childhood features, current functioning, coexisting conditions and other neurodevelopmental conditions. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-comprehensive|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]] [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-domains|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]]

**What the differential has to consider, and why.** Social-communication differences, restricted interests and sensory sensitivities overlap with other neurodevelopmental conditions, with trauma-related adaptations, with social anxiety and with hearing or visual differences, so assessment covers development, current functioning, mental and physical health and alternative explanations - neurodevelopmental, mental, physical, communication and sensory - before the construct is applied. Biological, genetic and imaging tests are not routine for the same reason: none of them discriminates the construct at the individual level. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-differential|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]] [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f24-tests|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]]

**Communication is part of the assessment.** The guideline's communication recommendations - explained language, checked understanding, communication aids and independent advocates - are conditions for the assessment's evidence quality rather than courtesy around the procedure, because accurate self-report and informed consent depend on them.

**Registration and support.** Teams coordinate assessment, interventions, housing, education and employment, so diagnosis is connected to support planning rather than standing alone. [[F24 NICE CG142 autism in adults diagnosis and management#^f25-teams|Autism in adults: diagnosis and management]]

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** Measurement validity is the methods-domain backbone of this article: the assessment's structure is an application of validity, differential-diagnosis and instrument-limit reasoning to one clinical construct. [[Measurement invariance]] [[Measurement validity and reliability]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Assessment pathways, instruments and eligibility differ by country and age group; the guidance cited here describes UK services, and no instrument in this library is a self-test.

## Recent research

- **2024 · Conceptual analysis (Autism in Adulthood).** The review argues that autistic girls and women without intellectual disability are often diagnosed late and after earlier misdiagnoses, with borderline personality disorder a particularly common mislabel, and it sets out features that look alike but may have different drivers. [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-misdiagnosis|McQuaid 2024]] [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-distinctions|McQuaid 2024]] It explains why the presentations are confusable but supplies no accuracy figures. [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-caution-conceptual|Appraisal: McQuaid 2024]]
- **2023 · Clinical cohort study (Psychiatrische Praxis).** In 106 adults referred with suspected autism, affective disorders, phobias and personality disorders were frequent differentials, and the AQ barely separated those later diagnosed from those not (area under the curve 0.527), so the authors favour interdisciplinary assessment with standardised scales over questionnaire triage. [[P37160157 Sappok 2023 Adult autism differentials and AQ utility#^p37160157-differentials|Sappok 2023]] [[P37160157 Sappok 2023 Adult autism differentials and AQ utility#^p37160157-aq|Sappok 2023]] [[P37160157 Sappok 2023 Adult autism differentials and AQ utility#^p37160157-conclusion|Sappok 2023]] Screening accuracy inside a referral clinic is not the same as accuracy in the community. [[P37160157 Sappok 2023 Adult autism differentials and AQ utility#^p37160157-caution-spectrum|Appraisal: Sappok 2023]]
