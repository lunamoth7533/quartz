# ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines

**Definition.** A mechanistic account of ADHD centres on prefrontal networks modulated by dopamine and noradrenaline according to arousal state. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

## Supported claims

- Prefrontal attention networks depend on dopamine and noradrenaline released by arousal. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- Benefit is attributed to alpha-2A and D1 receptors on different spines. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- Imaging in ADHD is described as showing underactive prefrontal regions with weakened connections. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-evidence|Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]
- Mechanistic plausibility does not reduce ADHD to one transmitter deficit. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-caution-transfer|AI synthesis: Prefrontal catecholamine mechanisms]]

## How it works

**Why the prefrontal cortex.** The prefrontal cortex regulates attention, screens out distraction, inhibits impulses and plans, and those circuits depend on noradrenaline and dopamine released according to arousal state. Too little and too much signalling both impair performance, so the relationship between arousal and function is an inverted U rather than "more transmitter, better attention" - the reason the same drug can help or harm depending on state and dose. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]]

**The receptor claim is the strong one.** Noradrenaline benefit is attributed to postsynaptic alpha-2A receptors on some dendritic spines and moderate dopamine benefit to D1 receptors on others. That locates the account at receptor and spine level rather than in whole-brain transmitter balance: the same network is tuned receptor by receptor, and the operative variable is the right receptors at moderate occupancy, not "more catecholamine". The specificity makes the account testable and predicts why non-stimulant alpha-2 agonists and reuptake blockers can act on the same system by different routes. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]]

**What the supporting evidence looks like.** Human imaging in ADHD is described as showing underactive prefrontal regions with weakened connections, and animal work shows alpha-2A effects on prefrontal function, including an ADHD-like profile after alpha-2A blockade. These are converging lines - human imaging, genetic linkage and animal pharmacology - rather than one decisive experiment, a causal demonstration or a clinical trial. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-evidence|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-limit|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]]

**What it does not license.** Mechanistic plausibility does not reduce ADHD to one transmitter deficit, and the review predates later treatment literature. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-caution-transfer|AI appraisal: Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]] [[P29760524 Berke 2018 What does dopamine mean#^p29760524-caution|What does dopamine mean?]] Cognitive profiles dissociate into timing, inhibitory and delay-related components rather than one core deficit, and trial evidence for medication is short-term and rater-dependent. The catecholamine account sits alongside those findings, not above them: it explains part of the presentation and supports specific treatment mechanisms, but does not absorb the heterogeneity, the measurement problems or the long-term evidence gaps. A well-specified mechanism with an explicit refusal to make it the whole story is the model this library follows for neurotransmitter accounts. [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-implication|Beyond the dual pathway model]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-caution-absence|AI appraisal: Comparative interventions for ADHD]] [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]]

**Reading rule.** Distinguish the circuit-level mechanism, the receptor subtype and the clinical effect. Each rests on different evidence, and only the clinical claim is tested in trials with named outcomes. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-stimulants|Comparative interventions for ADHD]]
