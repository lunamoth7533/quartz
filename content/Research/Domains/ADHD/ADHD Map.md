# ADHD Map

ADHD is a developmental condition with an established but uneven evidence base. This map separates what trials show about short-term symptoms from the long-term questions that remain open.

**Contents.** [[#Reference overview|Overview]], [[#Reference spine|Reference spine]], [[#Concept register|Concept register]], [[#Where this domain connects|Cross-domain links]], [[#Evidence boundaries|Evidence boundaries]], [[#Learning route|Learning route]], [[#Domains|Domains]]

## Reference overview

ADHD is a developmental condition defined by impairing inattention, hyperactivity and
impulsivity across settings. Its reference value here is that the evidence base exposes every
general problem in psychiatric research at once: rater effects, duration effects, heterogeneity of
mechanism, and the gap between symptom scales and everyday function.

**The construct is dimensional and developmental.** Symptoms persist into adulthood for many
people, sometimes below full criteria while remaining impairing; presentation changes with age, and
the diagnostic threshold is a convention rather than a natural boundary. Estimated adult
prevalence is around 2.5 percent, and validity of the adult construct is supported by descriptive,
predictive and concurrent evidence. [[ADHD across the lifespan]] [[ADHD diagnosis and measurement]] [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-base|ADHD in adults evidence base]]

**Mechanistic accounts are plural and partly dissociated.** The catecholamine account links particular drug actions and prefrontal functions to an inverted-U model of arousal and performance; neuropsychological
studies find timing, inhibitory and delay-related deficits behaving as independent components
rather than one core deficit; reinforcement-learning accounts formalise choice behaviour; and
network-level accounts describe functional organisation. No single account carries the condition.
[[ADHD and prefrontal catecholamines]] [[Reinforcement learning accounts of ADHD]] [[Attention and executive function]] [[Heterogeneity and presentations in ADHD]]

**Measurement is a first-class subject.** Self-report, informant report and clinician ratings
disagree systematically; drug effects appear on clinician-rated measures for more agents than on
self-report; and laboratory tasks correlate weakly with everyday function. A benefit claim that
does not name the rater is therefore incomplete. [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]]
[[ADHD diagnosis and measurement]]

**Interventions differ by outcome and horizon.** Stimulants and atomoxetine reduce core symptoms
relative to placebo at twelve weeks on both clinician- and self-rated scales; non-pharmacological
interventions showed benefit on clinician-rated measures in the 2025 adult synthesis; the synthesis did not establish medication benefits on quality-of-life outcomes, which is not proof of no benefit in every setting; and evidence at 26 and 52 weeks is thin, which is why
long-term claims rest on weaker designs. [[ADHD medication evidence]] [[Stimulant and non-stimulant mechanisms]] [[CBT for adult ADHD]] [[Short-term versus long-term outcomes]]

**Evidence boundaries.** Certainty ranges from very low to moderate depending on comparison and
outcome; missing evidence for an outcome is not evidence of no effect; and this hub contains no
dose or treatment guidance. [[Efficacy versus tolerability]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-caution-absence|AI synthesis: Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]

**Where to start.** [[ADHD diagnosis and measurement]] and [[ADHD across the lifespan]] for the construct, [[ADHD medication evidence]] and [[CBT for adult ADHD]] for interventions, and [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]] for why the numbers move. ## Reference spine

ADHD is a developmental condition defined by patterns of inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity that cause impairment. The reference literature emphasises three things the popular account omits: heterogeneity of cognitive profile, the role of the rater in measurement, and the gap between short-term symptom evidence and long-term functional questions.

Cognitive accounts are plural. Timing, inhibitory control and delay-related impairments behave as independent components in the same sample, so no single-deficit model fits the group. [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-finding|Beyond the dual pathway model]] [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-implication|Beyond the dual pathway model]] Catecholamine accounts describe an inverted-U relationship between prefrontal noradrenaline and dopamine signalling and network function, with benefit attributed to specific receptor actions. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD]] Reinforcement accounts connect behaviour to prediction error and learning rate. [[P27069377 Schultz 2016 Reward prediction error#^p27069377-rpe|Reward prediction error]] [[F123 Sutton and Barto Reinforcement Learning#^f123-framework|Reinforcement Learning]]

Measurement shapes findings. In a network meta-analysis of 133 trials, every drug tested beat placebo on clinician ratings in children and adolescents while only two did on teacher ratings, and adult results differed again. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]] Tolerability patterns were age-specific. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-tolerability|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]] Data at 26 and 52 weeks were insufficient, which the authors identify as the central gap. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-longterm|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]]

Adult evidence is smaller and more dependent on retrospective report, which changes reliability and the meaning of thresholds. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-base|ADHD in adults evidence base]] Functional outcomes - education, employment, relationships - are separate from symptom counts and belong in any honest summary. [[F23 WHO International Classification of Functioning#^f23-framework|International Classification of Functioning]]

## Evidence boundaries

Short-term symptom evidence is substantial; long-term functional and safety questions are unsettled and individual response is not predicted by mechanism. Nothing here is dosing or titration guidance.

## Evidence gaps

- Trial evidence concentrates on short-term core symptoms rather than long-term function or quality of life.
- Outcome ratings differ systematically between clinicians and participants.
- Adult evidence is younger than child evidence, and generalization across ages is not automatic.
