# Benefits and harms of ADHD interventions: umbrella review and platform for shared decision making.

**Authors.** Gosling CJ, Garcia-Argibay M, et al. (35 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41297970/)

**Published.** 2025 in BMJ (Clinical research ed.).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1136/bmj-2025-085875](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2025-085875) · PMID [41297970](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41297970/) · PMCID [PMC12651917](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12651917/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

In children and adolescents, alpha-2 agonists, amphetamines, atomoxetine, methylphenidate and viloxazine reduced symptoms in the short term with medium to large effects and moderate to high certainty; methylphenidate's benefit was consistent across rater types.
^p41297970-children

In adults, atomoxetine, cognitive behavioural therapy and methylphenidate had at least moderate-certainty evidence of medium-sized symptom benefit, while methylphenidate, amphetamines and atomoxetine were less well tolerated than placebo.
^p41297970-adults

No intervention had high-certainty evidence at the long-term time point.
^p41297970-longterm

## Scope as reported

Umbrella review of systematic reviews with meta-analyses of randomised trials against passive controls, with symptom outcomes analysed by rater (clinician, parent, teacher, self) and time point (12, 26 and 52 weeks); quality was rated with AMSTAR-2 and certainty with an algorithmic GRADE, and results feed an online decision platform.
^p41297970-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

An umbrella review inherits the trials and analytic choices of the meta-analyses it re-estimates; its certainty grades describe average effects against passive controls, not head-to-head comparisons or what will happen for one person.
^p41297970-caution-umbrella
