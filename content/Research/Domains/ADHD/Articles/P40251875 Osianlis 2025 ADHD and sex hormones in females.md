## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across the eleven included studies, the evidence mostly pointed to ADHD symptoms varying with sex hormones in females, especially around puberty and across the menstrual cycle.
^p40251875-evidence

The authors describe knowledge of this relationship as lacking and call for studies of further hormonal milestones, including menopause.
^p40251875-gaps

## Scope as reported

Searches of MEDLINE, Embase and PsycINFO from 1980 to January 2025, with studies grouped by hormonal phase; the studies were few, often small, and varied in populations and outcome measures.
^p40251875-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

With eleven small, heterogeneous studies and no pooled estimate, a hormonal influence on ADHD symptoms is a plausible hypothesis rather than an established mechanism, and it offers no basis for advice to any individual.
^p40251875-caution-preliminary
