# CBT for adult ADHD

**Definition.** Cognitive behavioural approaches for adult ADHD target practical skills, planning and the thought patterns that interact with attention difficulties. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-symptoms|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]

## Supported claims

- CBT with medication was more effective than medication alone for core symptoms in a Cochrane review. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-symptoms|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]
- The authors rated the evidence low-quality, limited by short follow-up, heterogeneous outcomes and geography. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-certainty|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]
- Secondary outcomes such as self-reported depression and anxiety improved in places, while quality-of-life results varied. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-secondary|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]

## How it works

**What the intervention is.** CBT for adult ADHD is a skills-based approach that addresses the practical and cognitive consequences of attentional and executive difficulties - planning, task initiation, time management and the beliefs that accumulate around repeated failure. The treatment model itself - thoughts, behaviour and feelings interacting, with practice between sessions - comes from the general psychotherapy literature. [[F49 OpenStax Psychology 2e types of treatment#^f49-psychotherapy|Types of Treatment]] [[F49 OpenStax Psychology 2e types of treatment#^f49-behavioural|Types of Treatment]] Protocols are tested in randomised trials against control conditions or as an addition to medication, and in the trials CBT is usually delivered alongside medication rather than instead of it, which is why the pooled results are about adding therapy to medication. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-symptoms|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]] [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-certainty|CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Comparative interventions for ADHD]]

**The pooled results, with their comparator.** Adding CBT to medication beat medication alone on clinician-rated and self-rated core symptoms, and CBT also beat a waiting list on self-reported symptoms, where the estimate was large. The estimate against supportive psychotherapy - the comparator that controls best for attention and contact time - was not statistically significant, which is why 'does CBT work' is under-specified without naming the alternative. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-symptoms|Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]] [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-certainty|Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]]

**Certainty and rater effects.** Certainty was low or very low for most comparisons because trials were small and outcome measures heterogeneous. Outcome also depended on the rater - clinician-reported and self-reported results differed - so a benefit claim should name who rated the outcome. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-certainty|Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]] [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-caution-rater|Appraisal: Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]]

**Wider adult evidence.** In the broader 2025 adult component synthesis, non-pharmacological interventions, including psychological components, were better than placebo only on clinician-reported measures, and medications were not efficacious on additional outcomes such as quality of life. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-longterm|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]

**Secondary outcomes.** Some secondary outcomes improved, including self-reported depression and anxiety, while quality-of-life results varied - a reminder that a symptom-scale result and a life-quality result are separate claims. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-secondary|Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-longterm|Comparative interventions for ADHD]]

**Reading rule.** Report the comparator, the rater and the certainty; a component analysis of average effects does not show that an intervention cannot help an individual, and missing evidence for an outcome is not evidence of no benefit. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-caution-absence|Appraisal: Comparative interventions for ADHD]] [[F23 WHO International Classification of Functioning#^f23-domains|International Classification of Functioning]]

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** The measurement of what CBT changes for adult ADHD is a functioning-and-outcomes question, so the endpoint chosen matters as much as the effect size - an instrument that does not index real-world function is measuring something else, which is the measurement-validity issue the methods domain treats as a first-order constraint. [[Functional outcomes and measurement]] [[Effect sizes and uncertainty]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Low-certainty, short-term evidence with varied outcome measures leaves durability and functional gains unresolved.

## Recent research

- **2025 · Umbrella review (BMJ).** Updates the certainty picture: re-estimating published meta-analyses, it found at least moderate-certainty evidence of a medium-sized benefit on adult ADHD symptoms for cognitive behavioural therapy, alongside atomoxetine and methylphenidate, but no high-certainty evidence at the long-term time point for any intervention. [[P41297970 Gosling 2025 Umbrella review of ADHD interventions#^p41297970-adults|Gosling 2025]] [[P41297970 Gosling 2025 Umbrella review of ADHD interventions#^p41297970-longterm|Gosling 2025]] Its estimates are averages against passive controls, so the comparator question above still applies. [[P41297970 Gosling 2025 Umbrella review of ADHD interventions#^p41297970-caution-umbrella|Appraisal: Gosling 2025]]
