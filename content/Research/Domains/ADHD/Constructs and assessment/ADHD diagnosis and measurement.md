# ADHD diagnosis and measurement

**Definition.** ADHD assessment combines history, rating scales and impairment across settings, because no confirmatory laboratory test exists. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]

## Supported claims

- The use of objective diagnostic measures is listed among the open questions in adult ADHD. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults review]]
- Evidence from included studies came from large samples and specific meta-analysis rules. [[P33549739 Faraone 2021 World Federation ADHD consensus statement#^p33549739-methods|World Federation ADHD consensus statement]]
- Non-pharmacological interventions showed benefit only on clinician-rated measures in a 2025 adult synthesis. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Comparative efficacy of ADHD interventions in adults]]
- Clinician-reported and self-reported outcomes can disagree, so a benefit claim should name the rater. [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-caution-rater|Appraisal: CBT for ADHD (Cochrane review)]]

## How it works

**Impairment is part of the definition.** ADHD is a developmental pattern of inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity that interferes with functioning or development, so impairment is part of the definition rather than a severity add-on, and functional measures belong in assessment alongside symptom counts. Diagnosis rests on history across settings, functional impact and developmental course rather than on a single questionnaire or task. [[F86 NIMH ADHD#^f86-definition|ADHD]] [[F23 WHO International Classification of Functioning#^f23-framework|International Classification of Functioning]]

**Adult assessment leans on recall.** Adult diagnosis relies more on retrospective history of childhood and on self-report, which introduces recall and rater problems that are less prominent in child assessment. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-base|ADHD in adults: evidence base]]

**No objective test.** Objective diagnostic measures are listed among the open questions in adult ADHD, and this library's own caution states that no individual diagnostic biomarker is established. Laboratory cognitive tasks correlate weakly with each other and with everyday function, so they do not replace clinical assessment: diagnosis is a judgement built from reported symptoms, impairment and history as judged by specifiable informants. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults: evidence base]] [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-caution-biomarker|Appraisal: ADHD in adults: evidence base]] [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-implication|Beyond the dual pathway model]]

**Rater and setting change the answer.** In the medication network meta-analysis every drug beat placebo on clinicians' ratings in children and adolescents while only two did on teachers' ratings, and the Cochrane review of psychological interventions reports that clinician-reported and self-reported results differed. A measurement claim in this domain should name the informant. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]] [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-caution-rater|Appraisal: Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]]

**Evidence thresholds shape what is asserted.** The consensus statement included studies with more than 2000 participants or meta-analyses meeting comparable thresholds, and excluded meta-analyses without a publication-bias assessment except for prevalence - a documented inclusion rule that explains part of the apparent certainty of consensus documents. [[P33549739 Faraone 2021 World Federation ADHD consensus statement#^p33549739-methods|ADHD consensus statement]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Comparative interventions for ADHD]]

**Reading rule.** For any ADHD claim, state the age group, the informant, the classification system and whether impairment was assessed; a functioning measure answers a different question from a symptom measure. [[F23 WHO International Classification of Functioning#^f23-framework|International Classification of Functioning]] [[P20410727 Sonuga-Barke 2010 Beyond the dual pathway#^p20410727-design|Beyond the dual pathway model]]

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** Diagnostic reliability sits on top of the measurement-validity literature in the methods domain - a screen that is reliable across time and informants is still not valid without content and construct evidence, and a diagnostic threshold inherits the scale's properties. [[Measurement validity and reliability]] [[Measurement invariance]]

## Limitation or common misconception

Rating scales measure reports rather than mechanisms, and informant disagreement does not settle a diagnosis either way.

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review (Journal of Child and Adolescent Psychopharmacology).** Shows that the outcome side of measurement is unstandardised as well: just over half of 167 ADHD medication trials defined response as a percentage fall on a rating scale, most often 30% but also 25%, 40% or 50%, and the rest set no threshold at all. [[P40365735 Roy 2025 Response definitions in ADHD medication trials#^p40365735-thresholds|Roy 2025]] [[P40365735 Roy 2025 Response definitions in ADHD medication trials#^p40365735-consensus|Roy 2025]] Responder rates from trials with different cut-offs are therefore not directly comparable. [[P40365735 Roy 2025 Response definitions in ADHD medication trials#^p40365735-caution-threshold|Appraisal: Roy 2025]]
- **2024 · Multinational survey with psychometric analysis (Journal of Attention Disorders).** Qualifies adult screening: in 72,627 adults across 42 countries the Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale screener was reliable and partially invariant across languages, countries and genders, yet about one in five respondents screened as at risk, which the authors read as low specificity. [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-limit|Lewczuk 2024]] [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-invariance|Lewczuk 2024]] [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-atrisk|Lewczuk 2024]] [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-specificity|Lewczuk 2024]] A positive screen is a prompt for assessment rather than a diagnosis. [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-caution-screening|Appraisal: Lewczuk 2024]]
