# Rater and measurement effects in ADHD

## Definition

Rater effects are systematic differences between informants who report on the same person. They are a property of the measurement situation, not random error.

## How it works

**Rater divergence at trial scale.** In a network meta-analysis of ADHD medication trials, every drug beat placebo on clinicians' ratings at 12 weeks in children and adolescents, while on teachers' ratings only methylphenidate and modafinil did; in adults, amphetamines, methylphenidate, bupropion and atomoxetine beat placebo. A single set of randomised comparisons therefore produced a broad effect on one rater's scale and a much narrower effect on another's - a large rater-dependent difference that is a property of the measurement situation rather than of the drug alone. [[P30097390 Cortese 2018 ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability#^p30097390-efficacy|ADHD medication efficacy and tolerability]]

**Setting and context are not noise by definition.** Parents and teachers observe different demands, tasks, contexts and comparison groups, so informant disagreement may reflect genuine situational variation rather than error - a child who copes in a structured classroom can struggle in an unstructured evening. The methodological question is not which informant is right but what each setting samples.

**Adult measurement.** Adult assessment leans on different sources again - self-report and retrospective recall of childhood - which changes both reliability and the meaning of a threshold. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-base|ADHD in adults: evidence base]]

**Component analyses inherit the dependency.** In adults, stimulants and atomoxetine beat placebo on both self-reported and clinician-reported scales, while cognitive behavioural therapy, cognitive remediation, mindfulness, psychoeducation and transcranial direct current stimulation were better than placebo only on clinician-reported measures. The Cochrane synthesis shows the same pattern and cautions that a benefit claim should name who rated it. [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-stimulants|Comparative interventions for ADHD]] [[P39701638 Ostinelli 2025 Comparative interventions for ADHD#^p39701638-nonpharm|Comparative interventions for ADHD]] [[P29566425 Lopez 2018 Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD#^p29566425-caution-rater|AI appraisal: Cognitive behavioural interventions for ADHD]]

**Instruments carry their own assumptions.** Comparing scores assumes an instrument measures the same construct the same way across groups and time; that property - measurement invariance - comes in configural, metric and scalar levels with progressively stronger constraints, and partial or absent invariance changes what a group comparison means. Scale development imposes a second set of requirements: content validity is assessed against a defined domain before administration, reliability statistics such as alpha, omega and test-retest coefficients describe consistency, and criterion and construct validity are assessed afterwards against other measures. Reliability is not validity; an instrument can produce consistent scores that consistently miss the construct. [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-definition|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-levels|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-practice|Measurement invariance]] [[P29942800 Boateng 2018 Developing and validating scales#^p29942800-phases|Developing and validating scales]] [[P29942800 Boateng 2018 Developing and validating scales#^p29942800-reliability|Developing and validating scales]] [[P29942800 Boateng 2018 Developing and validating scales#^p29942800-caution-score|AI appraisal: Developing and validating scales]]

**Why the review pipeline does not resolve it.** Pooling across raters would obscure the pattern, which is why trials and meta-analyses report them separately. Systematic reviews grade certainty partly by inconsistency and imprecision, which is what rater disagreement produces when results are pooled: GRADE rates certainty down for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias rather than averaging disagreement away, and reporting raters separately, as these trials do, is the honest alternative. [[P18436948 Guyatt 2008 GRADE#^p18436948-domains|GRADE]] [[P18436948 Guyatt 2008 GRADE#^p18436948-certainty|GRADE]]

**Where objective measurement would sit.** The 2025 evidence review lists objective diagnostic measures among the open questions in adult ADHD; no established individual biomarker exists in this library, so thresholds rest on reported symptoms, impairment and history as judged by specifiable informants. That is a measurement situation to describe rather than a defect to be papered over by preferring one informant on principle. [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-open|ADHD in adults: evidence base]] [[P40948064 Cortese 2025 ADHD in adults evidence base#^p40948064-caution-biomarker|AI appraisal: ADHD in adults: evidence base]]

## Evidence and status

Rater effects are well documented in ADHD trials; how best to combine informants for classification remains debated.
