# ADHD in girls and women

## Definition

ADHD in girls and women covers how ADHD presents, is recognised and is studied in females, whose diagnosed rates are far below males' in childhood, with the gap narrowing among people diagnosed as adults. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-ratio|Martin 2024]] The central question is how much of that gap reflects differences in the condition, in how it shows, and in who is referred and diagnosed. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-overshadowing|Martin 2024]] [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-caution-records|Appraisal: Martin 2024]]

## How it works

**The diagnosed sex ratio shifts with age.** In national healthcare records for 16,458 young people with ADHD in Wales, males outnumbered females about four to one overall, nearly five to one for diagnoses before age 12 and under two to one for diagnoses after 18. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-ratio|Martin 2024]] Adult screening points the same way: across 72,627 respondents in 42 countries, 21.4% of women and 18.1% of men screened as at risk, a rate far above adult prevalence that marks a prompt for assessment rather than a diagnosis. [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-atrisk|Lewczuk 2024]] [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-caution-screening|Appraisal: Lewczuk 2024]]

**Recognition comes later, often after other diagnoses.** In the Welsh records, females were diagnosed later on average (12.6 versus 10.9 years), and males were more likely to be prescribed ADHD medication. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-timing|Martin 2024]] Before their ADHD diagnosis, females more often had recorded anxiety, depression or other mental health diagnoses and antidepressant prescriptions, which the authors read as possible diagnostic overshadowing or initial misdiagnosis. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-overshadowing|Martin 2024]] Records cannot tell whether an earlier diagnosis was a missed ADHD presentation, a genuine co-occurring condition or both, and they do not count people who were never diagnosed. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-caution-records|Appraisal: Martin 2024]]

**Presentation differences are real but small.** A meta-analysis of 52 studies of diagnosed females and males found males more severe on hyperactivity-impulsivity, and on rating scales boys scored higher than girls on that dimension; in adults, men were rated more inattentive than women, with no difference in hyperactivity-impulsivity. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-hyperactivity|Young 2024]] [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-adults|Young 2024]] Every significant difference was small, and clinical interviews showed no sex difference in severity at any age. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-interview|Young 2024]] None of the reported differences shows girls or women as more inattentive than boys or men, so a mainly inattentive female picture is better read as less overt hyperactivity measured by particular scales. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-adults|Young 2024]] [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-caution-diagnosed|Appraisal: Young 2024]] Because every sample was already diagnosed, the meta-analysis cannot show what unrecognised girls and women look like. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-caution-diagnosed|Appraisal: Young 2024]]

**Co-occurring conditions shape recognition.** Across sex-stratified studies, co-occurring ADHD and PTSD was diagnosed more often in females (pooled odds ratio about 1.3), an excess that appeared only in adult samples. [[P40252259 Wilson 2025 Sex differences in ADHD and PTSD#^p40252259-sex|Wilson 2025]] [[P40252259 Wilson 2025 Sex differences in ADHD and PTSD#^p40252259-age|Wilson 2025]] A sex difference in a diagnosed combination can reflect exposure, recognition or referral as well as vulnerability. [[P40252259 Wilson 2025 Sex differences in ADHD and PTSD#^p40252259-caution-diagnosis|Appraisal: Wilson 2025]] With the earlier anxiety and depression diagnoses in the register data, this places much of the female picture at the boundary with internalising and trauma-related conditions, where differential reasoning is hardest. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-overshadowing|Martin 2024]]

**Hormonal influences are a hypothesis.** A systematic review found eleven studies relating ADHD symptoms to sex hormones or hormonal stages; most pointed to symptoms varying with hormonal state, especially around puberty and across the menstrual cycle. [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-evidence|Osianlis 2025]] The studies were few, often small and varied in populations and measures, and the authors call for work on further milestones, including menopause. [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-limit|Osianlis 2025]] [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-gaps|Osianlis 2025]] Hormonal variation is therefore a plausible influence, not an established mechanism, and it supports no advice for an individual. [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-caution-preliminary|Appraisal: Osianlis 2025]]

**Measurement is part of the gap.** The adult screener used across 42 countries was only partially invariant across genders, so comparing women's and men's raw scores assumes more than the data support. [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-invariance|Lewczuk 2024]] [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-caution-screening|Appraisal: Lewczuk 2024]] The contrast between rating scales and clinical interviews in the meta-analysis makes the same point from another direction. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-interview|Young 2024]]

**Reading rule.** Before reading a sex difference as a difference in ADHD, check whether the sample was diagnosed, referred or drawn from the population, which instrument and informant were used, and whether the figure counts diagnoses, prescriptions or symptoms; each of these changes the answer in this literature. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-limit|Young 2024]] [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-ratio|Martin 2024]] [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-caution-diagnosed|Appraisal: Young 2024]]

## Evidence and status

The evidence combines a meta-analysis of diagnosed samples, a national register cohort, a meta-analysis of diagnosed ADHD-PTSD comorbidity, a small systematic review of hormonal studies and multinational screening data. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-limit|Young 2024]] [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-limit|Martin 2024]] [[P40252259 Wilson 2025 Sex differences in ADHD and PTSD#^p40252259-limit|Wilson 2025]] [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-limit|Osianlis 2025]] [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-limit|Lewczuk 2024]] It shows that diagnosed females are fewer and diagnosed later, often after internalising diagnoses, and that severity differences among the diagnosed are small and depend on the instrument. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-timing|Martin 2024]] [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-interview|Young 2024]] It does not establish a distinct female form of ADHD, how many girls and women go unrecognised, or a hormonal mechanism. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-caution-diagnosed|Appraisal: Young 2024]] [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-caution-records|Appraisal: Martin 2024]] [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-caution-preliminary|Appraisal: Osianlis 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- How many girls and women with impairing ADHD are never diagnosed; register data count diagnoses, not missed cases. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-caution-records|Appraisal: Martin 2024]]
- Whether compensatory strategies, often proposed in clinical writing, hide impairment in girls and women; no source held here tests this.
- Whether sex-specific norms or thresholds would improve recognition without inflating diagnosis, given screening rates already far above prevalence. [[P38180045 Lewczuk 2024 ADHD screener across 42 countries#^p38180045-caution-screening|Appraisal: Lewczuk 2024]]
- How symptoms change across later hormonal transitions such as pregnancy and menopause. [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-gaps|Osianlis 2025]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Systematic review (Journal of attention disorders).** Gathers the small literature on sex hormones and ADHD symptoms in females: suggestive for puberty and the menstrual cycle, with menopause still unstudied. [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-evidence|Osianlis 2025]] [[P40251875 Osianlis 2025 ADHD and sex hormones in females#^p40251875-gaps|Osianlis 2025]]
- **2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Psychological medicine).** Separates rating scales from clinical interviews and finds sex differences in severity only on rating scales, all of them small. [[P39494848 Young 2024 ADHD symptom severity in females and males#^p39494848-interview|Young 2024]]
- **2024 · National register cohort (Journal of child psychology and psychiatry, and allied disciplines).** Documents later recognition of females after anxiety and depression diagnoses, the pattern behind the overshadowing hypothesis. [[P38864317 Martin 2024 Sex differences in ADHD diagnosis#^p38864317-overshadowing|Martin 2024]]

## Connections

- [[Heterogeneity and presentations in ADHD]] - sex is one axis of the heterogeneity described there: presentation labels record dominant symptoms, and sex differences in them are small and instrument-dependent.
- [[ADHD diagnosis and measurement]] - multi-source assessment and retrospective recall are where recognition of girls and women succeeds or fails, so informant and instrument choices weigh heavily here.
- [[ADHD across the lifespan]] - the sex ratio narrows with age at diagnosis, so part of the adult picture is people first recognised long after childhood.
- [[Rater and measurement effects in ADHD]] - sex differences appear on rating scales but not in clinical interviews, a rater-and-instrument effect of the kind that note describes.
- [[Co-occurrence and differential reasoning]] - diagnostic overshadowing is a differential-reasoning problem: an earlier anxiety or depression diagnosis may be a missed ADHD presentation, a co-occurring condition or both.
- [[Trauma and PTSD in autistic and ADHD people]] - where the adult female excess in diagnosed ADHD-PTSD comorbidity is weighed against exposure, recognition and referral explanations.
- [[Masking and camouflaging]] - the autism literature's account of strategies that hide difficulties; the compensatory strategies proposed for ADHD are an untested parallel.
- [[Measurement invariance]] - an adult ADHD screener was only partially invariant across genders, so raw-score comparisons between women and men assume what has to be tested.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** This note joins the ADHD domain's measurement problems to the clinical-psychiatry domain's differential reasoning and the research-methods domain's invariance checks: a sex difference in diagnosed rates can arise from biology, presentation, referral or instrument, and only designs that separate these can say which.
