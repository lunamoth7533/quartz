# Autonomic measurement and heart rate variability

## Definition

Autonomic measurement estimates the activity of sympathetic and parasympathetic pathways through indirect indices, of which heart rate variability is the most widely used. Each index reflects several influences at once.

## How it works

**Rest and reflex control.** Autonomic control keeps short-term blood pressure and heart rate stable, with parasympathetic activity predominating at rest, and the two divisions are cooperative as well as opposing - most organs receive both, and regulation depends on the balance of their activity rather than on one branch at a time. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-auto|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-cooperation|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

**Signals and integration.** Heart rate, blood pressure, skin conductance, pupil diameter and heart rate variability are the common measures, and each is downstream of multiple control loops. Thermoregulation demonstrates how autonomic, endocrine and behavioural responses are coordinated to a single demand, which is why a recorded change in one channel rarely identifies one cause. [[F63 Neuroscience Online central autonomic control#^f63-integration|Central Control of the Autonomic Nervous System]]

**Variability metrics.** Time-domain, frequency-domain and non-linear metric families describe different aspects of the same record, have different recording requirements and are not interchangeable; respiratory influences and baroreflex activity both contribute. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-metrics|Heart rate variability metrics]] Baseline values depend on recording length, age, sex and posture, and published norms are not interchangeable. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-context|Heart rate variability metrics]] The LF/HF ratio is controversial and is not a clean index of autonomic balance, because autonomic interactions are non-linear and respiration contaminates the band. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-lfhf|Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-confounds|Heart rate variability metrics]]

**Task and state dependence.** Autonomic measures respond to posture, breathing, movement, emotion, medication and time of day, and metric choice and recording length also influence a recorded value, so recording conditions determine what a number means and a single short recording cannot separate the influences involved. [[F32 OpenStax Divisions of the autonomic nervous system#^f32-cooperation|Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-context|Heart rate variability metrics]]

**Afferent side and the construct jump.** Interoceptive signals reaching cortex carry information about internal state, and the interoceptive system they feed has a proposed cortical representation, which is why autonomic measures are also used in studies of emotional processing - as markers of stress, emotion regulation and interoceptive state in the wider literature. The inference from a beat-to-beat series to a psychological state crosses at least two jumps - signal to physiology, physiology to construct - and each jump needs its own evidence. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Interoception]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Interoception]]

**Reading rule.** State the metric, the recording conditions, the population norm used and the construct claimed; then check whether the study reports all four. The library's stance is that autonomic claims stay at the level of physiology and model-building rather than personal interpretation. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-caution-marker|AI appraisal: Heart rate variability metrics]] [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-limit|Heart rate variability metrics]]

## Evidence and status

The physiological sources of heart rate variability are well characterised; the leap from a variability index to a psychological construct such as 'emotion regulation capacity' is generally not warranted by measurement alone. [[P29034226 Shaffer 2017 Heart rate variability metrics#^p29034226-limit|Heart rate variability metrics]]
