## Key points (as published)

The adrenal cortex is organised into zones with different outputs: mineralocorticoids such as aldosterone from the glomerulosa, glucocorticoids such as cortisol from the fasciculata, and androgens from the reticularis.
^f64-cortex

The adrenal medulla is neuroendocrine tissue that releases adrenaline and noradrenaline as part of the sympathetic response, linking rapid neural signalling to circulating hormones.
^f64-medulla

Cortisol secretion follows ACTH from the pituitary, which follows hypothalamic CRH, and corticosteroid levels feed back on the hypothalamus and pituitary to limit further release.
^f64-drive
