# Central Control of the Autonomic Nervous System and Thermoregulation

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 4 Chapter 3

**Source.** <https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/m/s4/chapter03.html>

## Key points (as published)

The chapter describes a central autonomic network: sites receiving visceral input interconnect with those controlling sympathetic and parasympathetic output, and with somatic, endocrine and limbic circuitry.
^f63-network

Pre-autonomic neurons are classified by type and location, showing that autonomic control originates from several distinct cell groups rather than one centre.
^f63-preganglionic

Thermoregulation is used to demonstrate integration of autonomic, endocrine and behavioural responses to a single physiological demand.
^f63-integration

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on central autonomic organisation, based on anatomical tracing and physiological experiments in animals.
^f63-limit
