# Blood Brain Barrier and Cerebral Metabolism

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 4 Chapter 11

## Key points (as published)

Brain endothelial cells are joined by continuous tight junctions that prevent paracellular movement, and they lack the transendothelial vesicular pathways found elsewhere.
^f105-tight

Astrocytes and other supporting cells are required for the barrier to develop and to maintain its properties.
^f105-support

Barrier function is selective rather than absolute: gases diffuse freely while ions, nutrients and larger molecules depend on specific transporters.
^f105-transport

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on barrier structure and cerebral metabolism. Findings derive substantially from animal and cell studies.
^f105-limit
