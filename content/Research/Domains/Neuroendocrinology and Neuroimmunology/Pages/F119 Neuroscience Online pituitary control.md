# Hypothalamic Control of Pituitary Hormone

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 4 Chapter 2

## Key points (as published)

Oxytocin and vasopressin are cleaved from prohormones and released along the hypothalamo-neurohypophyseal tract, packaged with their carrier peptides.
^f119-tract

Release of these peptides is controlled by specific physiological signals, such as plasma osmolality and sensors in the subfornical organ for angiotensin.
^f119-release

The chapter describes feedback regulation of pituitary hormones, including dopaminergic control of prolactin, so each axis has its own control logic.
^f119-feedback

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on hypothalamic control of pituitary hormones, covering several axes in summary form.
