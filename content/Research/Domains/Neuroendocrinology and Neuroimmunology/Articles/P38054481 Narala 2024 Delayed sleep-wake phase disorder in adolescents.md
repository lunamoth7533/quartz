## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review identifies delayed sleep-wake phase disorder as the commonest circadian rhythm disorder of adolescence, with reported prevalence between 1% and 16%, and notes frequent association with ADHD and autism as well as substance use, anxiety and depression.
^p38054481-prevalence

It lists history, sleep logs, actigraphy and dim-light melatonin onset among diagnostic measures, and sleep hygiene, chronotherapy, melatonin and bright light among treatment approaches.
^p38054481-assessment

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

An association between a timing disorder and neurodevelopmental conditions does not say whether delayed phase is intrinsic to the condition, a consequence of behaviour and light exposure, or both.
