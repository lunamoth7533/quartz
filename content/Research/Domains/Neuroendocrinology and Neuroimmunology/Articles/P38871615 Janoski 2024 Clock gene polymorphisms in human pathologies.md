## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review restates that circadian rhythms are synchronised by external cues such as light yet persist without them, produced by interlocking transcriptional-translational feedback loops that drive rhythmic expression of core clock genes.
^p38871615-entrain

It summarises genome-wide association and forward genetic studies in which single-nucleotide variants in core clock genes influence gene regulation and correlate with risk for various conditions, and calls for more thorough study of these variants.
^p38871615-variants

## Scope as reported

Narrative review of variant-phenotype associations across many conditions; it does not pool effect sizes.
^p38871615-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

A clock-gene variant that correlates with a condition shows a shared genetic influence, not that disrupted timing causes the condition; pleiotropy and small effects are the usual alternatives to rule out.
^p38871615-caution-association
