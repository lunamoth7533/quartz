## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review defines allostatic load as the cumulative burden of stressful life events and chronic stress, with allostatic overload arising when environmental challenges exceed a person's capacity to cope.
^p37253338-definition

It argues for assessing load with clinimetric indices and rating scales alongside biomarkers, to capture the psychosocial environment that current formulations miss, and links load to unexplained hormone values, illness behaviour, treatment response and residual symptoms.
^p37253338-assessment

## Scope as reported

Narrative review from a clinical-endocrinology perspective; it proposes models of care rather than testing them.
^p37253338-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Adding rating scales to biomarkers widens what 'load' covers, which helps clinical description but makes indices harder to compare across studies.
