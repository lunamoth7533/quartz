## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review states that autistic children and adolescents have sleep disturbances, insomnia above all, far more often than the general population, and that the resulting sleep loss is associated with daytime behaviour problems and parental stress.
^p38302207-insomnia

It describes behavioural interventions, parent education and melatonin as effective treatments and reviews the epidemiology of sleep disturbance in autistic youth.
^p38302207-treatment

## Scope as reported

Clinical narrative review; the abstract gives no prevalence figures, and its treatment statements summarise the literature rather than new trials.
^p38302207-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Linking sleep loss to daytime behaviour in clinical data leaves the direction open, because behaviour and family stress can disturb sleep as well as follow from it.
^p38302207-caution-direction
