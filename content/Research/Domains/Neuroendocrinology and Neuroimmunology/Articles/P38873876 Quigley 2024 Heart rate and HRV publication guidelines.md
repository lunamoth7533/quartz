## Reported findings (checked abstract)

A committee report for the Society for Psychophysiological Research gives methodological, interpretive and reporting guidance for heart rate and heart rate variability studies, covering electrocardiographic and photoplethysmographic recording in laboratory, ambulatory and brain-imaging contexts.
^p38873876-guidance

It weighs the strengths and weaknesses of recording and derivation methods, reviews the origin of the heartbeat and its neural control, and ends with design, analysis and reporting checklists that replace committee guidance several decades old.
^p38873876-checklists

## Scope as reported

Part 1 of a guideline series; it sets methodological standards rather than reporting new data.
^p38873876-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Reporting standards make studies comparable but do not by themselves validate a psychological interpretation of any heart rate variability index.
