## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Compared with healthy controls, adults with depression had lower resting SDNN, RMSSD, pNN50, low-frequency power and high-frequency power, with the largest standardised difference for SDNN.
^p38169979-lower

The low-frequency to high-frequency ratio did not differ between groups; the authors read the overall pattern as pointing to higher cardiovascular risk in depression.
^p38169979-lfhf

## Scope as reported

Case-control studies searched to December 2022 in Chinese and English databases; study quality rated with the EPHPP tool.
^p38169979-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Pooled differences in resting indices describe groups; the step to cardiovascular risk is the authors' inference rather than an outcome the studies measured, and none of it supports reading one person's value.
^p38169979-caution-inference
