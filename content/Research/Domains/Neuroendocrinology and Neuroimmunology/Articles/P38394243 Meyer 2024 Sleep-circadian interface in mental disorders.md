## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review describes sleep, circadian rhythms and mental health as reciprocally linked: disrupted sleep quality, continuity and timing can precipitate or worsen psychiatric symptoms in susceptible people, and those symptoms can in turn worsen sleep and disturb clock-controlled processes.
^p38394243-reciprocal

It presents sleep-circadian dysfunction as a transdiagnostic contributor across mood, anxiety and psychosis-spectrum disorders, notes that treatments aimed at it can reduce psychopathology, and argues that sleep and circadian factors need an integrated rather than separate treatment in research.
^p38394243-transdiagnostic

## Scope as reported

Narrative review with a biological-mechanism emphasis and a focus on adolescents and young adults; no systematic search or pooled estimates.
