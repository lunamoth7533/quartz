## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The barrier is formed by brain endothelial cells joined by tight junctions, supported by pericytes and astrocytes, and it restricts what enters the central nervous system.
^p32211826-components

The review is organised around unanswered questions, including how the barrier changes across regions, ages and disease states.
^p32211826-open

It cautions that barrier integrity is not simply open or closed: regulated transport, leakage and immune cell entry are distinct phenomena.
^p32211826-permeability
