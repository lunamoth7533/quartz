# Meta-analysis of soluble tumour necrosis factor receptors in severe mental illnesses.

**Published.** 2023 in Journal of psychiatric research.

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Soluble TNF receptor 1 was higher in severe mental illness overall and most clearly in bipolar disorder, while soluble TNF receptor 2 was also raised but with a smaller effect.
^p37515950-receptors

In subgroup analyses, younger age at onset, longer illness duration and psychotropic medication were each associated with higher receptor levels, especially for receptor 1.
^p37515950-moderators

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of studies from PubMed and Scopus; the authors call for diagnosis-specific samples and longitudinal study of each medication's effect to address causality.
^p37515950-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

When onset age, illness duration and medication each move the marker, a pooled patient-control difference partly reflects illness course and treatment rather than the condition alone.
