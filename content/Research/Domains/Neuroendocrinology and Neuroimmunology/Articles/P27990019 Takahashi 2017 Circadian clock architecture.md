## Reported findings (checked full text)

The core clock is a transcriptional-translational feedback loop in which clock proteins repress their own transcription over roughly 24 hours.
^p27990019-loop

Most tissues contain their own clocks that are coordinated by the suprachiasmatic nucleus, so circadian organisation is distributed rather than located in one structure.
^p27990019-tissue

Clock output influences metabolism, physiology and behaviour, which is why disrupted timing has wide-ranging rather than sleep-specific consequences.
^p27990019-outputs
