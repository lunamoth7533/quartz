## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Across 44 studies, alpha diversity and the relative abundance of Firmicutes and Bacteroidetes did not differ overall between people with depressive disorder and controls, although region and medication status shifted some subgroup estimates.
^p38065935-diversity

Several butyrate-producing genera were consistently depleted and some other genera enriched in depression; meta-regression left the heterogeneity unexplained, and the authors note that medication, diet, measurement method and diagnostic classification may shape findings.
^p38065935-genera

## Scope as reported

Meta-analysis of observational case-control studies searched to March 2023 across eight databases.
^p38065935-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Composition differences that move with medication, diet and region are what confounding would also produce; they describe groups and do not show that microbiota changes cause depression.
^p38065935-caution-confounds
