## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review frames a long-standing dispute about what glucocorticoids do during stress: whether they enable, stimulate or suppress the stress response.
^p10696570-contested

It proposes that glucocorticoid actions be classified as permissive, suppressive, stimulatory or preparative, depending on the context and timing.
^p10696570-four

Because the same hormone can fall into different categories at different times, no single summary such as 'stress hormone' captures its role.
^p10696570-timing
