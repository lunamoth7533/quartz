# Sex hormones and mood across the lifespan

## Definition

Sex hormones belong to one of the hypothalamic-pituitary loops: under hypothalamic control the anterior pituitary releases FSH and LH, and target-gland products feed back on the hypothalamus and pituitary. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-connection|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-lobes|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-feedback|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] In mood research the focus is less on hormone levels than on transitions across a lifetime - the menstrual cycle, pregnancy and the postpartum period, and perimenopause - when symptoms of several conditions change. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-phases|Handy 2022]] [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-overall|Wesseloo 2016]] [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-transitions|Kooij 2025]]

## How it works

**The menstrual cycle.** In a review across psychiatric conditions, the premenstrual and menstrual phases were the most consistent times of worsening, with strong evidence for increases in psychotic, manic and depressive symptoms, suicide attempts and drinking. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-phases|Handy 2022]] Anxiety, stress and binge eating tended to be raised throughout the luteal phase, while patterns for panic disorder, PTSD symptoms and borderline personality disorder were less consistent. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-luteal|Handy 2022]] [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-inconsistent|Handy 2022]] Proposed explanations span behavioural, psychological and neuroendocrine influences, and the review is a narrative synthesis rather than a meta-analysis. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-mechanisms|Handy 2022]] [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-limit|Handy 2022]] Because cycle phase is often estimated from reported dates, "premenstrual worsening" ties symptoms to timing without identifying which hormonal change is responsible. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-caution-phase|Appraisal: Handy 2022]]

**Pregnancy and the postpartum period in bipolar disorder.** In a meta-analysis of 5,700 deliveries in 4,023 women with bipolar disorder, previous postpartum psychosis or both, about 35% of deliveries were followed by a postpartum relapse. [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-overall|Wesseloo 2016]] Severe postpartum episodes occurred in 17% of women with bipolar disorder and 29% of those with previous postpartum psychosis. [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-severe|Wesseloo 2016]] Among women with bipolar disorder, relapse was reported in 66% of those who were medication-free in pregnancy and 23% of those taking prophylactic medication. [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-medication|Wesseloo 2016]] That contrast compares observational groups that differ in more than medication, so it shows association rather than the effect of a decision and is not a basis for individual treatment choices. [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-caution-observational|Appraisal: Wesseloo 2016]]

**Hormonal transitions in ADHD.** An expert review concluded that puberty, the menstrual cycle, pregnancy and (peri)menopause each make ADHD symptoms and mood problems worse, and that drug studies and tailored treatments for women are lacking. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-transitions|Kooij 2025]] It discusses interactions of oestrogen and progesterone with dopamine pathways and proposes that lower-oestrogen phases may affect cognition. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-oestrogen|Kooij 2025]] Women with undiagnosed ADHD are described as more vulnerable to premenstrual dysphoric disorder and postpartum depression, and to cardiovascular disease in perimenopause. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-risks|Kooij 2025]] Much of this rests on cross-sectional and self-reported evidence, so a hormonal mechanism remains a hypothesis awaiting within-person studies with measured hormones. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-limit|Kooij 2025]] [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Kooij 2025]]

**Perimenopause.** The menopausal transition is one of the windows named in the ADHD review, where it is linked to worsening symptoms and mood and, in undiagnosed women, to cardiovascular risk. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-transitions|Kooij 2025]] [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-risks|Kooij 2025]] A quantitative estimate of perimenopausal depression risk in the general population is not yet sourced in this library.

**Transitions, not levels.** In all three windows the unit of analysis is a change of hormonal state tied to a cycle phase or a life event, and the sources here time symptoms to that change rather than measuring hormone concentrations in each person. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-caution-phase|Appraisal: Handy 2022]] [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Kooij 2025]]

**Reading rule.** Before crediting a mood change to a hormone, name the window (cycle phase, postpartum, perimenopause), how it was identified (reported dates or measured hormones), the condition and the design, and keep population relapse figures separate from any individual decision. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-caution-phase|Appraisal: Handy 2022]] [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-caution-observational|Appraisal: Wesseloo 2016]]

## Evidence and status

Postpartum relapse risk in bipolar disorder is quantified by a meta-analysis of observational studies and is high, about one delivery in three in the groups studied. [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-overall|Wesseloo 2016]] Cycle-linked worsening is described consistently across several conditions in a narrative synthesis, with the strongest evidence for psychosis, mania, depression, suicide attempts and alcohol use. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-phases|Handy 2022]] For ADHD, the hormonal-transition account comes from an expert narrative review drawing on literature and lived experience rather than from pooled studies of measured hormones. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-limit|Kooij 2025]] [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Kooij 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- Which hormonal change, if any, drives cycle-linked worsening; most studies time symptoms to phase rather than measuring hormones. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-caution-phase|Appraisal: Handy 2022]]
- Relapse risk for women with both bipolar disorder and previous postpartum episodes could not be estimated. [[P26514657 Wesseloo 2016 Postpartum relapse in bipolar disorder#^p26514657-limit|Wesseloo 2016]]
- Whether hormonal transitions change ADHD symptoms within the same person, rather than differing between groups. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Kooij 2025]]
- Cycle effects on PTSD symptoms are inconsistent, and the size of perimenopausal depression risk outside ADHD is not yet sourced in this library. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-inconsistent|Handy 2022]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Expert narrative review (Frontiers in Global Women's Health).** Draws together research and lived experience on hormonal transitions in women with ADHD, adding pregnancy and perimenopause to the usual menstrual-cycle focus and flagging the lack of treatment research. [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-transitions|Kooij 2025]] [[P40692967 Kooij 2025 Female ADHD and hormonal fluctuations#^p40692967-risks|Kooij 2025]]
- **2022 · Comprehensive review (Harvard Review of Psychiatry).** Shows premenstrual and menstrual worsening to be transdiagnostic rather than specific to one disorder, while PTSD findings stay inconsistent. [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-phases|Handy 2022]] [[P35267252 Handy 2022 Psychiatric symptoms across the menstrual cycle#^p35267252-inconsistent|Handy 2022]]
