# Stress response and the HPA axis

**Definition.** Acute stress mobilises the fast sympathetic branch and the slower HPA axis, which ends in cortisol release. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-axis|What Is Stress?]]

## Supported claims

- The stress response begins with appraisal: the same demand can be experienced as threat or challenge. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-appraisal|What Is Stress?]]
- Cortisol mobilises energy and modulates multiple systems rather than acting as a single stress molecule. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-axis|What Is Stress?]]
- Acute stress responses are adaptive, while chronic activation is associated with wear on regulation, mood and health. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-chronic|What Is Stress?]]

## How it works

**The axis is a loop.** Corticotropin-releasing hormone (CRH) from the hypothalamus drives adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH) from the anterior pituitary, which drives cortisol release from the adrenal cortex, and corticosteroid levels feed back on the hypothalamus and pituitary to limit further release - which is why the axis is described as a loop rather than a chain. [[F64 OpenStax The adrenal glands#^f64-drive|The Adrenal Glands]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-feedback|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] The adrenal cortex is organised into zones with different outputs, with glucocorticoids such as cortisol coming from the fasciculata. [[F64 OpenStax The adrenal glands#^f64-cortex|The Adrenal Glands]]

**Two speeds, one system.** The sympathetic response is fast and neural; the HPA axis is slower and hormonal, ending in cortisol release, which is why the two are described as complementary rather than redundant. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-axis|What is stress?]] The adrenal medulla links them by releasing adrenaline and noradrenaline as part of sympathetic activation. [[F64 OpenStax The adrenal glands#^f64-medulla|The Adrenal Glands]] Because appraisal shapes the response, physiology and interpretation are part of one system. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-appraisal|What is stress?]]

**What glucocorticoids do is contested and context-dependent.** A long-standing dispute concerns whether glucocorticoids enable, stimulate or suppress the stress response: one tradition treats them as enabling or stimulating it, another as suppressing it to prevent overactivation. [[P10696570 Sapolsky 2000 Glucocorticoids and stress#^p10696570-contested|Glucocorticoids and stress]] One influential review proposes classifying their actions as permissive, suppressive, stimulatory or preparative depending on context and timing, so no single summary such as 'stress hormone' captures the role - though that classification is a proposal, not a settled taxonomy. [[P10696570 Sapolsky 2000 Glucocorticoids and stress#^p10696570-four|Glucocorticoids and stress]] [[P10696570 Sapolsky 2000 Glucocorticoids and stress#^p10696570-timing|Glucocorticoids and stress]]

**From acute to chronic.** The contrast between adaptive acute responses and chronic activation's wear on regulation, mood and health is the everyday version of the allostatic-load argument. [[F08 OpenStax What is stress#^f08-chronic|What is stress?]] [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-load|Stress mediators]]

**Measurement is not the mechanism.** Cortisol is pulsatile, and its measures depend on time of day, sleep, meals, recent stressors, binding proteins and the compartment sampled, so single measurements are weak indices of chronic axis activity. The allostasis framework describes cumulative cost rather than a single value, which is why the HPA-axis material in this library treats measures as constructs with confounds rather than as readouts of stress. [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-allostasis|Stress mediators]] [[P17290796 McEwen 2006 Stress mediators#^p17290796-load|Stress mediators]] [[F64 OpenStax The adrenal glands#^f64-limit|The Adrenal Glands]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-limit|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]]

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** The HPA axis is the physiological route the neuroendocrine-neuroimmune domain uses to connect stress to immune and metabolic signalling, so a stress claim in any clinical-psychiatry or neurodevelopmental article has to state which axis is being measured and how. ## Limitation or common misconception

Cortisol is not a synonym for stress: it follows a daily rhythm and responds to many inputs.
