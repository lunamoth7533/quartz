# Thyroid axis and mood

## Definition

The thyroid axis is one of the hypothalamic-pituitary control loops: hypothalamic releasing hormones drive the anterior pituitary, whose outputs include thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH), and products of the target gland feed back on the hypothalamus and pituitary. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-connection|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-lobes|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-feedback|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] In mood research it matters in three ways: hypothyroidism is associated with depression, lithium alters thyroid function, and thyroid hormone has been studied as an added treatment in bipolar disorder. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-association|Bode 2021]] [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-thyroid|McKnight 2012]] [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-trials|Seshadri 2022]]

## How it works

**A loop, not a chain.** Because target-gland products feed back on the hypothalamus and pituitary, the axis behaves as a loop rather than a one-way chain of commands. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-feedback|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] A rise in TSH is therefore the pituitary's response to falling thyroid output, which makes TSH the loop's most sensitive signal that something is pushing it. [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-caution-tsh|Appraisal: McKnight 2012]] The textbook presentation simplifies pulsatile release and tissue-specific receptor effects, so the loop is a model rather than a complete account. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-limit|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]]

**Thyroid dysfunction and depression.** In a meta-analysis of 25 population-based studies with 348,014 participants, hypothyroidism was associated with clinical depression (OR 1.30), whereas thyroid autoimmunity alone gave an inconclusive result. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-association|Bode 2021]] The association was stronger for overt than for subclinical hypothyroidism (OR 1.77 against 1.13), appeared in women but not in men in a post hoc analysis, and was considerably weaker than had been assumed. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-overt|Bode 2021]] [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-sex|Bode 2021]] [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-size|Bode 2021]] Direction cannot be read from these data: fatigue and low mood overlap with hypothyroid symptoms, and people with depression may be tested more often. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-caution-direction|Appraisal: Bode 2021]]

**Lithium and the thyroid.** In a systematic review of 385 studies, clinical hypothyroidism was more common with lithium than with placebo (OR 5.78) and TSH rose by about 4 units on average. [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-limit|McKnight 2012]] [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-thyroid|McKnight 2012]] The same synthesis linked lithium to higher calcium and parathyroid hormone, reduced urinary concentrating ability and weight gain, while average kidney filtration changed little and end-stage renal failure was rare. [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-other|McKnight 2012]] [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-renal|McKnight 2012]] These are group averages from mixed designs, not predictions of what one person's thyroid will do. [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-caution-group|Appraisal: McKnight 2012]] How lithium stabilises mood is itself unresolved, so its thyroid effects are documented adverse effects rather than evidence about how the drug works. [[P38697859 Bortolozzi 2024 Advances in lithium pharmacology#^p38697859-elusive|Bortolozzi 2024]]

**Thyroid hormone in bipolar depression.** One hypothesis links rapid cycling to too little thyroid hormone in the brain, although the mechanism in bipolar disorder is unclear. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-rationale|Seshadri 2022]] Open-label studies of levothyroxine at above-replacement levels reported better outcomes in bipolar depression and fewer recurrences in rapid cycling, but a randomised trial found no advantage over placebo and a three-arm trial found benefit only in women with rapid cycling. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-trials|Seshadri 2022]] The eight studies were too different to pool, and the review judged the evidence mixed and of low quality, with open-label promise not consistently replicated. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-quality|Seshadri 2022]] [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-limit|Seshadri 2022]] Improvement in open-label studies is the pattern that expectation and regression to the mean would also produce, so the record describes a research question rather than a treatment recommendation. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Seshadri 2022]]

**Reading rule.** For any thyroid-mood claim, ask whether it concerns overt or subclinical dysfunction, which sex and population were studied, whether the design can show direction, and whether a treatment effect comes from open-label or randomised evidence. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-overt|Bode 2021]] [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-caution-direction|Appraisal: Bode 2021]] [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-caution-evidence|Appraisal: Seshadri 2022]]

## Evidence and status

The feedback architecture of the axis is textbook physiology. [[F34 OpenStax Pituitary gland and hypothalamus#^f34-feedback|Pituitary Gland and Hypothalamus]] The association between hypothyroidism and depression is real but modest, and possibly confined to overt disease and to women. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-association|Bode 2021]] [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-sex|Bode 2021]] Lithium's association with hypothyroidism and raised TSH rests on a large systematic review combining randomised and observational evidence. [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-thyroid|McKnight 2012]] [[P22265699 McKnight 2012 Lithium toxicity profile#^p22265699-limit|McKnight 2012]] Evidence for thyroid hormone as an added treatment in bipolar depression is mixed and of low quality. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-quality|Seshadri 2022]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether the hypothyroidism-depression link runs from thyroid to mood, from mood to testing and diagnosis, or through shared factors. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-caution-direction|Appraisal: Bode 2021]]
- Why the association appeared in women and not in men; the sex contrast came from a post hoc analysis. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-sex|Bode 2021]]
- Whether any subgroup, such as women with rapid cycling, benefits from thyroid hormone; the only positive randomised signal came from a subgroup. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-trials|Seshadri 2022]]
- How thyroid measures change across mood episodes within the same person is not yet sourced in this library.

## Recent research

- **2022 · Systematic review (Brain Sciences).** Open-label promise for thyroid hormone in bipolar depression and rapid cycling was not replicated in randomised trials apart from one subgroup, and no pooled estimate was possible. [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-trials|Seshadri 2022]] [[P36421864 Seshadri 2022 Thyroid hormone augmentation in bipolar disorder#^p36421864-quality|Seshadri 2022]]
- **2021 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (JAMA Psychiatry).** Revised the hypothyroidism-depression link down to a modest association, stronger for overt disease and seen in women. [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-association|Bode 2021]] [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-overt|Bode 2021]] [[P34524390 Bode 2021 Hypothyroidism and clinical depression#^p34524390-sex|Bode 2021]]
