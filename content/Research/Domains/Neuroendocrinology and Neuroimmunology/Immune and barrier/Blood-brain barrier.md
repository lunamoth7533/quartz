# Blood-brain barrier

## Definition

The blood-brain barrier is the selective interface between blood and central nervous system tissue, formed primarily by brain endothelial cells with supporting cells around them.

## How it works

**What the barrier is made of.** Brain endothelial cells joined by continuous tight junctions form the barrier, supported by pericytes and other cells. The tight junctions prevent paracellular movement, and the endothelium lacks the transendothelial vesicular pathways found in other tissues. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-components|The blood-brain barrier]] [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-tight|Blood Brain Barrier]] Astrocytes and other supporting cells are required for the barrier to develop and maintain its properties. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-support|Blood Brain Barrier]]

**Glia contribute to the interface.** That dependence is the structural link between the barrier and the glial material in the neurobiology domain. Glial cells provide scaffolding, insulation, nutrient transport, waste handling and immune mediation, with recent work suggesting a roughly 1:1 ratio of glia to neurons rather than a large excess - which matters because the barrier and the immune surveillance of the brain are supported functions rather than passive properties of the endothelium. [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-glia|Nervous Tissue]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]]

**Selective, not sealed.** Barrier function is selective rather than absolute: gases diffuse freely, ions and nutrients ride specific transporters, and larger molecules are excluded or actively shuttled, so permeability is regulated and route-specific. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood Brain Barrier]]

**Permeability claims need a route and a molecule.** Regulated transport, leakage, injury and immune-cell entry are distinct phenomena, and barrier integrity is not a binary open-or-closed state. "The barrier is leaky" is therefore meaningless without naming which route and which cargo changed, and how it was measured. That framing is the discipline that separates barrier biology from barrier marketing, including claims that supplements "strengthen" a barrier whose transport is selectively regulated, not simply closed. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-permeability|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]] [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood-brain barrier]]

**Drug delivery.** For pharmacology, the barrier is the first selectivity filter a psychotropic must pass, by design: lipophilic agents cross, polar ones need transporters, and efflux pumps expel some drugs outright. [[Pharmacokinetics and ADME]]

**Reading rule.** Distinguish barrier permeability, regulated transport and immune surveillance; they involve different cells and different measurements, and the brain's interface is only a wall in the loosest sense. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-limit|Blood-brain barrier]]

## Evidence and status

Barrier structure and transport are established in cell and animal studies; human measurement relies on imaging and cerebrospinal fluid markers with substantial limitations. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-components|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]]

The review is organised around unresolved questions, including how the barrier changes across regions, ages and disease states, so claims that a treatment works by opening or protecting the barrier are research questions rather than established mechanisms. For disease interpretation, barrier change is entangled with inflammation and age, so imaging-based barrier findings are part of a causal story, not its conclusion. [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-open|The blood-brain barrier in health and disease]] [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-limit|The blood-brain barrier]]
