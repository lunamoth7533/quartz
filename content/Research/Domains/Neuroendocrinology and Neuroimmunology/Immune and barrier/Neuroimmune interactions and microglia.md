# Neuroimmune interactions and microglia

## Definition

Microglia are resident immune cells of the central nervous system that survey the parenchyma and respond to injury, infection and other perturbations. They are part of the neuroimmune interface, alongside circulating immune signals that reach the brain.

## How it works

**Resting microglia are not resting.** Two-photon imaging in living, uninjured cortex showed that microglia in their so-called resting state continually extend and retract motile processes to sample their territory. Blood-brain barrier disruption produced immediate, focal microglial activation, switching their behaviour from surveillance to shielding the affected site. Surveillance is the baseline state, which is why 'activation' needs a specified trigger and phenotype. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-response|Microglial surveillance]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-limit|Microglial surveillance]]

**Activation is a repertoire, not a switch.** Microglial states differ in what they do - phagocytosis, antigen presentation, cytokine release, trophic support - and the two-state activated/resting picture from early literature has given way to a spectrum of context-dependent phenotypes. That refinement matters for interpretation: a marker of "activation" in human tissue or imaging identifies that something in the microglial state changed, not which behaviour changed or whether it is harmful. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-surveillance|Microglial surveillance]] [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-response|Microglial surveillance]]

**The barrier shapes what can act.** The interface is selective rather than absolute: gases diffuse freely while ions, nutrients and larger molecules depend on specific transporters, and regulated transport, leakage and injury are distinct phenomena. A circulating cytokine's effect on the brain therefore depends on a route, not on diffusion alone. [[F105 Neuroscience Online blood-brain barrier#^f105-transport|Blood-brain barrier]] [[P32211826 Profaci 2020 Blood-brain barrier#^p32211826-permeability|The blood-brain barrier]] [[F02 OpenStax Nervous tissue#^f02-glia|Nervous Tissue]]

**Sickness behaviour anchors the psychiatric claims.** Peripheral immune signals reach the brain through several routes and produce sickness behaviour - a coordinated set of changes in activity, appetite, sleep, mood and social behaviour. The inflammation-to-behaviour literature works because this syndrome is well characterised, with defined signalling routes. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-sickness|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]

**The psychiatric extension.** Sustained inflammatory signalling is proposed to contribute to depressive symptoms in vulnerable individuals - that some depressive symptoms are maintained by inflammatory signalling in some individuals - through cytokine signalling, altered tryptophan metabolism and vagal transmission. It is presented as a hypothesis with proposed mechanisms, and because the routes are specified the proposal is testable even where it is not established. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-depression|From inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-mechanism|From inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-limit|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]

**Gut-to-brain signalling overlaps here.** The microbiota review lists immune signalling among its communication routes and treats microbial metabolites as candidate mediators while emphasising that many findings remain correlational with causal direction unresolved. The immune arm is therefore the best-specified of the microbiome routes, not a settled one. [[P31460832 Cryan 2019 Microbiota-gut-brain axis#^p31460832-routes|The microbiota-gut-brain axis]] [[P31460832 Cryan 2019 Microbiota-gut-brain axis#^p31460832-status|The microbiota-gut-brain axis]]

**Reading rule.** Specify the cell, the mediator, the model system and the direction of inference; 'neuroinflammation' spans everything from microglial surveillance to cytokine-driven sickness behaviour, and the popular version of the term sits at the far end of that range. [[P15831717 Nimmerjahn 2005 Microglial surveillance#^p15831717-limit|Microglial surveillance]] [[P22749156 Munkholm 2013 Cytokines in bipolar disorder#^p22749156-caution|Cytokines in bipolar disorder]]

## Evidence and status

Microglial surveillance and injury response are demonstrated directly in animals; human evidence for immune contributions to psychiatric conditions is correlational plus experimental challenge studies, and effect sizes vary. The psychiatric extension of the sickness-behaviour model is a hypothesis with supportive subgroup evidence, and the subset framing exists precisely because population-wide claims failed. [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-sickness|From inflammation to sickness and depression]] [[P18073775 Dantzer 2008 Inflammation to sickness and depression#^p18073775-depression|From inflammation to sickness and depression]]
