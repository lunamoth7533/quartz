# Structural versus functional measures

**Definition.** Clinical neuroscience measures different layers: anatomy, electrical activity over time and body chemistry. [[F15 NINDS Neurological diagnostic tests and procedures#^f15-modalities|Neurological Diagnostic Tests and Procedures]]

## Supported claims

- The NINDS overview covers history and examination, CT and MRI imaging, EEG and laboratory tests, and what each measures. [[F15 NINDS Neurological diagnostic tests and procedures#^f15-modalities|Neurological Diagnostic Tests and Procedures]]
- MRI is a family: conventional structural MRI images anatomy while functional MRI measures activity-related signal change. [[F15 NINDS Neurological diagnostic tests and procedures#^f15-caution-modality|AI synthesis: Neurological Diagnostic Tests and Procedures]]

## How it works

**Structural measures.** Structural measures index anatomy - volume, cortical thickness, tissue properties, tract integrity and lesion location - measured directly (or from estimated parameters). They are comparatively stable across sessions; their interpretation problem is that structure does not determine function, so a structural difference does not specify a functional consequence. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-mri|Brain imaging]] [[Bipolar MRI findings and their limits]]

**Functional measures.** Functional measures index activity-related signals or performance: blood-oxygenation, metabolic and electrophysiological signals index consequences of neuronal activity indirectly and vary with task, state and time. Their interpretation problem is that a signal can change for many reasons, including vascular and attentional ones. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-fmri|Brain imaging]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

**Task design is part of the functional measure.** A functional signal is measured during something - rest, a task, a challenge - and the design determines which process the signal can index; the same scanner run is a different measurement under a different task. A functional difference may reflect strategy, effort, arousal or performance differences rather than a fixed neural property, so task design determines interpretation. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-limit|Brain imaging]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-power|Neuroimaging reproducibility]] That is why functional claims name the task and contrast, and why resting-state and task-based findings with the same label may not be measuring the same construct. [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-practice|Neuroimaging reproducibility]] [[Attention]]

**Different questions, weakly correlated answers.** Structural and functional measures answer different questions and neither is a proxy for the other. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-mri|Brain imaging]] Structural and functional findings correlate weakly in many datasets, which is why a functional claim needs both a functional measure and a design that isolates the process of interest. [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-practice|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

**Stability is a property and a trap.** Structural measures' session-to-session stability makes them attractive phenotypes; the same stability makes it tempting to treat them as traits, when they also move with medication, age, illness course and training. Functional measures' state dependence is likewise double-edged: it complicates comparison, and it is exactly what makes functional measures able to detect state changes. Each measure's stability profile is evidence about what it can be used for. [[F28 OpenStax Brain imaging Psychology 2e#^f28-mri|Brain imaging]] [[P28053326 Poldrack 2017 Neuroimaging reproducibility#^p28053326-flexibility|Neuroimaging reproducibility]]

**Group differences and confounds.** Structural findings in psychiatric samples are group averages with overlap between groups, which is why they are not diagnostic. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|AI appraisal: ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]] Medication, illness duration and substance use are associated with structural measures, so findings in clinical samples are difficult to attribute to the condition itself. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]

**Group-to-individual is the hardest crossing.** Even highly reliable group differences support individual classification weakly, because between-group effect sizes that look large in group statistics translate into overlapping distributions where many individuals sit on the wrong side of any threshold. This gap is why imaging findings in psychiatry remain research statements rather than diagnostic tests. [[Interpreting group brain differences]] [[Association versus individual prediction]] Structural and functional findings are complementary descriptions at different levels, and neither can be converted into a diagnosis or an individual prediction without validation. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-limit|ENIGMA cortical abnormalities]]

**Reading rule.** State the measure, the analysis and the level of the claim; group-level structural differences and individual-level functional predictions are different objects. [[Association versus individual prediction]]

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** This article is the neurology domain's instance of the measurement-validity problem the methods domain owns: reliability, validity and the construct-measure gap are the same concepts with imaging-specific instantiations. [[Measurement validity and reliability]] [[Neuroimaging methods]]

## Limitation or common misconception

A measure can be reliable and objective and still answer only the question it was designed for.
