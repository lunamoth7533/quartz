## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The panel made 46 recommendations, including counselling on the natural history of tics, psychoeducation for teachers and peers, assessment for co-occurring disorders and periodic review of whether treatment is still needed.
^p31061208-recommendations

Options are medication, watchful waiting and the Comprehensive Behavioral Intervention for Tics (CBIT), chosen jointly by patient, caregiver and clinician after weighing benefits, harms and co-occurring disorders.
^p31061208-options

Deep brain stimulation is addressed for adults whose severe tics have not responded to other treatment, including how candidates are assessed.
^p31061208-dbs

## Scope as reported

A guideline summary on assessing and managing tics in Tourette syndrome and chronic tic disorders, from a panel of 9 physicians, 2 psychologists and 2 patient representatives, integrating a systematic review through an Institute of Medicine-compliant process with principles of care and inference.
