## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Diagnosis now rests on positive evidence - characteristic patterns in symptoms that are genuinely experienced, with signs varying within and between tasks over time.
^p35430029-positive

Stressful experiences often precede it but are absent in many patients.
^p35430029-stressors

Functional seizures, functional movement disorders, persistent postural-perceptual dizziness and functional cognitive disorder are treated as variants of one disorder sharing causes and mechanisms, each with distinctive features supported by neurophysiology and other biomarkers.
^p35430029-subtypes

Proposed mechanisms are limbic overactivity, an internal symptom model within predictive coding, and disrupted networks giving movement its felt voluntariness.
^p35430029-mechanisms

Tailored multidisciplinary treatment combining physical and psychological approaches is supported.
^p35430029-treatment

## Scope as reported

An expert narrative review in The Lancet Neurology, which opens by describing the disorder as common in neurological practice and covers four functional subtypes and their shared mechanisms; it synthesises rather than pools studies.
^p35430029-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

The mechanism accounts are group-level models from imaging and experimental work; they explain how symptoms can arise without structural damage but do not yet supply a test that confirms the diagnosis in one person.
