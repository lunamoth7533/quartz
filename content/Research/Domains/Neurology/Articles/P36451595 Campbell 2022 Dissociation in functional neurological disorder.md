## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Comorbid dissociative disorders were common in FND samples.
^p36451595-comorbid

Psychoform dissociation was higher than in healthy controls (g = 0.90) and neurological controls (g = 0.56), and highest in samples with functional seizures (g = 0.94 against healthy controls); somatoform dissociation was markedly higher than in healthy controls (g = 1.80).
^p36451595-scores

Greater dissociation went with more severe functional symptoms, poorer quality of life and brain alterations.
^p36451595-associations

## Scope as reported

Systematic review of 75 studies, mostly prospective case-control designs, searched to June 2021; heterogeneity and risk of bias were high, and samples with motor symptoms were under-studied.
^p36451595-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Dissociation here is mainly questionnaire-measured, and somatoform-dissociation items overlap with functional symptoms themselves, which inflates that comparison; the associations are cross-sectional.
^p36451595-caution-measure
