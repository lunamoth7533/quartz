## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Tourette syndrome (Tourette disorder in DSM-5-TR) is a common neurodevelopmental, hyperkinetic condition affecting up to 1% of people, with several motor and vocal tics beginning in childhood.
^p29763133-definition

It frequently co-occurs with ADHD, OCD, anxiety disorders, autism, sleep disorders and oppositional defiant disorder.
^p29763133-cooccurrence

Mild tics often need no treatment; drug or behavioural therapy becomes relevant when tics cause pain, injury, marked distress or disruption of school, work or social life.
^p29763133-treatment

Course varies: tics may remit by adulthood while associated symptoms persist, many adults keep tics, and some worsen.
^p29763133-course

Tic presentations rose sharply during the COVID-19 pandemic, most among adolescent girls.
^p29763133-pandemic

A regularly updated clinical reference chapter; it describes the pathophysiology and genetic basis as incompletely understood and notes that educating teachers and carers can reduce stigma.
