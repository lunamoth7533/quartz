## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Cognitive restructuring, third-wave components, sleep restriction and stimulus control were each associated with higher remission; sleep hygiene education was not essential, and relaxation was possibly counterproductive.
^p38231522-components

In-person, therapist-led programmes showed the largest benefit among delivery formats, and the best-performing combination raised remission by an estimated 33 percentage points over in-person psychoeducation.
^p38231522-format

## Scope as reported

Random-effects component network meta-analysis of published randomised trials, searched to July 2023; the primary outcome was remission after treatment. The authors note that undetected interactions between components could undermine the conclusions.
^p38231522-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Component effects are estimated indirectly across trials that bundled elements differently; they describe average contributions in trial populations, not which elements will help a particular person.
^p38231522-caution-components
