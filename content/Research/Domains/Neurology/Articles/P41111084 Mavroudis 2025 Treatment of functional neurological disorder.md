# Treatment of functional neurological disorder: an umbrella review of systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Cognitive behavioural therapy showed consistent benefit for functional seizures and mixed presentations, especially within multidisciplinary programmes.
^p41111084-cbt

Physiotherapy brought moderate improvement in functional motor symptoms; neuromodulation looked promising but inconsistent, and hypnosis or suggestion helped some people subjectively.
^p41111084-physio

Medicines were used mainly as adjuncts for co-occurring psychiatric conditions.
^p41111084-drugs

Overall certainty was moderate at best, limited by risk of bias and inconsistent outcome measures.
^p41111084-certainty

## Scope as reported

An umbrella review of 17 systematic reviews and meta-analyses of psychological, physiotherapy, neuromodulation, multidisciplinary and drug treatments, assessing effect direction, GRADE certainty and risk of bias across 10 methodological domains.
^p41111084-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Reviews in an umbrella synthesis often draw on the same small trials, so agreement between reviews can overstate independent confirmation; individual large trials should be checked before a benefit is treated as settled.
^p41111084-caution-overlap
