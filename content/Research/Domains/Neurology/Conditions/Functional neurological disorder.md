# Functional neurological disorder

## Definition

Functional neurological disorder (FND) is common in neurological practice: genuinely experienced symptoms such as seizures, abnormal movements, dizziness or cognitive complaints that follow recognisable patterns and vary within and between tasks over time. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-limit|Hallett 2022]] [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]] [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-subtypes|Hallett 2022]] It is diagnosed from positive features rather than by excluding other disease alone. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]]

## How it works

**A positive diagnosis.** Diagnosis rests on characteristic patterns of genuinely experienced symptoms, with signs that vary within one task and between tasks over time, and neurophysiology and other biomarkers can support it. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]] [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-subtypes|Hallett 2022]] The emphasis therefore moves from what tests fail to show to what the examination does show. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]]

**Four subtypes, one disorder.** Functional seizures, functional movement disorders, persistent postural-perceptual dizziness and functional cognitive disorder are treated as variants of one disorder with shared causes and mechanisms, each with its own distinctive features. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-subtypes|Hallett 2022]]

**Mechanism research.** Current models propose overactivity of limbic circuits, the development of an internal model of the symptom within a predictive-coding framework, and disturbance of the networks that normally give movement its sense of being voluntary. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-mechanisms|Hallett 2022]] These are group-level models from imaging and experimental work: they explain how symptoms can arise without structural damage but do not yet provide a confirmatory test for one person. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-caution-mechanism|Appraisal: Hallett 2022]]

**Trauma and stress: common, not required.** Stressful experiences often precede FND but are absent in many patients, so the diagnosis rests on the positive signs rather than on identifying a stressor. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-stressors|Hallett 2022]] [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]]

**Dissociation.** In a meta-analysis of 75 studies, comorbid dissociative disorders were common in FND, and psychoform dissociation was higher than in healthy controls (g = 0.90) and neurological controls (g = 0.56), most of all in samples with functional seizures. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-comorbid|Campbell 2022]] [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-scores|Campbell 2022]] Somatoform dissociation was markedly raised (g = 1.80), and more dissociation went with more severe functional symptoms, poorer quality of life and brain alterations. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-scores|Campbell 2022]] [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-associations|Campbell 2022]] Somatoform-dissociation items overlap with functional symptoms, which inflates that comparison, and the associations are cross-sectional. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-caution-measure|Appraisal: Campbell 2022]] Dissociative disorders are themselves defined by disrupted integration of consciousness, memory, identity or perception, and their differential includes neurological causes - the same border FND sits on. [[F85 MSD Manual Dissociative disorders#^f85-group|Overview of Dissociative Disorders]] [[F85 MSD Manual Dissociative disorders#^f85-differential|Overview of Dissociative Disorders]]

**Treatment evidence.** An expert review supports tailored multidisciplinary treatment that combines physical and psychological approaches. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-treatment|Hallett 2022]] An umbrella review of 17 systematic reviews found consistent benefit from cognitive behavioural therapy for functional seizures and mixed presentations, especially within multidisciplinary programmes, and moderate improvement in functional motor symptoms with physiotherapy. [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-cbt|Mavroudis 2025]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-physio|Mavroudis 2025]] Neuromodulation looked promising but inconsistent, and medicines were used mainly for co-occurring psychiatric conditions. [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-physio|Mavroudis 2025]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-drugs|Mavroudis 2025]] Overall certainty was moderate at best, and reviews that share the same small trials can overstate how independently a benefit has been confirmed. [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-certainty|Mavroudis 2025]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Mavroudis 2025]]

**Reading rule.** Before generalising across FND, name the subtype, how the diagnosis was made (positive signs or exclusion), how stressors and dissociation were measured, and whether a treatment claim comes from a trial or from a review of overlapping reviews. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]] [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-caution-measure|Appraisal: Campbell 2022]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Mavroudis 2025]]

## Evidence and status

The positive-sign approach and the grouping of four subtypes are set out in an expert narrative review rather than tested here as a diagnostic-accuracy question. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-limit|Hallett 2022]] Raised dissociation in FND is supported by a meta-analysis with high heterogeneity and risk of bias. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-scores|Campbell 2022]] [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-limit|Campbell 2022]] Psychological therapy and physiotherapy have the most support, at moderate certainty at best. [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-certainty|Mavroudis 2025]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether limbic, predictive-coding and agency-network findings are causes, consequences or correlates of symptoms. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-caution-mechanism|Appraisal: Hallett 2022]]
- How much of the dissociation signal reflects overlap between somatoform-dissociation items and the functional symptoms themselves. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-caution-measure|Appraisal: Campbell 2022]]
- Motor presentations are under-studied for dissociation, and treatment outcome measures are inconsistent. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-limit|Campbell 2022]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-certainty|Mavroudis 2025]]
- Quantitative estimates of childhood adversity in FND, and FND in autistic or ADHD people, are not yet sourced in this library.

## Recent research

- **2025 · Umbrella review (Journal of Neurology).** Across 17 reviews, CBT for functional seizures and physiotherapy for motor symptoms had the most consistent support, with certainty moderate at best. [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-cbt|Mavroudis 2025]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-physio|Mavroudis 2025]] [[P41111084 Mavroudis 2025 Treatment of functional neurological disorder#^p41111084-certainty|Mavroudis 2025]]
- **2022 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (BJPsych Open).** Quantified raised dissociation in FND, strongest with functional seizures, and linked it to severity and quality of life. [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-scores|Campbell 2022]] [[P36451595 Campbell 2022 Dissociation in functional neurological disorder#^p36451595-associations|Campbell 2022]]
- **2022 · Narrative review (The Lancet Neurology).** Set out the positive diagnosis and grouped four subtypes under shared mechanisms. [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-positive|Hallett 2022]] [[P35430029 Hallett 2022 Functional neurological disorder subtypes and mechanisms#^p35430029-subtypes|Hallett 2022]]
