# Overview of the Neurological Exam

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, section 16.1

## Key points (as published)

The neurological exam is presented as a structured set of tests whose purpose is to identify where function is lost, so that a deficit can be attributed to a region or pathway rather than to a symptom name.
^f33-purpose

The overview names the exam's domains, which correspond to the functional divisions of the nervous system: mental status, cranial nerves, motor, sensory, coordination and reflex testing.
^f33-domains

Causes of neurological deficits are grouped by mechanism - vascular, infectious, genetic, degenerative and traumatic - as an organising frame for interpretation.
^f33-causes

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section about the exam as a method. It is not a clinical protocol and does not describe how findings should be weighted in a diagnosis.
^f33-limit
