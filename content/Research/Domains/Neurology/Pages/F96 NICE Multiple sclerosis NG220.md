# Multiple sclerosis in adults: management (NG220)

**Publisher.** National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE), UK

**Source.** <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng220/chapter/recommendations>

**Verification.** official page opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F96.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The guideline covers coordination of care, information and support for adults with MS, alongside treatment of relapses and disease-modifying therapy.
^f96-care

It addresses symptom management - including bladder, bowel, fatigue, mobility and cognitive concerns - as part of standard care rather than as afterthoughts.
^f96-symptoms

As with other NICE products, recommendations are tied to an evidence review and to the funded service context.
^f96-context

## Scope and limitations

UK national guidance. It is a service-level document and not a source of individual treatment advice.
^f96-limit
