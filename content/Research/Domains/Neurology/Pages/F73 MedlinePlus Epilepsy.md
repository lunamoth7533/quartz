## Key points (as published)

Epilepsy is described as a brain disorder in which activity becomes abnormal, causing seizures or unusual behaviour and sometimes loss of awareness.
^f73-definition

The page lists conditions and events associated with seizures, including head injury, stroke, infection, genetic factors and metabolic causes.
^f73-triggers

Treatment is described as medication first for many people, with surgery, devices and dietary approaches used in specific situations; it links out rather than advising individuals.
^f73-management
