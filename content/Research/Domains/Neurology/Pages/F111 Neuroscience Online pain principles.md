## Key points (as published)

Nociceptors are free nerve endings that respond to damaging or potentially damaging stimuli and to chemicals released by damaged tissue.
^f111-nociceptors

Tissue damage triggers release of substances that sensitise nociceptors, which is how the same stimulus can become painful after injury.
^f111-inflammation

Pain information is transmitted to the central nervous system through defined pathways, and modulation occurs at several levels rather than only at the periphery.
^f111-transmission

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on pain physiology. It covers nociceptive mechanisms and does not address chronic pain management.
^f111-limit
