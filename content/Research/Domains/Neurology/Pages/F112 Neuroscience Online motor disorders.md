## Key points (as published)

Symptoms arising from basal ganglia dysfunction are described as extrapyramidal disorders, distinguishing them from pyramidal-tract weakness.
^f112-extrapyramidal

Resting tremor at about four to seven hertz is most often associated with Parkinson's disease, and tremor, chorea and dystonia are distinguished by pattern.
^f112-dyskinesias

The chapter maps disorders to circuits, so signs are interpreted through the anatomy that produces them rather than as isolated symptoms.
^f112-mapping

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on motor-system disorders, linking signs to anatomy in a teaching framework.
^f112-limit
