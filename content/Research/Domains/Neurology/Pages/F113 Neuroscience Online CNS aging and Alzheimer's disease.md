## Key points (as published)

The chapter lists changes that increase with age, including ventricular volume, glial number and size, myelin thickness and the presence of plaques and tangles.
^f113-aging

Alzheimer's disease is described as the most common neurodegenerative disorder and one that begins with mild memory deficits before progressing.
^f113-ad

The chapter sets out criteria for probable and definite diagnosis, with definite diagnosis requiring pathological confirmation, a distinction that matters for interpreting clinical labels.
^f113-diagnosis

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on aging and Alzheimer's disease. Prevalence figures are as reported at the time of writing.
