## Key points (as published)

Parkinson's disease is described as a progressive disorder in which dopamine-producing neurons in the brain degenerate, producing movement symptoms.
^f76-mechanism

Symptoms include tremor, rigidity, slowed movement and balance problems, together with non-motor features such as sleep and mood changes.
^f76-symptoms

There is no single definitive test for the condition as described here; diagnosis rests on history and examination, with tests used to support or exclude alternatives.
^f76-diagnosis
