# Suicide and self-harm

> [!warning] If you are at risk now
> This note is educational and cannot help in a crisis. If you are thinking about ending your life or harming yourself, or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line now.

## Definition

Research on suicide studies a spectrum of outcomes - suicidal ideation, suicide plans, suicide attempts and death by suicide - often grouped as suicidal thoughts and behaviours. [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-association|Septier 2019]] [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-prediction|Franklin 2017]] Self-harm, intentional self-injury whether or not death was intended, is studied alongside them and is written into the borderline personality disorder construct. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-features|Leichsenring 2023]] Group risk is raised across the conditions this library covers, yet decades of risk-factor research predict individual outcomes only slightly better than chance. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-prediction|Franklin 2017]]

## How it works

**Bipolar disorders: depression carries most of the signal.** In a bipolar cohort, depressive severity was by far the strongest correlate of suicidal ideation, manic symptoms were associated more weakly, and whether mixed states added risk depended on whether mood was coded by categories or by continuous scores. [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-depression|Macellaro 2025]] [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-mixed|Macellaro 2025]] [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-caution-coding|Appraisal: Macellaro 2025]] Affect intensity and lability are associated with suicide attempts alongside other markers of more severe illness. [[P40715078 Etain 2025 Polygenic scores and bipolar dimensions#^p40715078-lability|Etain 2025]] In adolescents with bipolar disorder, poorer sleep quality was followed a month later by more suicidal ideation and was worse before months with a suicide attempt, with effects running in both directions. [[P38917889 Stepan 2024 Sleep quality and future mood in adolescents#^p38917889-lag|Stepan 2024]] [[P38917889 Stepan 2024 Sleep quality and future mood in adolescents#^p38917889-caution-direction|Appraisal: Stepan 2024]]

**ADHD.** Across 57 studies, ADHD was associated with higher odds of suicidal ideation (3.53), plans (4.54), attempts (2.37) and death by suicide (6.69), and adjusted estimates changed the picture little. [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-association|Septier 2019]] [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-robust|Septier 2019]] Co-occurring depression, substance use and conduct problems may carry part of that association. [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-caution-confounding|Appraisal: Septier 2019]] In a Swedish cohort, starting ADHD medication was associated with lower two-year mortality, a difference driven by unnatural causes that included suicide; confounding by indication keeps this an association. [[P38470385 Li 2024 ADHD medication and mortality#^p38470385-causes|Li 2024]] [[P38470385 Li 2024 ADHD medication and mortality#^p38470385-caution-confounding|Appraisal: Li 2024]]

**Autism.** In Swedish registers, risk of suicide attempt and death by suicide was highest in autistic people without intellectual disability who also had ADHD, and was raised less in autistic people with intellectual disability. [[P31238998 Hirvikoski 2020 Suicide attempt and suicide in autism#^p31238998-adhd|Hirvikoski 2020]] Without intellectual disability, autistic females generally had higher risk than autistic males, and adjusting for other psychiatric diagnoses weakened the associations. [[P31238998 Hirvikoski 2020 Suicide attempt and suicide in autism#^p31238998-sex|Hirvikoski 2020]] [[P31238998 Hirvikoski 2020 Suicide attempt and suicide in autism#^p31238998-comorbidity|Hirvikoski 2020]] Self-harm and other behavioural signs of emotion dysregulation are higher in autistic adults than in controls, though milder than in borderline personality disorder. [[P38110995 Bemmouna 2023 Emotion dysregulation in autistic adults#^p38110995-levels|Bemmouna 2023]]

**PTSD.** A PTSD diagnosis was associated with about twice the risk of death by suicide (relative risk 2.09) and about four times the risk of suicide attempts (4.05). [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-suicide|Akbar 2023]] [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-attempts|Akbar 2023]] These samples lean towards veterans and are not complex PTSD samples, so transfer to ICD-11 complex PTSD needs its own studies. [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-caution-transfer|Appraisal: Akbar 2023]]

**Borderline personality disorder and eating disorders.** Suicidal behaviour and self-injury are among the defining features of borderline personality disorder. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-features|Leichsenring 2023]] In anorexia nervosa, mortality is nearly six times that of peers and a quarter of those deaths are by suicide. [[P40048192 Attia 2025 Eating disorders review#^p40048192-harms|Attia 2025]]

**What interventions have shown.** In borderline personality disorder, tailored psychotherapies improved self-harm and suicide-related outcomes compared with usual care, on low-quality evidence. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-tau|Storebø 2020]] In adolescents with bipolar disorder, dialectical behaviour therapy reduced suicide attempts compared with standard psychotherapy, partly through reduced emotion dysregulation, while mood improved equally in both arms. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-mood|Goldstein 2024]]

**Why individual prediction performs poorly.** A meta-analysis of 365 longitudinal studies found that risk factors predicted suicidal thoughts, attempts and deaths only slightly better than chance, with no broad category doing much better. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-prediction|Franklin 2017]] Predictive ability had not improved in 50 years, studies rarely combined factors, and five broad categories made up close to 80% of all tests. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-stagnation|Franklin 2017]] [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-homogeneity|Franklin 2017]] Low base rates are part of the reason: even a factor that multiplies risk several-fold flags far more people who will not attempt suicide than people who will. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-caution-base-rate|Appraisal: Franklin 2017]] Large relative risks in registers sit alongside low absolute risk for most individuals. [[P31238998 Hirvikoski 2020 Suicide attempt and suicide in autism#^p31238998-caution-register|Appraisal: Hirvikoski 2020]]

**Reading rule.** Keep three questions apart - whether a group has raised risk, whether a factor predicts who will act, and whether an intervention changes outcomes - and never read a risk factor or a score as a verdict about one person. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-caution-base-rate|Appraisal: Franklin 2017]] [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-caution-confounding|Appraisal: Septier 2019]]

## Evidence and status

Group-level associations are consistent: meta-analyses link ADHD and PTSD with each point on the suicidal spectrum, and a national register shows raised risk in autism that is highest with co-occurring ADHD. [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-association|Septier 2019]] [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-attempts|Akbar 2023]] [[P31238998 Hirvikoski 2020 Suicide attempt and suicide in autism#^p31238998-adhd|Hirvikoski 2020]] Heterogeneity is very high in the ADHD data, and the PTSD estimate for death by suicide rests on seven studies with a wide interval. [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-limit|Septier 2019]] [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-limit|Akbar 2023]] For bipolar disorders this library holds correlates of ideation from archived cohort data and one trial rather than a pooled rate. [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-limit|Macellaro 2025]] Prediction of individual outcomes remains poor. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-prediction|Franklin 2017]]

## Uncertainties

- A pooled estimate of suicide mortality in bipolar disorders is not yet sourced in this library; the bipolar evidence here concerns correlates and one trial. [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-caution-coding|Appraisal: Macellaro 2025]]
- How much of the raised risk in ADHD and autism runs through co-occurring depression, substance use and other conditions. [[P31129238 Septier 2019 Suicidal spectrum behaviors in ADHD#^p31129238-caution-confounding|Appraisal: Septier 2019]] [[P31238998 Hirvikoski 2020 Suicide attempt and suicide in autism#^p31238998-comorbidity|Hirvikoski 2020]]
- Whether findings from largely veteran PTSD samples apply to ICD-11 complex PTSD. [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-caution-transfer|Appraisal: Akbar 2023]]
- Whether combining many factors can reach useful accuracy, given that single factors were rarely combined in fifty years of studies. [[P27841450 Franklin 2017 Risk factors for suicidal thoughts and behaviors#^p27841450-homogeneity|Franklin 2017]]

## Recent research

- **2025 · Secondary analysis of a multisite cohort (International Journal of Bipolar Disorders).** Depressive severity dominated suicidal ideation, and a mixed-state signal appeared only under categorical coding of mood. [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-depression|Macellaro 2025]] [[P40517219 Macellaro 2025 Mixed states and suicidal ideation#^p40517219-mixed|Macellaro 2025]]
- **2024 · Randomised clinical trial (JAMA Psychiatry).** Dialectical behaviour therapy reduced suicide attempts in adolescents with bipolar disorder while mood improved equally in both arms. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] It tests one outcome in a single-site, mostly female sample. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-caution-scope|Appraisal: Goldstein 2024]]
- **2023 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (Suicide and Life-Threatening Behavior).** PTSD was associated with raised risk of death by suicide, attempts and ideation, most strongly in combat veterans. [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-suicide|Akbar 2023]] [[P36385705 Akbar 2023 PTSD and suicidal behavior#^p36385705-attempts|Akbar 2023]]

## Connections

- [[Association versus individual prediction]] - the general reason a strong group-level risk factor cannot sort individuals, shown starkly by suicide research.
- [[Bipolar depression and mixed features]] - where depressive severity and the coding of mixed states shape what bipolar suicidality studies conclude.
- [[Emotion dysregulation across conditions]] - reduced dysregulation partly mediated fewer suicide attempts in the bipolar adolescent trial, and dysregulation links several conditions to self-harm.
- [[Borderline personality disorder]] - suicidal behaviour and self-injury are part of the construct and the main targets of its best-tested therapies.
- [[Trauma and PTSD in autistic and ADHD people]] - where trauma meets the neurodevelopmental conditions, the setting in which PTSD, ADHD and autism risks combine.
- [[ADHD medication evidence]] - the register evidence that starting ADHD medication is associated with fewer unnatural deaths, including suicide.
- [[Co-occurring conditions in autism]] - co-occurring conditions, especially ADHD, carry much of the raised risk of suicidal behaviour in autism.
- [[Sleep and circadian disruption across conditions]] - poor sleep preceded suicidal ideation in adolescents with bipolar disorder, one of the few time-ordered signals sourced here.
