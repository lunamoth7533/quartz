# Borderline personality disorder

## Definition

Borderline personality disorder describes a long-standing pattern of instability in mood, self-image and relationships, with marked sensitivity to rejection and abandonment. [[F84 NIMH Borderline personality disorder#^f84-pattern|NIMH: borderline personality disorder]] It affects roughly 0.7% to 2.7% of US adults and includes abrupt shifts in identity and affect, impulsivity, intense anger, emptiness, suicidal behaviour, self-injury, stress-related paranoia and severe dissociation. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-features|Leichsenring 2023]]

## How it works

**Identity at the centre.** Identity disturbance, a lasting instability in how people see themselves, is one of the construct's defining features, and DSM-5's emphasis on unstable self-image, values and relationships is the definition most studies use. [[P42731903 Mullins 2026 Identity disturbance in borderline personality disorder#^p42731903-definition|Mullins 2026]]

**Onset and origins.** Features often emerge in adolescence or early adulthood, and a diagnosis can be made in young people who meet criteria. [[F84 NIMH Borderline personality disorder#^f84-onset|NIMH: borderline personality disorder]] Genetic factors and childhood adversity such as abuse both contribute to its origins. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-comorbidity|Leichsenring 2023]]

**Co-occurrence is the norm.** Most people with the diagnosis also have mood (83%), anxiety (85%) or substance use disorders (78%). [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-comorbidity|Leichsenring 2023]] With co-occurrence this common, features credited to borderline personality disorder may partly belong to the conditions that accompany it. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Leichsenring 2023]]

**Course: symptoms remit, functioning lags.** In a ten-year study of 175 treatment-seeking adults, 85% remitted and 12% relapsed, with remission coming more slowly than in major depression. [[P21464343 Gunderson 2011 Ten-year course of borderline personality disorder#^p21464343-remission|Gunderson 2011]] All criteria declined at similar rates, but social functioning stayed severely impaired and improved only modestly. [[P21464343 Gunderson 2011 Ten-year course of borderline personality disorder#^p21464343-function|Gunderson 2011]] Remission here means falling below the diagnostic threshold, so remission rates alone overstate how well people are doing. [[P21464343 Gunderson 2011 Ten-year course of borderline personality disorder#^p21464343-caution-remission|Appraisal: Gunderson 2011]]

**Treatment at class level: structured psychotherapies.** Evidence-based psychotherapies are the primary approach. [[F84 NIMH Borderline personality disorder#^f84-treatment|NIMH: borderline personality disorder]] Against usual care, psychotherapies tailored to the disorder lowered symptom severity (standardised mean difference -0.52, moderate-quality evidence), while smaller gains in self-harm, suicide-related outcomes and functioning rested on low-quality evidence. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-tau|Storebø 2020]] Dialectical behaviour therapy, about a third of all trials, reduced severity and self-harm and improved functioning compared with usual care, each estimate resting on a handful of small, low-quality trials; therapy models did not clearly differ, and harms could not be judged. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-dbt|Storebø 2020]] [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-models|Storebø 2020]] A clinical review reports medium effects over usual care for dialectical behaviour therapy and for psychodynamic therapy. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-treatment|Leichsenring 2023]] Outside borderline samples, dialectical behaviour therapy reduced suicide attempts in adolescents with bipolar disorder while mood improved equally in both arms. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-mood|Goldstein 2024]]

**Medication at class level.** No medication consistently improves the core features; medication is kept for severe co-occurring disorders or brief crisis management, and is used for specific symptoms rather than for the disorder itself. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-treatment|Leichsenring 2023]] [[F84 NIMH Borderline personality disorder#^f84-treatment|NIMH: borderline personality disorder]]

**The differential with bipolar II.** Biological comparisons - genetics, epigenetics, daily rhythms and imaging - show suggestive differences that are not yet good enough to guide diagnosis. [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-biology|Bayes 2019]] The more useful discriminators are family history, developmental antecedents, illness course, how mood states are experienced, personality style and relationships, while impulsivity, neuropsychological profile, sex distribution, co-occurring conditions and treatment response separate the two less well. [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-discriminators|Bayes 2019]] [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-weak|Bayes 2019]] These are patterns across studies rather than validated decision rules, and the two conditions can co-occur. [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-caution-rules|Appraisal: Bayes 2019]]

**The differential with complex PTSD.** Complex PTSD and borderline personality disorder share affect dysregulation, negative self-concept and relationship difficulty. [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-overlap|Atkinson 2024]] Most studies comparing them found distinct symptom profiles, and people who meet both are likely to report earlier and more frequent interpersonal trauma and greater impairment. [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-distinct|Atkinson 2024]] [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-comorbid|Atkinson 2024]] Distinct profiles at group level do not decide how an individual presentation should be labelled. [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-caution-distinct|Appraisal: Atkinson 2024]]

**Overlap with autism.** Autistic girls and women without intellectual disability are often diagnosed late, with borderline personality disorder or traits a common mislabel, because similar behaviour can have different drivers. [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-misdiagnosis|McQuaid 2024]] [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-distinctions|McQuaid 2024]] Emotion dysregulation and self-harm are higher in autistic adults than in controls but milder than in borderline personality disorder, with sensory sensitivity and camouflaging as predictors specific to the autistic group. [[P38110995 Bemmouna 2023 Emotion dysregulation in autistic adults#^p38110995-levels|Bemmouna 2023]] [[P38110995 Bemmouna 2023 Emotion dysregulation in autistic adults#^p38110995-specific|Bemmouna 2023]]

**Reading rule.** Name the diagnostic system and instrument, check which co-occurring conditions the sample carried, and keep remission of criteria apart from recovery of functioning; for differentials, weigh course, family history and development over single features such as impulsivity. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-caution-overlap|Appraisal: Leichsenring 2023]] [[P21464343 Gunderson 2011 Ten-year course of borderline personality disorder#^p21464343-caution-remission|Appraisal: Gunderson 2011]] [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-caution-rules|Appraisal: Bayes 2019]]

## Evidence and status

Treatment evidence is the strongest part: a Cochrane review of 75 randomised trials supports tailored psychotherapy over usual care, but only the effect on symptom severity passed its threshold for clinical relevance, and most other estimates are low certainty. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-tau|Storebø 2020]] [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-caution-certainty|Appraisal: Storebø 2020]] Course evidence comes from treatment-seeking cohorts followed with repeated standardised interviews. [[P21464343 Gunderson 2011 Ten-year course of borderline personality disorder#^p21464343-limit|Gunderson 2011]] The bipolar II and complex PTSD differentials rest on narrative and systematic reviews without pooled accuracy figures, and the autism overlap on a conceptual analysis and one cross-sectional study with self-reported diagnoses. [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-limit|Bayes 2019]] [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-limit|Atkinson 2024]] [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-caution-conceptual|Appraisal: McQuaid 2024]] [[P38110995 Bemmouna 2023 Emotion dysregulation in autistic adults#^p38110995-limit|Bemmouna 2023]]

## Uncertainties

- Whether borderline personality disorder, complex PTSD and some presentations of autism in women are distinct conditions, overlapping ones, or different descriptions of shared difficulties. [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-caution-distinct|Appraisal: Atkinson 2024]] [[P40018062 McQuaid 2024 Borderline personality and missed autism diagnosis#^p40018062-caution-conceptual|Appraisal: McQuaid 2024]]
- Which features separate bipolar II from borderline personality disorder in an individual, with known accuracy. [[P31749106 Bayes 2019 Bipolar II versus borderline personality disorder#^p31749106-caution-rules|Appraisal: Bayes 2019]]
- How far therapy results from borderline samples transfer to bipolar, ADHD, autistic or complex-trauma populations. [[P32368793 Storebo 2020 Psychological therapies for borderline personality disorder#^p32368793-caution-certainty|Appraisal: Storebø 2020]]
- Why social functioning improves so much less than symptoms over the long term. [[P21464343 Gunderson 2011 Ten-year course of borderline personality disorder#^p21464343-function|Gunderson 2011]]

## Recent research

- **2026 · Scoping review (Journal of Psychiatric Research).** Mapped how identity disturbance is defined and measured, finding DSM-5's unstable self-image and relationships the usual definition. [[P42731903 Mullins 2026 Identity disturbance in borderline personality disorder#^p42731903-definition|Mullins 2026]]
- **2024 · Randomised clinical trial (JAMA Psychiatry).** Dialectical behaviour therapy reduced suicide attempts in adolescents with bipolar disorder while mood improved equally in both arms, a structured borderline therapy acting on one target outside borderline samples. [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-suicide|Goldstein 2024]] [[P37703037 Goldstein 2024 DBT for adolescents with bipolar disorder#^p37703037-caution-scope|Appraisal: Goldstein 2024]]
- **2024 · Systematic review (Personality Disorders).** Most comparisons found distinct symptom profiles for complex PTSD and borderline personality disorder, the one exception drawing on a population without severe trauma. [[P38753372 Atkinson 2024 CPTSD versus borderline personality disorder#^p38753372-distinct|Atkinson 2024]]
- **2023 · Clinical review (JAMA).** Summarises psychotherapy as the treatment of choice with medium effects and finds no medication that consistently improves core features. [[P36853245 Leichsenring 2023 Borderline personality disorder review#^p36853245-treatment|Leichsenring 2023]]
