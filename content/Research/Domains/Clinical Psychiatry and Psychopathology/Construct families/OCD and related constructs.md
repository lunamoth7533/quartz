# OCD and related constructs

## Definition

Obsessive-compulsive disorder is characterised by obsessions - unwanted repeated thoughts or urges - and compulsions, which are repetitive behaviours or mental acts performed in response. [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-pattern|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]]

## How it works

**Course and co-occurrence.** Symptoms may begin in childhood or adulthood, fluctuate over time, and frequently co-occur with anxiety, depression and other conditions. [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-course|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]]

**Reinforcement account.** Compulsions can briefly relieve anxiety. In a cognitive-behavioural interpretation, that relief can negatively reinforce the response and help maintain the pattern, so an operant-learning account offers one explanation for why relief can maintain rituals. This applies a learning framework to symptoms; it is not a complete account of every person's OCD. [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-cycle|OCD publication]] [[F10 Noba Conditioning and learning#^f10-operant|Operant learning]]

**Treatment logic.** Exposure and response prevention applies extinction principles deliberately. The connection with extinction research is conceptual: learning can change a response without implying that the original association has simply been erased. [[Fear learning and extinction]]

**Limits of a single mechanism diagram.** A cycle of intrusion, anxiety, ritual and relief is a useful formulation, but a formulation is not a diagnostic test or proof of a single cause, and a symptom pattern described in a public overview is not a mechanism. NIMH describes contributions from genetics, biology and environmental factors and notes that causes are not fully understood. Research can examine learning, neural circuitry and clinical symptoms at different levels without assuming that one description replaces the others. Repetitive behaviour also occurs in other conditions, so its form alone does not determine its function, meaning or diagnostic context. [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-pattern|OCD publication]] [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-limit|Scope of the publication]] [[Levels of analysis]]

**Insight varies along the continuum.** Obsessional beliefs range from good insight to absent insight, and insight level is clinically informative - it predicts presentation and shapes how the cognitive piece of treatment is delivered - without changing the cycle structure above. Continuum language here is a measurement description, not a softening of the construct. [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-cycle|Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder]] [[Categorical versus dimensional classification]]

**Classification and its moving boundary.** OCD is classified separately from anxiety disorders in current systems, illustrating that categories are organised by pattern and mechanism rather than by shared emotion. [[F47 OpenStax Psychology 2e psychological disorders#^f47-definition|What Are Psychological Disorders?]] The boundary around obsessive-compulsive presentations has moved between systems, so claims about prevalence or boundary depend on, and have to name, the system and instrument used. Co-occurrence with other conditions is common and is one of the observations motivating dimensional alternatives to strict categories. [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-use|ICD-11 CDDR]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]] [[P24808851 Leitner 2014 Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD#^p24808851-clinical|Co-occurrence of autism and ADHD]]

**Network and dimensional readings.** If symptoms interact directly, an OCD presentation can be described as a network in which obsessions and compulsions strengthen one another, and comorbidity becomes overlap between networks rather than a second entity. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-claim|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|Network theory]]

**Reading rule.** Keep the descriptive pattern, the reinforcement mechanism and the treatment evidence separate; and when a claim compares 'OCD' groups across studies, name the instrument and system, since the construct boundary is not fixed. [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-practice|Measurement invariance]]

## Evidence and status

The construct has established reliability and treatment evidence; boundaries with related repetitive-behaviour presentations are less clear. NIMH describes exposure and response prevention as a CBT approach with evidence for reducing compulsive behaviour, and it is the best-established psychological treatment for the construct. The learning account and the treatment evidence are related but distinct claims: clinical improvement does not establish that reinforcement is the only mechanism of change, and questions about threat expectations, distress, avoidance and habits require their own measures and study designs. [[F82 NIMH Obsessive-compulsive disorder#^f82-cycle|OCD publication]] [[F10 Noba Conditioning and learning#^f10-operant|Operant learning]] The network reading is a theoretical proposal with early support, not an installed classification. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-status|Network theory]]
