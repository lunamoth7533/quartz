## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The disorder affects roughly 0.7% to 2.7% of US adults and involves abrupt shifts in identity, relationships and affect, impulsivity, intense anger, emptiness, suicidal behaviour, self-injury, stress-related paranoia and severe dissociation.
^p36853245-features

Most affected people also have mood (83%), anxiety (85%) or substance use disorders (78%), and both genetic factors and childhood adversity contribute.
^p36853245-comorbidity

Psychotherapy is the treatment of choice: dialectical behaviour therapy and psychodynamic therapy outperform usual care with medium effects, no medication consistently improves core features, and medication is kept for severe co-occurring disorders or brief crisis management.
^p36853245-treatment

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

With co-occurrence this common, samples of people with borderline personality disorder are rarely free of other diagnoses, so features credited to the disorder may partly belong to the conditions that accompany it; the prevalence range also reflects different instruments and samples.
