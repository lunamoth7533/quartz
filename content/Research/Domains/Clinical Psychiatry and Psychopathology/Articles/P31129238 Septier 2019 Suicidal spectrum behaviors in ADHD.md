# Association between suicidal spectrum behaviors and Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A systematic review and meta-analysis.

**Authors.** Septier M, Stordeur C, et al. (5 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31129238/)

**Published.** 2019 in Neuroscience and biobehavioral reviews (first online 2019-05-24).

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.1016/j.neubiorev.2019.05.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2019.05.022) · PMID [31129238](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31129238/)

**Verification.** Abstract-level check via PubMed on 2026-09-27 (metadata matched by PMID). Full text was not read; read status is queued.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Compared with people without ADHD, people with ADHD had higher odds of suicide attempts (pooled odds ratio 2.37), suicidal ideation (3.53), suicide plans (4.54) and death by suicide (6.69).
^p31129238-association

Pooling adjusted instead of crude odds ratios changed the results little, and the findings were generally robust in sensitivity analyses.
^p31129238-robust

## Scope as reported

A pre-registered random-effects meta-analysis of 57 studies with Newcastle-Ottawa quality ratings; heterogeneity was very high for attempts (I² about 98%) and for death by suicide (about 88%).
^p31129238-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Adjusted estimates control only for what each study measured; co-occurring depression, substance use and conduct problems may carry part of the association, which a pooled odds ratio cannot apportion.
^p31129238-caution-confounding
