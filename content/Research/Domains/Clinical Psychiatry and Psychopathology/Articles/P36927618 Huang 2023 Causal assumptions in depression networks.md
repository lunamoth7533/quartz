# Depression networks: a systematic review of the network paradigm causal assumptions.

**Authors.** Huang D, Susser E, et al. (4 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36927618/)

**Published.** 2023 in Psychological medicine (volume 53, issue 5, pages 1665-1680).

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review notes that the network paradigm assumes causal relations among symptoms, while most studies analyse only correlation structure.
^p36927618-premise

Of 41 studies, five (12%) addressed confounding other than measurement error, five checked measurement error after the fact, and not one discussed positivity.
^p36927618-assumptions

The authors conclude that the assumptions needed for causal claims were seldom discussed or tested, which undermines the credibility of network findings on depression.
^p36927618-conclusion

## Scope as reported

Systematic review of depression network studies only, assessed against causal-inference assumptions (measurement error, exchangeability and positivity).
^p36927618-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The audit covers depression studies only, but the assumptions it checks apply to any symptom network, including trauma networks, so central-symptom claims should be read as descriptions of association unless those assumptions are addressed.
^p36927618-caution-scope
