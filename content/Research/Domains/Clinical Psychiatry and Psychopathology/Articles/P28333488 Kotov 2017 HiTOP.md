## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The paper argues that categorical taxonomies suffer from arbitrary thresholds, unclear boundaries between disorders, frequent co-occurrence, within-disorder heterogeneity and diagnostic instability.
^p28333488-problem

HiTOP groups syndromes that covary into spectra, and characterises them dimensionally, which is intended to reduce heterogeneity and map comorbidity explicitly.
^p28333488-structure

It presents the model as a research effort for measurement and aetiology, not as a finished clinical classification.
^p28333488-purpose
