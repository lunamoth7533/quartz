# Comparing the symptom presentation similarities and differences of complex posttraumatic stress disorder and borderline personality disorder: A systematic review.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review starts from the shared features of affect dysregulation, negative self-concept and relationship difficulty, and from the debate over whether complex PTSD is distinct from borderline personality disorder.
^p38753372-overlap

Most included studies found distinct symptom profiles for the two conditions; the one study finding no difference used a population without severe trauma.
^p38753372-distinct

The conditions can co-occur, and people with both are likely to report earlier and more frequent interpersonal trauma and to show greater functional impairment.
^p38753372-comorbid

## Scope as reported

Narrative systematic review; the abstract does not report the number of included studies or any pooled estimate.
^p38753372-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Distinct profiles at group level support keeping the categories separate for description; they do not settle how an individual presentation should be labelled, which still depends on the instrument and on developmental and trauma history.
^p38753372-caution-distinct
