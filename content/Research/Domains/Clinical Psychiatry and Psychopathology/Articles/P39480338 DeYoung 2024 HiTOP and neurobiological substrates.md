# The hierarchical taxonomy of psychopathology and the search for neurobiological substrates of mental illness: A systematic review and roadmap for future research.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Replicated neuroimaging results were found for constructs at five levels of the hierarchy: the overarching p-factor, the externalising superspectrum, the thought-disorder and internalising spectra, the distress subfactor, and the depression symptom dimension.
^p39480338-replicated

The authors argue that dimensional targets are more promising for clinical neuroscience than diagnoses, while noting the limitations of a young field.
^p39480338-limits

They describe how HiTOP could be combined with RDoC and other transdiagnostic research frameworks.
^p39480338-integration

## Scope as reported

Systematic review restricted to imaging studies with at least 194 participants; the abstract reports no pooled effect sizes.
^p39480338-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

Replication at a level of the hierarchy shows that dimensional scores relate to brain measures in large samples; it does not show that the dimensions are biologically separate kinds, and such associations are group-level.
^p39480338-caution-dimensions
