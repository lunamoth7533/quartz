## Reported findings (checked full text)

The paper argues that stigma functions as a fundamental cause of health inequality because it constrains access to resources such as employment, housing and healthcare.
^p23488505-argument

It distinguishes structural, interpersonal and internalised forms of stigma and links each to measurable outcomes.
^p23488505-levels

The implication is that interventions targeting only individual behaviour will miss mechanisms operating at the level of law, policy and institutions.
^p23488505-implication
