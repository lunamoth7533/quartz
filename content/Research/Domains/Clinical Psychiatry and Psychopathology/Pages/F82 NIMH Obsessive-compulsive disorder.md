## Key points (as published)

OCD is described through obsessions - unwanted repeated thoughts or urges - and compulsions - repetitive behaviours or mental acts performed in response.
^f82-pattern

Compulsions may temporarily relieve anxiety. The publication describes exposure and response prevention as a CBT approach that can reduce compulsive behaviours.
^f82-cycle

Symptoms may begin in childhood or adulthood, vary over time, and often co-occur with anxiety, depression or other conditions.
^f82-course
