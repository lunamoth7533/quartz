## Key points (as published)

Schizophrenia is described as a serious mental illness involving psychosis, with changes in perception, thought and behaviour that interfere with functioning.
^f83-psychosis

Symptoms vary between people and are grouped as psychotic, negative and cognitive in this publication.
^f83-features

The page notes that onset is often in late adolescence or early adulthood, that early treatment matters for course, and that research continues into mechanisms.
^f83-course
