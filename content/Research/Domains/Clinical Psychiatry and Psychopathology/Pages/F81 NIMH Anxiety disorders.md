## Key points (as published)

Anxiety disorders are presented as a family that includes generalised anxiety, panic disorder, and several phobia and fear-related patterns.
^f81-family

Features described include persistent, excessive worry or fear that interferes with daily life, distinct from ordinary situational anxiety.
^f81-features

Treatment approaches described include psychotherapy, medication and combinations, with research on mechanisms and outcomes referenced generally.
^f81-treatment
