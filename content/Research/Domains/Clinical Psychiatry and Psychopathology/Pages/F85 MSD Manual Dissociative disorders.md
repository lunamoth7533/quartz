# Overview of Dissociative Disorders

**Publisher.** MSD Manual Professional Version

## Key points (as published)

Dissociative disorders are described as a group in which normally integrated functions of consciousness, memory, identity or perception are disrupted.
^f85-group

The professional overview stresses the differential from trauma- and stressor-related presentations and from neurological and substance-related causes.
^f85-differential

It cites functional-imaging research as an active area, noting that findings describe group patterns and are not diagnostic tests.
^f85-biology

## Scope and limitations

A professional reference overview. It is a clinical teaching resource rather than a systematic review, and it describes categories at the level of current practice.
^f85-limit
