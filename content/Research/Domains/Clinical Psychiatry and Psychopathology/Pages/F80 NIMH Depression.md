## Key points (as published)

NIMH describes depression as a common but serious mood disorder and reports that it can affect how a person feels, thinks and handles daily activity.
^f80-scale

The page distinguishes major depressive episodes from persistent depressive disorder and from bipolar depression, which matters because treatment implications differ.
^f80-patterns

It summarises research directions including genetics, brain circuits, and treatment studies, and directs readers to clinicians for individual decisions.
^f80-research
