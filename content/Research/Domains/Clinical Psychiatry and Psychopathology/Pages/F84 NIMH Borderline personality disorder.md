# Borderline Personality Disorder

**Publisher.** National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)

## Key points (as published)

The page describes borderline personality disorder through long-standing instability in mood, self-image and relationships, with marked sensitivity to rejection and abandonment.
^f84-pattern

It notes that features often emerge in adolescence or early adulthood and that diagnosis in young people is possible when criteria are met.
^f84-onset

Evidence-based psychotherapies are described as the primary approach, with medication sometimes used for specific symptoms rather than for the disorder itself.
^f84-treatment

## Scope and limitations

An official public education page. It describes the construct and treatment approaches at a general level.
