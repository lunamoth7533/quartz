# Categorical versus dimensional classification

## Definition

Categorical classification assigns people to groups; dimensional classification scores them along continuous variables. Most psychopathology measures are dimensional in their raw form, while clinical systems use categories with thresholds.

## How it works

**What categories buy.** Categories compress information into a shared label, they let clinical services and treatment trials define who is being studied, and the evidence base in most of this library is organised by them. ICD-11's clinical descriptions provide essential features, boundaries and course qualifiers for each category precisely so that the label means the same thing to different clinicians. [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-structure|ICD-11 CDDR]] [[F93 WHO ICD-11 CDDR#^f93-purpose|ICD-11 CDDR]]

**What categories cost.** Because a threshold has to fall somewhere, categories produce arbitrary thresholds and unclear boundaries between disorders, frequent co-occurrence, within-disorder heterogeneity and diagnostic instability - people near a boundary can move between labels across assessments. These are the documented weaknesses that motivated dimensional models. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]]

**Dimensional research frameworks.** HiTOP groups syndromes that covary into dimensional spectra and characterises phenomena dimensionally, so that heterogeneity is represented rather than averaged away and comorbidity is mapped explicitly, with the explicit framing that this is a research programme rather than an installed clinical system. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-purpose|HiTOP]] The NIMH RDoC framework organises research around dimensions of functioning across units of analysis from genes to self-report, likewise explicitly as a research approach rather than a clinical system. [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-purpose|Research Domain Criteria]] [[F122 NIMH RDoC constructs#^f122-domains|Definitions of the RDoC Domains and Constructs]]

**A third option: the network approach.** The network approach treats symptoms as interacting components rather than as indicators of one latent disorder - the disorder is the network - and predicts hysteresis and sudden transitions, treating comorbidity as overlap between symptom networks rather than as distinct co-occurring entities. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-claim|A network theory of mental disorders]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-consequences|A network theory of mental disorders]]

**Dimensional alternatives are not assumption-free.** A spectrum has to be measured, and comparing scores across groups or over time assumes measurement invariance - configural, metric and scalar constraints that are testable and often untested. Partial or absent invariance changes what a comparison means, whether the comparison is categorical or dimensional. [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-definition|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-levels|Measurement invariance]] [[P27942093 Putnick 2016 Measurement invariance#^p27942093-practice|Measurement invariance]]

**Each system wins where its assumptions hold.** Categories communicate efficiently, match service and regulatory structures, and work where a qualitative break exists or is a useful convention; dimensions preserve information, avoid threshold artefacts, and work where variation is continuous with graded risk. The documented pathologies - arbitrary thresholds, heterogeneity within categories, co-occurrence across them - are the category system's costs; complexity and communication burden are the dimension system's. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-problem|HiTOP]] [[F47 OpenStax Psychology 2e psychological disorders#^f47-cultural|What Are Psychological Disorders]]

**The hybrid is the current settlement, not a truce of convenience.** Clinical systems remain categorical because they support decisions and communication: practice uses categorical diagnoses for communication and service decisions while research increasingly measures dimensions alongside them, and several classifications now add dimensional measures beneath categorical diagnoses. The HiTOP-style research spectra are the dimensional research programme's concrete output; the categorical system persists because its functions - coding, coverage, communication - are real. [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-purpose|HiTOP]] [[P28333488 Kotov 2017 HiTOP#^p28333488-structure|HiTOP]] [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-matrix|Research Domain Criteria]]

**Where the argument actually matters for a reader.** Descriptive claims such as 'these features cluster together' survive either framework; claims about thresholds, prevalence and diagnostic stability are framework-dependent; and treatment evidence is usually organised by whatever definition a trial used. Naming the model a statement depends on is the practical discipline this note asks for. [[F121 NIMH RDoC#^f121-scope|RDoC]]

## Evidence and status

The empirical case for dimensionality is strong for many constructs; how to translate dimensions into decisions is unresolved, which is why hybrid models are proposed. The network approach is a theoretical proposal with early empirical support, not a replacement for classification in clinical use, and its own literature discusses its limitations. [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-status|Network theory]] [[P28127906 Borsboom 2017 Network theory#^p28127906-limit|Network theory]]
