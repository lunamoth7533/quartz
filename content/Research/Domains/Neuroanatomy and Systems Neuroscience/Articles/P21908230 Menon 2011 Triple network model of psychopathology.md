# Large-scale brain networks and psychopathology: a unifying triple network model.

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review presents large-scale network analysis as a new way to study problems of thinking and emotion across psychiatric and neurological conditions, and summarises methods for describing networks that work abnormally.
^p21908230-paradigm

Difficulty accessing, engaging and disengaging large-scale neurocognitive networks is described as prominent in several conditions, among them schizophrenia, depression, anxiety, dementia and autism.
^p21908230-disorders

The author proposes a triple network model in which aberrant mapping of what is salient underlies cognitive dysfunction, and points to parallels emerging between psychiatric and neurological disorders.
^p21908230-model

## Scope as reported

Single-author theoretical review synthesising imaging and network-analysis research; the model is a proposal, and the abstract reports no systematic search or pooled estimate.
^p21908230-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

A model that applies across many disorders explains shared features but, by design, not what distinguishes one diagnosis from another; its network labels summarise patterns of covariation and are hypotheses about mechanism rather than demonstrations of one.
