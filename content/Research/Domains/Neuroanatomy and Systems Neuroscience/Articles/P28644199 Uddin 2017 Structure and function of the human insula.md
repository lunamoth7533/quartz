## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The insula is a region of cortex concealed deep in the lateral sulcus, and its subdivisions have been drawn from cell architecture, sulcal landmarks and patterns of connection.
^p28644199-location

How many subdivisions it has depends on the parcellation method, with published schemes ranging from 2 to 13.
^p28644199-parcels

Its roles range from sensory and affective processing to higher cognition, and the review draws on lesion cases and recent imaging to argue that function varies considerably across the region.
^p28644199-heterogeneity

## Scope as reported

Concise narrative review built around lesion case reports and recent human imaging; no systematic search or pooled estimate is reported.
^p28644199-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

Because the count of subdivisions changes with the parcellation method, a result reported for anterior or posterior insula depends on the atlas used, and two studies using different schemes may not be describing the same territory.
^p28644199-caution-parcellation
