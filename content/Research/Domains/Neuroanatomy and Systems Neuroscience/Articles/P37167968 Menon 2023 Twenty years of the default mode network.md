## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The review traces how the default mode network was discovered and how accounts of its function changed over two decades.
^p37167968-history

It gathers evidence tying the network to thinking about oneself and about others, to recalling personal events, to language and stored meaning, and to spontaneous mind wandering.
^p37167968-functions

The author proposes that the network combines remembered, linguistic and semantic content and shares it widely across the brain, building a coherent internal narrative that underpins the sense of self.
^p37167968-narrative

## Scope as reported

Single-author narrative review that includes a personal reflection; the abstract reports no systematic search or pooled estimate.
^p37167968-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The internal-narrative account is the author's synthesis and proposal. It organises many findings but remains a theory to test, and the network label itself summarises patterns of covariation rather than demonstrating a mechanism.
