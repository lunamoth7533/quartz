## Reported findings (checked abstract)

The authors describe the anterior insula together with the anterior cingulate cortex as a salience network, which singles out the most relevant internal and external events to steer behaviour.
^p20512370-salience

In their model the insula first detects salient events, then shifts activity between large-scale networks so that attention and working-memory resources can be recruited; anterior-posterior insula interaction tunes autonomic reactivity, and close coupling with anterior cingulate gives fast access to motor output.
^p20512370-switching

The anterior insula is cast as a hub between networks for outward-directed attention and for self-related thought, offered as an economical explanation of insula function in neurotypical adults.
^p20512370-hub

## Scope as reported

Theoretical review proposing a network model from imaging and network-analysis evidence in neurotypical adults; its relevance to disorders of affective and social cognition is suggested rather than tested.
^p20512370-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the source.

The switching role is inferred largely from correlational imaging. Showing that the insula causes transitions between networks, rather than changing alongside them, needs lesion, stimulation or other interventional evidence.
^p20512370-caution-model
