# Hemispheric divergence of interoceptive processing across psychiatric disorders.

**Authors.** Adamic EM, Teed AR, et al. (5 authors) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39535878/)

**Published.** 2024 in eLife.

**Identifiers.** DOI [10.7554/eLife.92820](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.92820) · PMID [39535878](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39535878/) · PMCID [PMC11560129](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11560129/)

## Reported findings (checked abstract)

Two tasks - voluntary attention to heartbeat and breathing, and double-blind isoproterenol infusions that drive cardiorespiratory sensations from the body - both activated the granular and ventral dysgranular insula in both hemispheres.
^p39535878-insula

The patient group showed an asymmetric left-right pattern of overlap between the two tasks in the dysgranular insula, and less anterior agranular insula activation while anticipating sensations that never arrived.
^p39535878-asymmetry

Post-task changes in insula connectivity tracked anxiety and depression severity, and the authors propose the dysgranular mid-insula as the site where attention, prediction and bodily input meet.
^p39535878-interface

## Scope as reported

Preregistered functional MRI study of 46 patients with anxiety, depression and/or eating disorders and 46 propensity-matched controls, with top-down and bottom-up interoceptive tasks in the same scan session; the isoproterenol perturbation was double-blinded and placebo-controlled.
^p39535878-limit

## Library appraisal

These paragraphs are this library's own methodological appraisal, not statements from the paper.

The patient group covered anxiety, depression and eating disorders, not bipolar I, ADHD, autism or complex PTSD, so the findings cannot be carried over to those conditions without their own studies. Group activation maps also give no individual readout of interoception.
^p39535878-caution-scope
