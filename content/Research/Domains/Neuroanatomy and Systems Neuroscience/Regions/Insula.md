# Insula

## Definition

The insula is a region of cortex concealed deep in the lateral sulcus, divided on the basis of cell architecture, sulcal landmarks and connections into anywhere from 2 to 13 subdivisions depending on the method. [[P28644199 Uddin 2017 Structure and function of the human insula#^p28644199-location|Uddin 2017]] [[P28644199 Uddin 2017 Structure and function of the human insula#^p28644199-parcels|Uddin 2017]] It is the cortical target of the pathways that report the body's internal state, and its anterior part, with the anterior cingulate cortex, is described as the hub of a salience network. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Craig 2003]] [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-salience|Menon 2010]]

## How it works

**One name, several territories.** The insula's roles range from sensory and affective processing to higher cognition, and lesion cases and imaging show considerable variation in function across the region. [[P28644199 Uddin 2017 Structure and function of the human insula#^p28644199-heterogeneity|Uddin 2017]] Current imaging work describes it in cell-architectural zones: bodily-sensation tasks engage granular and ventral dysgranular insula, and anticipation engages anterior agranular insula. [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-insula|Adamic 2024]] [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-asymmetry|Adamic 2024]] Because the number of subdivisions changes with the parcellation, "anterior insula" in one study and another may not be the same territory. [[P28644199 Uddin 2017 Structure and function of the human insula#^p28644199-caution-parcellation|Appraisal: Uddin 2017]]

**Interoception runs from back to front.** A dedicated interoceptive system carries homeostatic signals from all tissues, and its primary cortical representation is placed in the dorsal posterior insula, where it gives rise to feelings such as pain, temperature, itch, hunger, thirst and air hunger. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-system|Craig 2003]] [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-insula|Craig 2003]] A re-representation in the right anterior insula is proposed as the basis for the felt sense of the bodily self and of emotion. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Craig 2003]] In an imaging study, attending to heartbeat and breathing and drug-induced cardiorespiratory sensations both activated insula in both hemispheres, and the authors place the meeting point of attention, prediction and bodily input in the dysgranular mid-insula. [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-insula|Adamic 2024]] [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-interface|Adamic 2024]]

**Salience and switching.** In the network model, the anterior insula detects salient events and then shifts activity between large-scale networks so that attention and working-memory resources can be recruited; interaction between anterior and posterior insula tunes autonomic reactivity, and coupling with anterior cingulate gives rapid access to motor output. [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-switching|Menon 2010]] The anterior insula is cast as a hub between networks for outward-directed attention and for self-related thought. [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-hub|Menon 2010]] A meta-analysis of 206 value studies fits this reading: anterior insula showed value effects in both directions, interpreted as arousal or salience rather than value. [[P23507394 Bartra 2013 Valuation system meta-analysis#^p23507394-salience|Bartra 2013]] The switching role is inferred mainly from correlational imaging, so whether the insula causes network transitions is not established. [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-caution-model|Appraisal: Menon 2010]]

**Emotional awareness and alexithymia.** The right-anterior re-representation is the anatomical basis most often proposed for knowing what one feels. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-self|Craig 2003]] Alexithymia, difficulty identifying and describing feelings, is linked to self-reported interoception, with the direction of the association depending on the questionnaire. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-direction|Van Bael 2024]] [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-facets|Van Bael 2024]] Both sides of that link are self-reports, so it describes how questionnaires relate rather than how the insula processes bodily signals. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-caution-method|Appraisal: Van Bael 2024]] Task and self-report also dissociate in patients: in 258 people using mental health services, heartbeat-task accuracy and confidence were lower than in comparison participants, while anxiety and depression went with higher self-rated interoceptive sensibility. [[P36709619 Critchley 2023 Cardiac interoception in mental health services#^p36709619-limit|Critchley 2023]] [[P36709619 Critchley 2023 Cardiac interoception in mental health services#^p36709619-accuracy|Critchley 2023]] [[P36709619 Critchley 2023 Cardiac interoception in mental health services#^p36709619-mismatch|Critchley 2023]]

**Psychiatric findings are shared, not specific.** Across PTSD, social anxiety disorder and specific phobia, negative emotional material evoked more amygdala and insula activity than in comparison groups, less consistently in PTSD than in the other two. [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-hyper|Etkin 2007]] [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-frequency|Etkin 2007]] In a mixed anxiety, depression and eating-disorder group, insular activity differed in its left-right pattern, and changes in insula connectivity tracked anxiety and depression severity. [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-asymmetry|Adamic 2024]] [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-interface|Adamic 2024]] Across six diagnoses including ADHD, autism and bipolar disorder, individual grey-matter deviations converged on the salience-ventral attention system, whose salience hub is the anterior insula. [[P37580620 Segal 2023 Heterogeneity of brain abnormalities#^p37580620-circuits|Segal 2023]] [[P37580620 Segal 2023 Heterogeneity of brain abnormalities#^p37580620-diagnoses|Segal 2023]] [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-salience|Menon 2010]] In ADHD, a mega-analysis found stronger resting coupling between striatum and fronto-insular cortex, with effect sizes no larger than about d = 0.15. [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-striatal|Norman 2024]] [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-effect|Norman 2024]]

**Reading rule.** Name the insular zone and the atlas, the signal (task activation, resting coupling, grey matter) and whether interoception was measured by task, confidence or questionnaire; insular activity in a task is not evidence of interoceptive awareness or emotion unless the design isolates them, and a finding shared across diagnoses cannot explain what is specific to any one. [[P28644199 Uddin 2017 Structure and function of the human insula#^p28644199-caution-parcellation|Appraisal: Uddin 2017]] [[P36709619 Critchley 2023 Cardiac interoception in mental health services#^p36709619-caution-measures|Appraisal: Critchley 2023]]

## Evidence and status

The insula's location and its role as the cortical target of interoceptive pathways are anatomically grounded, although the posterior-to-anterior map is a model supported by converging evidence rather than a measurement. [[P12965300 Craig 2003 Interoception#^p12965300-limit|Craig 2003]] The salience-network account is a theoretical proposal from correlational imaging in neurotypical adults. [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-limit|Menon 2010]] Psychiatric findings are group-level, come from task and structural designs, and are shared across diagnoses; the interoception imaging sourced here was done in anxiety, depression and eating disorders, not in the four conditions this library covers. [[P37580620 Segal 2023 Heterogeneity of brain abnormalities#^p37580620-caution-average|Appraisal: Segal 2023]] [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-caution-scope|Appraisal: Adamic 2024]]

## Uncertainties

- Which parcellation best matches function; subdivision counts from 2 to 13 make cross-study comparison uncertain. [[P28644199 Uddin 2017 Structure and function of the human insula#^p28644199-parcels|Uddin 2017]]
- Whether the anterior insula causes switches between networks or only changes alongside them. [[P20512370 Menon 2010 Salience network model of insula function#^p20512370-caution-model|Appraisal: Menon 2010]]
- Whether alexithymia involves altered insular processing: the evidence sourced here links it to self-reported interoception, not to insular measurements.
- Whether the PTSD insular over-activity found in task studies applies to complex PTSD. [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-caution-cptsd|Appraisal: Etkin 2007]]

## Recent research

- **2024 · Preregistered fMRI case-control study (eLife).** Separates top-down attention to bodily signals from bottom-up drug-induced sensations within the insula and proposes the dysgranular mid-insula as the point where they meet, in anxiety, depression and eating disorders. [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-interface|Adamic 2024]] [[P39535878 Adamic 2024 Insula interoception across psychiatric disorders#^p39535878-limit|Adamic 2024]]
- **2024 · Systematic review and meta-analysis (PLoS One).** Shows that the alexithymia-interoception link changes direction with the interoception questionnaire, which limits any direct inference to insular function. [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-direction|Van Bael 2024]] [[P39509403 Van Bael 2024 Subjective interoception and alexithymia#^p39509403-limit|Van Bael 2024]]
- **2023 · Normative-modelling study (Nature Neuroscience).** Individual grey-matter deviations rarely shared a region but converged on the salience-ventral attention system across six diagnoses. [[P37580620 Segal 2023 Heterogeneity of brain abnormalities#^p37580620-regional|Segal 2023]] [[P37580620 Segal 2023 Heterogeneity of brain abnormalities#^p37580620-circuits|Segal 2023]]
- **2023 · Transdiagnostic cross-sectional study (Autonomic Neuroscience).** Heartbeat-task accuracy, confidence and self-report pulled apart in patients, so "interoception" is several measurable things. [[P36709619 Critchley 2023 Cardiac interoception in mental health services#^p36709619-mismatch|Critchley 2023]] [[P36709619 Critchley 2023 Cardiac interoception in mental health services#^p36709619-caution-measures|Appraisal: Critchley 2023]]

## Connections

- [[Interoception and autonomic pathways]] - the afferent route that ends here, from lamina I and brainstem relays to posterior insula, with the reading rule for interoceptive measures.
- [[Alexithymia]] - difficulty identifying feelings, linked to self-reported interoception; whether insular processing explains it has not been tested in the sources gathered here.
- [[Default mode, salience and executive networks]] - the salience network the anterior insula anchors, and the triple-network account of switching between inward and outward modes.
- [[Emotion regulation]] - the emotional-processing side: insular signals are proposed to contribute to felt emotion, which regulation strategies then act on.
- [[Autonomic regulation]] - the output side of the loop the insula monitors; anterior-posterior insular interaction is proposed to tune autonomic reactivity.
- [[Prefrontal cortex]] - the neighbouring frontal cortex; dorsomedial prefrontal cortex shares salience-like value signals with anterior insula, and fronto-insular coupling recurs in ADHD findings.
- [[Psychophysiology methods]] - the peripheral measures used to index interoceptive and emotional state, and the inference steps they require.

**Cross-domain connection (curation).** The insula is where this domain's interoceptive anatomy meets psychology's emotional-awareness constructs and the computational domain's network models; the condition domains draw on it for anxiety, trauma and neurodevelopmental findings that are shared across diagnoses, and the methods domain's measurement material decides whether a task, a confidence rating or a questionnaire is standing in for "interoception".
