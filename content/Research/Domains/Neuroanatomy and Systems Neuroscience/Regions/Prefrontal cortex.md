# Prefrontal cortex

## Definition

The prefrontal cortex is the anterior association cortex of the frontal lobe: it links information arriving from other cortical areas and is associated with executive processing, which is treated as a set of interacting functions rather than a single faculty. [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-areas|Association and Executive Processing]] [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-executive|Association and Executive Processing]] Clinical reviews divide it into dorsolateral, ventrolateral, medial and orbitofrontal sectors with different emphases, and it sits at the late-maturing end of a cortical development that lasts decades. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-subdivisions|Jones 2021]] [[P34270921 Sydnor 2021 Neurodevelopment of the association cortices#^p34270921-decades|Sydnor 2021]]

## How it works

**Sectors with different emphases.** Dorsolateral cortex is tied to working memory, goal-driven attention, set switching, planning and problem solving; ventrolateral cortex to inhibition and to selecting and monitoring responses; medial cortex to self-knowledge, motivation, emotion regulation and revising goals; orbitofrontal cortex to inhibition, personality and social and emotional reasoning. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-subdivisions|Jones 2021]] These are tendencies from lesion and imaging work, not one function per sector. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-caution-mapping|Appraisal: Jones 2021]]

**A hub inside networks.** Association cortex receives converging input from primary and unimodal areas. [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-areas|Association and Executive Processing]] Prefrontal cortex also works within large-scale networks, so executive failure can follow damage anywhere in a parietal-temporal-frontal system, as in an atypical dysexecutive form of Alzheimer disease. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-networks|Jones 2021]]

**Executive control and working memory.** Executive function is described as working memory, flexibility and inhibition exercised through top-down control of processes across the brain. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-networks|Jones 2021]] Prefrontal networks regulate attention, screen out distraction, inhibit impulses and plan, and their performance depends on arousal-linked noradrenaline and dopamine, with both too little and too much impairing function. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-networks|Arnsten 2009]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-catecholamines|Arnsten 2009]]

**Valuation.** Across 206 fMRI studies, ventromedial prefrontal cortex and anterior ventral striatum carried a signal that rose with the subjective value of options and outcomes, which the authors call a valuation system. [[P23507394 Bartra 2013 Valuation system meta-analysis#^p23507394-valuation|Bartra 2013]] Dorsomedial prefrontal cortex instead showed value effects in both directions, like the anterior insula, read as arousal or salience rather than value. [[P23507394 Bartra 2013 Valuation system meta-analysis#^p23507394-salience|Bartra 2013]] Value-related activity does not show that a region computes value, so inferring valuation from ventromedial activity in a new task is reverse inference. [[P23507394 Bartra 2013 Valuation system meta-analysis#^p23507394-caution-reverse|Appraisal: Bartra 2013]]

**Slow maturation.** Cortical development runs for decades, moving from sensorimotor towards transmodal association cortex, which serves executive, social-emotional and mentalising functions. [[P34270921 Sydnor 2021 Neurodevelopment of the association cortices#^p34270921-decades|Sydnor 2021]] [[P34270921 Sydnor 2021 Neurodevelopment of the association cortices#^p34270921-axis|Sydnor 2021]] In 1,033 youths aged 8-23, the age-related decline in intrinsic fMRI amplitude began at different ages along this axis and tracked intracortical myelin maturation. [[P36973514 Sydnor 2023 Sensorimotor-association axis development#^p36973514-limit|Sydnor 2023]] [[P36973514 Sydnor 2023 Sensorimotor-association axis development#^p36973514-axis|Sydnor 2023]] The evidence supports prefrontal development continuing into the twenties, but "mature at 25" compresses gradual group trajectories into one round number. [[P34270921 Sydnor 2021 Neurodevelopment of the association cortices#^p34270921-caution-age|Appraisal: Sydnor 2021]] This long plasticity is also presented as a source of vulnerability to developmental psychopathology. [[P34270921 Sydnor 2021 Neurodevelopment of the association cortices#^p34270921-risk|Sydnor 2021]]

**ADHD accounts.** The classic prefrontal account ties ADHD to catecholamine regulation: noradrenaline at postsynaptic alpha-2A receptors and moderate dopamine at D1 receptors support prefrontal function, imaging studies reported under-active prefrontal regions with weaker connections, and alpha-2A blockade in animals produced an ADHD-like profile. [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-mechanisms|Arnsten 2009]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-evidence|Arnsten 2009]] Larger recent samples point to circuits: a mega-analysis of 1,696 youths with ADHD and 6,737 controls found stronger resting coupling of the striatum, centred on the caudate, with fronto-insular and supplementary motor cortex, with the largest effect near d = 0.15. [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-limit|Norman 2024]] [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-striatal|Norman 2024]] [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-caudate|Norman 2024]] [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-effect|Norman 2024]] Effects that small leave the groups almost entirely overlapping, and mechanistic plausibility does not reduce ADHD to one transmitter deficit. [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-caution-effect|Appraisal: Norman 2024]] [[P19621976 Arnsten 2009 Prefrontal catecholamines and ADHD#^p19621976-caution-transfer|Appraisal: Arnsten 2009]]

**Bipolar accounts.** In 6,503 individuals, bipolar disorder was associated with thinner frontal, temporal and parietal cortex, with some of the largest effects in left pars opercularis and rostral middle frontal cortex; medication use and longer illness were also associated with cortical measures. [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-thickness|Hibar 2018]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|Hibar 2018]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-course|Hibar 2018]] Higher body mass index adds to bipolar-related thinning in many of the same regions, and medication load stays associated with thinner cortex after allowing for it, so these cross-sectional differences mix diagnosis with treatment and health. [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-additive|McWhinney 2023]] [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-medication|McWhinney 2023]] [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-caution-confounds|Appraisal: McWhinney 2023]]

**PTSD accounts.** A meta-analysis of emotional-processing studies found reduced activity in ventromedial prefrontal and dorsal and rostral anterior cingulate cortex only in PTSD, whereas amygdala and insula over-activity was shared with phobic disorders; the authors read this as emotional dysregulation beyond exaggerated fear. [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-ptsd|Etkin 2007]] [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-hyper|Etkin 2007]] A resilience review reports preliminary evidence that prefrontal cognitive control, with hippocampal pattern separation, supports the perception of safety after single-event stressors. [[P38483288 Kalisch 2024 Neurobiology of stress resilience#^p38483288-safety|Kalisch 2024]] A decade review judges PTSD imaging not ready for clinical practice, and PTSD findings cannot be relabelled as complex PTSD mechanisms. [[P38632413 Hinojosa 2024 PTSD neuroimaging progress#^p38632413-status|Hinojosa 2024]] [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-caution-cptsd|Appraisal: Etkin 2007]]

**Reading rule.** Before crediting "the prefrontal cortex" with a function or a disorder, name the sector, the measure (lesion, task activation, resting coupling or thickness), the population and the effect size; a group difference is not a deficit in every person, and activity does not prove a function. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-caution-mapping|Appraisal: Jones 2021]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-caution-groups|Appraisal: Hibar 2018]] [[P23507394 Bartra 2013 Valuation system meta-analysis#^p23507394-caution-reverse|Appraisal: Bartra 2013]]

## Evidence and status

Prefrontal involvement in executive processing is established from lesion and imaging work, while sector specialisations are tendencies. [[F109 Neuroscience Online association and executive processing#^f109-limit|Association and Executive Processing]] [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-limit|Jones 2021]] Valuation evidence comes from a coordinate meta-analysis of laboratory choice tasks, and developmental timing from group trajectories. [[P23507394 Bartra 2013 Valuation system meta-analysis#^p23507394-limit|Bartra 2013]] [[P36973514 Sydnor 2023 Sensorimotor-association axis development#^p36973514-caution-inference|Appraisal: Sydnor 2023]] Condition findings are group-level and small or confounded: ADHD coupling effects near d = 0.15, bipolar thinning entangled with medication and body mass, and PTSD task findings that predate the complex PTSD category. [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-effect|Norman 2024]] [[P28461699 Hibar 2018 ENIGMA cortical abnormalities in bipolar disorder#^p28461699-medication|Hibar 2018]] [[P17898336 Etkin 2007 Functional neuroimaging of anxiety meta-analysis#^p17898336-caution-cptsd|Appraisal: Etkin 2007]]

## Uncertainties

- How sharply prefrontal sectors specialise, given that executive functions depend on distributed networks. [[P34881727 Jones 2021 Executive dysfunction and the prefrontal cortex#^p34881727-networks|Jones 2021]]
- When prefrontal development can be called complete; the trajectories are gradual and estimated for groups. [[P34270921 Sydnor 2021 Neurodevelopment of the association cortices#^p34270921-caution-age|Appraisal: Sydnor 2021]]
- Whether frontal thinning in bipolar disorder reflects the illness, its treatment or associated health factors. [[P36846964 McWhinney 2023 Obesity and cortical morphology in bipolar disorder#^p36846964-caution-confounds|Appraisal: McWhinney 2023]]
- Which prefrontal differences in PTSD predate trauma and which follow it. [[P38632413 Hinojosa 2024 PTSD neuroimaging progress#^p38632413-questions|Hinojosa 2024]]

## Recent research

- **2026 · Multisite mediation analysis (JAMA Psychiatry).** In 19 cohorts, reported childhood trauma was associated with bipolar diagnosis, and medial orbitofrontal and superior frontal thickness were among the few significant mediators, but brain morphology carried less than 1% of the association. [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-limit|Tozzi 2026]] [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-association|Tozzi 2026]] [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-mediation|Tozzi 2026]] The small mediated share shows how little of a risk association prefrontal structure carries. [[P42234441 Tozzi 2026 Childhood trauma and bipolar brain morphology#^p42234441-caution-mediation|Appraisal: Tozzi 2026]]
- **2024 · Voxel-wise mega-analysis (The American Journal of Psychiatry).** Shifts the ADHD question from an under-active prefrontal region to cortico-striatal coupling, and shows how small the group differences are. [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-striatal|Norman 2024]] [[P38476041 Norman 2024 Subcortico-cortical connectivity in ADHD#^p38476041-effect|Norman 2024]]
- **2024 · Narrative review (Physiological Reviews).** Places prefrontal cognitive control among candidate protective processes after trauma, with conclusions the authors call preliminary. [[P38483288 Kalisch 2024 Neurobiology of stress resilience#^p38483288-safety|Kalisch 2024]] [[P38483288 Kalisch 2024 Neurobiology of stress resilience#^p38483288-limit|Kalisch 2024]]
