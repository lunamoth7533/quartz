# Visual Processing: Cortical Pathways

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 2 Chapter 15

**Source.** <https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/m/s2/chapter15.html>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F104.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

Visual signals reach cortex through the lateral geniculate nucleus and arrive first in the striate cortex of the occipital lobe.
^f104-pathway

Cortical neurons are described by properties such as orientation, direction and movement sensitivity, established largely by single-unit recording.
^f104-properties

Extrastriate and association areas are then described as stages that build on primary visual analysis rather than as separate modalities.
^f104-extrastriate

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on visual cortical processing, largely based on animal electrophysiology.
^f104-limit
