## Key points (as published)

Somatosensory processing divides into a discriminative-touch and proprioceptive channel and a spinothalamic channel carrying crude touch, pain and temperature.
^f103-division

Receptor types differ in adaptation and receptive-field properties, which is how the system separates sustained pressure from vibration or stretch.
^f103-receptors

The chapter relates receptor properties to clinical examination: testing vibration, position sense and pinprick samples different pathways.
^f103-exam

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter on somatosensory pathways and receptor physiology.
