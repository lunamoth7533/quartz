# Limbic System: Hippocampus

## Key points (as published)

The hippocampal formation comprises the dentate gyrus, the hippocampus proper and the subicular cortex, located in the medial temporal lobe.
^f61-formation

Information enters through the perforant pathway from the entorhinal cortex to granule cells of the dentate gyrus, continues by mossy fibres to CA3 pyramidal cells, and then by Schaffer collaterals to CA1.
^f61-circuit

The transverse slice used for the classic three-pathway circuit - perforant, mossy fibre and Schaffer collateral - is why the structure became the model preparation for studying synaptic plasticity.
^f61-slices

CA1 output passes through the subiculum into the alveus, fimbria and fornix, and efferents also run from the subiculum to the entorhinal cortex and amygdala; afferents and efferents travel in the same bundles.
^f61-output

Patient and animal evidence links hippocampal damage to memory impairment, and special properties of hippocampal pyramidal cells are discussed as candidate substrates.
^f61-memory
