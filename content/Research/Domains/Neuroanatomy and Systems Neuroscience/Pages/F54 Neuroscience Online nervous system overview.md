# Overview of the Nervous System

**Publisher.** Neuroscience Online, McGovern Medical School (UTHealth), Section 2 Chapter 1

**Source.** <https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/m/s2/chapter01.html>

**Verification.** text opened and checked on 2026-09-25; retrieval saved as `receipts/source-checks/F54.txt`. Educational or official reference material, not primary research.

## Key points (as published)

The chapter divides the nervous system into central and peripheral parts and describes the brain and spinal cord lying in the cranial cavity and vertebral canal respectively.
^f54-divisions

It works through transverse sections of the spinal cord, brain stem and diencephalon, and coronal sections of the hemispheres, so structures are learned as they appear in section.
^f54-sections

The central and peripheral systems are described as acting together to integrate sensory information and control motor and cognitive function.
^f54-integration

## Scope and limitations

An open textbook chapter. It teaches structural organisation using sections and does not cover functional imaging or clinical syndromes.
^f54-limit
