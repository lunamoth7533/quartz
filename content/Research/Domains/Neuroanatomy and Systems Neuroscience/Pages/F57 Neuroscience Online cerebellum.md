## Key points (as published)

The cerebellar cortex has three layers - granule cell, Purkinje cell and molecular - with Purkinje cells forming a single-cell-thick layer between the others.
^f57-layers

Granule cells give rise to parallel fibres that contact many Purkinje cells, while each Purkinje cell receives enormous convergence, giving the circuit its unusual fan-in and fan-out.
^f57-circuit

Climbing-fibre input and parallel-fibre activity together support forms of plasticity in cerebellar learning, and the chapter notes effects that appear over days.
^f57-learning
