# Limbic System: Amygdala

## Key points (as published)

The chapter describes the amygdala's connections with cortical, thalamic, hypothalamic and brain-stem structures, which is why it is treated as a hub rather than a single-function centre.
^f60-connections

Stimulation studies produce intense emotional responses such as fear or aggression, and irritative lesions in temporal-lobe epilepsy can produce panic-like episodes.
^f60-emotion

Fear conditioning and its extinction are used as experimental models of how emotional associations form and change, with autonomic measures as readouts.
^f60-fear

## Scope and limitations

A textbook chapter presenting lesion, stimulation and conditioning evidence, largely from animal work and clinical case observation.
^f60-limit
