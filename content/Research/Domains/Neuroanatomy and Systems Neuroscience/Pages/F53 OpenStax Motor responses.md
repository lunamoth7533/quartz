## Key points (as published)

Primary motor cortex sits immediately anterior to the central sulcus; premotor and supplementary areas contribute to planning, sequencing and contextual selection of movement.
^f53-cortical

Descending pathways carry commands from cortex and brain stem to lower motor neurons, with lateral and medial systems distinguished by their targets and functions.
^f53-descending

Reflex arcs are described as the basic units of motor output: a sensory input, an integration step, and a motor response that can occur with minimal cortical involvement.
^f53-reflex

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section. The hierarchy it presents is a useful frame, not a complete account of how voluntary movement is generated.
^f53-limit
