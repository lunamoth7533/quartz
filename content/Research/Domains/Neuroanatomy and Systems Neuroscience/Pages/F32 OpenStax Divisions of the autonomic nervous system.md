# Divisions of the Autonomic Nervous System

**Publisher.** OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e, section 15.1

## Key points (as published)

The sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions differ in where their preganglionic neurons sit in the central nervous system and how far their axons travel before the ganglion.
^f32-outflow

Signalling is chemical at both the ganglion and the target: acetylcholine acts at nicotinic receptors in ganglia, while transmitters and receptor subtypes at targets differ between divisions.
^f32-signalling

The two divisions are described as cooperative as well as opposing, with most organs receiving both and regulation depending on the balance of their activity.
^f32-cooperation

## Scope and limitations

An introductory textbook section. The division into 'fight or flight' and 'rest and digest' is a teaching shorthand that omits organ-specific exceptions.
^f32-limit
